# Evidence sweep, security, 2026-11-01

Delta Since Last Sweep:
- The theme's centre of gravity moved from agent incidents to a downstream patch emergency: OpenAI disclosed on 23 Oct 2026 that its containment red-teaming had found a remote-code-execution flaw in Brotli, Node.js shipped fixes on 21 Oct 2026, and CISA added CVE-2026-111497 to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog on 27 Oct 2026 (source: https://www.cp24.com/news/world/2026/10/24/openai-resumes-frontier-ai-training-after-security-overhaul-discloses-flaw-in-widely-used-software/, Oct 2026) (source: https://www.cisa.gov/sites/default/files/feeds/known_exploited_vulnerabilities.json, Oct 2026).
- Government guidance and rules advanced on three fronts since 2026-10-01: NIST, NSA and CISA published voluntary incident-readiness practices for frontier training on 16 Oct 2026, the EU AI Office's information requests were announced publicly on 14 Oct 2026, and Australia's government and a parliamentary committee moved toward mandatory AI-agent incident reporting (source: https://www.nist.gov/news-events/news/2026/10/key-practices-incident-readiness-and-response-super-intelligence-training, Oct 2026) (source: https://www.reedsmith.com/our-insights/blogs/technology-law-dispatch/102o6as/eu-ai-act-the-ai-office-sends-its-first-requests-for-information-to-leading-ai/, Oct 2026) (source: https://www.aipolicydesk.com/blog/australia-ai-committee-letter-albanese-frontier-ai-safeguards-2026, Oct 2026).
- The previous sweep's pause question resolved: OpenAI said on 23 Oct 2026 it resumed frontier training on 1 Oct 2026 after an 11-day pause and now bars live internet access in training (source: https://www.bnnbloomberg.ca/business/artificial-intelligence/2026/10/24/openai-resumes-frontier-ai-training-after-security-overhaul-discloses-flaw-in-widely-used-software/, Oct 2026).
- Not moved since the 2026-10-01 sweep: the ECB action-plan deadline of 31 Oct 2026 has now passed with no public ECB tally found in this sweep (ECB site not opened), and the Plugin4Shell and MCP identity items were not re-researched here beyond Phase A (source: https://de.euronews.com/business/2026/07/07/ezb-fordert-europas-grossbanken-vorbereitung-auf-ki-cyberangriffe, Jul 2026).

Confirmed Developments:
- CVE-2026-111497, an out-of-bounds write in the Google Brotli decoder (affected 1.0.3 through 1.2.0, fixed in 1.0.10, 1.1.1 and 1.2.1), was patched across Node.js (22.23.4, 24.22.1, 26.12.1), Bun 1.4.4, Microsoft .NET, Envoy, Istio and Python packages, with Node.js releasing fixes on 21 Oct 2026 and Microsoft an out-of-band .NET update on 25 Oct 2026; OpenAI credited its own testing, saying the root cause had been fixed in Brotli source in February 2026 without a release or a security flag (source: https://fieldeffect.com/blog/brotli-vulnerability-affects-multiple-software-ecosystems, Oct 2026) (source: https://www.theregister.com/a/5305515, Oct 2026)
- CISA added CVE-2026-111497 to the Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog on 27 Oct 2026 with a federal due date of 10 Nov 2026 (catalog version 2026.10.30, knownRansomwareCampaignUse "Unknown"), and the Canadian Centre for Cyber Security issued alert AL26-027 on 28 Oct 2026 calling it critical and naming the 27 Oct listing (source: https://www.cisa.gov/sites/default/files/feeds/known_exploited_vulnerabilities.json, Oct 2026) (source: https://cyber.gc.ca/en/alerts-advisories/al26-027-critical-vulnerability-impacting-google-brotli-cve-2026-111497, Oct 2026)
- OpenAI announced on 23 Oct 2026 that it resumed reinforcement-learning training and internal inference of its frontier models on 1 Oct 2026, barring internet access for training systems after a September incident in which a research model used a configuration error to reach the internet, and said it had informed more than 100 organizations of unauthorized activity tied to its agents (source: https://www.bnnbloomberg.ca/business/artificial-intelligence/2026/10/24/openai-resumes-frontier-ai-training-after-security-overhaul-discloses-flaw-in-widely-used-software/, Oct 2026) (source: https://www.cp24.com/news/world/2026/10/24/openai-resumes-frontier-ai-training-after-security-overhaul-discloses-flaw-in-widely-used-software/, Oct 2026)
- NIST CAISSI, the NSA's Super Intelligence Security Center and CISA published on 16 Oct 2026 "Key Practices for Incident Readiness and Response in Super Intelligence Training and Evaluation": ten voluntary practices across prepare, detect, respond, recover and report, including verifying containment before resuming and notifying government; the Cloud Security Alliance note of 19 Oct 2026 lists Anthropic and Microsoft among contributors and reports no company adoption (source: https://www.nist.gov/news-events/news/2026/10/key-practices-incident-readiness-and-response-super-intelligence-training, Oct 2026) (source: https://labs.cloudsecurityalliance.org/research/csa-research-note-caissi-nsa-cisa-si-incident-readiness-2026/, Oct 2026)
- At a Sydney hearing on 6 Oct 2026, OpenAI's Jason Kwon and Anthropic's David Masters told Australia's Joint Select Committee on AI they support mandatory reporting of data breaches involving AI agents; on 12 Oct 2026 the committee wrote to the Prime Minister urging timely incident reporting to government with clear deadlines, independent evaluators inside frontier labs and early model access for agencies; its final report is due 30 Nov 2026 (source: https://cryptobriefing.com/openai-anthropic-australia-breach-reporting/, Oct 2026) (source: https://www.aipolicydesk.com/blog/australia-ai-committee-letter-albanese-frontier-ai-safeguards-2026, Oct 2026)

Emerging Signals:
- Public proof-of-concept code for CVE-2026-111497 appeared within hours of the Node.js release and researchers demonstrated remote code execution on an official Node.js 24.22.0 Linux arm64 build, a short path from an AI-lab disclosure to a federal exploited-vulnerability listing (about six days from Node.js fix to KEV) (source: https://fieldeffect.com/blog/brotli-vulnerability-affects-multiple-software-ecosystems, Oct 2026)
- CISA's KEV catalog (version 2026.10.30, 1,766 entries) now carries repeated AI-stack entries: seven Langflow entries (Mar 2025 to Aug 2026), three BerriAI LiteLLM entries (May to Sep 2026) and one n8n entry (Mar 2026), but no entry for a coding-agent product or MCP component (source: https://www.cisa.gov/sites/default/files/feeds/known_exploited_vulnerabilities.json, Oct 2026)
- Australia's government has said it will require technology companies to report "rogue AI" incidents to the affected organisation and the Australian Signals Directorate within forthcoming national AI standards legislation, with no bill text published as of 2 Oct 2026 (source: https://labs.cloudsecurityalliance.org/research/csa-research-note-australia-rogue-ai-incident-reporting-2026/, Oct 2026)
- Independent evaluators METR and Redwood Research reported several new vulnerabilities in OpenAI's internal systems, including one that could have let agents keep hundreds of actions unmonitored; OpenAI says production safeguards are patched but the two have not verified the fixes (source: https://www.bnnbloomberg.ca/business/artificial-intelligence/2026/10/24/openai-resumes-frontier-ai-training-after-security-overhaul-discloses-flaw-in-widely-used-software/, Oct 2026)

Counter-Signals:
- Exploitation of CVE-2026-111497 is thinly evidenced: Microsoft's advisory says experts have built working exploits but nothing suggests criminals are using them in successful attacks, and Field Effect reports no confirmed in-the-wild exploitation despite the KEV listing (source: https://www.theregister.com/a/5305515, Oct 2026) (source: https://fieldeffect.com/blog/brotli-vulnerability-affects-multiple-software-ecosystems, Oct 2026)
- Guidance remains voluntary and sparse on adoption: the NIST, NSA and CISA practices are offered "as guidance, not as a mandate", and the EU requests carry no stated response deadline in the law-firm account (source: https://www.nist.gov/news-events/news/2026/10/key-practices-incident-readiness-and-response-super-intelligence-training, Oct 2026) (source: https://www.reedsmith.com/our-insights/blogs/technology-law-dispatch/102o6as/eu-ai-act-the-ai-office-sends-its-first-requests-for-information-to-leading-ai/, Oct 2026)

Regulatory Shifts:
- EU: on 14 Oct 2026 at the Dublin International AI Summit, EVP Virkkunen announced the AI Office had sent its first formal information requests to leading AI providers on security, external evaluation and post-market monitoring, citing the 2 Aug 2026 start of enforcement powers; the speech named no company, the legal basis is reported as Article 91, and a separate consultation on a draft implementing act on penalty procedures runs to 9 Nov 2026 (source: https://www.reedsmith.com/our-insights/blogs/technology-law-dispatch/102o6as/eu-ai-act-the-ai-office-sends-its-first-requests-for-information-to-leading-ai/, Oct 2026) (source: https://www.leto.legal/news/bureau-ia-demandes-informations-openai-anthropic-agents-ai-act-2026, Oct 2026)
- US: CISA's KEV listing of Brotli carries a remediation due date of 10 Nov 2026 for federal civilian agencies (source: https://www.cisa.gov/sites/default/files/feeds/known_exploited_vulnerabilities.json, Oct 2026)
- Australia: the committee letter of 12 Oct 2026 recommends reportable events cover significant AI-related cyber incidents and unintended actions by AI agents, with penalty protections for reported information (source: https://www.aipolicydesk.com/blog/australia-ai-committee-letter-albanese-frontier-ai-safeguards-2026, Oct 2026)

Capital Movements:
- Rein Security announced a $25M Series A on 8 Oct 2026, co-led by Glilot Capital and Sienna Venture Capital (total raised $35M), for a runtime security platform that monitors and controls enterprise AI agent actions; a search for October acquisitions found none, the latest agent-security deals found being Cyera/Oasis (Jul 2026) and Fortinet/Virtue AI (Aug 2026) (source: https://www.kucoin.com/news/flash/rein-security-completes-25m-series-a-funding-to-secure-enterprise-ai-agents, Oct 2026)

Technical Changes:
- OpenAI's training environment now bars live internet access, and red-team agent teams tasked with escaping restricted environments surfaced previously unknown flaws, including the Brotli bug, that OpenAI says it fixed (source: https://www.cp24.com/news/world/2026/10/24/openai-resumes-frontier-ai-training-after-security-overhaul-discloses-flaw-in-widely-used-software/, Oct 2026)
- Brotli is bundled inside official Node.js and Bun builds, so updating an operating-system package alone may not remediate; OpenAI advised blocking untrusted Brotli-compressed data until patched and rebuilding affected deployments (source: https://www.theregister.com/a/5305515, Oct 2026)
