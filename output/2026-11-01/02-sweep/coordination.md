Confirmed Developments:
- The IESG approved the charter of the IETF agentproto working group on 8 Oct 2026; the group is listed as Active in the Applications and Real-Time Area with chairs Leslie Daigle and Orie Steele, and milestones for a use-cases draft (Dec 2026), architecture draft (Feb 2027) and agentic dialog management protocol drafts (Mar 2027) (source: https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/charter-ietf-agentproto/history/, Oct 2026) (source: https://datatracker.ietf.org/group/agentproto/about/, Nov 2026)
- AAIF announced four new hosted projects on 22 Oct 2026 at AGNTCon + MCPCon North America: Agent Skills (Anthropic), Open Responses (OpenAI), OpenEnv (Hugging Face) and OpenSandbox (Alibaba), taking the roster to ten from three at the December 2025 launch (source: https://aaif.io/news/agentic-ai-foundation-announces-four-new-projects, Oct 2026) (source: https://www.linuxfoundation.org/press/agentic-ai-foundation-announces-four-new-projects-agent-skills-open-responses-openenv-and-opensandbox, Oct 2026)
- MCP released the 2026-07-28 specification, which removes protocol-level sessions and the initialize handshake, adds server/discover and subscriptions/listen, moves Tasks into an official extension, and deprecates Roots, Sampling, Logging, HTTP+SSE and Dynamic Client Registration under a twelve-month minimum deprecation window (source: https://modelcontextprotocol.io/specification/2026-07-28/changelog.md, Jul 2026) (source: https://blog.modelcontextprotocol.io/posts/, Jul 2026)

Emerging Signals:
- The MCP roadmap (last updated 22 Aug 2026) names five priority areas for the next specification release: agentic messaging primitives (Tasks and Events, composition review), HTTP-native transport unification (HTTP over stdio, ETag caching), agent identity (DPoP, Workload Identity Federation, ID-JAG, token exchange, with an Agent Identity WG "forming"), improved primitives (tool result shape, progressive discovery) and SDK experience; it sets no date for the next release and calls itself "current thinking rather than firm commitments" (source: https://modelcontextprotocol.io/development/roadmap, Aug 2026)
- AAIF launched the Model Context Protocol Associate (MCPA) certification on 14 Sep 2026, aligned to MCP spec 2026-07-28, as its first certification (source: https://www.linuxfoundation.org/press/agentic-ai-foundation-launches-mcpa-certification-to-validate-mcp-expertise, Sep 2026)
- Open Responses adopted a final Linux Foundation technical charter on 22 Oct 2026 and moved schema generation to TypeSpec on 9 Oct 2026, with its last dated spec snapshot still 2026-04-24 (WebSocket transport, compaction endpoint) (source: https://github.com/openresponses/openresponses, Oct 2026) (source: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/openresponses/openresponses/main/CHANGELOG.md, Apr 2026)
- The official MCP Ruby SDK reached Tier 1 on 29 Sep 2026, extending the set of SDKs meeting the 100 percent conformance bar (source: https://blog.modelcontextprotocol.io/posts/, Sep 2026)

Counter-Signals:
- Agent Skills, now AAIF-hosted, has no tagged release or version number: the GitHub tags and releases lists are empty and the last commit to main is dated 9 Aug 2026, before the 22 Oct donation, so the governance transfer has not yet produced versioned artefacts (source: https://github.com/agentskills/agentskills, Nov 2026)
- The UCP monitor shows protocol governance outpacing implementation: AP2 Mandate on 37 stores and other payment capabilities on 3 to 4 stores, against 17,056 stores bound to Checkout (source: https://ucpchecker.com/capabilities, Nov 2026)

Technical Changes:
- MCP spec 2026-07-28 is stateless and adds `server/discover`, `subscriptions/listen`, Multi Round-Trip Requests, `ttlMs` and `cacheScope` caching fields, and an `extensions` capability field; Tasks are now the extension `io.modelcontextprotocol/tasks` (source: https://modelcontextprotocol.io/specification/2026-07-28/changelog.md, Jul 2026)
- Open Responses spec 2026-04-24 added WebSocket transport for `/v1/responses` and `POST /v1/responses/compact`; the OpenAPI snapshot folders under public/openapi/ are 2026-01-15 and 2026-04-24 (source: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/openresponses/openresponses/main/CHANGELOG.md, Apr 2026)
- The MCP Agents group now treats an "Agents Extension" as an open evaluation question to complement Tasks rather than a build item (source: https://modelcontextprotocol.io/community/interest-groups/agents.md, Oct 2026)
