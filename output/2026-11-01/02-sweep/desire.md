# Evidence sweep, desire, 2026-11-01

Window note: about one month since the 2026-10-01 sweep. Search used WebSearch plus the Bing news RSS discovery fallback; every cited page was opened and dated. Pages that could not be read are named under Contradictions.

Delta Since Last Sweep:
- OpenAI announced on 21 Oct 2026 that ChatGPT ads now reach 89 countries (26 added), with more than 100,000 advertisers having run campaigns. The 2026-10-01 sweep recorded 70+ countries and about 20,000 advertisers in late August (source: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/21/openai-chatgpt-ads-26-countries.html, Oct 2026).
- New York Governor Hochul signed S9051B / A10379C on 28 Oct 2026, barring minors' chatbots from acting as friend or romantic partner and from presenting as human, effective 1 Jan 2027. The 2026-10-01 sweep listed only California and Pennsylvania as state-level vehicles (source: https://iapp.org/news/a/new-york-s-governor-signs-youth-ai-chatbot-safety-bill, Oct 2026).
- Meta's Q3 call of 21 Oct 2026 put Muse at more than 10 million accounts in the US and Canada and named transaction fees and subscriptions, not ads, as its revenue lines, with no fee rate stated (source: https://www.ksat.com/business/2026/10/21/meta-q3-revenue-tops-estimates-and-zuckerberg-says-muse-accounts-have-topped-10-million/, Oct 2026).
- Character.AI, the largest dedicated companion platform, filed Chapter 11 in Delaware on 12 or 13 Oct 2026 (sources differ by a day) with a $20 million Disney stalking-horse bid (source: https://thenextweb.com/news/character-ai-files-for-bankruptcy-as-disney-bids-20m-for-some-assets, Oct 2026).
- Still not found: the Digital Fairness Act proposal (IAPP of 22 Oct 2026 expects the College presentation on 11 Nov 2026), an FTC 6(b) output, a Dutch leidraad draft, an Amazon v Meta suit, a suit against SB 1119 (source: https://iapp.org/news/a/a-view-from-brussels-final-countdown-to-the-digital-fairness-act-has-started, Oct 2026).

Confirmed Developments:
- OpenAI expanded ChatGPT ads by 26 countries on 21 Oct 2026, to 89 in total, including Argentina, Peru, Nigeria, South Africa and Ukraine. More than 100,000 advertisers have run campaigns, and the ad business reached an annualized USD 1 billion run rate in August 2026, up from USD 100 million in March. Free and Go users see ads; Plus, Pro and Enterprise stay ad-free (source: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/21/openai-chatgpt-ads-26-countries.html, Oct 2026) (source: https://thenextweb.com/news/chatgpt-ads-89-countries-100000-advertisers, Oct 2026)
- New York enacted a youth AI chatbot safety law on 28 Oct 2026 (S9051B / A10379C). It bars minors' chatbots from acting as a friend or romantic partner, from presenting as human and from building emotional dependency, requires multi-method age assurance with age data deleted afterwards, and allows Attorney General penalties up to USD 25,000 per violation. Effective 1 Jan 2027 (source: https://iapp.org/news/a/new-york-s-governor-signs-youth-ai-chatbot-safety-bill, Oct 2026) (source: https://thenextweb.com/news/new-york-ai-chatbots-children-law-hochul-baby-monitors, Oct 2026)
- Meta reported on 21 Oct 2026 that more than 10 million people in the US and Canada had set up Muse in about six weeks and about 25% use it on a given day. Zuckerberg said Meta will charge businesses for transactions Muse facilitates, with subscriptions for heavier use, and the fee rate was not stated (source: https://www.ksat.com/business/2026/10/21/meta-q3-revenue-tops-estimates-and-zuckerberg-says-muse-accounts-have-topped-10-million/, Oct 2026) (source: https://magica.com/news/meta-muse-commerce-revenue-per-user-bet, Oct 2026)

Emerging Signals:
- OpenAI is testing a new visual ad format shown while ChatGPT processes image-generation requests, for US users "later this month" (October 2026), labelled and separate from the generated image (source: https://9to5google.com/2026/10/05/chatgpt-is-adding-new-ads/, Oct 2026)
- OpenAI said on 21 Oct 2026 it plans more ad formats, buying options and measurement tools, new ways for businesses to reach people inside ChatGPT, and Sponsored Agents (testing with some US advertisers since September) (source: https://thenextweb.com/news/chatgpt-ads-89-countries-100000-advertisers, Oct 2026)
- Character.AI's Chapter 11 sale is expected to close in late November or early December 2026; user conversations are estate assets, and if no buyer keeps the platform running it will be shut down (source: https://www.techtimes.com/articles/328969/20261014/characterai-files-bankruptcy-12-million-users-private-conversations-now-disneys-bid.htm, Oct 2026)

Counter-Signals:
- Meta's Muse data and conversations inside its virtual machine are not shared with Meta's advertising systems, and no ads in Muse were reported on the Q3 call, so the personal-agent revenue unit remains the transaction or subscription, unlike ChatGPT (source: https://magica.com/news/meta-muse-commerce-revenue-per-user-bet, Oct 2026)
- In Germany, Verbraucherzentrale NRW (page dated 22 Oct 2026) treats ChatGPT ads as generally permitted if recognisable as commercial communication, notes no personalised ads in the EEA at launch, and mentions no complaint or legal action against OpenAI, so no consumer-body enforcement against assistant ads was found (source: https://www.verbraucherzentrale.nrw/wissen/digitale-welt/onlinedienste/werbung-in-chatgpt-worauf-sie-jetzt-achten-sollten-126563, Oct 2026)

Regulatory Shifts:
- New York S9051B / A10379C signed 28 Oct 2026, covering unsafe chatbot features for minors across 12 categories, with an Attorney General complaint portal and effective date 1 Jan 2027 (source: https://iapp.org/news/a/new-york-s-governor-signs-youth-ai-chatbot-safety-bill, Oct 2026)
- IAPP (22 Oct 2026) reports Commissioner McGrath is expected to present the Digital Fairness Act to the College on 11 Nov 2026; its listed core issues are personalisation exploiting vulnerabilities, dark patterns, influencer marketing and addictive design, and it does not mention chatbots or companions (source: https://iapp.org/news/a/a-view-from-brussels-final-countdown-to-the-digital-fairness-act-has-started, Oct 2026)

Capital Movements:
- OpenAI's ad business reached a USD 1 billion annualized run rate (announced 31 Aug 2026), and more than 100,000 advertisers have run campaigns as of 21 Oct 2026 (source: https://www.pymnts.com/news/artificial-intelligence/2026/openai-says-ad-business-reaches-1-billion-run-rate/, Aug 2026) (source: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/21/openai-chatgpt-ads-26-countries.html, Oct 2026)
- Character.AI filed Chapter 11 with Disney Worldwide Services as a USD 20 million stalking-horse bidder for a non-exclusive perpetual IP licence and related claims; the bid does not cover the platform or user accounts (source: https://thenextweb.com/news/character-ai-files-for-bankruptcy-as-disney-bids-20m-for-some-assets, Oct 2026) (source: https://www.techtimes.com/articles/328969/20261014/characterai-files-bankruptcy-12-million-users-private-conversations-now-disneys-bid.htm, Oct 2026)

Technical Changes:
- OpenAI's image-generation visual ad format (test for US users in October 2026) puts a sponsored unit inside a generation flow rather than below a text answer (source: https://9to5google.com/2026/10/05/chatgpt-is-adding-new-ads/, Oct 2026)
