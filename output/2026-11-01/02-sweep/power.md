# Evidence sweep, power, 2026-11-01

Delta Since Last Sweep:
- PJM refiled. The 2026-10-01 sweep recorded FERC's 29 Sep 2026 suspension of the Reliability Backstop Procurement (RBP) to 28 Feb 2027. PJM filed an amended proposal on 29 Oct 2026 and now targets a bidding window of 1 to 28 Mar 2027, with results on 10 May 2027 (source: https://microgridmedia.com/pjm-backstop-procurement-amended-filing-2026/, Oct 2026) (source: https://www.publicpower.org/periodical/article/pjm-files-amended-reliability-backstop-proposal-ferc, Oct 2026)

Confirmed Developments:
- The Senate failed to advance the Ratepayer Protection Act (H.R. 9340) on 30 Sep 2026: a cloture vote on the motion to proceed failed 57-43 (60 needed), the second Senate failure after Sen. Husted's unanimous consent request was blocked. Four Democrats (Hassan, Klobuchar, Ossoff, Warnock) voted yes; Democrats called the bill voluntary and "toothless" and offered a mandatory alternative (the GRID Savings Act). The House had passed it 417-3 on 16 Sep 2026 (source: https://rollcall.com/2026/09/30/voluntary-ratepayer-protection-bill-blocked-in-senate-again/, Sep 2026) (source: https://www.aljazeera.com/amp/news/2026/9/30/us-senate-rejects-bill-targeting-ai-data-centre-electricity-costs, Sep 2026)
- PJM filed an amended RBP proposal at FERC on 29 Oct 2026, after FERC's 29 Sep 2026 suspension order in ER26-3380. PJM's schedule: bidding window opens 1 Mar 2027 and closes 28 Mar 2027, procurement targets set 28 Apr 2027, results 10 May 2027. A requested FERC action date and comment deadline were not stated on either page (source: https://microgridmedia.com/pjm-backstop-procurement-amended-filing-2026/, Oct 2026) (source: https://www.publicpower.org/periodical/article/pjm-files-amended-reliability-backstop-proposal-ferc, Oct 2026)
- Texas permitting under the 21 Sep 2026 halt is contested on the record. FracTracker and Public Citizen (19 Oct 2026) reported 35 TCEQ actions on data center projects after the governor's letter; the governor's press secretary said the pause covers approval, not processing, and that no data center permits were approved. TCEQ then suspended data center stormwater notices of intent (Public Citizen counts at least 17) and disabled STEERS on 21 to 22 Oct 2026. Seven air emission-limit certifications remained "issued" as of the 26 Oct report (source: https://www.kut.org/energy-environment/2026-10-20/texas-continued-permitting-data-centers-after-abbotts-pause-report-finds, Oct 2026) (source: https://www.tpr.org/environment/2026-10-26/tceq-suspends-stormwater-approvals-other-data-center-related-reviews-appear-unchanged-after-watchdog-report, Oct 2026)
- Loudoun County, Virginia deferred its vote on a pause of up to 12 months on data center and substation legislative applications from 20 Oct to 17 Nov 2026. The Board had voted 7-1-1 on 15 Sep 2026 to have staff draft the resolution; the county attorney's opinion is that an indefinite moratorium is not legal, and the pause would cover new applications only (source: https://www.loudoun.gov/CivicAlerts.aspx?AID=10938, Oct 2026) (source: https://wjla.com/news/local/loudoun-county-data-centers-development-applications-board-supervisors-pause-northern-virginia-regulations-leesburg-catoctin-grid-reliability-dominion-energy-merger-power-lines-laws, Sep 2026)

Emerging Signals:
- Virginia Gov. Spanberger announced a Data Center Accountability Framework on 18 Sep 2026 (Executive Order 22): a bar on executive-branch NDAs for data center projects, stricter noise, emissions and water standards, a review of backup generation, and an AI task force. The legislative agenda for the 2027 session includes local approval for data centers over 25 MW and shifting transmission and generation costs to large loads. It is not a moratorium (source: https://broadbandbreakfast.com/gov-spanberger-moves-to-rein-in-virginias-data-center-boom-without-a-moratorium/, Sep 2026)
- Germany (non-English check): Die Linke tabled Bundestag motion 21/8111 (hib of 24 Sep 2026) asking for a moratorium on data center approvals until a needs and capacity analysis exists, and for a law on new data centers of 100 kW or more of grid connection. No adoption or committee outcome was found (source: https://www.bundestag.de/1217394, Sep 2026)
- Texas Senate Finance Chair Joan Huffman said she has "publicly committed to filing legislation" on the data center sales tax exemption in the next session, and Gov. Abbott said he would work to repeal it if reelected. No bill was reported filed as of that coverage; the exemption costs more than $1 billion a year (source: https://communityimpact.com/central-austin/texas-legislature/texas-spends-billions-on-tax-breaks-for-data-centers-theres-a-bipartisan-legislative-push-to-repeal-them/, Jul 2026)
