# Evidence sweep, agency, 2026-11-01

Delta Since Last Sweep:
- The window since the 2026-10-01 sweep is about one month, and the centre of the agency story moved from merchants and card networks to retail banks: on 13 Oct 2026 JPMorgan confirmed it blocks outside AI agents from retail accounts, and by 27 Oct 2026 American Banker counted five large US banks (JPMorganChase, Citi, Bank of America, Wells Fargo, Morgan Stanley) as blocking external agents (source: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/13/jpmorgan-ai-agents-account-access.html, Oct 2026) (source: https://www.americanbanker.com/news/banks-are-blocking-external-ai-agents-is-this-sustainable, Oct 2026)
- A private-standards layer for agent identity and permission appeared: Meta and Sierra announced the draft Personal Agent Protocol ("Poppy") on 6 Oct 2026, and American Banker reported on 15 Oct 2026 that Bank of America, BBVA, Synchrony and Wells Fargo had joined the effort, which does not yet cover payments (source: https://www.pymnts.com/news/artificial-intelligence/2026/meta-sierra-poppy-lets-customers-set-limits-ai-agents/, Oct 2026) (source: https://www.americanbanker.com/payments/news/why-some-banks-are-exploring-bring-your-own-ai-agent, Oct 2026)
- The Amazon-Meta Muse dispute stayed commercial: on the 29 Oct 2026 Q3 call Jassy called outside shopping agents "still not great" and pointed customers to Alexa for Shopping, while Walmart announced Agent Profiles with customer spending limits on 21 Oct 2026; no Amazon or Meta lawsuit was found in this window (source: https://thenextweb.com/news/alexa-for-shopping-jassy-ai-shopping-agents-meta-muse, Oct 2026) (source: https://www.unite.ai/walmart-introduces-agent-profiles-gateway-for-ai-shopping-agents/, Oct 2026)
- Public authorities still produced no binding agent-payment rule in the window: the CFPB 1033 rewrite was at OIRA in Aug 2026 with no proposal found in the Federal Register, while Singapore's MAS finalised AI risk guidelines that cover AI agents on 7 Oct 2026 with effect from Oct 2027 (source: https://www.pymnts.com/legal/bank-regulation/2026/cfpb-open-banking-rewrite-could-put-data-access-fees-at-center-of-section-1033-fight/, Aug 2026) (source: https://www.jdsupra.com/legalnews/singapore-issues-new-ai-risk-management-7293714/, Oct 2026)

Confirmed Developments:
- JPMorgan confirmed on 13 Oct 2026 that it does not permit third parties to access accounts using customers' banking credentials, so outside agents such as Meta's Muse, Instinct and OpenAI's dots can read some data (for example balances) through a connector such as Plaid but cannot send money or pay bills; Dimon said customers should understand "there's a liability transfer", and the bank gave no timeline for a sanctioned path that lets agents act directly (source: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/13/jpmorgan-ai-agents-account-access.html, Oct 2026) (source: https://www.pymnts.com/news/artificial-intelligence/2026/big-banks-will-not-let-outside-ai-agents-move-money/, Oct 2026)
- Other large US banks and brokers described their own blocks between 13 and 28 Oct 2026: Citi allows no third-party agent logins or transactions (read-only access for authorised open banking providers), Schwab blocks unauthorised agents including Muse and plans MCP servers for client-chosen AI models (read and research, not money movement, no date), and Wells Fargo confirmed on the record that it has taken steps to block unauthorised agents (source: https://www.pymnts.com/news/artificial-intelligence/2026/big-banks-will-not-let-outside-ai-agents-move-money/, Oct 2026) (source: https://www.americanbanker.com/news/banks-are-blocking-external-ai-agents-is-this-sustainable, Oct 2026)
- Bank of America emailed customers (reported 27 Oct 2026, spokesperson confirmed authenticity) not to share usernames and passwords with outside services "including an AI-enabled tool or agent", warning that "you're responsible for activity resulting from that access" (source: https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/27/bank-of-america-kindly-asks-that-you-dont-share-your-banking-password-with-your-ai-assistant/, Oct 2026) (source: https://www.androidauthority.com/bank-of-america-ai-agent-password-warning-3728947/, Oct 2026)
- Walmart announced on 21 Oct 2026 Agent Profiles (a separate account profile per AI agent, with approve-every-purchase or spending limits such as $50, $100 or $200, due within about a month), a Walmart Agent Gateway using MCP in beta by invitation, and terms requiring agents to identify themselves and barring them from receiving card data (source: https://www.unite.ai/walmart-introduces-agent-profiles-gateway-for-ai-shopping-agents/, Oct 2026) (source: https://thenextweb.com/news/walmart-ai-agents-shop-agent-profiles, Oct 2026)
- Amazon CEO Andy Jassy said on 29 Oct 2026 that few people shop through outside agents because the experience is "still not great", reported Alexa for Shopping use by more than 400 million customers in the past 12 months, and did not name Muse while Amazon's September block remains in place (source: https://thenextweb.com/news/alexa-for-shopping-jassy-ai-shopping-agents-meta-muse, Oct 2026) (source: https://tech.yahoo.com/ai/meta-ai/articles/amazon-ceo-andy-jassy-says-140000027.html, Oct 2026)
- Meta and Sierra announced the Personal Agent Protocol (Poppy) on 6 Oct 2026 as an open draft standard letting agents identify themselves to businesses and customers choose view or change permissions, with design partners including OpenAI, Visa, Mastercard, PayPal, Plaid, Bank of America and Wells Fargo; payments are deferred to a later extension and no version 1.0 date is given (source: https://www.pymnts.com/news/artificial-intelligence/2026/meta-sierra-poppy-lets-customers-set-limits-ai-agents/, Oct 2026) (source: https://forkast.news/the-same-banks-writing-the-rules-for-ai-agents-are-the-ones-locking-them-out/, Oct 2026)

Emerging Signals:
- Wells Fargo's updated Online Access Agreement takes effect 24 Nov 2026 and adds a Section 15 on third-party AI agents that, as read by Forkast, treats agent-initiated transactions as authorised by the customer, states use of an AI agent is "AT YOUR OWN RISK", and lets the bank limit or end agent access at any time; the contract text itself was not opened (source: https://forkast.news/the-access-tax-amazon-imposed-on-shopping-agents-just-arrived-at-your-bank/, Oct 2026)
- Tencent and Visa signed an MoU announced in Oct 2026 covering cross-border payments and, among four areas, exploring agentic commerce through Visa Intelligent Commerce, with no launch dates (source: https://thepaypers.com/payments/news/tencent-and-visa-sign-mou-on-cross-border-and-agentic-payments, Oct 2026)
- Mastercard opened early access to Agent Pay for Machines on 20 Oct 2026, built on the x402 protocol, with a live demo at Cloudflare's Connect in which an agent made nine payments to two merchants within one human-set budget; pilot participants include Cloudflare, Ant International, AWS, BVNK and Coinbase (source: https://decrypt.co/381373/mastercard-opens-up-ai-agent-payments-with-early-access-and-live-demo, Oct 2026)

Counter-Signals:
- Jassy's 29 Oct 2026 remarks and the Meta figure of more than 10 million Muse users in the US and Canada sit side by side, so adoption of outside agents is real but small enough for the incumbent to dismiss, and the dominant retailer still blocks them (source: https://thenextweb.com/news/alexa-for-shopping-jassy-ai-shopping-agents-meta-muse, Oct 2026)
- An American Bankers Association survey of more than 4,000 adults released around 27 Oct 2026 found 77% concerned about letting AI agents move money, and Schwab's CEO described blocked agent log-ins as running in the tens per week or "not many", so consumer appetite and measured volume for delegated money movement both stay low (source: https://www.americanbanker.com/news/banks-are-blocking-external-ai-agents-is-this-sustainable, Oct 2026) (source: https://www.investmentnews.com/transformation/schwab-ceo-says-firm-is-blocking-rogue-ai-agents-from-client-accounts/268786, Oct 2026)

Regulatory Shifts:
- Singapore's MAS issued final Guidelines on Artificial Intelligence Risk Management on 7 Oct 2026, covering AI agents and treating them as higher risk; governance requirements apply from 7 Oct 2027 and operational requirements from 7 Oct 2028, and MAS plans a 2027 consultation on additional agentic AI guidance (source: https://www.jdsupra.com/legalnews/singapore-issues-new-ai-risk-management-7293714/, Oct 2026) (source: https://www.leaprate.com/regulation/mas-finalises-ai-risk-guidelines-for-financial-firms/, Oct 2026)
- The CFPB sent its "Personal Financial Data Rights Reconsideration" proposal to OIRA in Aug 2026; a Federal Register query on 1 Nov 2026 returned only the Aug 2025 advance notice and no new proposed rule, and the Aug 2025 notice asked who may qualify as a consumer's authorised representative (source: https://www.pymnts.com/legal/bank-regulation/2026/cfpb-open-banking-rewrite-could-put-data-access-fees-at-center-of-section-1033-fight/, Aug 2026) (source: https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2025/08/22/2025-16139/personal-financial-data-rights-reconsideration, Aug 2025)

Capital Movements:
- None verified in the window.

Technical Changes:
- Walmart's Agent Gateway lets agents shop via MCP (beta, invitation only) and each agent gets its own profile created on first use, with category restrictions and rate limits (source: https://www.unite.ai/walmart-introduces-agent-profiles-gateway-for-ai-shopping-agents/, Oct 2026)
- Mastercard's Agent Pay for Machines uses an HTTP 402 response and a signed payment voucher that the provider verifies, with off-chain interaction and batched redemption, plus buyer and seller APIs, a toolkit and a sandbox (source: https://decrypt.co/381373/mastercard-opens-up-ai-agent-payments-with-early-access-and-live-demo, Oct 2026)
- Poppy publishes a standard file by which a company tells agents how to connect, how customers sign in and what the agent may do; Sierra's draft is described as v0.1 with payments, push notifications and detailed permissions as future work (source: https://www.pymnts.com/news/artificial-intelligence/2026/meta-sierra-poppy-lets-customers-set-limits-ai-agents/, Oct 2026) (source: https://thenextweb.com/news/personal-agent-protocol-sierra-meta, Oct 2026)

Contradictions:
- American Banker (15 Oct 2026) says Bank of America and Wells Fargo "joined" Poppy, while PYMNTS and Forkast describe them as design partners exploring a draft; neither Meta/Sierra nor the banks' own pages were opened, so bank commitment level is unverified.
- Bank of America is reported both as blocking agents (American Banker, 27 Oct 2026) and as the sender of a customer email that only warns against sharing credentials (TechCrunch); the American Banker article says Bank of America did not respond, so its blocking rests on secondary reports.
- JPMorgan's "block" permits read access through Plaid-type connectors, so "blocked" in headlines means no credentialed agent log-in or money movement, not no agent data access.
- Not opened or not usable: nypost.com (fetch refused) and golem.de (consent wall) on the 22-23 Oct 2026 reports that banks found unauthorised agents in customer accounts; mastercard.com and corporate Walmart pages were not opened. These are unverified, not evidence of absence.
