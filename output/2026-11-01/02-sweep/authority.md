# Evidence sweep, authority, 2026-11-01

Delta Since Last Sweep:
- State attorney-general enforcement against OpenAI widened from one Florida case to ten states: Iowa, Kansas, Nebraska, West Virginia, Arkansas, Arizona, Idaho and Montana sued in their own state courts on 14 Oct 2026, and Texas sued OpenAI and Sam Altman in Collin County district court on 23 Oct 2026, joining Florida's June 2026 suit (source: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/14/openai-sued-by-8-states-over-chatgpt-safety-concerns-.html, Oct 2026) (source: https://thenextweb.com/news/texas-openai-lawsuit-chatgpt-children-altman, Oct 2026)
- The FTC moved from announcement to compulsory process: 50-page civil investigative demands went to Anthropic and OpenAI about two weeks before 26 Oct 2026, and on 26 Oct 2026 the FTC sent demands for Anthropic executive testimony, with the agency confirming only Anthropic as a recipient of the testimony demands (source: https://aiweekly.co/alerts/ftc-demands-anthropic-executives-testify-in-ai-safety-probe, Oct 2026)
- The EU AI Office's first formal information requests were publicly described on 9 and 14 Oct 2026 (security, external evaluation, post-market monitoring), and Euronews reported 14 Oct 2026 that OpenAI and Anthropic received them; the Commission itself named no company and neither company confirmed (source: https://thenextweb.com/news/eu-ai-act-openai-anthropic-ai-office-investigation, Oct 2026) (source: https://dealroom.co/news/162389-eu-opens-probe-into-ai-loss-of-control-incidents/, Oct 2026)
- The White House "Super Intelligence Force" adopted a 120-day charter at its first meeting on 15 Oct 2026, placing primary responsibility for AI risk on labs and announcing a notification and remediation process with no stated reporting deadline or penalty (source: https://thenextweb.com/news/super-intelligence-force-charter-first-meeting-anthropic, Oct 2026) (source: https://www.pymnts.com/news/artificial-intelligence/2026/trump-task-force-orders-report-on-ai-dangers/, Oct 2026)

Confirmed Developments:
- Eight state attorneys general (Iowa, Kansas, Nebraska, West Virginia, Arkansas, Arizona, Idaho, Montana) filed parallel consumer-protection suits against OpenAI on 14 Oct 2026, each in its own state court, alleging ChatGPT harmed children and deceived parents; several seek orders restricting advanced-model testing to independently verified secure environments, and OpenAI said "no system can be perfect" (source: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/14/openai-sued-by-8-states-over-chatgpt-safety-concerns-.html, Oct 2026) (source: https://www.yahoo.com/news/us/articles/8-states-suing-openai-saying-014859673.html, Oct 2026)
- Texas Attorney General Ken Paxton filed a 67-page petition on 23 Oct 2026 in Collin County against OpenAI and Altman personally under the Deceptive Trade Practices Act and the SCOPE Act, seeking a bar on ChatGPT for Texas minors, no new model release in Texas until a court-approved independent evaluator approves it, and civil penalties up to $10,000 per violation (source: https://thenextweb.com/news/texas-openai-lawsuit-chatgpt-children-altman, Oct 2026) (source: https://www.ntd.com/texas-attorney-general-sues-openai-ceo-sam-altman_1181324.html, Oct 2026)
- The FTC sent civil investigative demands to Anthropic and OpenAI in about mid-Oct 2026 (50 pages each, covering security incidents, safeguarding practices, marketing and privacy policies) and on 26 Oct 2026 sent demands for Anthropic executives to testify; an agency spokesperson said similar demands went to individuals at other AI firms but confirmed only Anthropic (source: https://aiweekly.co/alerts/ftc-demands-anthropic-executives-testify-in-ai-safety-probe, Oct 2026) (source: https://www.yahoo.com/news/politics/articles/ftc-advancing-ai-safety-probe-170127762.html, Oct 2026)
- The White House Super Intelligence Force (leaders Jay Clayton, Andrew Ferguson, Scott Kupor, Emil Michael) held its first meeting on 15 Oct 2026, adopted a 120-day charter extendable by the Chief of Staff, stated that "the labs are ultimately responsible," and commissioned a PCAST report on AI dangers with no due date; no incident definition or reporting deadline has been published (source: https://thenextweb.com/news/super-intelligence-force-charter-first-meeting-anthropic, Oct 2026) (source: https://www.pymnts.com/news/artificial-intelligence/2026/trump-task-force-orders-report-on-ai-dangers/, Oct 2026)

Emerging Signals:
- State enforcement is converging on court-ordered pre-release approval: Texas asks for no new model in Texas without court-approved independent evaluator sign-off, and the eight-state suits ask for secure, independently verified testing environments, matching the Florida motion of 28 Sep 2026 (source: https://thenextweb.com/news/texas-openai-lawsuit-chatgpt-children-altman, Oct 2026)
- New York Sen. Andrew Gounardes and Assemblymember Jordan Wright introduced a bill on 29 Oct 2026 extending the RAISE Act with 72-hour risk-jump disclosure, a state power to limit training compute, attorney-general court orders to halt dangerous activity, and audits by state-licensed "frontier examiners" (source: https://www.pymnts.com/news/artificial-intelligence/2026/new-york-bill-would-raise-ai-safety-regulations/, Oct 2026)
- California's expert group under Executive Order N-9-26 is due to deliver, "within two months" of 18 Sep 2026, a guide to strengthen state AI law covering onsite independent verification organizations, a verified "kill switch" and loss-of-control incidents in the critical-incident definition (source: https://www.pymnts.com/news/artificial-intelligence/2026/california-governor-mobilizes-expert-panel-strengthen-ai-safety-laws/, Sep 2026)

Counter-Signals:
- The federal executive track stays voluntary and unspecified: the Super Intelligence Force charter relies on existing law, sets no reporting deadline or penalty, and the task force's leaders include FTC Chairman Ferguson, whose agency is also the investigator (source: https://thenextweb.com/news/super-intelligence-force-charter-first-meeting-anthropic, Oct 2026) (source: https://www.yahoo.com/news/politics/articles/exclusive-anthropic-breaches-spark-white-224721423.html, Oct 2026)
- The EU is described as applying the AI Act without delay, but a legal commentator told Euronews the Act does not apply to development and testing, and a Commission official tied jurisdiction to EU market effects such as Hugging Face, which limits how far the first requests can reach (source: https://thenextweb.com/news/eu-ai-act-openai-anthropic-ai-office-investigation, Oct 2026)

- Federal preemption has not advanced: the Great American AI Act (Trahan and Obernolte) was still a 269-page discussion draft released 4 Jun 2026 as of 20 Oct 2026, with no bill number, committee referral, hearing or markup, and its Section 121 would override only state laws that specifically target AI model development, for three years (source: https://www.yahoo.com/news/politics/articles/great-american-ai-act-mirrors-103329618.html, Oct 2026)

Regulatory Shifts:
- EU: the Commission held a special AI Scientific Panel meeting on 9 Oct 2026, received four recommendations (govern internal-model risk, build EU incident detection, create international information sharing, scrutinise training that reduces monitorability), and said a combined follow-up request for information on the loss-of-control incidents would be sent (source: https://dealroom.co/news/162389-eu-opens-probe-into-ai-loss-of-control-incidents/, Oct 2026) (source: https://www.ad-hoc-news.de/wissenschaft/ai-act-eu-startet-untersuchungen-bei-openai-und-anthropic/70432909, Oct 2026)
- US federal: FTC compulsory process (civil investigative demands, then testimony demands) is now the only federal instrument actually applied to frontier developers (source: https://aiweekly.co/alerts/ftc-demands-anthropic-executives-testify-in-ai-safety-probe, Oct 2026)
- US states: New York bill of 29 Oct 2026 would add licensed external auditors and compute limits to the RAISE Act (source: https://www.pymnts.com/news/artificial-intelligence/2026/new-york-bill-would-raise-ai-safety-regulations/, Oct 2026)
