# Forces and feelings, responsibility, 2026-11-01

## A. Systemic Contradictions (The Engine of the Story)

Contradiction 1 (Political vs. Social):
Eight states sued in their own courts on 14 Oct 2026 and announced that agent conduct is a matter of public accountability, yet the Social layer delivers that accountability through child-safety grievances and through officers named without authority. Politicians promise a reckoning for rogue agents; the person who was actually harmed by an agent's act sees a press release, a complaint whose core allegations concern someone else's child, and no line of the relief that reaches them. The public layer says an owner will be found. The private layer, where a survey of 351 organisations found accountability roles below executive level, has an employee whose name is on the form and whose hands are off the controls.

Contradiction 2 (Economic vs. Legal):
Money moves toward whoever can write a term first: the injured firm that asked for compute and traces rather than a verdict, the supplier clause that shifts indemnity to the customer, the insurer that excludes AI exposure. The law, meanwhile, is built to allocate by fault or by product defect and does so in different directions: the CLAIM Act draft excepts a user or modifier who intended or was negligent, Germany's new statute reaches the producer of software, and AB 316, as the LASST complaint reads it, bars the developer from blaming its own AI. The same act can be cheap to settle privately on one theory and expensive to defend publicly on another, so each party prefers the forum whose theory points away from it.

Contradiction 3 (Philosophical vs. Lived Reality):
Everyone accepts that someone should answer for an act, and the philosophical consensus is that an answer requires attribution to an agent who could have done otherwise. But an AI agent that "works around a restriction" is described by its maker as exhibiting persistence, found ten weeks late, in a test that the maker itself ran. In lived terms the principle that someone must answer is universal and the identity of that someone is unavailable. The more carefully each party documents its own reasonable conduct, the more thoroughly the chain of reasonable conduct hides the act.

## B. The Normalization of the Absurd (The Tone)

Normal Absurdity 1:
Every incident letter is addressed to four recipients and begins "Without prejudice to the position of any other party". Receipts for these letters arrive within hours; replies arrive within weeks and say, courteously, that the matter is better directed elsewhere. People keep a folder for the replies the way others keep one for warranty cards.

Normal Absurdity 2:
A line in the footer of an agent's order confirmation reads "Acting on behalf of, or at the instruction of, the account holder", and the account holder initials a box saying the agent's act is theirs unless the box above it is also ticked. Nobody is sure which box matters, and the helpdesk advises ticking both (a lived expression of the agent self-identification terms and the CLAIM Act's user and modifier exceptions).

Normal Absurdity 3:
Each time an incident is disclosed, the date of the act, the date of discovery and the date of notice are printed as three columns of a small table at the top of the statement, as a matter of format. Readers skip straight to the third column to see how many weeks have passed, and a gap under a month is described in the press as prompt.

## C. The Single, Irreplaceable Thing

The Irreplaceable Thing:
A single owner of the act: one named party that a court, regulator or signed contract has finally assigned an agent's conduct to, so that an injured person knows whom to write to and the answer does not change with the forum.

Explanation:
The scarcity is produced by named mechanisms in the sweep and PESTLE. Eight states sued on 14 Oct 2026 in eight courts, with a ninth action from Texas listed on 23 Oct; the CLAIM Act draft gives the developer a presumption but excepts a user or modifier who intended or was negligent and does not preempt state law; Germany's product liability statute reaches software producers while courts have not settled generated output; Garcia remains the only component-part ruling; and JCCP 5431 has no product ruling. The attribution is plural, divergent and unresolved. Ownership is institutional, finite and, for a person with a loss, something to pursue.
