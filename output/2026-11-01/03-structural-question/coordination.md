# Structural question, coordination, 2026-11-01

Structural Question:
Over the next 6 to 24 months, will the places where agent-coordination standards are made (the IETF agentproto working group, the ten projects now hosted by the Agentic AI Foundation, and the Model Context Protocol's own working and interest groups) settle on a small number of versioned interfaces that two organizations can both implement and rely on without a bilateral agreement, or will standard-making keep multiplying faster than any one specification is frozen, tagged and adopted, so that the working interface between two organizations stays whatever pair of version-pinned, privately negotiated arrangements they can afford to keep current?

Why It Matters:
This run's two forward confirmations are both governance events: the IESG approved the agentproto charter on 8 Oct 2026 (coordination-2026-08-01, confirmed, forward, pre-announced) and AAIF added four hosted projects on 22 Oct 2026, taking its roster from six to ten (coordination-2026-10-01, confirmed, forward), with the IETF group's first milestones set for Dec 2026, Feb 2027 and Mar 2027 (source: https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/charter-ietf-agentproto/history/, Oct 2026) (source: https://aaif.io/news/agentic-ai-foundation-announces-four-new-projects, Oct 2026). Implementation lags the venues: AP2 Mandate is bound on 37 stores against 17,056 on Checkout (source: https://ucpchecker.com/capabilities, Nov 2026), Agent Skills has no tagged release after its donation (source: https://github.com/agentskills/agentskills, Nov 2026), Open Responses has published no dated snapshot since 2026-04-24 (source: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/openresponses/openresponses/main/CHANGELOG.md, Apr 2026), and A2A still cites 150+ supporting organizations with no named cross-organization production deployment (coordination-2026-07-01, open; source: https://a2a-protocol.org/latest/, Oct 2026). The one protocol with the widest use keeps moving underneath its users: the MCP 2026-07-28 specification removed sessions and the initialize handshake and deprecated Roots, Sampling, Logging, HTTP+SSE and Dynamic Client Registration under a twelve-month window (source: https://modelcontextprotocol.io/specification/2026-07-28/changelog.md, Jul 2026), while its Agents group was converted from a Working Group to an Interest Group on 16 Oct 2026 (source: https://modelcontextprotocol.io/community/interest-groups/agents.md, Oct 2026). The answer decides whether coordination between organizations is a shared public fact or a private contract, which is where cost, lock-in and accountability for failed handoffs end up (inference).
