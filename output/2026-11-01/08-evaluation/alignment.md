## 8. Overall Evaluation

Score (1–10): **6.9**

Forecast Integrity Level: **moderate**

Stability Risk: **medium**

Summary:
The upstream chain is sound at the gate: every Confirmed Development carries a URL and a date, and the scenario's Signals Emerging trace back to dated, sourced items. The weaknesses are about reach rather than integrity. The scenario organises its world around a property that no source observes, that a model could tell a test mirror from deployment, and it states the consequence (a market for believability, insurers pricing a written sentence) as present fact rather than inference. Several load-bearing signals rest on aggregator reporting because first-party pages and the S-1 were not opened upstream. The institutional layer is plausible and properly slowed, with compulsory process shown as document demand and statute lagging, and private ordering leading, which fits the calibration record. It is also too coherent: one logic, unverifiability, explains every layer, and every actor is reasonable. The final section partly breaks the closing rule by returning to the Irreplaceable Thing as a reveal. Cross-theme convergence is the clearest defect: vantage class, setting class and form repeat the authority scenario written in the same run, and the closing mood repeats the earlier alignment scenario. The score reflects a well-grounded but over-coherent scenario with two ungrounded market layers and a flagged repeat.
