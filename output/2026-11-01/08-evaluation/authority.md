## 8. Overall Evaluation

Score (1–10): **7.1**

Forecast Integrity Level: moderate

Stability Risk: medium

Summary:
The scenario passes the citation gate on every Confirmed Development and anchors all six emerging signals in dated, sourced sweep items. Its structure is compliant, including the declared vantage, the assigned form and the word ranges. Its horizon is disciplined: legislative and rulemaking clocks are slowed, contractual pressure is named as the faster channel and marked as inference, and the probable direction does not assume a merits ruling or federal preemption. The main epistemic weakness is that the central mechanism, an independent evaluator who works under a court order and whose finding is not recognised elsewhere, projects from requests in Texas, New York and California rather than from any existing practice, so the scarcity of an accepted signature is partly inferred. Several upstream statements are slightly beyond their inputs (the S-1 as a "risk factor", California's lateness as fact, an annual report in place of an S-1). Institutional realism is moderate: friction exists, but courts and agencies are off-stage and the world resolves into one tidy logic. Narrative inflation is moderate. The decisive problem is convergence. The vantage, setting and form duplicate the same-run alignment scenario, the closing gesture repeats a pattern already seen in dependency, security and coordination, and the Irreplaceable Thing is a cousin of responsibility's. That caps distinctiveness and lowers confidence that this scenario adds an independent perspective.
