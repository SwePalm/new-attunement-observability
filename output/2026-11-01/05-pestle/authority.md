# PESTLE analysis, authority, 2026-11-01

Sources are the 2026-11-01 sweep unless marked as ledger or grading provenance. Items marked "inference" are not in any source.

### Political
Core Shift Thesis:
Political authority over frontier developers is being exercised by the actors who can file or demand today, state attorneys general and the FTC, while the federal executive track announces responsibility without attaching a deadline or penalty.

Forces:
1. Eight state attorneys general sued OpenAI on 14 Oct 2026, each in its own state court, and Texas sued OpenAI and Altman on 23 Oct 2026 in Collin County, joining Florida's June 2026 suit; attorneys general now act as the main US enforcement channel for frontier chatbots (source: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/14/openai-sued-by-8-states-over-chatgpt-safety-concerns-.html, Oct 2026).
2. The White House Super Intelligence Force adopted a 120-day charter on 15 Oct 2026, extendable by the Chief of Staff, stating that "the labs are ultimately responsible" and publishing no incident definition, reporting deadline or penalty; its leaders include FTC Chairman Ferguson, whose agency is also the investigator (source: https://thenextweb.com/news/super-intelligence-force-charter-first-meeting-anthropic, Oct 2026).
3. Federal preemption has not moved: the Great American AI Act was still a 269-page discussion draft on 20 Oct 2026 with no bill number or markup (source: https://www.yahoo.com/news/politics/articles/great-american-ai-act-mirrors-103329618.html, Oct 2026), and the Thune, Cruz, Klobuchar text remains unpublished (ledger authority-2026-07-02, open; grading file 2026-11-01).
4. California's Executive Order N-9-26 of 18 Sep 2026 tasks an expert group to recommend onsite independent verification organisations and a verified kill switch within about two months; the exact date is unverified, and on the 2026 record of slipping calendars a late or partial delivery is more likely than an early one (inference).

### Economic
Core Shift Thesis:
The cost of authority is moving from a single compliance programme to a stack of parallel exposures, and the market is pricing them as disclosure items long before any ruling sets their size.

Forces:
1. Anthropic's Form S-1 of 26 Oct 2026 discloses the October 2026 FTC civil investigative demand among its exposures, making regulatory process a line item for investors (ledger authority-2026-10-01, confirmed; source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2130357/000162828026067168/anthropicpbc-sx1.htm, Oct 2026).
2. The Texas petition seeks civil penalties up to USD 10,000 per violation and a bar on ChatGPT for Texas minors, so penalty exposure scales with user counts rather than with a fixed ceiling (source: https://thenextweb.com/news/texas-openai-lawsuit-chatgpt-children-altman, Oct 2026).
3. Illinois SB 315 sets penalties to USD 3 million and annual third-party audits from 1 Jan 2028 (ledger and 2026-08-04 deep dive, source: https://www.mofo.com/resources/insights/260715-illinois-raises-the-bar-on-frontier-ai-what-developers-need-to-know, Jul 2026), which makes an auditor market a statutory cost line; the audit supply is untested (inference).
4. If courts or statutes name specific evaluators, the first accredited examiners acquire pricing power, and insurers and procurement buyers may adopt one evaluator's sign-off before any statute does (inference, consistent with the private-ordering pattern of 2026).

### Social
Core Shift Thesis:
Public legitimacy is attaching to the reachable and the personal: a named defendant, a child-safety claim and a petition outweigh a task-force charter.

Forces:
1. The eight-state suits allege that ChatGPT harmed children and deceived parents, a framing that draws on consumer-protection norms and stays legible without technical knowledge (source: https://www.yahoo.com/news/us/articles/8-states-suing-openai-saying-014859673.html, Oct 2026).
2. Texas named Sam Altman personally, and the FTC sent testimony demands to Anthropic executives on 26 Oct 2026, so enforcement is publicly personalised (source: https://aiweekly.co/alerts/ftc-demands-anthropic-executives-testify-in-ai-safety-probe, Oct 2026).
3. OpenAI's reply that "no system can be perfect" (source: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/14/openai-sued-by-8-states-over-chatgpt-safety-concerns-.html, Oct 2026) sits against a federal message that labs are responsible, leaving the public with competing accounts of who answers.
4. Senate testimony on rogue AI agents on 30 Sep 2026 proceeded without an OpenAI witness after Sam Altman declined, per Sen. Hawley (ledger authority-2026-10-02, open; source: https://techpolicy.press/senate-hearing-weighs-threats-from-unrestrained-ai-agents-after-openai-hack, Oct 2026), which normalises absence from public accountability forums.

### Technological
Core Shift Thesis:
The technical capacity that authority now depends on is the ability to open, test and attest to a model, and it exists unevenly and mostly inside the developers.

Forces:
1. State suits seek testing of advanced models in independently verified secure environments, and Texas seeks release gating on a court-approved evaluator (source: https://thenextweb.com/news/texas-openai-lawsuit-chatgpt-children-altman, Oct 2026); both presuppose evaluation infrastructure that the sweep does not show to exist at scale (inference).
2. The EU AI Scientific Panel's four recommendations of 9 Oct 2026 include governing internal-model risk, building EU incident detection and scrutinising training that reduces monitorability (source: https://dealroom.co/news/162389-eu-opens-probe-into-ai-loss-of-control-incidents/, Oct 2026).
3. Delegated-authority standards already give machines receipts of who authorised an action: the OpenID Foundation AuthZEN profile of 15 Jun 2026 and IETF draft-klrc-aiagent-auth-00 (2026-08-04 sweep provenance; sources: https://openid.net/openid-foundation-advances-authorization-for-the-agent-era-with-new-authzen-working-group-drafts/ and https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/html/draft-klrc-aiagent-auth-00, Jun 2026), so the technical layer can attest authority faster than institutions can.
4. New York's 29 Oct 2026 bill would add 72-hour risk-jump disclosure and a state power to limit training compute, which would require state-level measurement of compute that no state currently performs (source: https://www.pymnts.com/news/artificial-intelligence/2026/new-york-bill-would-raise-ai-safety-regulations/, Oct 2026; inference on capability).

### Legal
Core Shift Thesis:
Authority is being tested in the forum with the least coordination: ten state courts, each applying general law, with removal and consolidation undecided and no merits ruling on preemption.

Forces:
1. No notice of removal had been found as of 1 Nov 2026 (ledger authority-2026-11-02, open), so state courts keep each case unless OpenAI moves; whether it does determines whether one court can fix the verification standard (source: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/14/openai-sued-by-8-states-over-chatgpt-safety-concerns-.html, Oct 2026).
2. The FTC's policy statement on suppression of accuracy, noticed 7 Jul 2026 (document 2026-13628), has no final, revised or withdrawn version as of 1 Nov 2026 (ledger authority-2026-08-02, open; source: https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/07/07/2026-13628/policy-statement-concerning-the-suppression-of-accuracy-in-artificial-intelligence-systems, Jul 2026).
3. Colorado's revised draft of 6 Oct 2026 and the 26 Oct 2026 hearing mean final rules are unadopted, which postpones the xAI injunction clock (ledger authority-2026-07-01 and 2026-08-07, open; source: https://labs.cloudsecurityalliance.org/research/csa-research-note-colorado-admt-chatbot-rulemaking-20261020/, Oct 2026).
4. The EU AI Office's first formal requests were described on 9 and 14 Oct 2026, but a commentator told Euronews that the Act does not apply to development and testing, so the reach of EU authority over US labs remains contested (source: https://thenextweb.com/news/eu-ai-act-openai-anthropic-ai-office-investigation, Oct 2026).

### Environmental
Core Shift Thesis:
The environmental layer for authority is mostly institutional environment: the physical and emergency-management settings in which AI risk is placed and the compute that new proposals would limit.

Forces:
1. New York's proposed state power to limit training compute treats data-centre-scale compute as a regulable physical resource (source: https://www.pymnts.com/news/artificial-intelligence/2026/new-york-bill-would-raise-ai-safety-regulations/, Oct 2026).
2. Illinois routes critical safety incident reports to the Illinois Emergency Management Agency and the Office of Homeland Security, placing model risk in the civil-contingency system (2026-08-04 deep dive provenance; source: https://www.mofo.com/resources/insights/260715-illinois-raises-the-bar-on-frontier-ai-what-developers-need-to-know, Jul 2026).
3. California's kill-switch and loss-of-control incident concepts in the critical-incident definition tie authority to the power to physically halt a running system (source: https://www.pymnts.com/news/artificial-intelligence/2026/california-governor-mobilizes-expert-panel-strengthen-ai-safety-laws/, Sep 2026).
4. Onsite verification organisations imply regulated physical sites where models are examined; the sweep records no site, standard or accreditation yet (inference).
