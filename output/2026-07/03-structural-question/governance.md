Why It Matters:
The EU Digital Omnibus (approved 16/29 Jun 2026) moved the AI Act's core obligations to Dec 2027 and Aug 2028, and the US executive order (Dec 2025) is actively litigating and defunding state AI laws that took effect in January. If this pattern holds, the operative "AI law" of 2027 will be written by insurers, buyers, and judges rather than legislatures — with profound consequences for who gets protected and who gets a seat at the table.
