- First sweep under method v2 (baseline: ungrounded 2026-02 v1 run).
- Learning's 2026 evidence splits cleanly: structured AI tutoring shows real measured gains, while unstructured AI reliance shows measured cognitive costs — the same technology, opposite outcomes, sorted by pedagogy.

Confirmed Developments:
- A systematic review of 28 quasi-experimental K-12 studies (4,597 students) found statistically significant positive effects of AI intelligent tutoring systems on learning outcomes versus conventional methods (source: https://www.engageli.com/blog/ai-in-education-statistics, 2026) (source: https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/oecd-digital-education-outlook-2026_062a7394-en.html, 2026)
- The OECD published its Digital Education Outlook 2026, formalizing international comparison of AI-in-education policy — learning is now a tracked governance domain, not just an edtech market (source: https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/oecd-digital-education-outlook-2026_062a7394-en.html, 2026) (source: https://etcjournal.com/2026/03/02/three-best-uses-of-ai-in-education-in-2026/, Mar 2026)

Emerging Signals:
- The preparation gap is quantified: 47% of recent graduates say AI already changed hiring in their field, but only 23% received substantive AI training in their studies (source: https://www.cnbc.com/select/class-of-2026-hiring-stats-and-ai-trends/, 2026)
- Entry-level roles now demand senior skills (7x more likely to require judgement/leadership), compressing the learning curve employers used to fund into pre-employment education (source: https://www.pwc.com/gx/en/news-room/press-releases/2026/pwc-2026-ai-jobs-barometer.html, 2026)

Counter-Signals:
- Unstructured AI use in learning contexts correlates with "metacognitive laziness" and reduced critical engagement — the tutoring gains do not generalize to free-form AI reliance (source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2512.12306, 2025)

Regulatory Shifts:
- None binding; OECD framework-building and national curriculum reviews are advisory so far.

Capital Movements:
- None verified this sweep.

Technical Changes:
- AI tutors now provide instant feedback, adaptive difficulty, and personalized paths at production quality; measurable gains typically require 3–6 months of consistent use (source: https://is4.ai/blog/our-blog-1/top-10-ai-tutoring-systems-2026-learning-outcomes-208, 2026)

Contradictions:
- Education systems must teach WITH AI (tutoring gains are real) and AGAINST AI (offloading erodes the skills education certifies) simultaneously — and most have frameworks for neither.
- Employers removed the entry-level jobs where skills were learned while raising skill requirements for entry — the learning ladder is being pulled up as its rungs move down into schooling.
