Confirmed Developments:
- A Jul 2026 study (n=580) found AI dependence associated with lower critical thinking, mediated by cognitive fatigue and buffered by AI literacy — dependency now has a documented psychological mechanism, not just a correlation (source: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0001691825010388, Jul 2026) (source: https://www.apa.org/monitor/2026/07-08/ai-job-skills-thinking, Jul 2026)
- One in five US teens report spending as much or more time with AI companions as with human friends — emotional dependency at population scale (source: https://www.commonsensemedia.org/press-releases/nearly-3-in-4-teens-have-used-ai-companions-new-national-survey-finds, 2025) (source: https://www.apa.org/monitor/2026/01-02/trends-digital-ai-relationships-emotional-connection, Jan 2026)

Emerging Signals:
- Infrastructure dependency is consolidating: MCP's React-scale adoption and hyperscaler capex (~$700B planned for 2026) concentrate the substrate most organizations' AI capability depends on (source: https://zylos.ai/research/2026-03-26-agent-interoperability-protocols-mcp-a2a-acp-convergence/, Mar 2026)
- Heavy emotional self-disclosure to AI is consistently associated with lower well-being in companion-user studies — the dependency gradient is dose-shaped (source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12928748/, 2026)

Counter-Signals:
- Dependence is not uniform harm: structured, deliberate AI use improves outcomes (critical thinking, learning gains in ITS studies), and workplace AI power users report better team connection — the variable is mode of use, not amount alone (source: https://www.mdpi.com/2075-4698/15/1/6, 2025)

Regulatory Shifts:
- California's companion-chatbot law is the first statute regulating emotional dependency by design (age verification, disclosure, no health-professional impersonation) (source: https://www.apa.org/monitor/2026/01-02/trends-digital-ai-relationships-emotional-connection, Jan 2026)

Capital Movements:
- Uninsurability is becoming the market's dependency signal: systems that are autonomous, opaque, unlogged, or single-provider-dependent face severe exclusions — insurers are pricing concentration risk before regulators name it (source: https://www.theactuary.com/2026/06/25/price-proof-insurance-policies-ai-enabled-world, Jun 2026)

Technical Changes:
- Persistent assistant memory (rolled out across major platforms by Mar 2026) deepens switching costs: the assistant that knows you is harder to leave (source: https://www.shareuhack.com/en/posts/claude-memory-feature-guide-2026, Mar 2026)

Contradictions:
- Institutions are building dependency-management frameworks for their systems (vendor risk, kill switches) while no equivalent framework exists for their people's cognitive reliance.
- The users most helped by AI companions (the isolated) are the same population most at risk from heavy use — benefit and harm concentrate in one group.
