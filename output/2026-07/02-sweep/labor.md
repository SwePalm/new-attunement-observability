Delta Since Last Month:
- First sweep under method v2 (baseline: ungrounded 2026-02 v1 run).
- The v1 baseline framed labor as a governance story; the 2026 evidence is a displacement story: AI-cited job cuts at six-figure scale, entry-level contraction, and agents explicitly replacing support and routine roles.
- Counter-evidence is also hardening: workforce reductions show no measured ROI correlation, and "AI" is increasingly suspected as a narrative cover for over-hiring corrections.
- Collective bargaining has emerged as the most concrete oversight mechanism, a channel absent from the February corpus.

Confirmed Developments:
- The US tech industry cut over 123,000 jobs in the first half of 2026 with AI the most-cited reason; Oracle's workforce fell ~21,000 (13%) in fiscal 2026 (source: https://www.forbes.com/sites/maryroeloffs/2026/06/04/tech-industry-loses-123000-jobs-this-year-ai-is-the-most-cited-reason-for-layoffs/, Jun 2026) (source: https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/22/the-running-list-major-tech-layoffs-in-2026-where-employers-cited-ai/, Jun 2026)
- Salesforce, Klarna, and Paycom have explicitly used AI agents to replace human support staff, with Salesforce reducing support from 9,000 to 5,000; Coinbase is piloting "one-person teams" managing agents (source: https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/22/the-running-list-major-tech-layoffs-in-2026-where-employers-cited-ai/, Jun 2026) (source: https://www.eweek.com/news/tech-layoffs-ai-investment-2026/, Jun 2026)

Emerging Signals:
- US entry-level postings are down ~35% since early 2023, with junior software development and data analysis postings down as much as 67%; young workers in AI-exposed occupations show a ~14% lower job-finding rate while older workers' employment holds (source: https://www.spglobal.com/en/research-insights/special-reports/ai-impact-on-employment-2026, 2026)
- AI-exposed entry-level roles are seven times more likely to require traditionally senior skills (judgement, leadership), compressing the career on-ramp (source: https://www.pwc.com/gx/en/news-room/press-releases/2026/pwc-2026-ai-jobs-barometer.html, 2026)
- Union AI protections are spreading through contracts: 58 NewsGuild-CWA agreements carry AI clauses; Microsoft union agreements require AI-impact notice; the Culinary Union secured bargain-before-deployment and tech-layoff severance (source: https://partnershiponai.org/these-3-agreements-secured-ai-protections-for-30000-union-workers/, 2026)

Counter-Signals:
- ~80% of executives report workforce reductions, but analysts find no correlation between reductions and improved ROI ("reductions create budget room, not return") (source: https://www.eweek.com/news/tech-layoffs-ai-investment-2026/, Jun 2026)
- The "AI layoffs" attribution itself is contested, analysts argue AI is partly a scapegoat narrative for post-2021 over-hiring corrections, and employment keeps growing in healthcare, government, and hospitality (source: https://www.shrm.org/topics-tools/news/technology/ai-layoffs-transformation-scapegoat, 2026)

Regulatory Shifts:
- The EU omnibus deferral moved Annex III high-risk obligations, which cover recruitment and employment AI, to 2 Dec 2027, delaying the strongest statutory constraint on workplace AI (source: https://www.gibsondunn.com/eu-ai-act-omnibus-agreement-postponed-high-risk-deadlines-and-other-key-changes/, Jun 2026)

Capital Movements:
- Layoffs are being explicitly repurposed as AI capital: GitLab cut ~14% of staff to fund AI infrastructure investment amid surging AI-workflow traffic (source: https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/22/the-running-list-major-tech-layoffs-in-2026-where-employers-cited-ai/, Jun 2026)

Technical Changes:
- Agent platforms are moving from assistive chat to role-replacing production workflows in support, operations, and routine engineering, per the layoff disclosures above (source: https://www.eweek.com/news/tech-layoffs-ai-investment-2026/, Jun 2026)

Contradictions:
- Companies cite AI capability as the reason for cuts while independent analysis finds no ROI correlation, displacement is running ahead of demonstrated substitution value.
- The career on-ramp is being removed (entry-level contraction) while employers simultaneously report senior-skill shortages that on-ramps exist to produce.
