Confirmed Developments:
- MCP reached ~97 million monthly SDK downloads by Mar 2026 (React-scale adoption in 16 months), with native support from Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, Microsoft, and AWS (source: https://zylos.ai/research/2026-03-26-agent-interoperability-protocols-mcp-a2a-acp-convergence/, Mar 2026) (source: https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/ai-agent-protocol-ecosystem-map-2026-mcp-a2a-acp-ucp, 2026)
- Google's Agent-to-Agent (A2A) protocol reached v1.0 in Apr 2026 with 150+ supporting organizations, defining peer discovery, task sharing, and cross-boundary coordination (source: https://www.programming-helper.com/tech/agent-to-agent-protocol-2026-google-a2a-standard, Apr 2026) (source: https://zylos.ai/research/2026-02-15-agent-to-agent-communication-protocols/, Feb 2026)

Emerging Signals:
- Commerce coordination protocols are proliferating on top of the stack: ACP (Stripe/OpenAI), UCP (Google, launched at NRF Jan 2026), MPP (Stripe/Tempo, Mar 2026) — the "agent economy" has competing rails before it has settled norms (source: https://opascope.com/insights/ai-shopping-assistant-guide-2026-agentic-commerce-protocols/, 2026)
- 84% of enterprises plan to increase agent investment in 2026, much of it multi-agent orchestration (source: https://www.ruh.ai/blogs/ai-agent-protocols-2026-complete-guide, 2026)

Counter-Signals:
- Coordination capability outruns coordination visibility: only 24.4% of organizations have full visibility into which of their agents talk to each other, and over half of agents run without security oversight or logging (source: https://agatsoftware.com/blog/ai-agent-security-enterprise-2026/, 2026)

Regulatory Shifts:
- None — protocol governance is currently private (foundations, vendor consortia), with no statutory framework addressing inter-agent coordination.

Capital Movements:
- Card networks and payment processors (Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Stripe) are investing directly in agent-coordination rails, treating protocol position as strategic infrastructure (source: https://www.paymentexecutive.com/post/agentic-commerce-2026-the-payments-revolution-where-ai-agents-actually-buy-stuff, Apr 2026)

Technical Changes:
- Marketplace-distributed agent capabilities became an attack surface: 824 of 10,700 skills on a major public agent-skill marketplace were found malicious by mid-Feb 2026 — coordination infrastructure inherits supply-chain risk (source: https://blog.cyberdesserts.com/ai-agent-security-risks/, Feb 2026)

Contradictions:
- Interoperability standards designed to make agents composable also make attacks composable — the same discovery mechanisms serve both.
- Enterprises are standardizing how agents coordinate before standardizing how humans supervise coordinated agents.
