- Largest structural change vs. the February narrative: EU high-risk AI obligations were deferred, not tightened — enterprise and insurance-market mechanisms are now hardening faster than statute.

Confirmed Developments:
- The EU Digital Omnibus package was endorsed by Parliament (16 Jun 2026) and approved by Council (29 Jun 2026), postponing AI Act Annex III high-risk obligations (recruitment, credit scoring, education, law enforcement) to 2 Dec 2027 and Annex I embedded systems to 2 Aug 2028 (source: https://www.gibsondunn.com/eu-ai-act-omnibus-agreement-postponed-high-risk-deadlines-and-other-key-changes/, Jun 2026) (source: https://www.traverssmith.com/knowledge/knowledge-container/eu-agrees-to-delay-key-ai-act-compliance-deadlines/, Jun 2026)
- AI Act transparency rules still take effect in Aug 2026, while AI-content watermarking obligations were delayed to 2 Dec 2026 (source: https://www.hklaw.com/en/insights/publications/2026/04/us-companies-face-eu-ai-acts-possible-august-2026-compliance-deadline, Apr 2026) (source: https://www.fisherphillips.com/en/insights/insights/eu-overhauls-ai-act-just-before-key-deadline, Jun 2026)
- The 2026 Edelman Trust Barometer measured an institutional trust gap on AI: 34% are comfortable with government use of AI, 46% with business, 56% with their own employer (source: https://www.edelman.com/trust/2026/trust-barometer, Jan 2026) (source: https://prsay.prsa.org/2026/01/23/edelman-trust-barometer-economic-anxiety-ai-fears-distrust-making-people-insular/, Jan 2026)

Emerging Signals:
- Agent-gateway architectures (policy evaluation and risk scoring of every tool invocation before execution) are becoming a recognized enterprise control category, though most organizations lack full visibility into agent-to-agent activity (source: https://labs.cloudsecurityalliance.org/research/csa-research-note-ai-agent-governance-framework-gap-20260403/, Apr 2026)
- 80.9% of surveyed technical teams are past planning into active agent testing or deployment, but only 37–40% report containment controls such as purpose binding and kill-switch capability (source: https://www.okta.com/newsroom/articles/ai-agents-at-work-2026-agentic-enterprise-security/, 2026)
- Usage drives trust: employees who report AI helped them at work express trust at rates 26–46 percentage points higher than non-beneficiaries (source: https://www.edelman.com/insights/familiarity-breeds-trust-in-ai, 2026)

Counter-Signals:
- Regulatory hardening is not monotonic: the EU chose simplification and deferral under competitiveness pressure, weakening the "compliance clock" assumption that dominated earlier trust narratives (source: https://www.traverssmith.com/knowledge/knowledge-container/eu-agrees-to-delay-key-ai-act-compliance-deadlines/, Jun 2026)
- The "AI trust crisis" framing is complicated by evidence that direct experience converts skeptics; distrust concentrates among non-users and lower-income groups rather than being uniform (source: https://www.edelman.com/insights/familiarity-breeds-trust-in-ai, 2026)

Regulatory Shifts:
- ISO is making AI exclusions the default in US Commercial General Liability policies (CG 40 47 / CG 40 48 endorsements), pushing AI risk into specialty markets (source: https://www.theactuary.com/2026/06/25/price-proof-insurance-policies-ai-enabled-world, Jun 2026)

Capital Movements:
- Affirmative AI liability insurance products are scaling: Armilla (underwritten at Lloyd's) covers hallucinations and model drift, and Testudo launched a claims-made enterprise product in Jan 2026 (source: https://www.theactuary.com/2026/06/25/price-proof-insurance-policies-ai-enabled-world, Jun 2026)

Technical Changes:
- Runtime agent-control infrastructure (gateways, purpose binding, kill switches, audit trails) is productizing as a distinct layer between models and enterprise systems (source: https://labs.cloudsecurityalliance.org/research/csa-research-note-ai-agent-governance-framework-gap-20260403/, Apr 2026)

Contradictions:
- Enterprises are deploying agents faster than they can contain them: ~81% deploying or testing versus ~37–40% with containment controls.
- Standard insurance is excluding AI risk at the exact moment adoption makes coverage most necessary, creating an assurance vacuum specialty products only partially fill.
