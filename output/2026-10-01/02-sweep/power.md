# Evidence sweep, power, 2026-10-01

Delta Since Last Sweep:
- The PJM track moved from execution to a regulatory stop. The 2026-09-25 sweep recorded PJM holding training and publishing execution materials for the Reliability Backstop Procurement (RBP). On 29 Sep 2026 FERC partly rejected and suspended the filing, forcing PJM to postpone the auction window that was due to open 30 Sep 2026. FERC suspended the filing until 28 Feb 2027 (source: https://www.yahoo.com/news/us/articles/ferc-chair-slams-pjm-mess-134855049.html, Sep 2026) (source: https://finance.yahoo.com/energy/articles/biggest-us-grid-data-center-043445216.html, Sep 2026)
- Local siting pauses kept spreading in the six days since 25 Sep: Palm Springs, California extended its data center moratorium on 30 Sep 2026 and Milwaukee's City Plan Commission endorsed a one-year citywide moratorium the same day (source: https://kesq.com/news/2026/09/30/palm-springs-city-council-approves-extending-data-center-moratorium/, Sep 2026) (source: https://urbanmilwaukee.com/2026/09/30/commission-endorses-milwaukee-data-center-moratorium-after-fiery-hearing/, Sep 2026)
- Texas: no new executive action found since the 21 Sep TCEQ halt; the new item is a legal-authority challenge to it. TCEQ's compliance update is still due 19 Oct 2026 and no lawsuit has been reported (source: https://www.sacurrent.com/news/abbott-orders-pause-of-air-permits-for-data-centers-experts-question-whether-he-has-the-authority/, Sep 2026)
- Oracle/Project Jupiter and H.R. 9340: no new development found beyond the 25 Sep baseline.

Confirmed Developments:
- On 29 Sep 2026 FERC accepted but suspended PJM's Reliability Backstop Procurement filing (a one-time auction to procure about 6.8 GW of new capacity against a 6,831 MW shortfall driven by data center demand). FERC approved the $555/MW-day offer cap but found cost allocation, transmission owner exit rules and load-serving entity collateral unresolved. Chair Laura Swett said the Commission would not be forced into accepting a "deeply flawed, eleventh-hour" mechanism. PJM confirmed the delay and said it would work quickly to address the remaining concerns; the suspension runs to 28 Feb 2027, so the 2 Dec 2026 results date in PJM's schedule cannot hold (source: https://www.yahoo.com/news/us/articles/ferc-chair-slams-pjm-mess-134855049.html, Sep 2026) (source: https://finance.yahoo.com/energy/articles/biggest-us-grid-data-center-043445216.html, Sep 2026)
- PJM suspended the new data center power auction on 30 Sep 2026 after FERC's order, with implementation pushed to at least late February 2027 pending a FERC paper hearing (source: https://cryptobriefing.com/pjm-suspends-new-data-center-power-auction-amid-regulatory-review/, Sep 2026) (source: https://finance.yahoo.com/energy/articles/ceg-nrg-tln-stocks-spotlight-175147633.html, Sep 2026)

Emerging Signals:
- Palm Springs City Council voted unanimously on 30 Sep 2026 to extend its data center moratorium (originally 45 days from 26 Aug, expiring 10 Oct) for 22 months and 15 days while a zoning update is drafted, because current codes do not define data centers (source: https://kesq.com/news/2026/09/30/palm-springs-city-council-approves-extending-data-center-moratorium/, Sep 2026)
- Milwaukee's City Plan Commission voted unanimously on 28 Sep 2026 (reported 30 Sep) to endorse a citywide moratorium on new data centers for up to one year or until a zoning framework is adopted; it goes next to the Common Council, whose vote date was not verified (source: https://urbanmilwaukee.com/2026/09/30/commission-endorses-milwaukee-data-center-moratorium-after-fiery-hearing/, Sep 2026)
- Moratorium geography is now statewide in two states, not only municipal: New York's Executive Order 62 (14 Jul 2026) paused discretionary DEC permits for data centers of 50 MW or more, and Texas followed on 21 Sep 2026. The law firm source counts over 100 municipalities having adopted or considered moratoriums and at least 14 states with comparable bills introduced (source: https://www.jonesday.com/es/insights/2026/07/new-york-enacts-first-statewide-data-center-moratorium, Jul 2026)

Counter-Signals:
- The legitimacy of the Texas halt is contested. Public Citizen and Environment Texas say TCEQ has previously stated it lacks authority to halt all permits for a whole industry (it declined a similar request on cement kilns in 2024) and that durable change needs the legislature. TCEQ says it will comply. A court challenge would test this, but none has been reported (source: https://www.sacurrent.com/news/abbott-orders-pause-of-air-permits-for-data-centers-experts-question-whether-he-has-the-authority/, Sep 2026)
- FERC's refusal to wave through PJM's emergency mechanism shows the federal regulator slowing, not accelerating, a supply-side fix on ratepayer-protection grounds, which cuts against the narrative that the power constraint is met by emergency procurement. TD Cowen called the result "acute" uncertainty for generators (source: https://finance.yahoo.com/energy/articles/ceg-nrg-tln-stocks-spotlight-175147633.html, Sep 2026)

Regulatory Shifts:
- FERC's 29 Sep 2026 order on PJM RBP (docket ER26-3380): partial rejection plus five-month suspension to 28 Feb 2027, a paper hearing, and an invitation to refile addressing cost allocation, collateral and exit rules, which FERC said could expedite approval (source: https://finance.yahoo.com/energy/articles/biggest-us-grid-data-center-043445216.html, Sep 2026)
- The PUCT large-load interconnection rule (summarised 18 Sep 2026, so inside the prior sweep's window but not recorded there in detail) applies to new loads of 75 MW or more: a $100,000 study fee, $50,000 per MW security at the intermediate agreement stage, security release in 20 percent steps, and forfeiture if energization misses 24 months after the scheduled date (source: https://www.jdsupra.com/legalnews/puct-sets-financial-commitments-for-6245689/, Sep 2026)

Capital Movements:
- Independent power producers sold off on the FERC order: Constellation fell 4.2 percent to a more than two-month low, NRG to its lowest level in over 17 months, and Talen 0.5 percent on 30 Sep 2026 (source: https://finance.yahoo.com/energy/articles/ceg-nrg-tln-stocks-spotlight-175147633.html, Sep 2026)

Contradictions:
- Two summaries of the FERC order differ on its form. One says FERC "partially rejected" the filing on 29 Sep and approved the $555/MW-day cap; another says FERC "accepted but suspended" it to 28 Feb 2027. Both agree on the substance (open issues, delay to late February 2027). The order text on FERC's eLibrary was not opened, so the exact disposition is unverified. Reuters (reuters.com) and Bloomberg pages could not be opened by the fetch tool, and Forbes returned HTTP 403.
- Date mismatch: one summary says the auction window was scheduled to open 30 Sep 2026; PJM's own presentation (listed in search results, not opened) lists 29 Sep as the anticipated order date, 30 Sep as window open, 21 Oct as window close and 2 Dec as results. Treat these as PJM's pre-order plan.
- Non-English check: a German-language search (Bundesnetzagentur, Rechenzentren, Netzanschluss) returned only secondary summaries (AgNes network cost reform from 2029; a BMWE draft of 17 Jul 2026), none opened and none dated in the window. NZZ ran a 1 Oct 2026 Texas data center backlash report that was not opened. Nothing from non-English sources is used.
