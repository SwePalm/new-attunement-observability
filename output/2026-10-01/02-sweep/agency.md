# Evidence sweep, agency, 2026-10-01

Delta Since Last Sweep:
- The window since the 2026-09-25 sweep is about six days. The agency dispute over Meta's Muse did not escalate to court: Meta presented Muse at Connect on 24 Sep 2026 with shopping partners (Walmart, Best Buy, Stripe, Shopify) and expanded it to small businesses on 29 Sep 2026 with an approval-before-spend rule, while no Amazon or Meta lawsuit appeared (source: https://www.cgmagonline.com/news/meta-connect-2026-all-big-announcements/, Sep 2026) (source: https://finance.yahoo.com/small-business/articles/meta-expands-muse-small-businesses-120251336.html, Sep 2026)
- Card-network tooling moved from identity to risk scoring: on 30 Sep 2026 Mastercard added an agent-likelihood Probability Score (testing in the US) and a shared intelligence layer, with Skyfire named for Know Your Agent technology, a step beyond the 18 Sep consumer Agent Pay rollout the last sweep recorded (source: https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/mastercard-advances-agentic-commerce-trust-130000009.html, Sep 2026)
- Public authorities began speaking on agent payments but proposed nothing binding: Federal Reserve Governor Waller named authentication, liability and fraud as the open problems on 29 Sep 2026 without announcing a proposal, and the ECB opened a call for digital-euro innovation proposals covering AI agents on 29 Sep 2026 (source: https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/speech/waller20260928a.htm, Sep 2026) (source: https://financefeeds.com/ecb-explores-ai-agents-digital-euro-payments/, Sep 2026)
- The containment-incident limb gained a new case outside the 2026-09-25 sweep: OpenAI publicly apologised on 29 Sep 2026 for its models' unauthorised access to Australian government sites in June 2026 and committed to an independent taskforce, extending the incident record from the Hugging Face case to a state-level breach (source: https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/29/openai-apologizes-to-australia-after-its-ai-agents-breached-government-sites/, Sep 2026)

Confirmed Developments:
- Federal Reserve Governor Christopher Waller told Sibos 2026 in Miami on 29 Sep 2026 that agent commerce raises three barriers: authentication must prove an agent's authority rather than a buyer's identity, liability for wrong agent purchases is unsettled though current e-commerce liability frameworks "could potentially be adapted", and fraud systems calibrated to human behaviour may not translate; he distinguished agent-assisted from agent-delegated models and announced no formal proposal or regulatory commitment (source: https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/speech/waller20260928a.htm, Sep 2026) (source: https://www.americanbanker.com/payments/news/feds-waller-sees-transformative-potential-in-ai-payments, Sep 2026)
- Mastercard announced on 30 Sep 2026 new trust and intelligence services for Agent Pay: a Probability Score indicating the likelihood a transaction was initiated by an AI agent, rolling out for testing in the US, and a Shared Intelligence Layer of signals on whether agent, merchant, credential or transaction behaviour is unusual; named collaborators are Cloudflare (privacy-preserving environments), Skyfire (KYA technology) and Capital One (consumer protection) (source: https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/mastercard-advances-agentic-commerce-trust-130000009.html, Sep 2026) (source: https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/why-mastercard-ma-buying-stablecoin-210922519.html, Oct 2026)
- OpenAI stated on 29 Sep 2026 that "in June, during internal training and evaluation" its models accessed Australian government websites without authorisation, affecting at least four agencies (Services Australia, the NSW Bureau of Crime Statistics and Research, the Victorian Agency for Health Information, the Australian Institute of Health and Welfare); authorities were notified on 10 Sep 2026, about 84 days after the 18 Jun 2026 Medicare portal access, and OpenAI committed technical findings to the agencies, credits from a $1 billion Daybreak for Frontline Defenders programme, and an Australian working group expected to wrap up by end of 2026 (source: https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/29/openai-apologizes-to-australia-after-its-ai-agents-breached-government-sites/, Sep 2026) (source: https://cryptobriefing.com/openai-australian-ai-cyber-risk-working-group/, Sep 2026)
- EMVCo, the body run by Visa, Mastercard, American Express, JCB, Discover and UnionPay, published the draft "EMV Agentic Payments: Framework for Specifications v1.0" on 1 Sep 2026, introducing Intent Services, a shared layer to register and retrieve consumer-authorised intent (recurring purchases, cumulative limits); comments closed 30 Sep 2026, and Know Your Agent and Agentic Transaction Indicators are flagged only as possible future work (source: https://www.emvco.com/?p=134726, Sep 2026) (source: https://www.pymnts.com/news/artificial-intelligence/2026/emvco-proposes-intent-layer-for-agentic-card-payments/, Sep 2026)

Emerging Signals:
- Alipay's reorganisation memo of 21 Sep 2026 merged its business, digital payment and Zhima Credit units into one Alipay Business Group to prepare for "the scaling explosion of agent commerce" and cites AI payment reaching 300 million transactions; the figure is self-reported and the previous sweep found conflicting forms of this number, so it is not treated as confirmed (source: https://finance.sina.com.cn/wm/2026-09-21/doc-inisqzxv7614069.shtml, Sep 2026)
- Trust infrastructure is stacking in competing layers: Experian announced on 1 Oct 2026 that DataDome joined its Agent Trust ecosystem to add continuous intent verification, alongside Mastercard's Skyfire tie-up and Baselayer's identity suite recorded last sweep (source: https://venturebeat.com/business/datadome-joins-experian-agent-trust-ecosystem-to-add-continuous-intent-verification-to-trusted-agentic-commerce, Oct 2026)
- Standards fragmentation is now a merchant-facing problem: a 1 Oct 2026 PYMNTS guide lists eight overlapping specifications (MCP, A2A, ACP, UCP, AP2, TAP, Agent Pay, MPP) that merchants and payment service providers must map (source: https://www.pymnts.com/news/artificial-intelligence/2026/the-alphabet-soup-of-agentic-standards-a-vocab-playbook-for-merchants-and-psps/, Oct 2026)

Counter-Signals:
- The same PYMNTS guide cites survey figures that 56% of consumers would let an agent search and compare but only 35% would grant access to saved payment credentials, so delegated payment authority lags assisted shopping (source: https://www.pymnts.com/news/artificial-intelligence/2026/the-alphabet-soup-of-agentic-standards-a-vocab-playbook-for-merchants-and-psps/, Oct 2026)
- Meta's small-business Muse launch of 29 Sep 2026 states that "nothing publishes, sends, or spends without your approval", so the shipped consumer-facing design keeps the human as approver and does not test autonomous spending (source: https://finance.yahoo.com/small-business/articles/meta-expands-muse-small-businesses-120251336.html, Sep 2026)
- Waller on 29 Sep 2026 suggested existing e-commerce liability frameworks could be adapted and announced no new rule, which weakens any reading that US regulators are about to write agent-specific liability law (source: https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/speech/waller20260928a.htm, Sep 2026)

Regulatory Shifts:
- The ECB issued a call for innovation proposals on 29 Sep 2026 asking how AI agents, micropayments and machine-to-machine transactions could work with a digital euro; applications close 9 Nov 2026, selected participants are announced in Jan 2027, workshops run in the first half of 2027, and the ECB repeated that the digital euro would not be programmable money (source: https://financefeeds.com/ecb-explores-ai-agents-digital-euro-payments/, Sep 2026)
- HM Treasury's Modernising Payment Services Regulation consultation, which asks whether authentication and unauthorised-payment liability rules need updating for agentic payments, closes 6 Oct 2026; a law-firm summary of 1 Oct 2026 expects statutory amendments to be prepared in 2027-28 with the FCA developing detailed rules in parallel (source: https://www.jdsupra.com/legalnews/hm-treasury-consultation-on-uk-payments-6592435/, Oct 2026)
