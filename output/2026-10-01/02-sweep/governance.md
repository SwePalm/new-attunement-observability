# Evidence sweep, governance, 2026-10-01

Delta Since Last Sweep:
- The US federal executive moved from rhetoric to a signed, voluntary instrument. On 29 Sep 2026 the White House hosted six frontier developers signing a "Joint Commitment on Frontier Responsibilities", with no penalties, regulator or deadline, which Trump called "morally binding" (source: https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/9/29/trump-top-tech-firms-sign-accord-to-self-police-ai-development, Sep 2026) (source: https://www.aitechdaily.com/trump-ai-joint-commitment-frontier-responsibilities/, Sep 2026). The previous sweep only had the 19 Sep "AI Force" announcement and the 14 Sep "hoax" post.
- California closed its 2026 signing window on 30 Sep 2026. SB 1000, which was on the Governor's desk in the previous sweep, was signed and chaptered as an urgency statute, so it took effect on signing (source: https://www.gov.ca.gov/2026/09/30/californias-nation-leading-ai-framework-just-got-stronger-governor-newsom-signs-more-first-in-the-nation-worker-protections-and-more/, Sep 2026) (source: https://calmatters.digitaldemocracy.org/bills/ca_202520260sb1000, Sep 2026).
- A second Senate vehicle appeared next to the unreleased Thune/Cruz/Klobuchar draft. Democratic Senators Warner and Schatz introduced an AI risk management bill on 24 Sep 2026 requiring frontier models to be submitted for government testing 45 days before release (source: https://www.mlex.com/mlex/articles/2529863, Sep 2026). Roll Call reports Cruz objected when Warner tried to pass it (source: https://rollcall.com/2026/09/29/congress-continues-back-seat-role-as-ai-execs-feted-at-white-house/, Sep 2026).
- A US-China governance channel on AI opened during Xi's 23-25 Sep state visit: a "Dialogue on Superintelligence" and a commitment to explore an incident communication mechanism (source: https://en.ilsole24ore.com/art/the-us-and-china-are-to-open-a-direct-channel-of-communication-to-manage-incidents-AJkJR3PB, Sep 2026) (source: https://en.mercopress.com/2026/09/25/trump-and-xi-end-the-state-visit-with-the-trade-truce-extended-and-no-substantive-deals, Sep 2026).
- EU: nothing new on the AI Act or the ChatGPT DSA designation in the Commission feed for 25 Sep to 2 Oct 2026. The only AI-adjacent items are a copyright feedback call (29 Sep) and a feedback call on the KIDS Act proposal (2 Oct) (source: https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/news, Oct 2026).

Confirmed Developments:
- On 29 September 2026 President Trump and the heads or senior officers of Anthropic (Amodei), Google (Pichai), Meta (Zuckerberg), OpenAI (Brockman), Nvidia (Huang) and xAI (Musk) signed the "Joint Commitment on Frontier Responsibilities". It sets four layers: internal controls monitoring model capabilities and alignment (including cyber and bio/chem risk), an internal oversight team, partnerships with independent external auditors chosen by each company, and a board-level committee reviewing auditor reports. It contains no enforcement mechanism, disclosure requirement or implementation deadline, and the companies pledge to "meet regularly" on standards (source: https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/9/29/trump-top-tech-firms-sign-accord-to-self-police-ai-development, Sep 2026) (source: https://www.aitechdaily.com/trump-ai-joint-commitment-frontier-responsibilities/, Sep 2026)
- Governor Newsom signed SB 1000 on 30 September 2026. It deletes the one-million-monthly-user threshold from the California AI Transparency Act's definition of covered provider, replaces "AI detection tool" with "disclosure verification tool", adds an assistive-technology exemption, and carries an urgency clause so it took effect on enactment. The same day he signed 12 other AI-related bills including AB 2713, SB 947, SB 951 and SB 1159 (source: https://calmatters.digitaldemocracy.org/bills/ca_202520260sb1000, Sep 2026) (source: https://www.gov.ca.gov/2026/09/30/californias-nation-leading-ai-framework-just-got-stronger-governor-newsom-signs-more-first-in-the-nation-worker-protections-and-more/, Sep 2026)
- Senators Warner, Schatz and Kim (per one outlet; two sponsors per mlex) introduced a frontier-AI risk management bill on 24 September 2026 that would require developers to give a Commerce Department safety committee model access at least 45 days before release, with civil penalties reported at up to $250,000 per day. Coverage names the bill variously as the AI Risk Management and Security Act of 2026 and the AI Risk Management and Safety Act, and no S. number was seen (source: https://www.mlex.com/mlex/articles/2529863, Sep 2026) (source: https://www.winzheng.com/en/article/us-senate-ai-risk-management-security-act-2026, Sep 2026)
- The US and China agreed during Xi Jinping's state visit (ended 25 September 2026) to a "Dialogue on Superintelligence" on risks and benefits and to a bilateral communication channel for AI incidents. The Mercopress account is weaker, describing only "a commitment to explore" an incident mechanism, and the trade truce was extended to 10 January 2027 without other substantive deals (source: https://en.ilsole24ore.com/art/the-us-and-china-are-to-open-a-direct-channel-of-communication-to-manage-incidents-AJkJR3PB, Sep 2026) (source: https://en.mercopress.com/2026/09/25/trump-and-xi-end-the-state-visit-with-the-trade-truce-extended-and-no-substantive-deals, Sep 2026)

Emerging Signals:
- The White House issued an executive order, "Inaugurating the Era of Super Intelligence", directing agencies to call AI "super intelligence" in official documents and giving the science adviser 60 days (to about late Nov 2026) to propose legislative language defining the term and whether it should supersede existing legal AI definitions. The State Department had already instructed staff to use "SI" after Trump's UN speech (source: https://www.nextgov.com/artificial-intelligence/2026/09/white-house-unveils-super-intelligence-executive-order-and-industry-accord/416325/, Sep 2026) (source: https://es.euronews.com/2026/09/24/trump-destierra-artificial-ordena-a-los-funcionarios-llamarla-superinteligencia, Sep 2026)
- Trump said he would appoint someone to oversee compliance with the Joint Commitment, after announcing on 19 Sep an "AI Force" and an upcoming AI czar. No name, instrument or budget has been published (source: https://www.aitechdaily.com/trump-ai-joint-commitment-frontier-responsibilities/, Sep 2026)
- Speaker Johnson made no legislative commitment at the White House event and pointed to industry self-regulation principles, while the accord text itself says that "over time, it may make sense to codify these steps into laws or regulations" (source: https://rollcall.com/2026/09/29/congress-continues-back-seat-role-as-ai-execs-feted-at-white-house/, Sep 2026)

Counter-Signals:
- The accord weakens rather than advances binding governance. It has no penalties, no regulator and no deadline, signatories choose their own auditors, and critics quoted by Al Jazeera call it an industry marking its own homework. It sits beside a federal executive that has called safety concerns a hoax, while California (SB 813, AB 1405, SB 1000) adds verification duties for the same developers (source: https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/9/29/trump-top-tech-firms-sign-accord-to-self-police-ai-development, Sep 2026)
- The US-China channel is thin. The Mercopress account says the leaders agreed only to "explore" an incident mechanism and reached no substantive deals on tariffs, Taiwan or chips, so the headline channel has no named agency, date or text (source: https://en.mercopress.com/2026/09/25/trump-and-xi-end-the-state-visit-with-the-trade-truce-extended-and-no-substantive-deals, Sep 2026)

Regulatory Shifts:
- California SB 1000 took effect immediately on 30 September 2026 (urgency clause), so the AI Transparency Act now reaches generative AI systems of any size that are publicly accessible in California. The Act's operative date was already 2 Aug 2026 (source: https://calmatters.digitaldemocracy.org/bills/ca_202520260sb1000, Sep 2026) (source: https://www.gov.ca.gov/2026/09/30/californias-nation-leading-ai-framework-just-got-stronger-governor-newsom-signs-more-first-in-the-nation-worker-protections-and-more/, Sep 2026)
- The executive order of 29 September 2026 sets a 60-day clock for proposed statutory language on "super intelligence" (source: https://www.nextgov.com/artificial-intelligence/2026/09/white-house-unveils-super-intelligence-executive-order-and-industry-accord/416325/, Sep 2026)
