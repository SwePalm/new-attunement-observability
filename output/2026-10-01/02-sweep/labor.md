# Evidence sweep, labor, 2026-10-01

Delta Since Last Sweep:
- The California bills the 2026-09-25 sweep listed as awaiting the Governor were all signed on 30 Sep 2026: SB 947 (Chapter 859) and SB 951 (Chapter 860) are chaptered, and the Governor's office lists AB 1883 and AB 1331 among the worker-protection bills signed the same day; the 2026-09-25 sweep recorded only enrolment (source: https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/billStatusClient.xhtml?bill_id=202520260SB947, Sep 2026)
- The realized-employment series reversed again: the BLS release of 2 Oct 2026 shows September payrolls up only 29,000 and unemployment at 4.2% (up from 4.1%), with July revised from +21,000 to -10,000 and August from +162,000 to +133,000, undoing the 4 Sep revision that the 2026-09-25 sweep reported (source: https://www.bls.gov/news.release/empsit.htm, Oct 2026)
- Challenger's September report (released 1 Oct 2026) shows announced cuts of 43,281 (down 18% on August), AI as the fifth-most cited reason (3,961, about 9% of the month) and year-to-date AI-cited cuts at 120,136; the 2026-09-25 sweep had only the August figures (source: https://www.challengergray.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Challenger-Report-September-2026.pdf, Oct 2026)
- Germany's metal and electrical bargaining round gained a published demand package on 23 Sep 2026 (5% pay, contractual job and future security, profit-sharing) with talks from 7 Oct 2026; the package as published does not mention artificial intelligence (source: https://www.igmetall.de/presse/pressemitteilungen/2026/ig-metall-beschliesst-forderungspaket-5-prozent-mehr-geld-sichere-jobs-und-gewinnbeteiligung, Sep 2026)

Confirmed Developments:
- Governor Newsom signed California SB 947 (No Robo Bosses Act) and SB 951 (AI-specific Cal/WARN technology-displacement notice) on 30 Sep 2026; SB 947 bars sole reliance on automated systems for discipline or termination and requires human corroboration plus written notice, reported as operative 1 Jul 2027, and SB 951 requires layoff notices to identify AI-automated job functions, reported as operative 1 Jan 2027 (source: https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/billStatusClient.xhtml?bill_id=202520260SB951, Sep 2026) (source: https://www.upi.com/Top_News/US/2026/10/01/california-artificial-intelligence/5411790881596, Oct 2026)
- US employers announced 43,281 job cuts in September 2026, down 18% from August and 20% from September 2025, the lowest September since 2022; year-to-date cuts are 573,195 (down 39%), Technology leads with 165,925 (29% of all cuts, up 54%), and announced hiring plans were 90,787 in September, the lowest September since 2011, leaving year-to-date hiring plans up only 3% (source: https://www.challengergray.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Challenger-Report-September-2026.pdf, Oct 2026) (source: https://www.cpapracticeadvisor.com/2026/10/01/u-s-companies-announce-fewest-job-cuts-for-a-september-since-2022/190969/, Oct 2026)

Emerging Signals:
- Business opposition to the signed California law is moving to definitional grounds: Chamber of Progress told UPI the "primarily relies" standard in SB 947 is never defined and leaves employers no objective standard, a possible basis for later challenge or guidance requests; Techstrong reports Uber and Lyft opposition softened after gig-worker provisions and advance notice were removed (source: https://www.upi.com/Top_News/US/2026/10/01/california-artificial-intelligence/5411790881596, Oct 2026) (source: https://techstrong.ai/ai-at-the-edge/robotics/newsom-signs-no-robo-bosses-act-banning-ai-only-worker-firings-in-california/, Sep 2026)
- Germany's largest bargaining round of the year is framing the future of work as job security and profit-sharing rather than AI: the IG Metall Vorstand demand of 23 Sep 2026 asks for "tarifliche Sicherung von Zukunft und Beschäftigung" for the roughly 3.7 million covered workers, with the peace obligation ending 31 Oct 2026 and warning strikes possible from 1 Nov 2026 (source: https://www.igmetall.de/presse/pressemitteilungen/2026/ig-metall-beschliesst-forderungspaket-5-prozent-mehr-geld-sichere-jobs-und-gewinnbeteiligung, Sep 2026)

Counter-Signals:
- Aggregate evidence of AI-driven labor loss weakened further this window: Challenger records AI at about 9% of September cuts, behind Market/Economic Conditions, Closings, Demand Downturn and sitting fifth, and Andy Challenger attributes the subdued layoff activity to a wait-and-see posture over energy costs, an uncertain war in Iran and a possible rate hike, not to AI (source: https://www.challengergray.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Challenger-Report-September-2026.pdf, Oct 2026)
- BLS describes employment in information, professional and business services and every other major industry as changing little in September, with health care (+17,000) the main gain, so the headline weakness is not concentrated in AI-exposed sectors this month (source: https://www.bls.gov/news.release/empsit.htm, Oct 2026)
- New York's AI checkbox on WARN filings had drawn zero AI attributions from over 160 filers a year in, per a May 2026 Hunton analysis, which suggests mandatory formats can under-record AI-linked layoffs and may bear on how California's SB 951 notices are used (source: https://www.hunton.com/hunton-employment-labor-perspectives/new-york-warn-act-no-ai-related-layoffs-reported-in-first-year-of-adding-ai-related-disclosure-to-the-system, May 2026)
