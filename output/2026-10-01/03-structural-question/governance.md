# Structural question, governance, 2026-10-01

Structural Question:
Over the next 6 to 24 months, when a frontier developer's safety claim has to
be checked by someone other than the developer, which instrument will fix who
counts as an independent examiner and what that examiner may test (the White
House "Joint Commitment on Frontier Responsibilities" with auditors each
company chooses, a Commerce Department pre-release testing committee of the
kind the Warner and Schatz bill proposes, California's disclosure-verification
duties, or the definitions the 29 Sep 2026 executive order asks the science
adviser to propose for "super intelligence"), and will the working meaning of
an AI audit be set by the signatories meeting among themselves before any
statute, regulator or court says what an audit is?

Why It Matters:
The theme's centre moved this run from deadline-watching to the question of who
verifies. On 29 Sep 2026 six developers signed a commitment with four layers of
internal and external checking and no penalty, regulator or deadline, in which
each company picks its own auditor and the signatories pledge to "meet
regularly" on standards (source:
https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/9/29/trump-top-tech-firms-sign-accord-to-self-police-ai-development,
Sep 2026). In the same week Democratic senators introduced a bill requiring
frontier models to be given to a Commerce Department safety committee 45 days
before release, and Roll Call reported that Cruz objected when Warner tried to
pass it (source: https://www.mlex.com/mlex/articles/2529863, Sep 2026), while
California's SB 1000 took effect on signing on 30 Sep 2026 and removed the
user-count threshold from its transparency act, so a verification duty now
reaches generative systems of any size (source:
https://calmatters.digitaldemocracy.org/bills/ca_202520260sb1000, Sep 2026).
The grading file for this run keeps governance-2026-07-02 open, with Congress
still only drafting, so the one binding instrument in force is a state's and
the only federal one is voluntary, which means the working definition of
"independent" is being written in the gap between them.
