# Structural question, labor, 2026-10-01

Structural Question:
Over the next 6 to 24 months, as California's SB 947 (human corroboration
before AI-driven discipline or termination) and SB 951 (layoff notices that
must identify AI-automated functions) come into force, will the records they
create show where AI is actually removing work, or will employers satisfy both
duties with a countersignature and a blank field while the displacement
happens at the hiring door, where no notice, corroboration or count is
triggered, and will collective agreements such as the Blizzard/Microsoft
contract and Germany's 2026 metal round, not statute, become the only place
where the introduction of AI into a job is negotiated before it happens?

Why It Matters:
The theme's centre moved in this run from whether AI-cited layoffs are large
to where the law and the data can see them. Governor Newsom signed SB 947 and
SB 951 on 30 Sep 2026 (labor-2026-09-01 graded falsified: the claim expected a
veto of SB 947), the first US statutes to regulate AI-driven termination and
AI-attributed displacement notice (source:
https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/billStatusClient.xhtml?bill_id=202520260SB951,
Sep 2026). Yet the aggregate series point away from layoffs: Challenger
recorded 43,281 announced cuts in September 2026, the lowest September since
2022, with AI the fifth-most cited reason at 3,961, while announced hiring
plans of 90,787 were the lowest September since 2011 (source:
https://www.challengergray.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Challenger-Report-September-2026.pdf,
Oct 2026). Realized payrolls rose only 29,000 in the BLS release of 2 Oct 2026,
which the sweep reads as a hiring freeze rather than layoffs, and New York's
AI checkbox on WARN filings drew zero AI attributions from over 160 filers in
its first year (source:
https://www.hunton.com/hunton-employment-labor-perspectives/new-york-warn-act-no-ai-related-layoffs-reported-in-first-year-of-adding-ai-related-disclosure-to-the-system,
May 2026). New laws that watch the exit, and data that cannot see the
entrance, decide whether the labor market's AI story is ever written down.
