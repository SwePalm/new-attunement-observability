# Forces and feelings, labor, 2026-10-01

## A. Systemic Contradictions (The Engine of the Story)

Contradiction 1 (Political vs. Social):
The political forces answer AI at work by guarding the moment of removal: SB
947 forbids discipline or termination on automated output alone, and SB 951
requires layoff notices to name automated functions. The Social forces show
what that guard produces in practice: a new low-status class of reviewers
confirming recommendations at throughput speed, and templated rejections that
cite an automated review "corroborated" by a human. The law that exists so
that a person decides has created a job whose whole content is agreeing, and
the applicant or employee on the receiving end gets an answer from no one.

Contradiction 2 (Economic vs. Legal):
The Economic forces adjust by freezing entry: announced hiring plans of 90,787
in September 2026 were the lowest September since 2011, announced cuts were the
lowest September since 2022, and payrolls rose only 29,000. The Legal forces
are triggered only by an exit: a termination, a layoff of covered size, a
notice period. Directly grounded in the sweep, where the BLS and Challenger
series together point to a hiring freeze rather than layoffs, and where New
York's AI checkbox found zero attributions in a year. The market does its work
at a door the law does not watch.

Contradiction 3 (Philosophical vs. Lived Reality):
Philosophically, the institutions of work claim that competence is built by
doing: a junior is wrong cheaply, is corrected, and becomes the senior who
will review the next junior. The lived reality of the entry rung, as the
Social and Technological forces describe it, is a role defined as
confirmation: forty decisions an hour, no authority to overturn any, no time
to be corrected. The world needs experienced reviewers to make human
corroboration meaningful, and it has stopped producing the people who would
become them.

## B. The Normalization of the Absurd (The Tone)

Normal Absurdity 1:
The countersign. Every flagged decision in an HR system comes to a named
person who ticks "supported by evidence" in about eleven seconds, and the
tick is referred to in meetings as "the human in the loop". Employees say
"I'll get it corroborated" the way people once said they would get it
notarised, and nobody expects the corroborator to know the person involved.

Normal Absurdity 2:
The plan that is also a headline. A company announces it will hire three
hundred people this year, and the number appears in the quarterly
presentation and in the careers-page banner. It does not appear as three
hundred open roles. People apply to "planned positions" the way they once
joined waiting lists, and a status of "future opening" is checked weekly, like
a parcel. This is the lived expression of the sweep's Challenger finding of
announced hiring at its lowest September level since 2011.

Normal Absurdity 3:
The blank that is correct. Layoff notices carry a field headed "AI-automated
functions" and the field almost always reads "None applicable" or
"workflow optimisation". Everyone, including the staff who fill it in, knows
that a blank is the normal outcome, in the way that nobody expected New York's
checkbox to be ticked. Support groups for the laid-off swap templates for
requesting the underlying record.

## C. The Single, Irreplaceable Thing

The Irreplaceable Thing:
A paid year of being bad at it: a position that exists on purpose to be done
badly, funded without having to earn its keep in output.

Explanation:
Announced hiring plans were the lowest for a September since 2011, announced
cuts were low, and payrolls barely moved, which the sweep reads as a freeze,
and the junior role is the first thing a freeze defers. The thing is scarce
because it is an institutional line on a budget, not a person: a salary that
the organisation agrees, in advance, will cost more than it returns. Statutes
now protect people from being removed by a machine; none create or require
the slot where someone may get a task wrong for months while someone
experienced has the time to correct it. A protagonist can chase it, one
application at a time. It differs in kind from company, witness or
recognition: it is a funded vacancy, and this theme's evidence compels no
repeat of the presence motif.
