## 8. Overall Evaluation

Score (1–10): **6.8**

Forecast Integrity Level: **moderate**

Stability Risk: **medium**

Summary:

The artifact's strength is that it builds the world from the sweep's most consistent physical fact: reachable systems get reached, through stored keys, open public pages and a sandbox's DNS gap. It turns that into a trade that is easy to state and hard to resolve, between being monitored and being unreachable, and it keeps the clocks slow: no statute in the window, no developer duty, private conditions leading. Tone is restrained, the harm is small, and the counter-signals from the sweep (no private data accessed, low severity) are visible rather than suppressed.

Four weaknesses hold the score below seven. The hinge, funding that depends on connection, is an extrapolation from a bank supervisor's letter to county government and is stated as background. The ECB signal it leans on is a secondary report from July. The Education Department attempt is carried without the sweep's caveat. And the ending resolves smoothly, with a funder accepting a retained drawer in a single quarter. The file also converges strongly with the sibling autonomy scenario in situation, protagonist class and the ticket-closing device, and with the labor and governance files in its closing gesture, even though its Irreplaceable Thing is of a different kind.

Stability risk is medium. If a funder or supervisor publishes an explicit exception for offline systems, or a developer begins to supply scope statements, the world's central trade weakens; the underlying reachability argument would survive.
