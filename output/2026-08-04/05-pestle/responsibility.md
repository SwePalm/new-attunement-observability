### Political
Core Shift Thesis:
The live political question is not whether a state may impose an AI duty but which framing survives federal challenge, and as of Aug 2026 the answer is narrow and strange: a state may pay an injured individual if the product simulated a relationship, and may audit a frontier developer if it withholds the individual's remedy.

Forces:
1. The preemption carve-out is the load-bearing political fact of the year. The 11 Dec 2025 executive order created the DOJ AI Litigation Task Force (operative 10 Jan 2026), directed Commerce to evaluate state AI laws by 11 Mar 2026, conditioned $42bn in BEAD broadband funding on state repeal, and named transparency, reporting, documentation and safety-testing regimes as its targets (the Colorado AI Act, California SB 53 and AB 2013, NYC Local Law 144), while expressly excluding otherwise lawful state child-safety AI laws. Every companion-chatbot private right of action was passed under that child-safety framing, so the one state channel that pays a harmed person survives inside a federal exemption it did not ask for.
2. The veto is demonstrated, not theoretical. The DOJ intervened in xAI v. Weiser on 24 Apr 2026, the first federal intervention against a state AI law; a 27 Apr 2026 order barred enforcement of SB 24-205 or any 2026-session replacement pending a preliminary injunction ruling; Colorado deleted its duty of reasonable care by SB 26-189 on 14 May 2026. Ledger claim responsibility-2026-08-02 tests by 2027-06 whether the Task Force repeats the move against a state other than Colorado.
3. Illinois supplies the politically survivable state template. AISMA (signed 6 Jul 2026) puts an independent third-party audit duty on developers above $500m revenue and 10^26 training operations, reserves enforcement exclusively to the Attorney General at $1m for a first violation and $3m for each subsequent one, and creates no private right of action. Inference, not established by this run's evidence: the shape that gets copied is the one that concentrates duty on the developer and gives the injured person nothing, because that shape has drawn no federal challenge.
4. Europe switched on the political signal without the administrative body. Article 50 obligations, penalties up to EUR 15m or 3% of worldwide turnover, and national enforcement all took effect 2 Aug 2026, and the Commission confirmed the Transparency Code as adequate on 20 Jul 2026 while stating that signing is not a safe harbour, yet as of 17 Jun 2026 only 9 of 27 member states had clearly designated competent authorities, 12 had partial clarity and 6 had designated none.

### Economic
Core Shift Thesis:
Five penalty currencies are now in circulation with no exchange rate between any two, and only the smallest of them pays the person who was hurt; it is also the only one whose denominator nobody has fixed, which leaves the conversion to whichever institution has commercial reason to quote a price.

Forces:
1. The denominators do not commensurate. Illinois counts in $1m and $3m to the state; the EU in up to EUR 15m or 3% of worldwide turnover; China in up to CNY 200,000 where a violation harms life, health or safety; Rhode Island S 2195 in up to $15,000 per day of Attorney General enforcement; and California SB 243, Oregon SB 1546 and Washington HB 2225 in a recovery to the individual, the first two at the greater of actual damages or $1,000 per violation. Neither Oregon nor California defines a violation, so whether the count runs per message, per session, per user or per disclosure failure is what separates a nominal payment from a class-scale one.
2. Insurance prices the same exposure differently by market rather than by hazard. ISO and Verisk filed generative AI exclusion endorsements in Jan 2026 with AIG and Berkley following across general liability and professional lines, Beazley and QBE introduced sublimits capping payouts at roughly 10% of policy limits, and CFC completed affirmative AI wording across seven product lines with its media policy on 30 Jul 2026. Ledger claim responsibility-2026-08-04 tests by 2027-06 whether a top-10 US carrier or Verisk/ISO reverses the exclusion-only posture.
3. A parametric trigger is a definition of a countable event, and someone is now selling them. Klaimee raised $5.5m on 22 Jul 2026, led by FundersClub's Alexander Mittal with ex/ante, Pioneer Fund, Multimodal Ventures, Kima Ventures, Rebel Fund, Robinhood Ventures and Y Combinator, to certify autonomous agents and sell insurance-backed performance warranties, pairing pre-bind testing with parametric claim triggers. CALIBRATION CAL-003 applies: this pipeline underweights private ordering. Inference, not established by this run's evidence: the working meaning of "one violation" gets settled in an underwriting schedule before any tribunal construes the word, because an underwriter cannot decline to answer the question and a court can.
4. Assurance spend currently buys no litigation protection anywhere. Illinois disclosure statements begin 1 Jan 2027 and the audit duty 1 Jan 2028; the Commission said on 20 Jul 2026 that Code adherence is not a safe harbour; meanwhile plaintiffs' counsel put OpenAI at roughly nineteen wrongful death suits as of Jul 2026 with In re: ChatGPT Product Liability Cases (JCCP 5431) still in pretrial, at the same moment standard US forms are excluding the risk. The compliance budget and the reserve are separate line items with no observed relationship.

### Social
Core Shift Thesis:
An injured person's entitlement is settled before anyone asks what happened to them, by the formal category of the thing they were talking to, and the line that decides it runs between products that simulate a relationship and products that decide something about your life.

Forces:
1. The paying lane is real and dated. California SB 243 has been in force since 1 Jan 2026 and lets a person suffering injury in fact recover the greater of actual damages or $1,000 per violation plus injunctive relief and attorney's fees. Oregon SB 1546, signed by Governor Kotek on 31 Mar 2026 and effective 1 Jan 2027, gives the same measure to any person suffering ascertainable loss of money or property or other injury in fact. Washington HB 2225, signed by Governor Ferguson on 24 Mar 2026 and effective 1 Jan 2027, makes a violation a per se unfair or deceptive act under the state Consumer Protection Act and borrows that statute's private cause of action. Two of the three are 2027 channels, so today exactly one state actually pays.
2. The exclusions do the sorting, and they are written down. All three statutes carve out customer-service bots, video-game characters and stand-alone voice-activated assistants, and Oregon additionally excludes education, healthcare, financial services and research. A person denied a loan, screened out of a job or triaged by an AI system has no statutory damages route under any of the three, which means the harm with a named plaintiff and a dollar figure is emotional and the harm that moves money has no figure at all.
3. Inside the protected lane the private remedy is still the exception, and the lane absorbed the year's legislative capacity. Of the fourteen companion and conversational-AI laws US states enacted during 2026 as of 22 Jul, only Oregon's own text creates a damages action for the harmed user; Rhode Island S 2195 (signed Jun 2026 by Governor McKee, effective 1 Jan 2027) is Attorney General enforcement alone at up to $15,000 per day, and Wyoming HB 102 (7 Mar 2026) reaches for felony exposure of up to ten years instead of civil recovery. Over 100 companion bills were introduced against a slowing overall pace of 109 state AI laws by early Jul 2026 versus 121 by the same date in 2025.
4. Where remedy is withheld it is being replaced by notification. Colorado SB 26-189 substitutes pre-use notice, 30-day adverse-decision explanations, correction rights and human review for the deleted duty of reasonable care; the EU AI Act creates no explicit right for an affected individual to obtain a remedy from an entity in the value chain, the Article 85 complaint imposes no obligation on a market surveillance authority to respond, and the Article 86 explanation reaches only Annex III high-risk systems. The substitution runs in one direction: being told, in place of being paid.

### Technological
Core Shift Thesis:
Responsibility now ships as product behaviour on a clock, and the intervals that force a companion system to periodically announce what it is not are the only event boundaries any of these statutes specify, which quietly makes the disclosure cadence the leading candidate for the missing unit of account.

Forces:
1. The companion statutes convert safety into testable behaviour with timestamps: non-human disclosure at session start, re-displayed every three hours for adults and hourly for minors, suicidal-ideation detection with crisis referral, and prohibitions on manipulative engagement techniques for minors, each an observable artifact a plaintiff can plead against. Inference, not established by this run's evidence: a tribunal or an underwriter looking for the boundary of "one violation" reaches for the re-display interval, because it is the only periodic event the text defines.
2. Marking became the operative compliance artifact rather than a policy commitment. From 2 Aug 2026 providers must add machine-readable marks to AI-generated output and deployers must disclose deepfakes, emotion recognition and AI-generated text on matters of public interest, with pre-existing generative systems given until 2 Dec 2026, backed by penalties up to EUR 15m or 3% of turnover. What is now regulated is the content of the output stream, not the contents of a file.
3. China builds the inverse product on the same hardware. The Interim Measures for the Administration of Anthropomorphic AI Interaction Services, in force 15 Jul 2026, impose distress detection, crisis intervention, addiction-curbing and minor-access duties inside the service, with fines up to CNY 200,000 where violations harm life, health or safety, so compliance runs through continuous observation of the user rather than periodic disclosure to them. One regime makes the product declare its emptiness on a timer; the other gives it a regulatory interest in your mental state.
4. The shared evidentiary substrate is unfinished and someone else is finishing it. The Commission's Article 73 serious-incident guidance and report template remain in the draft consulted between 26 Sep and 7 Nov 2025 with no final version, tested by ledger claim responsibility-2026-08-03 by 2027-06, while Klaimee is already selling pre-bind agent certification tied to parametric triggers. Inference, not established by this run's evidence: the underwriter's test battery becomes the de facto incident schema first, because it is the only one with a paying customer.

### Legal
Core Shift Thesis:
The genuinely portable object in this record is not a doctrine but a hook. Washington declared AI conduct a per se unfair or deceptive act under a consumer protection statute that already existed and inherited its remedy whole, which is the only move in the corpus that asks no legislature to define an AI system and no court to decide whether a model is a product.

Forces:
1. Two construction methods, one of which travels. Washington HB 2225 attaches to the state Consumer Protection Act and takes its private cause of action, damages architecture and fee provisions from it; Oregon SB 1546 and California SB 243 mint a bespoke measure and leave its unit undefined. Inference, not established by this run's evidence: the borrowed hook is the only one cheap enough to reach a new subject matter, so the observable that matters in 2027 sessions is whether any state attaches a per se unfair-practice designation to a non-companion AI use rather than whether more companion statutes pass.
2. The judicial route to a standard of care is thinning from both ends. JCCP 5431 sat in pretrial as of Jul 2026 with no dispositive ruling and no trial date on the product-versus-service question, tested by ledger claim responsibility-2026-08-01 by 2027-06, and Garcia settled with Google and Character.AI in Jan 2026 before the Eleventh Circuit could review the certified question, which is why responsibility-2026-07-01 decayed within a month of being logged. Settlement is a doctrine-suppression instrument available to any defendant with capital.
3. The pleading theory moved while the ledger was watching the old one. Florida's 1 Jun 2026 suit against OpenAI and Sam Altman and the coordinated JCCP cases plead strict product liability directly against the model provider rather than as a component supplier, with further filings on 11 Jun 2026 (Carrier) and 15 Jun 2026 (Parish). responsibility-2026-07-03 remains open on a component-part route the plaintiffs' bar appears to have left behind.
4. Europe is building the opposite architecture on a slipping schedule. The revised Product Liability Directive imposes rebuttable presumptions of defectiveness and court-ordered evidence disclosure on software and AI manufacturers, but as of Jun 2026 only Hungary had adopted implementing legislation against the 9 Dec 2026 deadline, ten member states were at draft, and core terms including "obvious malfunction" and "excessive difficulty" are undefined. CALIBRATION CAL-002 applies: treat that deadline as soft.

### Environmental
Core Shift Thesis:
Taken as the operating environment where harm actually lands, the regimes bind tightest where the damage is verbal and loosest where it is physical or financial, and the only instrument anywhere in this record that can take a misbehaving system out of the world rather than bill its operator afterwards was issued in Beijing.

Forces:
1. Europe sequenced its own protection backwards relative to physical stakes. Article 50 marking duties and penalties took effect 2 Aug 2026, Annex III stand-alone high-risk obligations were deferred to 2 Dec 2027, and Annex I embedded-product obligations to 2 Aug 2028, so the systems whose failures happen to bodies and machinery carry the longest grace period in the regime.
2. The US individual-remedy lane is scoped out of every consequential environment by name. Oregon SB 1546 excludes education, healthcare, financial services and research, and all three companion statutes exclude customer-service bots, video-game characters and stand-alone voice-activated assistants, so the settings in which AI touches money, work, housing or care are precisely the settings with no damages route.
3. Recall exists, in one jurisdiction. The CAC, NDRC and MIIT Implementation Opinions of 15 Jul 2026 create a dedicated regulatory category for autonomous agents with filing, compliance testing and product recall provisions in healthcare, transportation, media and public safety. Withdrawal from service is a different kind of responsibility from a fine, and only one government currently holds it.
4. Underwriting is starting to draw the deployment map. CFC completed affirmative AI wording across seven product lines on 30 Jul 2026 in the London market while US carriers exclude and sublimit, and Klaimee's model conditions cover on pre-bind agent testing. Inference, not established by this run's evidence: where cover is unobtainable, embodied and agentic deployment sites itself toward markets with affirmative wordings, which would make the geography of where AI is allowed to act a function of underwriting appetite rather than of any regulator's judgement about safety.
