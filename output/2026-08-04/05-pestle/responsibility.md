### Political
Core Shift Thesis:
The political contest has moved from whether to regulate AI responsibility to who may write the coupling between ex ante assurance and ex post liability, and in 2026 the US federal executive is actively vetoing states that attempt it while the EU turns on cheap duties and defers expensive ones.

Forces:
1. The DOJ AI Litigation Task Force intervened in xAI v. Weiser on 24 Apr 2026, the first federal intervention against a state AI law; a 27 Apr 2026 order barred enforcement of SB 24-205 or any 2026-session replacement pending a preliminary injunction ruling, and Colorado deleted its duty of reasonable care via SB 26-189 on 14 May 2026. Preemption litigation now functions as a standing veto over state-authored substantive duties.
2. Illinois AISMA (signed 6 Jul 2026) establishes the politically survivable state template: an audit duty on developers above $500m revenue and 10^26 training operations, exclusive Attorney General enforcement, penalties of $1m first and $3m per subsequent violation, and no private right of action. Inference, not established by this run's evidence: other states copy the shape that concentrates the duty on the developer while withholding the remedy from the individual, because that is the shape that has not yet drawn a federal challenge.
3. The Commission and AI Board confirmed the Code of Practice on Transparency of AI-generated Content as adequate on 20 Jul 2026 while stating explicitly that signing is not a safe harbour, and the Digital Omnibus held 2 Aug 2026 for Article 50, penalties and national enforcement while deferring Annex III to 2 Dec 2027 and Annex I to 2 Aug 2028. The political settlement is transparency now, substantive risk duties later.
4. China created a dedicated regulatory category for autonomous agents on 15 Jul 2026 via the CAC, NDRC and MIIT Implementation Opinions, with filing, compliance testing and product recall in healthcare, transportation, media and public safety, so a state instrument for withdrawing agents from service exists in Beijing while California has not yet decided whether a model is a product at all.

### Economic
Core Shift Thesis:
Responsibility is being priced before it is adjudicated: insurance and certification markets are setting the cost of AI care by jurisdiction rather than by hazard, and assurance spend currently buys no litigation protection.

Forces:
1. The liability market split transatlantically: ISO and Verisk filed generative AI exclusion endorsements in Jan 2026 with AIG and Berkley following across general liability and professional lines, Beazley and QBE introduced sublimits capping payouts at roughly 10% of policy limits, while CFC completed affirmative AI wording across seven product lines with its media policy on 30 Jul 2026. The same exposure carries two prices depending on where the policy is written.
2. Klaimee raised $5.5m on 22 Jul 2026, led by FundersClub's Alexander Mittal with ex/ante, Pioneer Fund, Multimodal Ventures, Kima Ventures, Rebel Fund, Robinhood Ventures and Y Combinator, to certify autonomous agents and sell insurance-backed performance warranties with parametric claim triggers. Agent behaviour becomes a warrantable good, and certification becomes a purchase condition rather than a regulatory one.
3. Illinois requires an independent third-party audit annually from 1 Jan 2028 with disclosure statements from 1 Jan 2027, while the Commission has said adherence to the transparency Code is not a safe harbour, so audit is a procurement and compliance line item with no established defensive return. Inference, not established by this run's evidence: audit reports get sold to enterprise buyers and underwriters, who will pay for them, rather than pleaded to courts, which have not said they count.
4. Frontier developers must reserve against an unpriced tail: plaintiffs' counsel put OpenAI at roughly nineteen wrongful death suits as of Jul 2026 with In re: ChatGPT Product Liability Cases (JCCP 5431) still in pretrial and no ruling on the product-versus-service question, at the same moment standard US carriers are excluding the risk from their forms.

### Social
Core Shift Thesis:
What an injured person is owed has fragmented into four incompatible entitlements, and which one applies is decided by where the transaction was booked rather than by what happened.

Forces:
1. Illinois audits the developer and gives the injured user nothing from that machinery: no private right of action, Attorney General enforcement only, reaching only developers above $500m revenue and 10^26 training operations, with no obligation binding before 1 Jan 2027. The audit produces a document no harmed person can invoke.
2. Colorado SB 26-189 (signed 14 May 2026, effective 1 Jan 2027) replaces a substantive entitlement with a procedural one: pre-use notice, 30-day adverse-decision explanations, correction rights and human review for automated decision-making technology, in place of the deleted duty of reasonable care and the risk-assessment and AG-notice regime.
3. China's Interim Measures for the Administration of Anthropomorphic AI Interaction Services, in force 15 Jul 2026, place distress detection, crisis intervention, addiction-curbing and minor-access duties inside the companion product, with fines up to CNY 200,000 where violations harm life, health or safety, making the provider a supervisor of the user's psychological state as a condition of operating.
4. In the US the remedy runs through litigation capacity rather than statute: the chatbot injury docket kept growing with new filings on 11 Jun 2026 (Carrier) and 15 Jun 2026 (Parish), but with no dispositive ruling and no trial date, compensation reaches only those whose counsel can carry a multi-year coordinated proceeding.

### Technological
Core Shift Thesis:
The operative technology of responsibility is now evidence production rather than harm prevention: machine-readable marking, incident telemetry and pre-bind agent test batteries are the artifacts that regulators, auditors and underwriters actually consume.

Forces:
1. From 2 Aug 2026 providers must add machine-readable marks to AI-generated output and deployers must disclose deepfakes, emotion recognition and AI-generated text on matters of public interest, with pre-existing generative systems given until 2 Dec 2026, backed by penalties up to EUR 15m or 3% of worldwide turnover. Marking moved from best practice to the compliance artifact that carries the fine.
2. Insurers are beginning to require pre-bind technical testing as a condition of cover, with Klaimee pairing agent certification and audit with parametric triggers rather than underwriting on disclosure alone. Inference, not established by this run's evidence: underwriting test batteries diffuse faster than any published standard, because failing them removes cover rather than merely inviting criticism.
3. The Article 73 serious-incident machinery is unfinished: the Commission's guidance and incident report template remain in the draft form consulted between 26 Sep and 7 Nov 2025, with no final version published, even though the obligation was nominally scheduled from Aug 2026. Inference, not established by this run's evidence: whoever finalises that template defines the shared evidentiary substrate that auditors check, insurers price off and litigants subpoena, which makes it the cheapest available coupling mechanism between the assurance and liability layers.
4. The Illinois audit runs against the developer's own published safety framework rather than an externally authored standard, so a firm's internal evaluation and logging stack becomes the audited object. Inference, not established by this run's evidence: frameworks converge on what existing telemetry can already evidence, because a finding of non-conformance with your own text is the failure mode that carries the penalty.

### Legal
Core Shift Thesis:
Two opposite responsibility architectures now bind the same developer depending on jurisdiction, and the institution best placed to couple assurance to liability, an appellate court fixing a standard of care, is the one a well-funded defendant can most cheaply settle out of the room.

Forces:
1. In re: ChatGPT Product Liability Cases (JCCP 5431) sat in pretrial as of Jul 2026 with no dispositive ruling and no trial date on whether ChatGPT is a product or a service, which is the precondition for any judicial coupling; ledger claim responsibility-2026-08-01 tests whether that ruling issues by 2027-06.
2. Settlement operates as doctrine suppression: Garcia settled with Google and Character.AI in January 2026 before the Eleventh Circuit could review the certified question, which is why responsibility-2026-07-01 decayed within a month of being logged and why no appellate ruling on AI product classification exists.
3. The revised EU Product Liability Directive imposes rebuttable presumptions of defectiveness and court-ordered evidence disclosure on software and AI manufacturers, but as of Jun 2026 only Hungary had adopted implementing legislation against the 9 Dec 2026 deadline, ten member states were at draft, and core terms including "obvious malfunction" and "excessive difficulty" are undefined. CALIBRATION CAL-002 applies: treat that deadline as soft.
4. Plaintiff pleading migrated from component-part liability to direct strict liability against model providers, with Florida's 1 Jun 2026 suit against OpenAI and Sam Altman and the coordinated JCCP cases naming the developer itself; responsibility-2026-07-03 remains open and may now be measuring a route the plaintiffs' bar has left behind.

### Environmental
Core Shift Thesis:
Read as the physical and infrastructural operating environment in which harm actually lands, responsibility binds hardest where harm is informational and softest where it is physical, and the first mechanisms for removing a misbehaving system from the world are being imported from tangible-goods safety law.

Forces:
1. Article 50 marking duties and penalties took effect on 2 Aug 2026 while Annex I embedded-product obligations were deferred to 2 Aug 2028 and Annex III stand-alone high-risk obligations to 2 Dec 2027, so the AI whose failures are physical carries the longest grace period in the EU regime.
2. China's Implementation Opinions of 15 Jul 2026 apply filing, compliance testing and product recall to agents in healthcare, transportation, media and public safety, making recall the first operative instrument for withdrawing a deployed agent from a physical operating environment rather than fining its operator after the fact.
3. The revised EU Product Liability Directive extends a regime built for tangible defects, personal injury and property damage, to software and AI with reversed burdens of proof, so the doctrinal vocabulary now being applied to model behaviour is inherited from physical product safety and its undefined terms are being tested on intangibles first.
4. Insurance capacity for physically deployed systems diverges by market: affirmative AI wording across seven CFC product lines in London against US exclusions and sublimits at roughly 10% of policy limits. Inference, not established by this run's evidence: where cover is unobtainable, embodied agent deployment gets sited toward markets with affirmative wordings, so underwriting geography starts shaping deployment geography.
