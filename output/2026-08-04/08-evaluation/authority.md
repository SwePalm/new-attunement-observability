## 3. Institutional Realism Audit

Assessment: **low to moderate realism**

Notes:

- Friction is real and specific in two places. The AI Office's constraint is named as throughput rather than will, which is the correct and unglamorous mechanism and makes "probability of being reached" a defensible governing variable rather than a slogan. Illinois is realistic in the opposite direction: an Attorney General, a filing date, a named deputy, penalties to USD 3 million, reporting into a civil-contingency intake. Both are well observed.
- Power asymmetry by geography is drawn carefully and is the scenario's strongest realism claim: two people harmed identically receive different remedies depending on which legislature held its nerve and which member state constituted an office. This follows the designation data and the state-count data without embellishment.
- The dominant realism failure is that the world is a 39-month freeze of a single July 2026 frame. The scene is November 2029. Between 2 Aug 2026 and that Thursday, the scenario requires: the AI Office to have opened no proceeding against any named provider; no US court to have reached the merits on federal preemption; no federal preemption statute to have passed; no member state beyond the nine to have completed designation; and the FTC theory to have remained untested. Every one of those is an assertion of institutional inaction sustained for over three years, and none is argued for. The sweep supports the starting state, not its persistence.
- The EU's own non-compliance machinery is missing. Eighteen of twenty-seven member states missed a 2 Aug 2025 designation deadline. Infringement proceedings are the ordinary Commission response to exactly that, and the scenario neither runs them nor explains their absence. Similarly, no budget line, no hiring, no first case, no learning curve at the AI Office across three years. A regulator with the largest competence ever granted over frontier models is modelled as permanently static.
- Litigation exists in this world only as a delaying instrument. The single reference to it, "the independent auditor finally retained after the duty slipped two years in litigation", has courts postponing a duty and never resolving one. `07-scenario/responsibility.md` from this same run builds a world around a live docket, and this theme's own ledger carries authority-2026-08-03 on a filed challenge to SB 315, so the pipeline is not blind to the adversarial channel. The authority scenario admits it and then permits it to do only the one thing that confirms the thesis.
- Industry has no agency, as covered in section 2. Nadia's employer prepares, prices and complies. It does not lobby, litigate, relocate its Dublin entity, threaten market exit, or spend against the Illinois statute, despite the sweep recording USD 145 million of directly relevant political expenditure across the named actors in a single cycle. A world about weak authority in which the regulated parties are entirely passive is missing its most documented force.
- Transitions are asserted rather than shown. Section 2 closes by declaring "What followed was not collapse but a habit", which forecloses three years by fiat. The Illinois disclosure date (1 Jan 2027) and audit date (1 Jan 2028) are moved by an invented litigation outcome rather than a described one.
- The world is too elegant. Four numbered forces, one governing variable, no defector, no regulator that acts, no firm that ignores a duty, no member state that gets ahead. The 2029 world contains no institution that behaves differently from how it behaved in the sweep's snapshot.

## 4. Horizon Discipline Check

Pass/Fail: **Pass**

Notes:

- Horizon is held. November 2029 is roughly 3.3 years out, inside 0-5 years. No sci-fi drift, no new technical primitive, no capability extrapolation. The world runs on statutes, dockets, wall charts and tabletop exercises.
- Institutional shifts are proportional to the timeline and are, if anything, conservatively small: an escalation board convention, an incident taxonomy mapped onto a civil-contingency schema, an onboarding line, a two-column planning slide. These are two-to-three-year changes, correctly scaled. Nothing statutory is invented.
- CAL-002 is named in 7.2 and applied in the right direction. Treating Illinois's 1 Jan 2027 and 1 Jan 2028 dates as contestable because the EU's replacement dates are themselves replacements is exactly the use the heuristic was written for, and the 2029 world's slipped audit duty is the world-building consequence of it. Credit this.
- CAL-002 is then overextended. The heuristic says announced regulatory timelines slip; the scenario uses it to license three years in which no regulator anywhere completes an action. Slippage of published dates is not the same proposition as institutional stasis, and the second does not follow from the first. This is the same failure mode this run's alignment evaluation identified with CAL-003: a calibration heuristic treated as a warrant for a specific world state rather than as a correction to attention.
- CAL-004 is named and applied well against the FTC claim, correctly noting that a finalisation already in motion would measure retrieval coverage rather than foresight. This is the sharpest single line of self-restraint in the artifact.
- CAL-003 is named but not used. The scenario says CAL-003 "points at where the real hardening lands first, in procurement terms, insurance conditions and authorization protocol", and then the world contains no procurement term and no insurance condition. Only the protocol limb appears, and that limb is correctly hedged as drafts rather than adoption. So the heuristic is cited as a forecast and ignored as a design input, which is the inverse of the alignment scenario's error but no better disciplined.
- The horizon overreach that is not flagged: every ledger observable the scenario relies on resolves by 2027-03 or 2027-06, and the world is set 29 to 32 months after the last of them. The scenario presents those four claims as the instruments that adjudicate its question while depicting a state of affairs none of them can reach.
- One internal inconsistency at the boundary between world and ledger. The escalation board carries the annotation "RFI answered Mar 2029, no proceeding". Under this run's own authority-2026-08-01, "a public formal request for information" is one of the qualifying first formal enforcement steps. By the ledger's operationalisation the AI Office in this world has acted; by the narrative's framing it has not. The scenario takes both readings at once, which slightly softens the thesis it is testing.

## 6. Narrative Inflation Risk

Assessment: **moderate**

Notes:

- Anti-inflation work is present and genuine. Section 9 opens by refusing the villain reading and enumerates what each actor did legitimately: Brussels took real powers for real reasons, Illinois legislated in the ordinary way, the FTC used an ordinary administrative instrument, Colorado's parties settled. Two explicit inference flags sit in the body. The register is procedural rather than alarmed, and the world is neither dystopian nor utopian.
- Against that, coherence inflation is severe and is declared rather than smuggled. Section 5 states outright that reachability, "rather than the size of the fine, the governing variable". Three of the four structural pillars are restatements of it, Section 4's four behavioural shifts are all consequences of it, and Sections 6 and 9 return to it. The upstream exploration set out a genuinely three-part decomposition (competence, capacity, adjudication) and bracketed the future between an adjudicated and an unadjudicated path. The scenario keeps only the unadjudicated path and collapses the three-part decomposition into a single variable.
- Emotional over-concentration around scarcity is structural. One Irreplaceable Thing is named upstream, centred in Section 3, restated in Section 6 and used as the closing cadence of Section 9. Nobody in the world wants anything else, nobody has a competing interest, and there is no scene in which a binding no exists and turns out not to help.
- The Probable Direction section is where inflation and thin forecasting meet. Its actual forecast is seven words ("The probable path is settlement by non-decision"), and the remaining 140 words are ledger identifiers and calibration codes. The section that should be doing the hedging and branching does bookkeeping instead, which leaves the world's single logic unchallenged by its own pullback layer. Measured against the sibling artifacts in this run, alignment and responsibility both spend a majority of 7.2 on substantive branching, so this is a defect specific to this file rather than a run-wide convention.
- One rhetorical tell shared with the siblings: no actor deviates. The absence of an opportunist, a defector or a mistake is what allows one variable to govern a world for three years without turbulence.

## 8. Overall Evaluation

Score (1-10): **6.4**

Forecast Integrity Level: **moderate**

Stability Risk: **high**

Summary:

This scenario is well anchored and honestly hedged at the sentence level, and structurally over-confident at the level of the world it builds. Its signal work is strong: every figure and date reaches the narrative from the sweep unaltered, the two protocol drafts that a less careful artifact would have installed as deployed infrastructure are explicitly marked as drafts, and the scenario's own central premise, that firms rank enforcers by reachability, is flagged as an inference with no firm's escalation logic in the record. That disclosure is the instrument working as designed.

The scenario outran its evidence in three places. First and largest, the capital layer is gone. The sweep's only Capital Movements item records USD 145 million of directly relevant political expenditure across named actors, `05-pestle` carries it twice and `06-forces-feelings` builds its second contradiction on it, and not one figure survives into the scenario. The regulated parties therefore appear as passive subjects rather than as actors who spent money producing the weakness the world is about. Second, the world is a 39-month freeze. Between the sweep's snapshot and November 2029 nothing completes anywhere: no proceeding, no merits ruling, no statute, no further designation, no infringement action against eighteen non-designating member states, no capacity growth at an office with the largest competence ever granted over frontier models. CAL-002 is invoked to license this, but a heuristic about slipping deadlines does not license institutional stasis. Third, litigation exists only to delay, which is what keeps the scarcity at the centre airtight.

Stability risk is high because the world's founding condition is falsifiable inside ten months: authority-2026-08-01 alone, resolving 2027-06, would break it.
