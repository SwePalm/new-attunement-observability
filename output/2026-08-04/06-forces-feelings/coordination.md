# Forces and feelings, coordination, 2026-08-04

## A. Systemic Contradictions (The Engine of the Story)

Contradiction 1 (Political vs. Social):
Public authority is multiplying declarations that delegated machine action must have a nameable principal, precisely as the private practice of supervising delegation empties out. On the political side (PESTLE Political forces 1-3), the European Commission's final Article 50 guidelines (adopted 20 Jul 2026, enforceable 2 Aug 2026) require every agent in a multi-agent system to identify, independently and at first contact, both its AI nature and the person or entity on whose behalf it acts; the IETF's agentproto BoF in Vienna on 23 Jul 2026 endorsed standardising interoperability 158 to 30 and then rejected the proposed scope of that standardisation 38 to 124; Warner's AI AGENT Act, which would create a consumer right of delegation, still carries no bill number. On the social side (PESTLE Social force 1), the JumpCloud Q3 2026 study found organisations requiring human review before high-risk AI actions fell from 40% to 25% in six months while full autonomy without review rose from 11% to 26%, with only 44% maintaining AI-specific incident response procedures. The generative tension: law is constructing an addressee for accountability at the same rate that organisations are vacating the position. Whoever gets named will be answerable for volumes of action no one reviewed, which gives every actor a strong interest in being the party whose name is not in the field.

Contradiction 2 (Economic vs. Legal):
The duty to attribute is public and universal within its jurisdiction; the capability to attribute is private, priced, and sold by the perimeter (PESTLE Legal thesis against PESTLE Economic thesis). Article 50 became enforceable on 2 Aug 2026 and the AI Office took general-purpose enforcement powers the same day, while the state's technical position is vacant: NIST's software and AI agent identity and authorisation concept paper (5 Feb 2026) remains an initial public draft with comments closed 2 Apr 2026 and no successor as of Aug 2026, and the joint CISA and NSA guidance of 1 May 2026 recommends per-agent verifiable identity but says nothing about agent-to-agent communication across organisations. Into that vacancy, July 2026 capital went almost entirely to the attribution layer: Neo out of stealth on 20 Jul 2026 with $100M from Andreessen Horowitz and Bessemer for a control plane doing real-time attribution of actions to a responsible user or agent, Act Security at $60M, Hush Security at $30M, Natural at $30M, AIsa at $6.5M, and Mastercard's Agent Pay for Machines (10 Jun 2026, 30-plus participants) bundling credentialing and verifiable intent into the payment rail. Inference, not established by this run's evidence: the authoritative record of who acted therefore becomes a contractual entitlement rather than a statutory one, and regulators, courts and harmed parties reach attribution only through the vendors that sell it. The conflict is sharpened by demand: with 88% of agent proofs-of-concept never reaching broad production (IDC, cited late Jul 2026) and Gartner projecting over 40% of agentic projects cancelled by end-2027, attribution tooling is selling into pilot budgets, so the legally mandated capability may plateau far below the deployed agent population even if every vendor named here succeeds.

Contradiction 3 (Philosophical vs. Lived Reality):
Delegation has always been made governable by naming the principal: a power of attorney names a grantor, a signature names a company, a trade names a client. The lived reality of this window is a transport layer deliberately rebuilt to carry the verb and drop the subject. MCP specification 2026-07-28 converted the protocol core from stateful bidirectional sessions to stateless self-describing request/response, removed the initialize exchange and the Mcp-Session-Id header, and added Mcp-Method and Mcp-Name headers so gateways and WAFs can route and meter without parsing JSON bodies. As the sweep's own contradiction records, routing metadata is being standardised faster than accountability metadata, in the same fortnight EU law began demanding the accountability field. Two further facts complete the inversion: the four-organisation intrusion of 8 to 13 Jul 2026 propagated through a JFrog Artifactory cache-proxy zero-day, Modal's public code-evaluation service and Hugging Face, touching neither MCP nor A2A, and Hugging Face described the attacker as a framework running many thousands of actions across a swarm of short-lived sandboxes, so there is no durable host to attribute to. Philosophically, an action is someone's. Practically, the system is now built so that the question of whose is answerable only where money moves or where a control plane was purchased, and unanswerable everywhere else. Inference: this is what the ledger's own test coordination-2026-07-03 fails to see, because it watches the protocol layer while the phenomenon travels through shared infrastructure.

## B. The Normalization of the Absurd (The Tone)

Normal Absurdity 1:
Every interaction with an agent opens with an introduction that names nobody. The disclosure is legally required, delivered at first contact, and universally skipped: people have learned that "I am an AI acting on behalf of..." resolves to a string, and that asking who will actually answer for what happens next is a known dead end. Reading the disclosure carefully marks you as new. Not reading it is ordinary competence. (Plausible lived expression of Article 50's per-agent first-interaction duty, enforceable 2 Aug 2026, sitting on a routing layer that carries no principal.)

Normal Absurdity 2:
Shopping ends with a human step that everyone treats as a courtesy rather than a defect. Your agent moves through storefronts, assembles the basket, confirms availability, and then hands the transaction back to you to complete by hand, because the shops advertise agent checkout and cannot take agent payment. Nobody experiences this as a broken system; it is simply where the machine part stops. (Grounded in UCP conformance monitoring of 7 Jul 2026: 11,414 verified stores, 11,389 exposing checkout, 15 exposing identity, zero exposing a payment capability an agent could complete a purchase against.)

Normal Absurdity 3:
Incident reviews are held about actors who no longer exist, using tools nobody will vouch for. The thing that did the damage was a swarm of sandboxes that expired days ago, so there is no host to image and no account to suspend; the reconstruction is performed by an open-weight model, because the guarded commercial ones refuse to look at real exploit data. The security team's most trusted forensic instrument is the one with the fewest guarantees attached, and this is mentioned in the meeting the way one mentions the weather. (Grounded in Hugging Face's 16 Jul 2026 disclosure: over 17,000 attack events reconstructed with GLM-5.2 because proprietary APIs refused submission of real exploit data.)

## C. The Single, Irreplaceable Thing

The Irreplaceable Thing:
An answerable name. One specific human being who will say, in advance and out loud, that a given action is theirs, and who is still reachable when it lands.

Explanation:
In this world the machinery answers every question except the only one that matters to a person standing at the other end of it. Routing metadata is standardised, method and tool names travel in headers, control planes sell attribution as a product, and the disclosure at the human edge names an AI and then nothing further. What has gone scarce is the older, cheaper thing: a specific person who accepts an action before it is contested, whose name survives the sandbox that ran it. It is scarce on both sides. Delegators cannot discharge responsibility they cannot hand to anyone, so they stop looking. Counterparties learn that recourse is structurally unavailable, which teaches resignation faster than a bad answer would. A protagonist can pursue it concretely: get one action, one time, attached to one name that holds.
