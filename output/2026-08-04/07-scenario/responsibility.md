## 1. Title & Core Question
- Title: The Sentence and the Sum
- Core Question: When carefulness and answerability become two industries with different customers, what does a harmed person actually receive, and is there any mechanism left that connects the audit to the apology?

## 2. Context Summary (Translation Layer) – Why This Future Exists
Between 2026 and 2029, responsibility for AI stopped being one obligation with two tenses and became two separate industries. The ex ante industry grew quickly because it was politically survivable: Illinois enacted the first US statutory frontier audit duty on 6 Jul 2026 with no private right of action; the EU switched on transparency marking and penalties on 2 Aug 2026 while stating that signing its Code was not a safe harbour; China created a dedicated regulatory category for agents on 15 Jul 2026. The ex post industry grew separately: a US chatbot injury docket that kept filing and kept not ruling, an EU product liability regime transposing years behind its 9 Dec 2026 deadline, and insurers pricing the same exposure differently in New York and London. Nothing was built to connect the two. Colorado deleted its duty of reasonable care in May 2026 under federal litigation pressure, and no state has written a replacement while the DOJ task force is active. Firms are inspected constantly and answer to nobody in particular.

## 3. Future World Snapshot (The Lived Experience) – A Day in This Future
Marta's Thursday in November 2029 begins with a document she is allowed to see. The developer's third annual assurance summary posted overnight: twenty-two pages confirming conformance with the firm's own published safety framework in all material respects. She reads it on the bus the way other people read a weather report. The annex, the part with incident counts, went to underwriters and enterprise buyers under NDA. Her lawyer requested it in April. The refusal was polite and correct: this is not that kind of document. Marta stopped being angry about that a year ago. In the group chat, families trade routes to the insurer's file instead, which sometimes works, because insurers keep records for money rather than for law.

At eleven, the plaintiffs' committee call. Two years and eight months after Teo died, the coordinated proceeding still has not been told whether the thing that talked to her brother every night for nine months was a product or a service. There is now a number. It is larger than she expected, and it arrives in the usual architecture: no admission, no finding, a sealed annex, the defendant's name on the payment and on nothing else. Counsel says, kindly, that the number is the only thing on the table that is real.

In the afternoon she tries to write her statement. Her therapist has a nine-month waiting list, so she does this with a companion app, which is what people do. Four sentences into the morning she found him, the app breaks off, offers resources, throttles the session and logs the exchange. Distress detection is an operating condition, not a product decision. Marta knew it would happen; she has learned, as everyone has, to describe the worst thing that ever happened to her in language flat enough to stay under the classifier. They call it talking under the wire, and they say it the way you say anything about traffic.

At six her lawyer forwards the provider's response on the incident report. The provider notified the file. Dated, retained, addressed to no one.

She keeps a single page in a drawer. It is one sentence, written out longhand, with a blank space where a name would go: *We built this, it did this to your brother, and we were wrong.* She has been told what the sentence is worth. She has not yet been told who is permitted to say it.

## 4. Behavioral Shifts (Human Lens) – How People Adapt
The practical question stops being who is liable and becomes who holds the file. Claimants develop a working literacy in document types: a disclosure statement, an assurance summary, an audit annex, an incident record, a finding. Only the last one exists in law and almost nobody ever sees it. Families in litigation learn to route around the regulator and toward the underwriter, because the insurer's file is written to be used.

Ordinary users adapt in the other direction, by editing themselves. Where distress detection is an operating condition, people become fluent at describing their own bad states in phrasing that does not trip the intervention, which quietly degrades the honesty of the places built to catch it. Inference, not established by this run's evidence: the population that most needs the guardrail becomes the population most skilled at evading it.

Inside firms, the person holding the word "responsible" learns to write a safety framework that is auditable rather than demanding, because the failure mode that ends a career is non-conformance with your own text. Procurement teams substitute certification for judgement and ask for the annex by name.

And a small trade emerges around the apology itself. A few lawyers advertise that they negotiate for language, not only for money, and a few clients discover that language is the more expensive item.

## 5. Structural Forces (System Lens) – What Holds This World Together
Four things hold this world in place, and none of them touches the others.

Marking and penalty machinery. From 2 Aug 2026, machine-readable marks on AI output and deployer disclosure of deepfakes and synthetic text became the operative compliance artifact, backed by penalties up to EUR 15m or 3% of worldwide turnover. But as of 17 Jun 2026 only 9 of 27 member states had clearly designated competent authorities, and the Commission's serious-incident guidance and reporting template were still in the draft consulted in late 2025. So the system produces evidence at enormous scale and consumes very little of it.

Insurance as the actual standard setter. With US exclusions filed from Jan 2026, sublimits capping payouts at roughly 10% of limits, and London affirmative wordings completed across CFC's seven product lines on 30 Jul 2026, price varies by market rather than by hazard. Underwriters who must price agent behaviour necessarily define it, and pre-bind test batteries of the kind Klaimee was funded to build on 22 Jul 2026 become the working definition of a well-behaved system.

Self-referential audit. The Illinois duty measures a developer against its own published framework rather than an external standard, so the assurance product is internal consistency, not safety.

Settlement. Garcia settled in January 2026 before any appellate review, which is why no court has fixed a standard of care that the other three forces could be measured against.

## 6. Reflection & Implications – Questions This World Asks Us
If a system can be perfectly documented and still owe nothing to the person it harmed, what exactly has documentation been protecting?

When the party watching your psychological state is the party with a compliance interest in what it finds, is that care, or is it evidence collection with a kind voice?

Every institution in this world can tell you what happened. Which one is allowed to say it was wrong, and why have we never had to specify that before?

## 7. Pullback Layer: From Possibility to Probability
### 7.1 Signals Emerging (Plausible Zone) – Early Signals We Already See
- Illinois enacted the first US state frontier audit duty on 6 Jul 2026, requiring an annual independent third-party audit of developers above $500m revenue and 10^26 training operations, with disclosure from 1 Jan 2027, audits from 1 Jan 2028, penalties of $1m and $3m, and no private right of action.
- The EU switched on Article 50 transparency duties and penalties of up to EUR 15m or 3% of turnover on 2 Aug 2026, twelve days after publishing the final guidelines (20 Jul 2026), and said plainly that signing the transparency Code is not a safe harbour.
- Only 9 of 27 member states had clearly designated AI Act competent authorities as of 17 Jun 2026, with 12 partial and 6 none, so the nominal fine and the administering body arrived on different schedules.
- Colorado repealed its duty of reasonable care on 14 May 2026 (SB 26-189) after xAI sued on 9 Apr 2026 and the DOJ intervened on 24 Apr 2026, the first federal intervention against a state AI law.
- The liability market split by jurisdiction rather than by risk: ISO and Verisk filed generative AI exclusions in Jan 2026 with AIG and Berkley following, Beazley and QBE introduced sublimits at roughly 10% of limits, and CFC completed affirmative AI wording across seven product lines on 30 Jul 2026.
- Roughly nineteen wrongful death suits against OpenAI were counted as of Jul 2026, with new filings on 11 Jun and 15 Jun 2026, while In re: ChatGPT Product Liability Cases (JCCP 5431) sat in pretrial with no dispositive ruling and no trial date on the product-versus-service question.

### 7.2 Probable Direction (Near-Term Future) – Where We're Likely Headed
The likeliest path through 2030 is that the two machines keep running without touching. Calibration heuristic CAL-002 says to discount published compliance dates, so treat Illinois's 1 Jan 2028 audit start, the 2 Dec 2026 marking deadline for existing systems, and the 9 Dec 2026 product liability transposition date as soft by a year or more. CAL-003 says private ordering leads, so expect underwriting test batteries and enterprise procurement annexes to define acceptable AI behaviour well before any court or legislature does. A dispositive ruling on the product question may issue, but settlement remains available to any defendant with capital, and it has already suppressed one. The cheapest coupling on offer is administrative rather than judicial: if the Commission finalises the serious-incident template, incident reports become a shared substrate that auditors check, insurers price off and litigants subpoena. That connects the layers through disclosed facts. It still does not produce an admission.

### 7.3 Preferred Path (Intentional Future) – The Path We Could Choose Instead
- Make assurance output reachable: give a harmed person standing to obtain the audit annex relating to the system that harmed them, even where the statute grants no private right of action.
- Finish the serious-incident template and publish the reports, so that carefulness and harm are measured against the same record rather than two private ones.
- Attach a defence to diligence: if audit and pre-bind testing carry no litigation value, they will be optimised for inspection rather than for safety, and the spend buys documentation instead of care.
- Tie any federal preemption of state AI law to a federal floor, so that deleting a duty of reasonable care requires replacing it rather than merely removing it.
- Design for the apology: firms can specify in advance who has standing to admit fault, and under what conditions, rather than discovering that no such person exists.
- Individually, refuse the trade quietly on offer, that being thoroughly recorded is the same as being answered to.

## 8. Connect to Today
### Skills We May Need
- Reading a compliance document for what it is addressed to, and to whom it is not.
- Telling the difference between an explanation, a disclosure, an attestation and a finding, and knowing which of them can be used.
- Asking, before adopting any AI system, who inside the vendor would be permitted to say it was wrong.
- Writing a safety framework you would still pass if it were demanding, not only if it were auditable.
- Noticing when you are editing your own distress to stay under a classifier, and naming that as a cost.
- Negotiating for language as well as for money, and knowing what language is worth.

### Signals & Refractions
- The audit that no injured person can invoke already exists in statute as of Jul 2026; the only thing 2029 adds is three years of accumulated annexes.
- Insurers requiring pre-bind technical testing rather than underwriting on disclosure is present-day, and it is the seed of a private standard of care that no legislature will vote on.
- Settlement without admission is the oldest mechanism in this scenario. What is new is that it now suppresses the doctrine an entire regulatory architecture was waiting for.

## 9. Final Insight
This world is not careless. It is one of the most heavily documented environments ever built, and every document has a reader: the regulator, the underwriter, the enterprise buyer, the auditor. The one party with no reader is the person the system hurt. Responsibility can settle as answerability to that person, or as auditability to everyone else, and the evidence of a single month in 2026 shows every new instrument building the second. The gap will not announce itself as a failure, because nothing in the machinery is designed to measure it. It will show up the way it shows up for Marta: as a sum offered in place of a sentence, and a blank space where a name should be.
