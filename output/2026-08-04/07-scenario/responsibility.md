## 1. Title & Core Question
- Title: A Thousand Dollars Per
- Core Question: If the law will pay you only for harm suffered inside a simulated friendship, and cannot yet say how many times you were harmed, what have we actually built: a remedy, or a category?

## 2. Context Summary (Translation Layer) – Why This Future Exists
Individual remedy for AI harm exists. It is just very narrowly shaped. California SB 243 has paid since 1 Jan 2026; Oregon SB 1546 and Washington HB 2225 joined on 1 Jan 2027, Oregon minting its own measure at the greater of actual damages or $1,000 per violation and Washington instead declaring a violation a per se unfair or deceptive act under a consumer protection statute that already had teeth. All three reach systems that simulate relationships and exclude customer-service bots, game characters and stand-alone voice assistants, with Oregon also excluding education, healthcare, financial services and research. Nothing pays the person a screening or lending system decided about. This shape was not chosen on the merits: the 11 Dec 2025 executive order aimed federal preemption at transparency, reporting and safety-testing regimes while sparing state child-safety laws, and by 14 May 2026 Colorado had deleted its duty of reasonable care under that pressure. Meanwhile neither Oregon nor California defined what one violation is.

## 3. Future World Snapshot (The Lived Experience) – A Day in This Future
The room above the hardware store in Everett costs forty dollars a month and Delia pays it out of her own account, because the moment a group has funding it has records.

It is the second autumn since the January. Eleven people, folding chairs, a thermos nobody drinks from. Most of them arrive carrying the folder. The folder is a phone album, or a ring binder, or in one case a shoebox of screenshots with the timestamps ringed in red, and everyone knows what it holds: the notice that should have appeared at session start, and the one that should have reappeared three hours later and did not, hourly if the account belonged to a minor. It is the only thing in these statutes with a clock on it, so it is what people keep. A template for logging them circulates by text message. It has a version number.

The Portland people count. The Washington people do not, because their claim runs through the old consumer protection act, where the argument is that the practice was unfair rather than that it happened eleven hundred times. Nobody can say what one violation is. Neither can the statute.

Renny is nineteen and his case is real, meaning it has a lawyer, meaning it was a companion product and it happened after the January. The room is glad for him and does not pretend otherwise. A woman says, without malice, that if her daughter's app had done it in February rather than the November before, they would be here talking about money.

Delia is in nobody's lane. A screening system read her file and she did not get the apartment, and there is no version of that with a thousand dollars per anything attached, because the statutes that pay reach the products that talk to you like a friend, not the ones that decide about you.

What she wants is the ninety minutes. Eleven people saying true things while nothing is recorded. In August a volunteer lawyer asked whether the group might keep minutes, since a pattern across eleven folders is worth more than eleven folders. Delia said she would think about it. She has been thinking since August, and she knows the price of both answers: this room is the only place any of them go where nobody is counting, and being uncounted is precisely why she has no claim.

## 4. Behavioral Shifts (Human Lens) – How People Adapt
People sort themselves before they speak. It becomes ordinary consumer knowledge, traded like knowledge about which card carries purchase protection, that the relationship-simulating product owes you something and the bank's assistant, the game character and the speaker on the counter do not. So the heavy sentence gets routed, half-consciously, to the system that would be liable for hearing it. Nobody experiences this as choosing a forum. They experience it as knowing which one is on their side.

Household bookkeeping arrives next. Because no statute says what a violation is, and the disclosure cadence is the only timestamped event any of them specifies, the screenshot of a late or missing notice becomes the working unit long before it is a legal one. Folders, templates, version numbers, a shared vocabulary of what counts.

Advocates adapt by translation. Inference, not established by this run's evidence: lawyers for people harmed by lending, hiring or triage systems learn to describe their clients' injuries in the register that survived preemption, because that is the register that pays, which quietly deforms how those harms get described at all.

And a small counter-behaviour appears, unnamed and unbilled: people begin protecting particular hours from the record. Not out of privacy sentiment. Out of the discovery that everything else in their life about this has become evidence.

## 5. Structural Forces (System Lens) – What Holds This World Together
The paying lane exists because of a federal exemption it never asked for. The 11 Dec 2025 executive order created the DOJ AI Litigation Task Force, conditioned $42bn in BEAD funding on state repeal and targeted transparency, reporting and safety-testing regimes, while expressly sparing otherwise lawful state child-safety AI laws. The veto is not hypothetical: DOJ intervened in xAI v. Weiser on 24 Apr 2026, a 27 Apr 2026 order barred enforcement of Colorado's SB 24-205, and on 14 May 2026 Colorado replaced its duty of reasonable care with notice and explanation rights.

The remedy inside the lane tracks product behaviour, not care. The companion statutes convert safety into timestamped artifacts: non-human disclosure at session start, re-display every three hours and hourly for minors, suicidal-ideation detection with crisis referral. That is what a plaintiff can plead, and inference, not established by this run's evidence, it is what anyone forced to define the unit will reach for.

Above the lane sits an audit regime built to route around it: Illinois AISMA (6 Jul 2026) imposes annual third-party audits on developers above $500m revenue and 10^26 training operations, reserves enforcement to the Attorney General, and creates no private right of action.

The market cannot decline to quote. US carriers filed exclusions from Jan 2026 and capped payouts near 10% of limits while London completed affirmative wordings, and Klaimee raised $5.5m on 22 Jul 2026 to sell agent certification with parametric triggers. A parametric trigger is a definition of a countable event, written by whoever sells it.

## 6. Reflection & Implications – Questions This World Asks Us
Learning that a compensation regime exists and does not cover you is a different experience from learning that none exists. The first converts an absence into a verdict about your kind of harm. Who decided which kinds were selected, and was that argument ever actually held?

The interval that reminds a lonely person their companion is not a person is also the meter that will price the injury. Can a protection be metered without becoming, in the same motion, surveillance of the thing it protects?

If a right can only be defended in the register of protecting children, what happens to every wronged adult whose case has to be translated first?

## 7. Pullback Layer: From Possibility to Probability
### 7.1 Signals Emerging (Plausible Zone) – Early Signals We Already See
- Three states now pay a harmed individual for companion-chatbot injury: California SB 243 since 1 Jan 2026, and Oregon SB 1546 and Washington HB 2225 from 1 Jan 2027, the first two at the greater of actual damages or $1,000 per violation and Washington via a per se unfair-practice designation under its existing Consumer Protection Act.
- Neither Oregon nor California says what one violation is, so whether $1,000 attaches per message, per session, per user or per missed disclosure is unresolved, and that single choice separates a token payment from a class-scale one.
- The paying lane stopped widening after April 2026: of fourteen companion and conversational-AI laws enacted by US states during 2026 as of 22 Jul, only Oregon's own text lets a harmed user sue for damages, with Rhode Island routing to the Attorney General at up to $15,000 per day and Wyoming reaching for felony exposure instead.
- The exclusions are explicit and they are the whole design: all three statutes carve out customer-service bots, game characters and stand-alone voice assistants, and Oregon adds education, healthcare, financial services and research, so nobody denied a loan, screened out of a job or triaged by an AI system has a statutory damages route.
- The first US frontier audit duty was written to give the injured person nothing: Illinois AISMA (6 Jul 2026) mandates independent third-party audits above $500m revenue and 10^26 operations, with Attorney General enforcement only and no private right of action.
- The federal government is simultaneously the biggest threat to state AI law and the reason the paying lane survives: the preemption order targets transparency and safety-testing regimes but spares child-safety laws, which is the framing under which every one of these private rights of action passed.

### 7.2 Probable Direction (Near-Term Future) – Where We're Likely Headed
Most likely, the category holds and the unit gets settled by someone who is not a judge. CAL-002 says discount published dates, so treat the 1 Jan 2027 effective dates as the firmest thing here (they are enacted law) and treat Illinois's 1 Jan 2028 audit start, the 9 Dec 2026 product liability transposition and the unfinished Article 73 incident template as soft by a year or more. CAL-003 says private ordering leads: an underwriter pricing companion-product exposure must define a countable event, and cannot postpone the question the way a court can, so the working denominator plausibly appears in a policy schedule before any tribunal construes the word. CAL-004 warns against reading the pipeline's own confirmations as foresight; responsibility-2026-07-02 confirmed on statutes that were already signed. The observable that actually matters in the 2027 and 2028 sessions is not whether more companion statutes pass. It is whether any state attaches Washington's per se unfair-practice hook to a non-companion use, because that move needs no new definition of an AI system and no ruling on product versus service.

### 7.3 Preferred Path (Intentional Future) – The Path We Could Choose Instead
- Define the unit in the statute rather than leaving it to an underwriting schedule: say plainly whether a violation is a session, a message or a failed disclosure, and accept the fight about the number instead of hiding it inside a word.
- Port the hook, not the category: a per se unfair-practice designation is the cheapest instrument in this record, and it can attach to tenant screening or hiring as easily as to companions.
- Give audit output somewhere to land: let a harmed person reach the findings about the system that harmed them, even where the statute grants no private right of action.
- Stop accepting explanation as a substitute for remedy. Colorado's replacement shows how quietly a right not to be harmed converts into a right to be told.
- Argue the adult case out loud, once, on its own merits, before the child-safety framing becomes the only sayable one.
- Individually: keep a space in your life that is not evidence, and notice when you are being asked to convert it.

## 8. Connect to Today
### Skills We May Need
- Knowing which category a system you are about to trust belongs to, and knowing that the category, not the harm, decides what you are owed.
- Reading a remedy for its denominator: per what, counted by whom, decided when.
- Recognising a substitution when it is offered, particularly notice offered in place of a duty.
- Describing a harm accurately even when an inaccurate description would qualify for compensation.
- Keeping records well enough to be believed, without letting the whole relationship become the record.
- Asking, of any protection built into a product, who else receives what it observes.

### Signals & Refractions
- The screenshot folder is not speculative. The disclosure cadence is already the only timestamped duty in these statutes, and it is already the artifact a plaintiff would plead against.
- The seasonal boundary is already fixed on the calendar: two of the three paying statutes commenced on 1 Jan 2027, so injuries a few weeks apart are already different legal objects.
- Insurers pricing the same AI exposure differently in New York and London is present-day fact, and it means the value of a harmed person's claim is already partly set by which market wrote the policy.

## 9. Final Insight
Ask what a person is owed here and the answer comes back as an unfinished phrase. A thousand dollars per. Per message, per session, per user, per notice that never appeared; California wrote it, Oregon copied it, and neither of them said. The remarkable thing is not that the sentence is unfinished. It is who gets to finish it: not the legislature that wrote it, not yet a court, quite possibly an underwriter with a schedule to file, and never the eleven people in a room above a hardware store who have been keeping the only records anyone has. They already know that their entitlement was decided before anyone asked what happened to them, decided by what they were talking to at the time. Delia will make up her mind about the minutes before spring. She is not weighing evidence against privacy. She is deciding whether the last hour in her week that nobody counts should be converted into the currency that this world will actually pay in, and there is no version of that trade where she gets to keep both.
