## 1. Title & Core Question
- Title: The Body That Can Call You
- Core Question: When every institution can assert authority over a system and almost none can be held to a decision, what is a refusal worth, and who is left with the standing to give one?

## 2. Context Summary (Translation Layer) – Why This Future Exists
In the summer of 2026 authority over AI stopped waiting to be confirmed. On 2 Aug 2026 the European Commission's AI Office switched on direct enforcement over general-purpose model providers, including model access for evaluation and fines up to the higher of EUR 15 million or 3 percent of worldwide annual turnover, in a Union where as of 17 Jun 2026 only 9 of 27 member states had clearly designated both required national authorities. The FTC voted 2-0 on 1 Jul 2026 to propose that state AI laws are impliedly preempted by a federal scheme that imposes no binding output standards, and commentators read it as signalling rather than law. The case that would have settled the American question went quiet on 27 Apr 2026 while the challenged Colorado statute was rewritten underneath it. Illinois, meanwhile, simply passed a law. What followed was not collapse but a habit: obey whichever body can reach you, and stop asking who is entitled to rule.

## 3. Future World Snapshot (The Lived Experience) – A Day in This Future
Nadia is deputy head of model assurance at a frontier developer with about eleven hundred staff, a Dublin entity, and a legal address in Chicago that was chosen for tax reasons in 2021 and turned out to be the most consequential decision anyone there ever made. It is a Thursday in November 2029. Her signature is required on four internal gates. It has never stopped anything.

The escalation board on the wall behind her desk is ordered by reachability, not by penalty. The Illinois Attorney General sits at the top, because the Attorney General has a docket, a filing date and a named deputy who returns calls. Four rows down, in grey, sits the line that could take 3 percent of worldwide turnover, annotated in the same handwriting as everything else: *RFI answered Mar 2029, no proceeding.* New hires ask about the ordering roughly once a quarter. The answer given in onboarding is cheerful and true: you prepare for the body that can call you, not the body that could theoretically end you.

At eleven she is in a tabletop exercise at a county facility, because critical safety incident reports go to emergency management, so the people who receive her catastrophic risk assessment also receive flood plans. The morning scenario is a substation fire. The afternoon scenario is a training run that will not halt. Nobody in the room finds the pairing strange, least of all Nadia, who wrote the incident taxonomy that maps one schema onto the other and is quietly proud of it.

At four she is in quarterly planning, watching one slide with two columns. The left column is dated and priced: the disclosure statements, the independent auditor finally retained after the duty slipped two years in litigation. The right column is also priced, and the meeting's only real argument is about the ratio.

What she wants she cannot requisition. She wants one refusal with standing behind it, delivered to her face by someone who cannot later withdraw it, so that for once the answer to *are we allowed* is not an estimate.

## 4. Behavioral Shifts (Human Lens) – How People Adapt
People stop reading law as a rule and start reading it as a probability, and they become fluent in the difference. The operative question in a governance meeting is no longer whether a requirement applies but whether the applying body has ever done anything, so institutional biography replaces legal analysis: how many proceedings has it opened, how long did the last one take, who is the deputy, does she call back.

Oversight professionals adapt to holding titles without leverage by borrowing enforcement from outside. The internal memo that will be noted and filed gets attached to an external date and an external name, because a deadline with a stranger's signature on it moves an executive committee and an internal judgement does not. This is a real skill and it corrodes the person who develops it.

Ordinary people adapt by geography and by paperwork. Where you live determines whether your grievance has an address, so residency, incorporation and choice of subsidiary quietly become instruments of rights allocation, and knowing which entity to complain to becomes a class-marked competence.

And everyone learns to accept provisional answers gracefully. The phrase that ends most disputes is not *that is prohibited* but *nobody has tested that yet*, said without embarrassment, because it is accurate.

## 5. Structural Forces (System Lens) – What Holds This World Together
Four things hold this world. First, competence relocated upward faster than capacity could follow. The Digital Omnibus on AI, in force late Jul 2026 and framed as burden reduction, gave the AI Office exclusive supervisory competence over AI systems built on a provider's own GPAI models and over systems inside very large online platforms and search engines, moving supervision away from national authorities that mostly had not been constituted. Enforcement concentrates in one office of finite throughput, which makes the probability of being reached, rather than the size of the fine, the governing variable.

Second, statute binds where a named enforcer exists. Illinois SB 315 (signed 6 Jul 2026) is unambiguous because it is ordinary state law with the Attorney General behind it, penalties to USD 3 million, and reporting routed to the Illinois Emergency Management Agency and the Office of Homeland Security rather than a technology regulator, which is why frontier risk now sits on a civil-contingency calendar.

Third, the deferrals hold the shape in place. Annex III obligations moved to 2 Dec 2027 and Annex I embedded-product obligations to 2 Aug 2028, narrowing near-term EU exposure to model providers.

Fourth, the machine layer answers what the human layer cannot. AuthZEN's Jun 2026 profiles made delegated authority, approval and attestation first-class inputs to an authorization decision, and the IETF agent-auth draft put the delegating principal in the token's sub claim, so a system can prove who authorised it in milliseconds. ## 6. Reflection & Implications – Questions This World Asks Us
If a rule's force comes from the reachability of its enforcer rather than from its validity, have we replaced law with logistics, and would we notice the substitution from inside?

When the only unambiguous authority left is ordinary enacted statute with a named official behind it, what have we learned about the value of the plain, unglamorous instruments we spent a decade calling slow?

A machine can now be told exactly who authorised it and whether that person may. The institutions above it cannot get the same answer about themselves. Which of those two gaps is the emergency?

## 7. Pullback Layer: From Possibility to Probability
### 7.1 Signals Emerging (Plausible Zone) – Early Signals We Already See
- Brussels acquired its largest-ever powers over frontier model providers on 2 Aug 2026, including model access for evaluation and fines up to the higher of EUR 15 million or 3 percent of worldwide annual turnover, and as of 3 Aug 2026 had opened no formal proceeding against any named provider.
- A simplification measure produced concentration: the Digital Omnibus on AI, in force late Jul 2026, moved exclusive supervisory competence to the AI Office while pushing Annex III obligations to 2 Dec 2027 and Annex I to 2 Aug 2028.
- The national tier those powers were meant to rest on was largely unbuilt: as of 17 Jun 2026, 9 of 27 member states had clearly designated both required authorities, 12 partially and 6 unclear, against a deadline of 2 Aug 2025.
- Washington moved the preemption fight from courts into administrative reinterpretation: the FTC voted 2-0 on 1 Jul 2026 to propose that state AI laws may be impliedly preempted, published it in the Federal Register on 7 Jul 2026 with comments closing 31 Jul 2026, and commentators read it as "signaling and pressure, not legal preemption".
- The venue that would have decided the question went quiet. xAI v. Weiser recorded no docket entry after 27 Apr 2026, enforcement of the Colorado AI Act was halted by joint motion rather than contested ruling, and Colorado rewrote the statute itself on 14 May 2026.
- Fewer legislatures acted while the ones that did acted harder: 29 states with 109 AI laws as of 1 Jul 2026 against 39 states with 121 a year earlier, in the same quarter Illinois enacted the country's first third-party frontier audit mandate with Attorney General enforcement.

### 7.3 Preferred Path (Intentional Future) – The Path We Could Choose Instead
- Fund the tier that receives the complaint before granting the tier that issues the fine, so designation is treated as the precondition of competence rather than a formality that trails it.
- Require any body claiming preemptive authority to name the standard it is preempting toward, since a rule cannot be displaced by a scheme that has not been written.
- Let one case reach the merits. Where mootness is engineered by consent, courts and legislatures should preserve at least one live vehicle, because doctrine is a public good that only litigation produces.
- Give internal oversight roles a budget and a reporting line rather than a title, and publish which they have, so symbolic governance is visible in procurement.
- Adopt the delegation and approval profiles deliberately rather than by drift, and treat "who authorised this" as an auditable claim with a retention duty attached.
- Individually: learn which body actually has jurisdiction over your harm before you need it, and treat that as ordinary civic literacy rather than specialist knowledge.

## 8. Connect to Today
### Skills We May Need
- Institutional biography: judging a regulator by what it has done, not by what it may do.
- Distinguishing competence from capacity, out loud, in rooms that would rather not hear it.
- Borrowing standing: attaching an internal judgement to an external date so that it moves.
- Reading a deferral: recognising when a postponed obligation has quietly become a different obligation.
- Making a refusal stick: writing a no that survives the meeting it was said in.
- Jurisdictional literacy: knowing which entity, in which place, owes you an answer.

### Signals & Refractions
- The escalation ordering is already implied: enormous EU competence went live on 2 Aug 2026 with no proceeding opened against any named provider, while Illinois attached an Attorney General, a filing date and penalties to USD 3 million.
- Frontier risk on the civil-contingency calendar is not invented: Illinois routes critical safety incident reports and catastrophic risk assessments to the Illinois Emergency Management Agency and the Office of Homeland Security.
- Titles outrunning power is measured, not imagined: peer-reviewed mapping of 351 organisations found AI oversight roles usually placed below executive level and frequently symbolic, a title without authority or resources.

## 9. Final Insight
Nothing here requires bad faith. Brussels took real powers for real reasons. Illinois legislated in the ordinary way. The FTC used an ordinary administrative instrument. Colorado's parties settled rather than fought, which is usually a virtue. And the sum of all that reasonable behaviour is a world in which assertion is abundant and consequence is scarce, where the practical test of a rule is whether its enforcer has a phone number, and where the people paid to hold the line inside organisations hold a title instead. What is missing is not regulation. There is more of it every quarter. What is missing is a binding no: a decision made by someone with the standing to make it, delivered plainly, that cannot be quietly withdrawn. A society can run for a long time on anticipated obedience. It just never finds out what it actually believes until someone finally refuses, and is made to mean it.
