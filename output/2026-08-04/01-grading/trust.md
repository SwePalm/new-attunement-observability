# Signal grading, trust, 2026-08-04

Scorecard:
- open: 4, confirmed: 0, decayed: 1, falsified: 0, expired: 0

Grade Details:
- trust-2026-02-03: open, region-specific configuration keeps spreading (10 of 16 tracked AI model offerings now show clear public evidence of EU data residency, 4 conditional or gated, 2 none) but it is not yet default deployment architecture and the claim resolves 2029-02 (source: https://aiprovidertrust.com/questions/eu-data-residency/, Aug 2026)
- trust-2026-02-04: open, formalization is advancing but is still not the operating norm: 74% of boards have approved major AI investments while only 52% have set AI governance expectations and 78% of executives lack strong confidence they could pass an independent AI governance audit within 90 days (source: https://www.grantthornton.com/services/advisory-services/artificial-intelligence/2026-ai-impact-survey, 2026)
- trust-2026-07-01: open, the AI Omnibus is in force and the Commission's framework page lists 2 December 2027 as the Annex III application date, with no further deferral adopted or proposed as of this run (source: https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/regulatory-framework-ai, Aug 2026)
- trust-2026-07-02: open, the Article 50 transparency obligations became applicable only on 2 August 2026 (two days before this run), with enforcement assigned to national market surveillance authorities, the AI Office and the EDPS and fines up to 15M EUR or 3% of turnover, so no enforcement action could yet have issued (source: https://commission.europa.eu/news-and-media/news/safer-and-more-transparent-ai-2026-08-02_en, Aug 2026)
- trust-2026-07-03: decayed, standard commercial lines moved the opposite way, with carriers filing the ISO generative AI exclusions (CG 40 47, CG 40 48, CG 35 08) and Berkley's absolute AI exclusion for D&O, E&O and fiduciary lines, while affirmative AI wording stays concentrated in Lloyd's market specialists such as CFC and in MGAs, and AXA, Allianz and Zurich still have no dedicated AI agent line (source: https://www.insurancejournal.com/news/national/2026/07/22/878480.htm, Jul 2026)

Surprises:
- trust-2026-07-03 decayed within a single month, and in a direction the claim did not consider. The sweep assumed the insurance market would harden trust by extending affirmative AI cover into standard lines. Instead the standard-lines market is retreating: ISO generative AI exclusions took effect Jan 2026, carriers filed to adopt them through mid-2026, and affirmative cover concentrated exactly where the claim excluded it, in the Lloyd's and MGA specialty channel (CFC across seven products, Jun to Jul 2026; Armilla as coverholder). The "beyond Lloyd's specialty programs" qualifier, written to make the claim demanding, is what falsified the mechanism.
- The AI Act deadline claims are behaving with unusual firmness. trust-2026-07-01 was written expecting slippage pressure, but the Omnibus entered into force and the 2 December 2027 date is now the Commission's published position with no new deferral in circulation, and the 2 August 2026 transparency obligations were not delayed despite the wider simplification push. This runs against CAL-002's assumption that published compliance dates soften.
- The two 2026-02 seeded claims (trust-2026-02-03, trust-2026-02-04) remain ungradeable in either direction after two runs. Both have real supporting evidence and real contradicting evidence, and neither names a threshold that would decide the matter. They will sit open until 2029-02 unless the pipeline retires them.
