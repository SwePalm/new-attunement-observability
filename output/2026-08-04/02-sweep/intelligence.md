# Evidence sweep, intelligence, 2026-08-04

Delta Since Last Sweep:
- The 2026-07-03 sweep framed measurement as a benchmark-saturation problem. Since then the binding constraint on frontier intelligence has visibly shifted to access control: the US applied export-control authority to model access itself twice in June and July 2026, an event class absent from the previous sweep and from the ledger.
- A genuinely unsaturated capability measure appeared and then moved fast. ARC-AGI-3, at which all frontier models scored below 1% at its March 2026 launch, reached 30.16% on 24 July 2026, which is a different dynamic from the HLE erosion the last sweep tracked.
- Institutional measurement capacity moved backwards relative to the last sweep's assumption. EO 14409's classified benchmarking framework missed its 1 August 2026 statutory-style deadline with zero public deliverables, while CAISI's public model-assessment reporting remains halted, which is the mechanism behind this run's decay of intelligence-2026-07-03.
- The EU became the only jurisdiction with live, exercisable powers to compel frontier-model evaluation, effective 2 August 2026, two days before this run.

Confirmed Developments:
- ARC-AGI-3, the interactive agentic benchmark on which every frontier system scored below 1% at its 25 March 2026 launch, was pushed to 30.16% by Claude Opus 5 (High) on 24 July 2026, with the next-best model, GPT-5.6 Sol, at 7.8% and Claude Opus 4.8 at 1.5% (source: https://arcprize.org/results/anthropic-claude-opus-5, Jul 2026) (source: https://benchlm.ai/benchmarks/arcagi3, Jul 2026)
- US export-control authority was applied to frontier model access for the first time: a BIS letter dated 12 June 2026 required an approved export license for any foreign person, including Anthropic's own foreign-national employees, to access Claude Fable 5 and Mythos 5; Anthropic suspended access globally, controls were lifted 30 June 2026 and access restored from 1 July 2026 (source: https://www.anthropic.com/news/redeploying-fable-5, Jul 2026) (source: https://www.csis.org/analysis/department-commerce-restricted-access-anthropics-latest-models-what-comes-next, Jun 2026)
- The EO 14409 deadline of 1 August 2026 for a classified frontier-model benchmarking process lapsed with no Federal Register notice and no NIST, CISA or OSTP publication, alongside two other undelivered items (voluntary disclosure framework, federal cyber workforce plan) (source: https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/white-house-ai-framework-deadline-002011007.html, Jul 2026) (source: https://cryptobriefing.com/classified-ai-benchmark-deadline-passes/, Aug 2026)
- The European Commission's enforcement powers over general-purpose AI model providers became applicable on 2 August 2026, including the power to request information, obtain access to models for evaluation, require corrective measures, and fine up to the higher of EUR 15 million or 3% of worldwide annual turnover (source: https://www.wsgr.com/en/insights/eu-ai-act-enforcement-phase-begins.html, Aug 2026) (source: https://qz.com/eu-ai-act-enforcement-fines-openai-anthropic-google-080326, Aug 2026)

Emerging Signals:
- Frontier capability is being unbundled from frontier price: Claude Opus 5 shipped 24 July 2026 at $5/$25 per million tokens, within 0.5% of Fable 5's CursorBench 3.2 peak, roughly half Fable 5's input price (source: https://thursdai.news/releases/2026-07, Jul 2026)
- Government-mediated capability access consolidated into named programs during July 2026: the White House launched Gold Eagle on 14 July 2026 as the EO 14409 clearinghouse, alongside Anthropic's Project Glasswing (announced 7 April 2026) and OpenAI's Daybreak tiers, with CNBC reporting on 17 July 2026 that the White House now influences which companies get frontier access, a characterization officials denied (source: https://vorplabs.com/ai-regulatory-updates/reports/2026-07-frontier-model-access, Jul 2026)
- ARC Prize itself flagged that Opus 5's ARC-AGI-3 run was executed only at High reasoning effort because of a short testing window, so the headline record is a partial measurement of the model's ceiling (source: https://arcprize.org/results/anthropic-claude-opus-5, Jul 2026)

Counter-Signals:
- The capability race met capital resistance in the same window as the capability jump: Alphabet raised FY2026 capex guidance to $195B to $205B and posted a negative free cash flow quarter of -$5.9B, its first since the 2004 IPO, while Meta's Q2 free cash flow fell 91% year on year to $784M and its stock dropped 9% to 10% after the 29 July 2026 report (source: https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/ai-capex-scorecard-earnings-week-july-2026, Jul 2026)
- Frontier capability availability is now discontinuous rather than monotonic: two separate US interventions restricted access to newly released frontier models within six weeks, Fable 5 and Mythos 5 from 12 to 30 June 2026 and GPT-5.6 Sol restricted to government-vetted partners from 26 June to 9 July 2026, both under Export Control Reform Act authority that predates any AI-specific statute (source: https://www.techtimes.com/articles/321497/20260724/voluntary-paper-mandatory-practice-white-house-ai-review-hits-august-1-deadline.htm, Jul 2026)

Regulatory Shifts:
- From 2 August 2026 the AI Office and Member State authorities are responsible for implementing, supervising and enforcing the AI Act, with the AI Office holding enforcement powers over GPAI models; obligations for models placed on the market before 2 August 2025 must be met by 2 August 2027 (source: https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/guidelines-gpai-providers, Aug 2026)
- US frontier-model designation authority moved from the civilian standards body to the intelligence community: EO 14409 gives the NSA Director designation authority behind classified benchmarks with undisclosed thresholds and no transparent appeal, and administration officials directed CAISI to halt public reporting on its model assessments one week after the order was signed (source: https://www.lawfaremedia.org/article/voluntary--until-the-government-is-your-customer, Jun 2026)
- As of a claim check on 27 July 2026 the EO 14409 framework existed only as a draft circulated to OpenAI, Anthropic and Google per single-source reporting, with CAISI's reviewer role appearing in no official document (source: https://vorplabs.com/ai-regulatory-updates/frontier-model-review-framework, Jul 2026)

Capital Movements:
- Reported and guided 2026 capex from the July 2026 earnings week: Microsoft CY2026 guidance approximately $175B (FY26 actual $115.9B), Amazon trailing-twelve-month capex $173.0B with no forward guide, Meta FY2026 raised to $130B to $145B, Alphabet FY2026 raised to $195B to $205B (source: https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/ai-capex-scorecard-earnings-week-july-2026, Jul 2026)
- Amazon's Q2 2026 net income of $62.6B included a $53.4B one-time gain on its Anthropic stake, meaning a single lab equity position accounted for the majority of a hyperscaler's quarterly earnings (source: https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/ai-capex-scorecard-earnings-week-july-2026, Jul 2026)
- Pre-earnings analyst framing had combined 2026 hyperscaler capex at $725B to $785B, roughly 77% above 2025 (source: https://nextwavesinsight.com/hyperscaler-q2-2026-earnings-ai-capex-roi/, Jul 2026)

Technical Changes:
- The measurement frontier moved from static academic exams to interactive environments in which the agent must learn unfamiliar task mechanics through action and feedback: Claude Opus 5 scores 97.5% on ARC-AGI-1 and 90.4% on ARC-AGI-2 (Max) but 30.16% on ARC-AGI-3 (source: https://arcprize.org/results/anthropic-claude-opus-5, Jul 2026)
- Public autonomy-horizon measurement has fallen behind release cadence: METR's time-horizon page was last updated 8 May 2026, covering Claude Mythos Preview, Gemini 3.1 Pro and GPT-5.4, and therefore predates the June and July 2026 model wave (Fable 5, Mythos 5, GPT-5.6, Gemini 3.6 Flash, Opus 5) (source: https://metr.org/time-horizons/, May 2026)

Contradictions:
- The same state that is buying frontier capability is also the actor that twice removed it from the market, and the legal instrument used (export control) is the one instrument explicitly outside the voluntary framework EO 14409 was written to establish.
- Capability measurement is simultaneously the most consequential public artifact in the field and the one the US government has decided to stop publishing, while the only body with live compulsory evaluation powers as of August 2026 is a foreign regulator.
- Alphabet's first negative free cash flow quarter since 2004 and Meta's 91% free cash flow decline landed in the same fortnight as the largest single-benchmark capability jump of 2026, so the market repriced the spend downward while the capability signal moved upward.
