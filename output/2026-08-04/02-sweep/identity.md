# Evidence sweep, identity, 2026-08-04

Delta Since Last Sweep:
- Agent identity moved from the 2026-07 sweep's "emerging signal" (agent gateways as a security domain) to a shipping enterprise product line with at least one generally available component and a published eight-risk taxonomy, both dated late July 2026.
- Standards bodies entered the identity picture for the first time in this theme's record: OpenID Foundation COAZ/COAZ-MCP drafts, a multi-vendor MCP security interop with a 16 Oct 2026 test deadline, an ephemeral subject identifier spec in public review, and W3C WebAuthn Level 3 at Proposed Recommendation. The 2026-07 sweep cited no standards body.
- The regulatory channel shifted from AI Act watermarking deferral (2026-07 sweep) to data-protection substance on whether AI-held identity data is personal data, via EDPB guidelines adopted 8 Jul 2026.
- Capital Movements was "None" last sweep; this month has a named acquisition (Okta and Permiso Security, 30 Jul 2026) explicitly scoped to human, non-human and agentic identities.
- Against the ledger: identity-2026-07-02 was graded confirmed on April 2026 evidence, but the consumer-facing launch (Tinder Verification) posted 23 Jul 2026, after the claim was logged. The April item was a partner announcement, the July items are integrations.

Confirmed Developments:
- Agent identity became a shipping enterprise product category in July 2026 rather than a research topic: Okta announced Agent Gateway (research release, validates agent identity plus the associated user credential in real time and issues short-lived credentials without code changes, covering Claude Code, GitHub Copilot, Salesforce Agentforce and Amazon Bedrock AgentCore), Agent-to-Agent Connections (general availability, temporary runtime tokens with chain of custody embedded for multi-agent handoffs) and Resource Access Certifications for AI Agents (early access, automated revocation of stale agent connections), while the Cloud Security Alliance published an eight-risk agent identity taxonomy naming orphaned agent identities, privilege creep, static long-lived credentials and agent impersonation (source: https://www.okta.com/newsroom/articles/okta-july-2026-product-innovations/, Jul 2026) (source: https://cloudsecurityalliance.org/articles/securing-agent-identities-8-risks-every-ciso-must-address, Jul 2026)
- Authorization standards now treat an AI agent and the human it acts for as two separable identities in the same request: the OpenID Foundation AuthZEN Working Group published COAZ and COAZ-MCP on 31 Jul 2026, requiring every JSON-RPC message from an AI client to be authorized before execution and capturing the distinct identity of the human on whose behalf the agent acts to prevent confused-deputy failures, while Okta expanded Cross App Access as a cross-vendor mechanism for secure AI agent connections on 23 Jun 2026 and shipped agent lifecycle governance for regulated environments on 25 Jun 2026 (source: https://openid.net/getting-cozy-with-coaz-securing-apis-and-ai-agents-with-standardized-authorization/, Jul 2026) (source: https://www.okta.com/newsroom/, Jun 2026)

Emerging Signals:
- Proof-of-human moved from partner announcement to shipped integrations in a single week in late July 2026: World posted "Tinder Verification: Real connections with real people powered by World ID" and "Okta: Proof of human for the agentic web" on 23 Jul 2026 and "Vercel: Human in the loop for the agentic web" on 22 Jul 2026, roughly three months after the 17 Apr 2026 partner announcements that the ledger already graded as confirmation (source: https://world.org/blog/announcements, Jul 2026)
- The OpenID Foundation's AI Identity Management Community Group opened a multi-vendor MCP security interoperability event testing OAuth 2.1, OAuth Client ID Metadata Document and Enterprise Managed Authorization (Identity Assertion JWT Authorization Grant plus ID-Chaining), with a participation commitment deadline of 10 Aug 2026, a first successful test due 16 Oct 2026, and consolidated results to be unveiled at the Gartner Identity and Access Management Summit in December 2026 (source: https://openid.net/call-for-participation-demonstrate-mcp-based-ai-agent-security-with-open-identity-standards-2/, Jul 2026)
- Human credential infrastructure advanced on the web platform in the same month: W3C proposed advancing Web Authentication Level 3 to Recommendation on 20 Jul 2026, and the Verifiable Credentials Working Group published a Group Note draft of Verifiable Credentials Overview v1.1 on 30 Jul 2026 (source: https://www.w3.org/news/2026/proposed-advancement-of-webauthn-3-to-w3c-recommendation/, Jul 2026) (source: https://www.w3.org/news/2026/group-note-draft-verifiable-credentials-overview-v1-1/, Jul 2026)

Counter-Signals:
- Identity standards are moving toward unlinkability at the same time as toward stronger attribution: the OpenID Foundation opened public review of OpenID Connect Ephemeral Subject Identifier 1.0 on 17 Jul 2026 (review to 15 Sep 2026, vote announcement 2 Sep 2026, voting 16 to 30 Sep 2026), a mechanism for authenticating without a permanent identifier that can be correlated across relying parties (source: https://openid.net/public-review-period-for-proposed-openid-connect-ephemeral-subject-identifier-1-0-final-specification/, Jul 2026)
- The EDPB's July 2026 texts are consultation drafts open for feedback to 30 Oct 2026, not settled law, and the anonymisation framework (no record isolation, no linkage, no inference) plus the Board's acknowledgement that GC and Others may permit incidental or residual collection with safeguards means the personal-data status of AI-held identity profiles is being clarified in both directions rather than only tightening (source: https://www.edpb.europa.eu/news/edpb-sheds-light-on-anonymisation-and-web-scraping-for-generative-ai-and-adopts-final-version_en, Jul 2026)

Regulatory Shifts:
- The EDPB adopted guidelines on anonymisation and on web scraping for generative AI on 8 Jul 2026, applying purpose limitation, transparency, timestamp recording, pre-training validation and data minimisation to training-data collection, and holding that processing special categories of personal data is in principle prohibited absent both an Article 6 basis and an Article 9(2) exception; both are open for feedback until 30 Oct 2026 (source: https://www.edpb.europa.eu/news/edpb-sheds-light-on-anonymisation-and-web-scraping-for-generative-ai-and-adopts-final-version_en, Jul 2026)
- The EDPB asked the European Commission on 17 Jul 2026 for a legal basis allowing data protection authorities and other EU digital regulators to exchange information including confidential data across regulatory domains, citing rising complaint complexity driven by increased AI use as a strain on DPA enforcement capacity (source: https://www.edpb.europa.eu/news/edpb-calls-for-legal-basis-for-cross-regulatory-information-sharing_en, Jul 2026)

Capital Movements:
- Okta signed a definitive agreement on 30 Jul 2026 to acquire Permiso Security, a cloud-native identity security platform for threat detection across human, non-human and agentic identities, terms undisclosed, expected to close in Okta fiscal Q3 2027 with no change to guidance issued 27 May 2026; Okta cited 58% of executives reporting an AI-related security incident or near miss in the past year (source: https://www.okta.com/newsroom/press-releases/okta-signs-definitive-agreement-to-acquire-permiso-security/, Jul 2026)

Technical Changes:
- Short-lived, task-scoped credentials with embedded chain of custody are becoming the default agent identity primitive: Okta's Agent Gateway issues short-lived credentials to agents without code modification and its Agent-to-Agent Connections issue temporary runtime tokens restricting access to task-specific data while enforcing which agents may invoke which (source: https://www.okta.com/newsroom/articles/okta-july-2026-product-innovations/, Jul 2026)
- COAZ generalizes mapping of arbitrary protocol information models into AuthZEN authorization requests, and COAZ-MCP applies it so that Model Context Protocol traffic carries both the agent identity and the delegating human identity into the authorization decision (source: https://openid.net/getting-cozy-with-coaz-securing-apis-and-ai-agents-with-standardized-authorization/, Jul 2026)

Contradictions:
- The same vendors are shipping proof-of-human and agent identity in the same week (Okta appears on both sides in late July 2026), so the strongest personhood-attestation infrastructure is being built by parties whose other product line depends on agents acting with delegated human authority.
- Standards work is pulling in opposite directions: agent authorization pushes toward richer, chained, attributable identity carried in every message, while ephemeral subject identifiers push toward authentication that leaves no correlatable trace.
- Regulators are asking for more cross-regulator information sharing about individuals (EDPB, 17 Jul 2026) in the same month they publish tests for when data about individuals stops being personal data at all (EDPB, 8 Jul 2026).
