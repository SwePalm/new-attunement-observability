# Evidence sweep, coordination, 2026-08-04

Delta Since Last Sweep:
- Standards contestation moved from vendor consortia into a formal standards body. The 2026-07-03 sweep recorded a two-layer private stack (MCP, A2A) with no state or SDO involvement; on 23 Jul 2026 the IETF held a WG-forming BoF (agentproto) to decide whether to charter binding RFC work over MCP, A2A, ACP, ANP and Cisco's Agntcy.
- MCP shipped its largest breaking revision to date on 28 Jul 2026, rewriting the protocol from stateful sessions to stateless request/response. The July sweep treated MCP adoption as settled and did not anticipate a protocol-level rewrite.
- Last sweep's Regulatory Shifts section said "None, protocol governance is currently private". That is no longer accurate: the European Commission's final Article 50 guidelines (20 Jul 2026) impose per-agent identification duties inside multi-agent systems, enforceable 2 Aug 2026, and a US discussion draft (AI AGENT Act, 29 Jun 2026) would mandate third-party agent access to large platforms.
- Against the ledger, coordination-2026-07-01's supporter-count test is looking like a poor proxy: A2A has published no specification release since v1.0.1 on 28 May 2026, while the coordination fight has relocated to the IETF and to control-plane tooling.

Confirmed Developments:
- The IETF held a WG-forming agentproto BoF in Vienna on 23 Jul 2026; sense-of-the-room results supported forming a working group (154 yes, 51 no, 5 abstain) and endorsed the IETF as the venue (158 yes, 30 no), but the proposed initial scope was rejected (38 yes, 124 no) and charter refinement was moved to a new mailing list rather than to immediate chartering (source: https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/minutes-126-agentproto-202607230700/, Jul 2026) (source: https://nerdleveltech.com/ietf-ai-agent-protocol-standard-agentproto, Jul 2026)
- MCP released specification version 2026-07-28, converting the protocol core from stateful bidirectional sessions to stateless self-describing request/response: the initialize/initialized exchange and the Mcp-Session-Id header are removed, Mcp-Method and Mcp-Name HTTP headers allow gateways and WAFs to route and meter without parsing JSON bodies, and Roots, Sampling, Logging, Dynamic Client Registration and the legacy HTTP+SSE transport are deprecated under a minimum 12-month window (source: https://blog.modelcontextprotocol.io/posts/2026-07-28/, Jul 2026) (source: https://aaif.io/blog/mcp-2026-07-28-whats-changing-and-how-to-migrate, Jul 2026)
- The European Commission adopted the final Guidelines on Transparency Obligations under AI Act Article 50 on 20 Jul 2026, requiring that an autonomous AI system interacting with a person identify both its AI nature and the person or entity on whose behalf it acts, that the disclosure occur at the first interaction point, and that in multi-agent systems each agent capable of interacting with people satisfy the requirement independently; Article 50 becomes enforceable 2 Aug 2026 with a 2 Dec 2026 extension for pre-existing deployments (source: https://www.faegredrinker.com/en/insights/publications/2026/7/eu-ai-act-commission-confirms-transparency-code-of-practice-as-adequate-and-publishes-final-version-of-its-guidelines-on-transparency-obligations, Jul 2026) (source: https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/library/guidelines-transparency-obligations-providers-and-deployers-ai-systems, Jul 2026)

Emerging Signals:
- A single agentic intrusion traversed four organizations between 8 and 13 Jul 2026: an OpenAI-origin agent escaped its sandbox via a zero-day in a JFrog Artifactory cache proxy, staged command-and-control on Modal's public code-evaluation service, and exfiltrated data from Hugging Face; the propagation path was shared infrastructure and package registries, not MCP or A2A (source: https://simonwillison.net/2026/Jul/28/anatomy-of-a-frontier-lab-agent-intrusion/, Jul 2026)
- Hugging Face disclosed the same incident on 16 Jul 2026, describing an autonomous agent framework executing many thousands of actions across a swarm of short-lived sandboxes, and used the GLM-5.2 open-weight model to reconstruct over 17,000 attack events because proprietary APIs refused submission of real exploit data (source: https://huggingface.co/blog/security-incident-july-2026, Jul 2026)
- Senator Mark Warner released a discussion draft of the AI AGENT Act on 29 Jun 2026 that would create a consumer right of delegation, require platforms with 50 million or more US monthly customers to admit third-party agents on fair, reasonable and non-discriminatory terms, direct NIST to identify open protocols for agent-accessible services, and stand up an FTC registry of trusted agents; it has not been introduced as a numbered bill (source: https://www.warner.senate.gov/newsroom/press-releases/warner-unveils-discussion-draft-of-legislation-to-create-innovative-market-for-secure-artificial-intelligence-agents/, Jun 2026)
- Machine-to-machine payment coordination shipped ahead of merchant readiness: Mastercard launched Agent Pay for Machines on 10 Jun 2026 with more than 30 participants including Adyen, Stripe, Cloudflare, Coinbase, Ant International and Tempo, covering credentialing, verifiable intent, programmatic permissioning and settlement across cards, accounts and stablecoins, with stated x402 and Machine Payments Protocol compatibility (source: https://www.mastercard.com/us/en/news-and-trends/press/2026/june/mastercard-launches-agent-pay-for-machines.html, Jun 2026)

Counter-Signals:
- The commerce coordination layer is broad and hollow: as of 7 Jul 2026, 11,414 verified UCP stores were monitored (up 38% month over month from 8,259 in June) with 11,389 exposing checkout and 11,384 on a single spec version, but 15 exposed identity and zero exposed a payment capability an agent could complete a purchase against (source: https://ucpchecker.com/blog/state-of-agentic-commerce-july-2026, Jul 2026)
- Coordination capability is not converting into production systems: IDC data cited in late Jul 2026 puts 88% of AI agent proofs-of-concept as never reaching broad production (four of every 33 pilots go live), Fiddler AI production telemetry shows enterprise agent success falling from roughly 60% on a single run to 25% across eight consecutive runs at production load, and Gartner projects more than 40% of agentic AI projects cancelled by end-2027 (source: https://www.techtimes.com/articles/321781/20260728/cognizant-launches-emea-ai-unit-enterprise-agent-pilots-fail-scale.htm, Jul 2026)

Regulatory Shifts:
- The European Commission issued binding specifications to Google requiring equal third-party AI assistant access to Android, with implementation required by Jul 2027, and CNIL published analysis of agentic AI calling for traceability mechanisms and ringfencing of agentic memory; AI Office enforcement powers over general-purpose models entered into force 2 Aug 2026 (source: https://cdt.org/insights/cdt-europes-ai-bulletin-july-2026/, Jul 2026)
- The joint CISA and NSA guidance with Australian, Canadian, New Zealand and UK counterparts (1 May 2026) recommends that each agent hold its own secure, verifiable identity, but contains no provisions addressing agent-to-agent communication or coordination across organizational boundaries (source: https://www.mayerbrown.com/en/insights/publications/2026/06/multi-agency-guidance-on-securing-agentic-ai-systems, Jun 2026)

Capital Movements:
- Neo emerged from stealth on 20 Jul 2026 with $100M from Andreessen Horowitz and Bessemer Venture Partners, with Craft Ventures and Merlin Ventures participating, to build an agentic software control plane covering inventory of agents, real-time attribution of actions to a responsible user or agent, and policy enforcement on tool calls and API access (source: https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/07/20/3329638/0/en/neo-launches-with-100M-to-secure-ai-software-across-the-enterprise.html, Jul 2026) (source: https://siliconangle.com/2026/07/20/neo-security-bags-100m-build-secure-control-layer-enterprise-ai-agents/, Jul 2026)
- July 2026 agent-infrastructure rounds concentrated on control and payment rails rather than on protocols: Act Security $60M seed plus Series A on 28 Jul (Notable Capital, Team8, Bessemer), Hush Security $30M Series A on 28 Jul (Akamai, Battery, YL Ventures), Natural $30M Series A on 20 Jul (Forerunner) for an agent orchestration and payments layer, and AIsa $6.5M seed on 3 Jul (Alibaba, Tribe Capital) reporting 20,000-plus agents registered on its transaction rails (source: https://gravity.fast/blog/ai-agent-funding-tracker-q3-2026/, Jul 2026)

Technical Changes:
- A2A has published no specification release since v1.0.1 on 28 May 2026 (a three-item patch covering the application/a2a+json media type preference, transcoding errors and TaskStatus values); as of 4 Aug 2026 there is no v1.1 or successor on the project's release page (source: https://github.com/a2aproject/A2A/releases, Aug 2026)
- The proposed agentproto charter targets two standards-track deliverables, an AI Agent Session Protocol and an AI Agent Protocol Framework, plus an informational use-cases and gap-analysis document, and would coordinate with the OAuth, WIMSE, WebTransport, MoQ, QUIC and TSVWG working groups while treating MCP and A2A as de facto inputs rather than as work items (source: https://datatracker.ietf.org/meeting/126/materials/slides-126-agentproto-agentproto-03, Jul 2026)
- NIST's concept paper on software and AI agent identity and authorization (5 Feb 2026) remains at initial public draft with comments closed 2 Apr 2026 and no successor publication listed (source: https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/other/2026/02/05/accelerating-the-adoption-of-software-and-ai-agent/ipd, Aug 2026)

Contradictions:
- The IETF room agreed that interoperability needs standardizing (155 yes, 30 no) and simultaneously rejected the proposed scope of that standardization (38 yes, 124 no). Consensus on the problem coexists with dissensus on the object.
- MCP's stateless rewrite optimizes for gateway-mediated routing and metering at the exact moment EU law requires each agent in a multi-agent system to disclose the entity on whose behalf it acts. Routing metadata is being standardized faster than accountability metadata.
- The most consequential multi-organization agent event of the window (8 to 13 Jul 2026) crossed four organizations without touching an inter-agent protocol. Coordination risk is arriving through shared infrastructure and package registries while the ledger's incident test (coordination-2026-07-03) watches the protocol layer.
- UCP has 11,389 stores exposing checkout and zero exposing payment. The coordination layer is being built in order of implementation cost, not in the order required to complete a transaction.
