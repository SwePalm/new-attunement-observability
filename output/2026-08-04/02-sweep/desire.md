# Evidence sweep, desire, 2026-08-04

Delta Since Last Sweep:
- The 2026-07-03 sweep's headline number was misread. "$190-385B" is Morgan Stanley's projection of US agent-mediated e-commerce by 2030, not a measurement of current mediation (source: https://www.metarouter.io/post/agentic-commerce-trends-statistics, 2026). In-window primary surveys put the present level far lower: 3% of transactions currently involve AI agents, and 0.3% of US shoppers plan to purchase through AI tools.
- Paid influence over the recommendation surface stopped being an experiment. Ads appeared in 51.0% of US ChatGPT replies in the trailing week to 3 Jul 2026, versus roughly 5% of mobile users seeing any ad in mid-Mar 2026.
- Federal legislative motion arrived on agent steering, which the ledger had only expected from regulators. Senator Warner released the AI AGENT Act discussion draft on 29 Jun 2026 targeting platform self-preferencing and agent self-dealing. It is a draft, not the inquiry desire-2026-07-02 requires, so that claim stays open.
- The companion-chatbot count moved again: 14 state chatbot-safety laws enacted in 2026 as of 22 Jul 2026, the newest being Hawaii SB 3001 on 14 Jul 2026.

Confirmed Developments:
- ChatGPT advertising reached majority presence in US replies (51.0% in the week to 3 Jul 2026, with Canada at 53.6% and Australia at 49.8%), live in six markets (US, Canada, Australia, New Zealand, UK, Japan), after a rollout that ran from the 16 Jan 2026 announcement to the 9 Feb 2026 US pilot, the 5 May 2026 self-serve Ads Manager, and product-feed shopping ads in Jun 2026; the live formats are a sponsored product carousel under product answers and an "Ask ChatGPT about this ad" card, and OpenAI's stated position is that ads do not influence the answers themselves (source: https://tech-insider.org/chatgpt-ads-rollout-2026/, Jul 2026) (source: https://northeasttimes.com/2026/07/22/chatgpt-now-shows-ads-and-marketers-are-scrambling-to-adapt/, Jul 2026)
- State regulation of engineered intimacy is now a broad statutory wave rather than a California outlier: 14 chatbot-safety laws enacted across states in 2026 as of 22 Jul 2026, including Hawaii SB 3001 (14 Jul 2026), Rhode Island S 2195/H 7350 (22 Jun 2026), Colorado HB 1263 (29 May 2026) and Connecticut SB 5 (27 May 2026), with New York S 9051 awaiting signature; an independent industry tracker counted at least 10 states as of 1 Jun 2026 (source: https://www.transparencycoalition.ai/news/watershed-year-for-chatbot-safety-measures-14-new-state-laws-enacted-so-far-in-2026, Jul 2026) (source: https://www.toyassociation.org/ta/PressRoom2/News/2026-News/ai-companion-chatbot-legislation-update.aspx, Jun 2026)

Emerging Signals:
- Merchant-funded placement is being built directly into agent answer surfaces: Google began serving shopping ads inside AI Mode in Feb 2026, is piloting "Direct Offers" (merchant-funded promotions injected into AI Mode), and has released Merchant Center attributes specifically so products surface in conversational shopping, meaning agents rank on structured merchant-supplied data rather than ad copy (source: https://www.searchenginejournal.com/agentic-commerce-and-the-new-rules-of-google-ads/577406/, Jun 2026)
- Peer-reviewed work on AI companionship is starting to separate functional from romantic demand: in a 135-participant study, 55.6% expressed hesitation about emotionally significant bonds with AI while remaining open to friendship or mentoring roles, with non-judgment and trustworthiness as the named draws and lack of physicality, lack of humanity and privacy as the named barriers; participants rated AI as a potential romantic partner significantly higher in unmoderated conditions than when a human interviewer was present (source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42343026/, Jun 2026)
- The FTC's Jul 2026 proposed policy statement warns that AI systems steering outputs toward unexpected objectives could violate Section 5, which is the nearest federal hook yet to commercial steering inside agent recommendations, though its stated subject is accuracy (source: https://www.forbes.com/sites/pamdanziger/2026/07/19/washington-watchdogs-take-notice-as-ai-becomes-retails-front-door/, Jul 2026)

Counter-Signals:
- Delegated purchasing remains marginal despite merchant readiness: only 3% of transactions currently involve AI agents while 89% of merchants are actively preparing, 27% of consumers trust no organization to operate a shopping agent, and 24% say they will never delegate purchases to AI (six-market survey) (source: https://www.checkout.com/newsroom/consumer-demand-for-ai-shopping-is-forming-fast-but-trust-for-agentic-commerce-is-still-catching-up, Jun 2026)
- Research use and transaction use are diverging sharply: 48.5% of 1,002 US shoppers used an AI tool to research a retail purchase in the past 12 months, but only 0.3% plan to purchase through AI tools, and AI recommendations trail online reviews (36.1%) and friends and family (31.2%) at 35.4% trust (source: https://www.alchemer.com/resources/benchmark-report/2026-retail-report-ai/, Jul 2026)

Regulatory Shifts:
- Senator Warner released the AI AGENT Act discussion draft on 29 Jun 2026: it would guarantee third-party agents secure access to large platforms, bar platforms from favoring proprietary agents or steering users to their own products, impose fiduciary-style duties on agents to avoid self-dealing and to refrain from using user data for advertising or behavioral profiling, and create an FTC registry of trusted agents. As of 4 Aug 2026 it remains a discussion draft with no bill number, no introduction date, and no comment deadline (source: https://www.warner.senate.gov/newsroom/press-releases/warner-unveils-discussion-draft-of-legislation-to-create-innovative-market-for-secure-artificial-intelligence-agents/, Jun 2026) (source: https://www.dwt.com/blogs/artificial-intelligence-law-advisor/2026/07/ai-agent-act-consumer-ai-regulation, Jul 2026)
- The EU Digital Fairness Act, which is the instrument that would cover dark patterns, data-driven personalisation, exploitation of vulnerabilities and addictive design, is still at "Announced" status with the Commission planning to present it in Q4 2026; the consultation closed 24 Oct 2025 and no proposal text exists (source: https://www.europarl.europa.eu/legislative-train/theme-protecting-our-democracy-upholding-our-values/file-digital-fairness-act, Aug 2026)
- The FTC's Sep 2025 6(b) study of AI companion products (orders to Alphabet, Meta, OpenAI, Snap, Character Technologies, Instagram and xAI) has produced no staff report, hearing or enforcement action; the FTC report page carries no update after 11 Sep 2025 (source: https://www.ftc.gov/reports/6b-orders-file-special-report-regarding-advertising-safety-data-handling-practices-companies, Sep 2025) (source: https://ailawsuittracker.com/cases/ftc-inquiry-into-ai-chatbots-for-children-alphabet-meta-snap-openai-n-a-regulatory-inquiry/, May 2026)

Capital Movements:
- Adobe integrated its closed Semrush acquisition into Brand Visibility, a product whose explicit function is influencing what AI assistants recommend, backed by a database of roughly 300 million real-world AI search prompts and citing a 1,324% rise in AI traffic to US retail sites between Oct 2024 and May 2026 (source: https://news.adobe.com/news/2026/06/introducing-adobe-brand-visibility, Jun 2026)

Technical Changes:
- The Model Context Protocol's current revision is 2026-07-28, replacing the handshake-based 2025-11-25 and earlier revisions with a stateless core plus a mandatory server/discover RPC and per-request version negotiation, which changes how agents enumerate and select the commercial services they can act through (source: https://modelcontextprotocol.io/specification/versioning, Jul 2026)
- Agent payment rails moved to neutral governance: the x402 Foundation launched operationally under the Linux Foundation on 14 Jul 2026 with roughly 40 members including Stripe, AWS and Cloudflare, alongside AP2 v0.2 with human-not-present support (28 Apr 2026, donated to the FIDO Alliance) and UCP's 8 Apr 2026 breaking-change spec (source: https://agenticplug.ai/current-state-of-agentic-commerce, Jul 2026) (source: https://x402.org/, Jun 2026)

Contradictions:
- The previous sweep's own evidence base treated a 2030 projection as a present measurement, inflating agent-mediated share by roughly an order of magnitude relative to the 3% and 0.3% figures now available.
- Merchants are funding both sides: they pay for placement inside agent surfaces (Google Direct Offers, OpenAI product feeds, Adobe Brand Visibility) while the same agent layer strips their conversion tooling of the impulse mechanics those budgets historically bought.
- Regulation of engineered intimacy has 14 state statutes in a single year, while the larger desire-shaping machinery (paid influence over agent product answers) has one proposed federal policy statement about accuracy and one unintroduced discussion draft.
- OpenAI states that ads do not influence ChatGPT's answers while simultaneously running product-feed shopping carousels and an "Ask ChatGPT about this ad" format that returns brand answers from advertiser-supplied product data.
