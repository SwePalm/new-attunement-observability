Turn 1 – Conceptual Frame

Start with the number nobody can spend. Oregon SB 1546 and California SB 243 both give a harmed person the greater of actual damages or $1,000 per violation, and neither statute says what one violation is. Per message, per session, per user, per disclosure failure: the text does not choose, and the choice is the whole remedy, because the same words produce either a nominal payment or a class-scale one depending on where the counter clicks (Baker Botts, Mar 2026). This is not a drafting oversight worth a footnote. It is the conceptual centre of what responsibility has become in this domain.

Every instrument in this month's record is a counting instrument, and each counts something different. Illinois counts capability and size, reaching only developers above $500m revenue with models trained above 10^26 operations, and counts penalties at $1m for a first violation and $3m for each subsequent one. The EU counts turnover, at up to EUR 15m or 3% worldwide from 2 Aug 2026. China's Interim Measures for the Administration of Anthropomorphic AI Interaction Services, in force 15 Jul 2026, count in fines to CNY 200,000 where a violation harms life, health or safety. Rhode Island's S 2195 counts in days, at up to $15,000 per day of Attorney General enforcement. The individual-remedy statutes count in violations of an undefined kind. Five denominators, no conversion between any two of them.

The classical picture of responsibility is relational: A is answerable to B for what A did to B. What the corrected record shows is that relation being replaced by measurement, and measurement requires a unit that somebody must pick. Whoever picks the unit sets the size of the obligation without ever arguing about its existence. That is a quieter form of power than writing the duty, and it is currently unexercised in the one lane where a private person can collect.

Washington HB 2225 is the interesting exception, because it declines to mint a number at all. Signed 24 Mar 2026 and effective 1 Jan 2027, it makes a violation a per se unfair or deceptive act under the state Consumer Protection Act and lets that older statute supply the cause of action, the damages architecture and the fee provisions (Hunton, Mar 2026). So there are two ways to build an individual remedy for AI harm: mint a new currency and leave its unit undefined, or plug the new conduct into a currency that already circulates. Only one of those is portable.

Turn 2 – Societal Reframing

The socially decisive fact of the corrected record is not that individuals have no remedy. Three states route damages to the harmed person, and California's SB 243 has been operative since 1 Jan 2026. The decisive fact is what sorts them. Remedy in 2026 is allocated by the formal category of the product a person was using at the moment of injury, and that category has no relationship to the severity of what happened.

The sorting is explicit in the exclusions. California, Oregon and Washington all carve out customer-service bots, video-game characters and stand-alone voice-activated assistants, and Oregon additionally excludes education, healthcare, financial services and research (Mayer Brown, Apr 2026; Troutman, Mar 2026). A system that simulates a relationship is in. A system that decides whether you get the loan, the interview, the apartment or the triage slot is out. So the harm with a named plaintiff and a dollar figure is emotional, and the harm that moves money has no damages route under any of the three statutes. Fourteen companion and conversational-AI safety laws were enacted by US states during 2026 as of 22 Jul, and of those fourteen only Oregon's own text lets a harmed user sue for damages; the rest route to an Attorney General, a licensing restriction, or the criminal law, with Wyoming HB 102 reaching for felony exposure of up to ten years instead of civil recovery (Transparency Coalition, Jul 2026).

Reframed, this is a taxonomy of standing rather than a hierarchy of protection. Before anyone asks whether care was taken, the person is sorted by what they were talking to. The sorting also carries a political inheritance. The 11 Dec 2025 executive order names transparency, reporting, documentation and safety-testing regimes as preemption targets while expressly sparing otherwise lawful state child-safety AI laws, which is the framing under which the companion statutes passed. The federal government is therefore simultaneously the main threat to state AI law and the reason the only paying lane survives.

That has a cost that is easy to miss. If the remedy is defensible only in the register of protecting the vulnerable, then the argument for compensating a wronged adult never gets made, because it is not the argument that wins. Society has produced a right to be paid for harm suffered inside a simulated intimacy, and no right to be paid for harm suffered inside an automated decision, and the second gap is now harder to close than it was before the first was filled, because the first can be cited as proof that individuals can already sue. Colorado is the demonstration. SB 26-189, signed 14 May 2026, deleted the duty of reasonable care against algorithmic discrimination and substituted pre-use notice, 30-day adverse-decision explanations, correction rights and human review: a right to be told, in place of a right not to be harmed.

Turn 3 – Psychological Implications

Look at this from inside the relationship the statutes actually reach, because that is where the law is now physically present. The state companion statutes convert safety into testable product behaviour: non-human disclosure at session start, re-displayed every three hours for adults and hourly for minors, suicidal-ideation detection with crisis referral, and prohibitions on manipulative engagement techniques for minors (Fisher Phillips, Mar 2026). Responsibility here does not sit in a filing cabinet. It arrives as an interruption, on a clock, inside a conversation someone is having because they wanted company.

The clock does two things at once, and their combination is unusual. Psychologically, it is a periodic disenchantment: every three hours, or every hour if you are a minor, the relationship is required to tell you what it is not. Legally, that same interval is the metering device. An interval is what makes a continuous relationship countable, and countability is exactly what the $1,000-per-violation measure lacks. Inference, not established by this run's evidence: whichever tribunal first construes "violation" is likely to reach for the disclosure cadence, because it is the only event boundary the statutes actually specify, in which case the intimate experience of being periodically reminded that nothing is there becomes the tick of the damages meter.

China builds the opposite psychology on the same product. From 15 Jul 2026 the Interim Measures put distress detection, crisis intervention, addiction-curbing and minor-access duties inside the service, so the machine is deputised to notice your state and act on it. One regime makes the product declare its own emptiness at fixed intervals; the other makes it watch you closely enough to intervene, with a compliance interest in what it sees. Both are called responsibility. Neither is what a user would describe as being cared for.

Then there is the psychology of the sorted-out majority, which the exclusions create and nobody has to look at. Learning that a compensation regime exists but does not cover you is not the same experience as learning that no regime exists. The first converts an absence into a judgement, and the judgement is legible: your kind of harm was considered and not selected. The US chatbot injury docket shows what the alternative currently costs, with plaintiffs' counsel putting OpenAI at roughly nineteen wrongful death suits as of Jul 2026 and In re: ChatGPT Product Liability Cases (JCCP 5431) still in pretrial with no dispositive ruling and no trial date on whether a model is a product at all. That is the route available to people the statutes exclude: years, capital, and a threshold question that has not been answered.

Turn 4 – Institutional Dynamics

Two counting regimes, no exchange rate, and the institutions that would build one are each disabled in a specific way.

Courts are the obvious candidate and the most reliably removed. Responsibility-2026-07-01 decayed this run because Garcia, the case that produced the AI-as-product holding, settled with Google and Character.AI in Jan 2026 before the Eleventh Circuit could review it. Settlement is a doctrine-suppression mechanism available to any defendant with capital, which means the body best placed to import audit content into a standard of care is also the body a funded defendant can most cheaply keep out of the room. The trial level meanwhile runs ahead of the appellate level in the opposite direction, with a plaintiff verdict in K.G.M. v. Meta on 25 Mar 2026 and strict product liability pleaded directly against model providers rather than as component supply, which is why responsibility-2026-07-03 stays open on a mechanism that is quietly migrating underneath it.

Legislatures are constrained from above. The DOJ AI Litigation Task Force intervened in xAI v. Weiser on 24 Apr 2026, the first federal intervention against a state AI law, followed by a 27 Apr 2026 order barring enforcement of SB 24-205 or any replacement enacted that session. A state contemplating a duty of care over consequential AI decisions now legislates into that.

Regulators in Europe hold the competence and not the apparatus. The AI Act creates no explicit right for an affected individual to obtain a remedy from an entity in the value chain, the Article 85 complaint imposes no obligation on a market surveillance authority to respond, and the Article 86 explanation reaches only Annex III high-risk systems (CDT, Apr 2026). As of 17 Jun 2026, only 9 of 27 member states had clearly designated competent authorities, so the EUR 15m exposure that switched on 2 Aug 2026 has, in most of the Union, no one to administer it. The Article 73 serious-incident guidance and template remain in the draft consulted between 26 Sep and 7 Nov 2025.

Which leaves the institution that cannot avoid picking a unit, because it has to quote a price. Klaimee raised $5.5m on 22 Jul 2026 to certify autonomous agents and sell insurance-backed performance warranties, pairing pre-bind testing with parametric claim triggers, and a parametric trigger is by construction a definition of a countable event. CFC completed affirmative AI wording across seven product lines on 30 Jul 2026 while ISO and Verisk filed exclusions in Jan 2026 with AIG and Berkley following, and Beazley and QBE capped payouts at roughly 10% of limits. CAL-003 warns that this pipeline underweights exactly this channel. Inference, not established by this run's evidence: the practical meaning of "one violation" may be fixed in an underwriting schedule before any court construes the word, and if it is, the conversion between the individual lane and the audit lane will have been performed by parties who were pricing risk rather than allocating justice.

Turn 5 – Long-Term Trajectory

On 1 January 2027 the architecture becomes visible in a single day. Oregon SB 1546 and Washington HB 2225 begin, adding two paying lanes to California's one. Rhode Island's S 2195 begins with Attorney General enforcement alone at up to $15,000 per day. Colorado's SB 26-189 begins, replacing a duty of care with a right to an explanation. Illinois AISMA disclosure statements begin, one year ahead of the audit duty in 2028. Five instruments, one date, four incompatible answers to what a person is owed, and no mechanism that translates between them.

A person injured in the first week of that January will not first be asked whether anyone was careless. They will be asked what, formally, they were interacting with. If it simulated a relationship and they are in Oregon, Washington or California, there is a number, though nobody yet knows its denominator. If it decided something about their money, their work or their care, the same states have excluded them by name. If the developer is above $500m revenue and 10^26 operations, the developer will be audited and the injured person will still get nothing from that machinery, because Illinois reserves enforcement to the Attorney General. If they are in the EU, they may lodge a complaint that nobody is obliged to answer, in a member state that may not yet have designated who would answer it.

Two observables inside the horizon decide whether this hardens. The first is the unit. Fix "violation" at per message and the companion remedy becomes systemically significant; fix it at per user and it stays a gesture. The second is portability, and the structural question names the vehicle: Washington's per se unfair-practice hook, which required no legislature to define an AI system and no court to settle product versus service. If a 2027 session attaches that hook to credit, hiring, housing or care triage, the individual lane stops being a category and becomes a technique. If no session does, the three statutes will have been the high-water mark, cited thereafter as evidence that individuals can already sue.

CAL-002 applies to every date above, since this pipeline has repeatedly overestimated the firmness of announced timelines and the Digital Omnibus is the standing example. CAL-004 applies to how this theme's own confirmations are read: responsibility-2026-07-02 confirmed on statutes signed in March and April 2026, before the claim was written, which is why the corrected record now shows a remedy the pipeline had already missed once.

The long-term stake is narrower and sharper than a contest between audit and liability. It is whether harm caused by an AI system remains something a person can only be paid for when it happened inside a simulated relationship. That is not a limit anyone argued for. It is the residue of which framing survived federal preemption pressure, and residues of that kind tend to set, because the argument that would dislodge them was never held.
