### Political
Core Shift Thesis:
Legislatures are converging on visible, viewer-facing disclosure and on punishing the removal of marks, but their calendars are slower than the platform layer by months to years, so the political settlement arrives into a perceptual environment that platforms have already shaped.

Forces:
1. Tampering sanctions under formal review: South Korea's National Assembly Science, ICT, Broadcasting and Communications Committee bill-review subcommittee took up AI Basic Act and Network Act amendments on 15 Sep 2026 that would sanction removal or alteration of AI marks (Kim Dae-sik bill: up to 2 years' prison or KRW 20M; Lee Sang-hwi bill: administrative fine up to KRW 30M) and deferred them to the October regular session (source: https://www.imaeil.com/page/view/2026092412193369072, Sep 2026). Tracked as perception-2026-09-01.
2. EU marking date held without enforcement: the European AI Board's ninth plenary on 17 Sep 2026 set no new deadline and granted no further relief, so the 2 Dec 2026 Article 50(2) machine-readable marking date for pre-2 Aug systems stands, while no named Article 50 action against a provider or deployer has been recorded (source: https://labs.cloudsecurityalliance.org/research/csa-research-note-eu-ai-act-article50-watermarking-deadline/, Sep 2026). The absence of enforcement is from the grading file (perception-2026-08-02, open).
3. US subnational provenance politics stalled at the executive step: New York's Stop Deepfakes Act (S6954B) passed both chambers in June 2026 and its action history still ended at Assembly passage as of 25 Sep 2026, and Oregon's SB 1571 investigation into unlabeled AI videos of Rep. Janelle Bynum had no written determination (sources: https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/bills/2025/S6954/amendment/B, Jun 2026; https://www.opb.org/article/2026/07/09/artificial-intelligence-ai-generated-political-advertisements-oregon/, Jul 2026). Both items are from the grading file and ledger, not the sweep.
4. Election tests without synthetic-media attribution: the most visible election-information scare of the interval, fabricated polls exposed in Aug 2026, involved no AI-generated media, and the Nov 2026 US midterms remain the open test for perception-2026-07-03 (source: https://www.mainepublic.org/npr-news/2026-08-28/what-a-fake-poll-reveals-about-worries-around-prediction-markets-and-the-midterms, Aug 2026). From the grading file.

### Economic
Core Shift Thesis:
Reach, not labeling, is the currency through which disclosure is actually enforced, and detection is being sold to enterprises as a fraud service, so the economic weight of the regime sits with creators' distribution and institutions' risk budgets rather than with viewers' understanding.

Forces:
1. Distribution as penalty: Instagram on 31 Aug 2026 introduced the "AI-generated profile" badge and said undisclosed AI-generated profiles would be restricted from Reels, Explore and in-feed suggestions to non-followers until labeled, with detection and notification of unlabeled profiles to follow "in the coming weeks" (source: https://www.cnet.com/tech/services-and-software/instagram-ai-generated-profile-label-fake-influencer-profiles-2/, Aug 2026). The limit touches new audiences only; existing followers are unaffected.
2. Detection priced for enterprises: KPMG LLP took a minority stake in Reality Defender on 11 Sep 2026 and will integrate its synthetic media detection into cyber and fraud-prevention services (source: https://www.biometricupdate.com/202609/kpmg-investment-in-reality-defender-backs-hiring-spree-to-fight-deepfakes, Sep 2026).
3. A demand market for removal: "AI watermark remover" searches rose 60% week over week and a GitHub remover project passed 15,000 stars within about a week of Anthropic's watermark announcement (source: https://gizmodo.com/people-are-rushing-to-find-ways-to-remove-claudes-ai-text-watermark-2000800433, Aug 2026).
4. Marked synthetic personas as an enterprise product: Google's Gemini 3.8 Live with Live Avatar ships to allowlisted enterprise customers with SynthID on every avatar's audio and video, so synthetic customer-facing faces arrive pre-marked for a reader other than the customer (source: https://www.engadget.com/2268587/google-video-avatars-gemini-3-8-live-agent/, Sep 2026).

### Social
Core Shift Thesis:
Disclosure is being populated by the conscientious and the confused rather than by the deceptive, so labels become information about who chose to tell, and the social meaning of an unlabeled account drifts toward "human by default".

Forces:
1. Self-disclosure dependency: on launch day of Instagram's rule, CNET found none of six prominent AI influencer accounts labeled (source: https://www.cnet.com/tech/services-and-software/instagram-ai-generated-profile-label-fake-influencer-profiles-2/, Aug 2026).
2. Stripping as ordinary behaviour: the immediate, visible user response to text watermarking was a rush to remove it (source: https://gizmodo.com/people-are-rushing-to-find-ways-to-remove-claudes-ai-text-watermark-2000800433, Aug 2026).
3. Producer confusion about how to label: Korea's AI Basic Act support desk logged 513 inquiries in two months, 52.6% of online inquiries about how to label (source: https://www.imaeil.com/page/view/2026092412193369072, Sep 2026).
4. Perception as a user setting: TikTok's user-controlled "AI-generated content" slider in Manage Topics, shipped Nov 2025, lets viewers set how much AI content they are shown (source: https://techcrunch.com/2025/11/18/tiktok-now-lets-you-choose-how-much-ai-generated-content-you-want-to-see/, Nov 2025). From the grading file, not the sweep.

### Technological
Core Shift Thesis:
Marks are spreading fastest where they are invisible and gated, device-level integration is slipping, and the first evidence has appeared that the mark can change what it marks.

Forces:
1. Invisible text marks with a gated reader: Claude Fable 5.1 and Mythos 5.1 launched on 1 Sep 2026 watermarking text and file outputs, and a detection API opened in private preview for regulators, law enforcement, media, fact-checkers, researchers and obligated enterprises (sources: https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-text-watermark, Sep 2026; https://www.pcworld.com/article/3224834/claudes-updated-fable-and-mythos-models-watermark-their-replies.html, Sep 2026).
2. Sampling drift: Lasso Security's 17 Sep 2026 study reports that SynthID-Text watermarking, tested on open-weight models, changed refusal behaviour on HarmBench prompts and reduced tool-call accuracy on six of seven models, with the largest room for change in uncertain argument values such as queries, paths and recipients (sources: https://arstechnica.com/security/2026/09/ai-text-watermarking-can-make-models-more-vulnerable-to-adversarial-prompts/, Sep 2026; https://www.unite.ai/lasso-study-finds-text-watermarking-shifts-llm-refusals-and-tool-calls/, Sep 2026).
3. Device-layer slippage: iOS 27 reached general release on 14 Sep 2026 without SynthID, which Apple placed in a later 2026 software update (source: https://www.macworld.com/article/2986799/ios-27-features-compatiblity-siri-ai-updates.html, Sep 2026).
4. Capture-time provenance as a phone default: Google ships C2PA Content Credentials by default on Pixel Camera photos, and Samsung's Galaxy S26 auto-tags AI-generated photos with C2PA metadata (source: https://security.googleblog.com/2025/09/pixel-android-trusted-images-c2pa-content-credentials.html, Sep 2025). From the ledger (perception-2026-07-02), not the sweep.

### Legal
Core Shift Thesis:
The law is specifying the form of disclosure (visible, full-duration, machine-readable) with increasing precision while leaving removal largely unpunished and distribution decisions unaddressed.

Forces:
1. Visibility specified by regulators: Korea's Ministry of Science and ICT casebook requires deepfake outputs to carry a visible mark across the full playback and does not accept a hidden watermark alone, while allowing metadata or invisible marks for artistic works (source: https://www.imaeil.com/page/view/2026092412193369072, Sep 2026).
2. A softer operative regime than the headline: the Ministry reads Article 31 as binding only AI business operators, exempts individual creators, applies the KRW 30M fine only after an ignored corrective order, defers fact-finding and fines to the end of the grace period on 22 Jan 2027, and has no current provision penalizing stripping (source: https://www.imaeil.com/page/view/2026092412193369072, Sep 2026).
3. Platform preservation duties proposed: Korean Network Act amendments by Jo In-cheol and Park Choong-kwon would require platforms to prevent removal of AI marks, with an administrative fine up to KRW 10M in a companion proposal (source: https://www.imaeil.com/page/view/2026092412193369072, Sep 2026).
4. Operative US state disclosure law: California's AI Transparency Act (SB 942 as amended by AB 853) became operative on 2 Aug 2026 (source: https://www.morganlewis.com/pubs/2026/08/new-california-ai-disclosure-rules-become-operative, Aug 2026), alongside the EU Article 50(2) machine-readable marking date of 2 Dec 2026 (source: https://labs.cloudsecurityalliance.org/research/csa-research-note-eu-ai-act-article50-watermarking-deadline/, Sep 2026).

### Environmental
Core Shift Thesis:
Read here as the physical and device environment in which seeing happens: provenance is anchored at the camera and the screen, and lost at every physical boundary between them, so where a person looks matters more than what the file carries.

Forces:
1. The screen as the legal site of disclosure: Korea's requirement that deepfakes carry a visible mark for the whole of playback relocates disclosure from the file to the physical display (source: https://www.imaeil.com/page/view/2026092412193369072, Sep 2026).
2. Capture hardware as provenance anchor: camera-level C2PA signing on Pixel and auto-tagging on Galaxy S26 make the phone, rather than the platform, the origin of provenance for everyday photos (source: https://security.googleblog.com/2025/09/pixel-android-trusted-images-c2pa-content-credentials.html, Sep 2025). From the ledger.
3. Real-time synthetic presence in service settings: marked live avatars for enterprise customers bring synthetic faces into the ordinary environments of banking and customer service, where the viewer's attention is on the task rather than the medium (source: https://www.engadget.com/2268587/google-video-avatars-gemini-3-8-live-agent/, Sep 2026).
