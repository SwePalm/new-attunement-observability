## 3. Institutional Realism Audit

Assessment: moderate realism

Notes:
- Friction is present where the evidence shows it: the antitrust suit is still in pretrial motions in mid-2028, European enforcement still runs through requests to unnamed providers, the US designation framework is still unpublished, and "nothing in public law yet keys an obligation to a reading". These are slow clocks set deliberately and consistent with the grading file, where all four institutional claims (intelligence-2026-08-02, -08-03, -08-04, -08-07) produced no qualifying event through the densest release wave on record.
- Power asymmetry is visible and specific: labs measure their own acceleration on scales they choose, government measures only rivals, and the choice of operative reading falls to whichever party needs a number, which here is a lender and a county, not a regulator.
- The world is too tidy in two places. First, the county tender that adds harness, version and date "because the previous tender quoted a number without a version and it meant something else six days later" imports the exact Artificial Analysis rebase interval into a local procurement lesson. That is an elegant echo rather than a plausible institutional learning path. Second, the credit union's loan form asks small businesses which model their operations depend on and its neutral reading, which assumes a level of capability-literacy in small-business lending that no upstream item supports.
- No lab is shown defending the adapter harness figure in public, no evaluator is shown under funding strain from the per-run verification costs the PESTLE records ($5.6K to $26K), and no one outside the puzzle club resists the task-broker market. The counter-currents the exploration identifies (jaggedness, METR's scepticism about full R&D automation) appear only in 7.1 and the households' "can it do this job" habit.

## 4. Horizon Discipline Check

Pass/Fail: Pass, with one internal inconsistency recorded

Notes:
- The horizon runs from September 2026 to June 2028, about 1.7 years, well inside 0 to 5 years. No sci-fi drift: nothing in the world requires a capability or institution absent from the September 2026 record except the successor benchmark and the task-broker market, both incremental.
- Calibration heuristics are applied as restraint. CAL-002 (announced regulatory timelines slip): no statutory threshold keyed to a reading appears, the EO 14409 framework is still unpublished, and 7.2 says statutory thresholds remain unlikely. CAL-003 (non-state mechanisms lead): the first binding uses of a reading are a loan form and a procurement tender. CAL-006 (the fastest channel is often not the most formal institution): the operative reading is chosen by a lender and a county rather than by NIST or the AI Office. CAL-007 is respected in 7.3, whose items are documentation and contract changes rather than docket outcomes.
- Internal inconsistency: the scenario's own readings are slower than its own Probable Direction. It moves the neutral figure from 62.7% (Sep 2026) to about 71 (Apr 2027) and past 80 only in Nov 2027, while 7.2 says to "expect it to climb faster than most forecasts allow, since recent thresholds described as implausible have fallen within weeks". The in-world trajectory would falsify ledger claim intelligence-2026-09-01 (80% under the Standard harness by 2027-03). The grading file's candidate heuristic (capability benchmark thresholds resolving within one to two cycles; not an active CALIBRATION.md entry) points the other way. The scenario therefore slows the capability clock that the evidence says runs fast, while correctly slowing the institutional clock that the evidence says runs slow. This is a genuine defect of horizon calibration, even if a benchmark plateau near saturation is defensible on its own terms.
- The scenario does not repeat an active calibration bias in its institutional world-building.

## 7. Cross-Theme Convergence Risk

Assessment: moderate

Notes:
- Comparison data available: the ten scenarios from the 2026-08-04 and 2026-08-13 runs, and, from this run, the learning scenario and the forces-feelings files for agency, belonging, learning and perception that existed when this evaluation was written.
- The "record controlled by the party least interested in publishing it" attractor, flagged by two consecutive runs, is not reached. The scenario's central finding is the opposite shape: an excess of published, verified, dated readings, with the contest over which one binds whom. There is one adjacency, recorded rather than scored away: the labs' self-published R&D automation figures are a case of the measured party producing the measurement, and 7.3's government item ("not left to the parties being measured") gestures at the attractor. It is a secondary strand, not the conclusion.
- Structural form is distinct from the prior corpus. A single public number revisited at intervals, with each section anchored on a reading, is not used by any earlier scenario, and no earlier scenario is organised around a measurement series.
- Residual convergence is real on three points. First and most substantive, the Irreplaceable Thing converges with this run's learning scenario. Learning's is "company in the stuck part", an adult staying beside a young person while they do not yet understand; this scenario's is being stumped together at a table until someone sees the rule. The first draft of this scenario used a grandfather watching his granddaughter solve a puzzle, which was near-identical to learning's father beside his daughter; it was changed to a peer group before evaluation, but the shared emotional object (company during not-knowing, without intervention or scoring) survives. Perception's "the same screen" and belonging's "being missed" are also forms of shared presence, so a run-level attractor toward togetherness as the scarce good may be emerging. Second, both this scenario and learning are built as dated returns across 2027 to 2028 with a working-class or retired adult as the centre, which is also the shape of the 2026-08-13 memory scenario. Third, Section 9 opens with a negation of wrongdoing ("The numbers in this window were not hidden, disputed or faked"), the same rhetorical move that opens the final insights of the 2026-08-04 alignment and authority scenarios and the 2026-08-13 autonomy scenario.
- Tonal homogeneity: the flat, restrained register and the refusal of a villain are shared with most of the prior corpus.

## 8. Overall Evaluation

Score (1–10): 7.7

Forecast Integrity Level: moderate

Stability Risk: medium

Summary:
The scenario's epistemic strength lies in how directly its premise is built from measurement facts that are dated, sourced and verifiable: two ARC Prize figures 37 points apart for one model, an index rebase that turned 66 into 53 without any model changing, lab-published self-acceleration figures on lab-chosen scales, a government capability figure aimed only at a rival, and an antitrust complaint that treats agreement on pace as an output restraint. From those it derives a genuinely generative mechanism, that a shared velocity reference is most likely to be adopted by non-competitors (lenders, public buyers) because competitors cannot agree on one without legal exposure. That mechanism is the scenario's main forecast, and it follows the non-state-first heuristics rather than the known regulatory-speed bias. Signal anchoring is strong.

Four weaknesses hold the score below 8. The central adoption mechanism, lenders and county tenders writing harness, version and date into binding documents, has no observed precursor in the sweep and is depicted with an elegance (a tender learning from a six-day rebase) that the evidence does not support. The in-world capability trajectory is slower than the scenario's own Probable Direction and slower than the grading record suggests, so the scenario slows the one clock the evidence says runs fast. A few reader-facing characterisations of ARC cite nothing upstream, and one figure comes from grading without saying so. And the Irreplaceable Thing converges with this run's learning scenario on the value of company during not-knowing, even after a pre-evaluation revision.

Stability risk is medium. A neutral Standard-harness reading at or above 80% before March 2027, or a court or regulator adopting a specific capability series in a ruling, would each reshape this world, the first by speeding the readings, the second by moving the operative choice to the state.
