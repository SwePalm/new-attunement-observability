# Structural question, belonging, 2026-09-25

Structural Question:
In the 6 to 24 months after California's SB 1119 takes effect on 1 Jul 2027, while the EU KIDS Act is still being negotiated and the largest general-purpose vendor already forbids its teen model to call itself a friend, will rules that define the simulated relationship as the harm actually reduce the emotional reliance of the most isolated young users, or will they remove the relational language while the reliance continues underneath it, so that the only thing that reliably changes is what the system is permitted to say on the user's eighteenth birthday?

Why It Matters:
Within five weeks the theme acquired three instruments that all target the same object. OpenAI made ChatGPT for Teens the default for predicted minors on 18 Aug 2026, barring romantic language, claimed feelings, self-description as a friend and fostering emotional dependence; California signed SB 1119 on 10 Sep 2026, requiring pre-release child-safety risk assessments, time-limiting defaults and audits from 2029; and the Commission's EU KIDS Act proposal of 17 Sep 2026 requires that companions for under-18s be off by default and "cannot simulate interpersonal relationships in ways that create emotional dependency". Every one of them is keyed to an age bracket and to the system's relational speech. The best new evidence points somewhere else. The JAMA Pediatrics study of 39,761 Ontario students (31 Aug 2026) found 21% using chatbots for emotional support, with 57.7% of that group at clinical-level difficulty against 29.2% of non-users, and its authors read the use as a possible marker of distress rather than a cause; the Folk and Dunn panel study that confirmed belonging-2026-07-02 (ledger, Mar 2026) found the effect running in both directions, isolation predicting chatbot use and chatbot use predicting isolation. If reliance is partly a symptom, a rule that changes the vocabulary of the listener without changing who has anyone else to talk to will be judged a success on the metric it can audit and leave the underlying condition unmeasured.

Volatility Level:
medium

Volatility note: the regulatory clocks are slow and should be assumed slower than announced. SB 1119 is operative 1 Jul 2027 but its independent audits do not fall due until 1 Jan 2029; the KIDS Act is a proposal with a visible Council split (Czech Republic and Estonia against blanket bans); New York's S9051B has sat undelivered to the governor since 5 Jun 2026 (belonging-2026-08-01, open); the FTC's 6(b) inquiry has published nothing in a year (belonging-2026-08-07, open). What moves fast is vendor default design, which shipped before any statute required it. The question is consequential because the fast clock and the slow clock are both aimed at the words the system uses, and neither is aimed at the distribution of human company among the users who lean on it most.
