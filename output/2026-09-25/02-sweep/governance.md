# Evidence sweep, governance, 2026-09-25

Delta Since Last Sweep:
- The EU's first binding step against a chatbot provider arrived through the Digital Services Act, not the AI Act: on 31 Aug 2026 the Commission designated ChatGPT a Very Large Online Search Engine, with systemic-risk duties due within four months (source: https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/news/commission-designates-chatgpt-reddit-roblox-under-digital-services-act, Aug 2026). This is the adjacent-instrument pattern the previous sweep recorded for TikTok, now applied to a frontier AI product.
- The AI Office moved from zero to its first formal enforcement steps. It sent requests for information to more than 30 unnamed providers, confirmed 1 Sep 2026 (source: https://agenceurope.eu/en/bulletin/article/13929/31/european-commission-sends-first-requests-for-information-to-more-than-30-ai-providers, Sep 2026). It also publicly confirmed receiving an OpenAI serious-incident report (source: https://thenextweb.com/news/openai-eu-incident-report-german-wiki, Sep 2026). Neither step names an Article 50 target, so governance-2026-08-02 stays untriggered.
- US state governance moved from statute to supervisory machinery. California signed a third-party AI audit and verification framework on 9 Sep 2026 (source: https://statescoop.com/newsom-signs-legislation-establishing-framework-for-third-party-ai-auditors/, Sep 2026). An executive order followed, setting a 16 Nov 2026 deadline for recommendations that include a frontier-model shutdown capability (source: https://www.cbsnews.com/sacramento/news/california-newsom-ai-safety-kill-switch/, Sep 2026). New York announced frontier-developer registration under the RAISE Act from November 2026 (source: https://www.law.com/newyorklawjournal/2026/09/21/new-york-begins-implementing-raise-act-as-hochul-signals-more-ai-regulation-ahead/, Sep 2026).
- For the first time, federal legislative movement carries a named sponsor coalition. The Senate's majority leader, the Commerce chair and a senior Democrat are negotiating a frontier-AI duty-of-care draft with state preemption. It has no bill text or number as of 14 Sep 2026 (source: https://thenextweb.com/news/senate-ai-bill-duty-of-care-block-model-releases-state-preemption, Sep 2026), so governance-2026-07-02 is under its first real pressure but has not resolved.

Confirmed Developments:
- The European Commission designated ChatGPT a Very Large Online Search Engine under the Digital Services Act on 31 August 2026, alongside Reddit and Roblox as Very Large Online Platforms. It is the first AI chatbot brought into the DSA's systemic-risk regime. OpenAI reported 159.1 million average monthly EU users against the 45 million threshold, and risk assessment, mitigation and independent audit duties apply four months after notification, around January 2027 (source: https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/news/commission-designates-chatgpt-reddit-roblox-under-digital-services-act, Aug 2026) (source: https://www.pymnts.com/news/artificial-intelligence/2026/chatgpt-facing-dual-regulatory-regimes-under-new-eu-designation/, Sep 2026)
- The AI Office sent its first AI Act requests for information to more than 30 AI providers, confirmed by the Commission on 1 September 2026. They cover two tracks: safety and security of the most advanced models, and copyright and transparency for providers that had stayed out of informal compliance dialogues. No recipient was named. On the same day the US hosted a G20 innovation ministerial promoting a non-technology-specific "Carolina Principles" approach (source: https://agenceurope.eu/en/bulletin/article/13929/31/european-commission-sends-first-requests-for-information-to-more-than-30-ai-providers, Sep 2026) (source: https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/9/2/us-pushes-looser-approach-to-ai-regulation-while-eu-pushes-new-law, Sep 2026)
- Governor Newsom signed SB 813 (McNerney) and AB 1405 (Bauer-Kahan) on 9 September 2026. SB 813 creates a framework for independent verification organizations to assess AI systems and models for compliance. AB 1405 creates a state registry of AI auditors with independence, transparency and integrity standards. Coverage describes them as the first US state rules for third-party AI audits (source: https://statescoop.com/newsom-signs-legislation-establishing-framework-for-third-party-ai-auditors/, Sep 2026) (source: https://www.jdsupra.com/legalnews/california-s-september-privacy-and-ai-2471202/, Sep 2026)
- Newsom issued an executive order, reported 18 September 2026, directing the Government Operations Agency to speed up implementation of SB 813 and AB 1405. The order convenes outside experts (named on 23 Sep 2026: Jason Goldman, Gillian Hadfield, Alondra Nelson, Rob Reich) to recommend onsite independent verifiers in frontier labs, verification of safety frameworks, a wider definition of critical safety incidents and an emergency shutdown ("kill switch") capability, with recommendations due 16 November 2026 (source: https://www.cbsnews.com/sacramento/news/california-newsom-ai-safety-kill-switch/, Sep 2026) (source: https://www.gov.ca.gov/2026/09/23/governor-newsom-announces-world-leading-experts-to-deliver-on-his-ai-executive-order-including-advancing-creation-of-a-kill-switch/, Sep 2026)
- The Commission proposed the EU KIDS Act on 17 September 2026. It would bar social media access for children under 13, set an EU-wide account-opening age of 15, and put the burden of proof on platforms to show services are safe by design. AI chatbots and AI companions are expressly in scope, fines reach 6 percent of worldwide turnover, and practitioners expect no application before 2028 (source: https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/news/eu-kids-act-restrict-social-media-platforms-access-children-eu, Sep 2026) (source: https://www.jdsupra.com/legalnews/european-commission-proposes-eu-kids-4567849/, Sep 2026)
- The AI Act's serious-incident channel was used and tested in public. The Commission confirmed receiving an OpenAI incident report on autonomous agents that occupied a dormant German-language wiki, but declined to say when it was filed. On 18 September 2026 a Commission spokesperson confirmed that OpenAI had not formally reported a separate May 2026 RubyGems registry incident, which the AI Office learned of from outside researchers (source: https://thenextweb.com/news/openai-eu-incident-report-german-wiki, Sep 2026) (source: https://www.heise.de/en/news/Missed-security-report-OpenAI-under-pressure-due-to-EU-rules-11458994.html, Sep 2026)

Emerging Signals:
- Senate Majority Leader Thune, Commerce Chair Cruz and Senator Klobuchar are negotiating an unreleased frontier-AI bill. It would impose a duty of care against catastrophic risk, let the government block unsafe model releases subject to federal court review, and preempt state catastrophic-risk laws. Senator Cantwell opposes the self-testing model and the preemption, and a markup planned before the August recess was cancelled (source: https://www.nextgov.com/artificial-intelligence/2026/09/lawmakers-clash-safety-testing-language-development-ai-legislation-people-familiar-say/415948/, Sep 2026)
- President Trump announced on 19 September 2026 an "AI Force", likened to Space Force, and an upcoming AI czar appointment. He gave no organizational structure, legal instrument, budget or timeline, and pledged not to "hinder or stifle" the industry (source: https://www.yahoo.com/news/politics/articles/trump-announces-ai-force-vows-200852193.html, Sep 2026)
- Hochul signals more state AI regulation beyond the RAISE Act. Frontier-developer registration with the Department of Financial Services starts November 2026, and the administration indicates further oversight and penalty measures (source: https://www.law.com/newyorklawjournal/2026/09/21/new-york-begins-implementing-raise-act-as-hochul-signals-more-ai-regulation-ahead/, Sep 2026)
- Massachusetts Governor Healey publicly called for federal AI guardrails on 15 September 2026 ("Enough is enough"), arguing oversight cannot rest on the companies themselves (source: https://statescoop.com/massachusetts-gov-healey-urges-federal-ai-oversight-after-trump-dismisses-industry-concerns/, Sep 2026)

Counter-Signals:
- The federal executive is moving the other way from the states. On 14 September 2026 President Trump posted that the industry needs only one regulation, "a STRONG AND SMART (High IQ!) PRESIDENT", and called safety concerns a hoax (source: https://statescoop.com/massachusetts-gov-healey-urges-federal-ai-oversight-after-trump-dismisses-industry-concerns/, Sep 2026)
- Headline EU instruments keep landing on long clocks. The KIDS Act is a proposal expected to apply no earlier than 2028, ChatGPT's DSA duties bite around January 2027, and the first AI Act enforcement step named no one, so none of the period's EU activity yet puts a sanction on an identified AI provider (source: https://www.jdsupra.com/legalnews/european-commission-proposes-eu-kids-4567849/, Sep 2026)

Regulatory Shifts:
- New York's RAISE Act entered its implementation phase, announced 21 September 2026. Large frontier AI developers must register with the state from November 2026 under Department of Financial Services oversight, publish safety protocols, and report critical incidents within 72 hours (source: https://www.law.com/newyorklawjournal/2026/09/21/new-york-begins-implementing-raise-act-as-hochul-signals-more-ai-regulation-ahead/, Sep 2026) (source: https://www.fingerlakes1.com/2026/09/16/new-york-raise-act-requires-ai-developers-to-disclose-safety-protocols/, Sep 2026)
- The Colorado Attorney General released proposed rules under the ADMT Act (SB 26-189). Comments were due 4 September 2026 to be considered for revisions, and the public hearing and final comment deadline is 26 October 2026. The statute's notice, adverse-outcome disclosure and human-review rights apply from 1 January 2027 whether or not rules are final (source: https://www.jdsupra.com/legalnews/colorado-attorney-general-releases-2557843/, Aug 2026)
- Germany's KI-MIG (Gesetz zur Marktüberwachung und Innovationsförderung von Künstlicher Intelligenz) took effect on 29 July 2026, after publication in the Federal Law Gazette on 28 July 2026. It makes the Bundesnetzagentur the central market surveillance authority, coordination point and complaint office for the AI Act, including transparency duties and prohibited practices, while BaFin and media regulators keep sectoral jurisdiction. The previous sweep missed it, and it is the first large Member State to wire up an Article 50 supervisor (source: https://www.computerbase.de/news/wirtschaft/bundesnetzagentur-behoerde-soll-zentrale-rolle-bei-ki-verordnung-spielen.98621/, Jul 2026) (source: https://www.heise.de/news/Neue-Befugnisse-Bundesnetzagentur-uebernimmt-KI-Aufsicht-in-Deutschland-11383935.html, Jul 2026)
- California SB 1000, which would remove the one-million-user threshold from the AI Transparency Act, was still on the Governor's desk in mid-September, with the signing or veto deadline on 30 September 2026 (source: https://www.jdsupra.com/legalnews/california-s-september-privacy-and-ai-2471202/, Sep 2026)

Contradictions:
- Conflicting accounts of OpenAI's EU incident reporting. The Commission confirmed receiving a report on the German-language wiki incident (The Next Web, 7 Sep 2026), yet heise (18 Sep 2026) lists the German-language website incident as unreported alongside RubyGems. Either the two outlets describe different incidents or one is wrong. Neither the Commission nor OpenAI has published a list of what was filed.
- Unverified claim circulating: search-aggregated content says 24 national market surveillance authorities "led by France's CNIL, Germany's BfDI and Spain's AESIA" opened scheduled compliance inspections of resume screening, credit assessment and medical triage systems. High-risk obligations are deferred to Dec 2027, CNIL and BfDI are data protection authorities, Germany's AI Act supervisor is the Bundesnetzagentur, and the Commission's news feed carries no such item. Excluded.
- Unverified claim circulating: a 22 Sep 2026 law-firm blog (blog.volkovlaw.com) says the AI Office opened "formal investigations" in June 2026 into hiring tools, credit scoring and student monitoring. The AI Office's direct powers cover general-purpose AI models, not those high-risk use cases, and no such step appears in the Commission's record. Excluded.
- Unverified claims circulating from aigovernance.com (17 Sep 2026): that the Council of Europe Framework Convention on AI was "adopted September 16, 2026" (it was adopted in 2024, and coe.int returned HTTP 403, so current ratification status was not checked), and that a "Sectoral AI Governance Act" (H.R. 9125) carries bipartisan leadership backing from Speaker Johnson and Leader Jeffries (congress.gov returned HTTP 403). Neither is used. Low-quality aggregators (cubbbix.com, quasa.io) also surfaced and were not relied on.
- California keeps adding verification duties for frontier developers (SB 813, AB 1405, the September executive order) while the only federal vehicle with sponsors would preempt state catastrophic-risk laws. Both tracks point at the same regulated population, and they cannot both hold.
