# Evidence sweep, power, 2026-09-25

Delta Since Last Sweep:
- Texas escalated from named-company pledges (the 2026-08-13 sweep) to a statewide executive halt: after pausing ERCOT approvals for new data center grid connections on 3 Aug 2026, Governor Abbott on 21 Sep 2026 directed TCEQ to halt all data center permits until ERCOT and TWDB audits finish, with a TCEQ report due 19 Oct 2026. This runs alongside a PUCT SB 6 rule adopted in softened form (graded under power-2026-08-08) (source: https://gov.texas.gov/news/post/governor-abbott-directs-tceq-to-halt-data-center-permits, Sep 2026) (source: https://www.texastribune.org/2026/09/21/texas-data-center-moratorium-water-energy/, Sep 2026)
- The power constraint reached the balance sheet of a flagship Stargate tenant for the first time in this corpus. Oracle sent a force majeure notice to Blue Owl's Stack Infrastructure on Project Jupiter (2.45 GW, New Mexico) over potential power delays, and the $18B project loan is trading below 90 cents on the dollar. Earlier sweeps recorded grid limits as cancellations (power-2026-07-03), not as contractual distress at an operating project (source: https://www.thehindubusinessline.com/info-tech/oracle-invokes-force-majeure-over-new-mexico-data-centre-project-jupiter/article71505688.ece, Sep 2026)
- The federal cost-allocation track split by chamber: the House passed H.R. 9340 417-3 on 16 Sep 2026 (power-2026-08-02 confirmed), and the next day Sen. Heinrich blocked Sen. Husted's unanimous consent request. RTO/ISO show-cause responses have moved to mid-November after FERC's abeyance grants (source: https://www.newsweek.com/data-center-bill-hits-roadblock-senate-what-happens-next-12458535, Sep 2026) (source: https://www.utilitydive.com/news/ferc-exelon-comed-powerhouse-hillwood-data-center/831127/, Sep 2026)
- National-level siting pauses spread beyond the US for the first time in this corpus. The Scottish Parliament voted 80-26 on 16 Sep 2026 that no planning or consenting decisions on hyperscale data centres should be made until national guidance is complete (source: https://www.deadlinenews.co.uk/2026/09/17/msps-back-pause-on-hyperscale-data-centre-decisions-until-new-guidance-is-produced/, Sep 2026)

Confirmed Developments:
- Governor Abbott directed the Texas Commission on Environmental Quality on 21 Sep 2026 to halt all permits for data center projects until the ERCOT grid audit and the Texas Water Development Board water audit are complete, with TCEQ to report to the Governor by 19 Oct 2026. The order extends his 3 Aug 2026 pause on new data center grid connections and follows a 14 Sep 2026 directive to penalize missing water-use reports (source: https://gov.texas.gov/news/post/governor-abbott-directs-tceq-to-halt-data-center-permits, Sep 2026) (source: https://www.texastribune.org/2026/09/21/texas-data-center-moratorium-water-energy/, Sep 2026)
- Oracle sent a force majeure notice to Blue Owl's Stack Infrastructure unit on Project Jupiter, a 1,400-acre, 2.45 GW Stargate campus in southern New Mexico due online in 2028, citing potential delays in securing power. Oracle cannot terminate the lease but may defer higher operating-stage rent by up to three years. Oracle says the notice does not by itself establish a delay; Blue Owl says the financial commitments are unchanged (source: https://www.thehindubusinessline.com/info-tech/oracle-invokes-force-majeure-over-new-mexico-data-centre-project-jupiter/article71505688.ece, Sep 2026) (source: https://www.bnnbloomberg.ca/business/artificial-intelligence/2026/09/24/oracle-triggers-force-majeure-on-data-centre-project-over-power-delays-source-says/, Sep 2026)
- On 17 Sep 2026 Sen. Martin Heinrich, ranking member of Senate Energy and Natural Resources, blocked Sen. Jon Husted's unanimous consent request to pass the Ratepayer Protection Act (H.R. 9340) one day after the House passed it 417-3, arguing it does not go far enough in making data centers pay. The bill would create a PURPA standard for facilities of 100 MW or more to cover the full incremental cost of upgrades (source: https://www.newsweek.com/data-center-bill-hits-roadblock-senate-what-happens-next-12458535, Sep 2026) (source: https://www.yahoo.com/news/politics/articles/senate-democrat-blocks-ratepayer-protection-113911752.html, Sep 2026)
- MISO filed a Section 205 tariff proposal (ER26-3650) on 28 Aug 2026, after FERC placed its Section 206 proceeding (EL26-70) in abeyance on 14 Aug 2026. The filing covers visibility, PMU monitoring, ramp-rate and voltage/frequency ride-through requirements for large loads (over 50 MW) and computational loads (at least 25 MW of IT equipment), and requests a 4 Dec 2026 effective date (source: https://www.utilitydive.com/news/large-load-reliability-requirements-miso-ferc/829278/, Sep 2026) (source: https://groundtogrid.substack.com/p/when-large-load-solutions-meet-the, Sep 2026)
- The Scottish Parliament passed an amended motion 80-26 (15 abstentions) on 16 Sep 2026 stating that no planning or consenting decisions on hyperscale data centre applications should be made until new national planning guidance is complete. It rejected the Scottish Greens' formal moratorium. The Scottish Government separately made Environmental Impact Assessment mandatory for data centres above 50 MW and committed to guidance by the end of 2026 (source: https://www.deadlinenews.co.uk/2026/09/17/msps-back-pause-on-hyperscale-data-centre-decisions-until-new-guidance-is-produced/, Sep 2026) (source: https://uk.news.yahoo.com/msps-back-moratorium-hyperscale-ai-163800026.html, Sep 2026)
- On 22 Sep 2026 FERC rejected ComEd's notice cancelling a transmission security agreement for PowerHouse Hillwood's 1.8 GW, $20B Joliet, Illinois data center and deferred the contract dispute to the Northern District of Illinois. Commissioners used concurrences to attack the developer's $1 letter of credit as credit support and to call for standardized large load cost recovery terms (source: https://www.utilitydive.com/news/ferc-exelon-comed-powerhouse-hillwood-data-center/831127/, Sep 2026) (source: https://www.rtoinsider.com/146251-ferc-commissioners-trade-concurrences-collateral-data-center/, Sep 2026)

Emerging Signals:
- Capacity-conditioned access is spreading across RTOs: ISO-NE's response to the FERC show-cause order centres on "bring-your-own-new-capacity" requirements for new loads above 50 MW (source: https://www.rtoinsider.com/144063-iso-ne-details-large-load-proposal-restarts-order-1920-work/, Sep 2026)
- PJM moved its Interim Resource Adequacy Service and Reliability Backstop Procurement from concept to execution, holding participant training on 14 Sep 2026 and publishing execution materials on 16 Sep 2026 (source: https://groundtogrid.substack.com/p/when-large-load-solutions-meet-the, Sep 2026)
- Vendor financing of compute capacity is meeting antitrust friction from inside the vendor: Nvidia paused parts of its July 2026 revenue-sharing and rent-back program with AI cloud operators after internal staff flagged antitrust exposure and partners objected to restrictions on who could lease chips (reported by WSJ, not independently verified by the outlet) (source: https://thenextweb.com/news/nvidia-pauses-ai-cloud-revenue-sharing-deals, Aug 2026)

Counter-Signals:
- Local refusal is not a universal public stance: a representative Bitkom survey of 1,006 people in Germany found a majority would view a new data center in their own town positively, although 88 percent criticise its energy use (source: https://www.heise.de/news/Deutschland-will-neue-Rechenzentren-11438486.html, Sep 2026)
- The Jupiter notice may be defensive rather than a sign of project failure. Oracle calls force majeure notices "commonplace" at this scale, the notice is not an event of default, and Oracle stays liable for other costs and full-term rent (source: https://www.bnnbloomberg.ca/business/artificial-intelligence/2026/09/24/oracle-triggers-force-majeure-on-data-centre-project-over-power-delays-source-says/, Sep 2026)

Regulatory Shifts:
- The US Department of Justice reportedly opened an inquiry and sent Nvidia a formal information request over whether its $17-20B Groq licensing-plus-hiring deal (Dec 2025) was structured to avoid merger review. First reported by the New York Times on 9 Sep 2026 and confirmed by Reuters; DOJ has not publicly confirmed (source: https://thenextweb.com/news/doj-nvidia-groq-licensing-antitrust-review, Sep 2026) (source: https://www.techtarget.com/it-infrastructure/news/366650378/DOJ-reportedly-probes-Nvidia-Groq-deal-for-antitrust-concerns, Sep 2026)
- Scotland made Environmental Impact Assessment mandatory from 17 Sep 2026 for proposed data centres above 50 MW, and applications below the threshold are assessed case by case (source: https://news.stv.tv/politics/large-scale-data-centres-will-require-environmental-assessment-scottish-government-confirms, Sep 2026)
- After the abeyance grants, FERC's decision in the ComEd matter states that RTOs and ISOs have until mid-November 2026 to respond to the June large load show-cause orders, including proposals for pro forma Cost Recovery Agreements (source: https://www.utilitydive.com/news/ferc-exelon-comed-powerhouse-hillwood-data-center/831127/, Sep 2026)

Capital Movements:
- Project Jupiter's $18B construction loan from about 20 banks is trading at stressed levels below 90 cents on the dollar. A gauge of Oracle credit risk hit a record high on 24 Sep 2026, and Oracle shares fell about 4 percent to a 29 percent year-to-date decline (source: https://www.thehindubusinessline.com/info-tech/oracle-invokes-force-majeure-over-new-mexico-data-centre-project-jupiter/article71505688.ece, Sep 2026)
- Nvidia's pause on parts of its revenue-sharing and rent-back financing model for AI cloud operators removes a vendor-backed capital channel for smaller neoclouds. Nvidia says the model "is still in place" (source: https://thenextweb.com/news/nvidia-pauses-ai-cloud-revenue-sharing-deals, Aug 2026)

Technical Changes:
- MISO's proposal would make PMU monitoring, ramp-rate limits and voltage/frequency ride-through tariff conditions for computational loads, turning the 2026 load-loss events into interconnection engineering requirements (source: https://www.utilitydive.com/news/large-load-reliability-requirements-miso-ferc/829278/, Sep 2026)
- NERC's proposed computational load entity registration category covers facilities with 20 MW or more of aggregate connected load at 60 kV or above. FERC's 16 Jul 2026 order requires the first standards and Rules of Procedure changes by 31 Dec 2026 and phase-two filings by Mar 2027 (source: https://www.powermag.com/bringing-a-power-plants-worth-of-load-onto-the-grid-nercs-interconnection-reckoning/, Sep 2026)

Contradictions:
- Texas is halting state approvals by executive letter (ERCOT connections 3 Aug, TCEQ permits 21 Sep) while its utility regulator adopted a softened SB 6 interconnection rule in the same weeks. The binding regulatory channel loosened as the discretionary executive channel tightened.
- The DOJ Nvidia-Groq inquiry concerns AI compute (inference chips) but is framed as merger-review avoidance, not infrastructure concentration. This run's grader held power-2026-07-01 open. The next grader should decide explicitly whether a reported, DOJ-unconfirmed deal inquiry meets that claim's "formal antitrust inquiry" bar.
- Unverified claim circulating: aiandnews.com (30 May 2026) says the European Commission has opened an antitrust inquiry into exclusive cloud deals between hyperscalers and AI labs. It gives no case number, no named companies and no Commission source, and no reputable outlet corroborated it. Not used, and it should not be used to grade power-2026-07-01.
- Source contamination caught: an ad-hoc-news.de (boerse-global) German write-up framed the Scottish vote as a "12-Monats-Pause". Deadline News and the Yahoo UK/Newsquest report describe an open-ended pause until national guidance is complete, with no twelve-month term. Not used.
- The Senate block came from a Democrat arguing H.R. 9340 is too weak, not from opponents of cost allocation, so the bill's stall is not evidence of reduced political appetite for data center cost discipline.
