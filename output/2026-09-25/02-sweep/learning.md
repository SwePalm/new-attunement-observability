# Evidence sweep, learning, 2026-09-25

Delta Since Last Sweep:
- The direction of K-12 motion reversed inside the 43-day window. The previous sweep recorded institution-wide access expanding (NUS) and vendor tools shipping to students; this window's dominant K-12 events are restrictions by the two largest US districts, New York City (announced 2 Sep 2026) and Los Angeles Unified (reported 2 Sep 2026), both scoped to the 2026-27 school year (source: https://www.nyc.gov/mayors-office/news/2026/09/mayor-mamdani-and-chancellor-samuels-put-students-first-with-nat, Sep 2026) (source: https://www.edsurge.com/news/new-york-and-los-angeles-ban-student-facing-ai-for-now-should-other-schools-follow, Sep 2026)
- Florida moved from a stalled development-stage rule to board adoption: the State Board of Education approved both the K-12 amendment to Rule 6A-1.0957 and new college Rule 6A-14.0719 on 16 Sep 2026, resolving learning-2026-08-04 and learning-2026-08-05 (source: https://www.cfpublic.org/education/2026-09-16/florida-approves-statewide-ai-rules-for-k-12-schools-and-colleges, Sep 2026)
- The first large-scale randomized evidence on a production AI tutor arrived, but as a working paper rather than a peer-reviewed article: the two-year Khanmigo cluster trial (NBER w35620, Aug 2026), which keeps learning-2026-07-02 open (source: https://www.nber.org/papers/w35620, Aug 2026)
- Corpus gap from before the window, not captured by the previous sweep: Google's 4 Aug 2026 announcement making Gemini in Classroom available to students of all ages, rolled out on web 10 Aug and mobile 17 Aug 2026 (source: https://workspaceupdates.googleblog.com/2026/08/gemini-in-google-classroom-is-expanding-to-users-of-all-ages-with-contextualized-Gemini-starter-prompts-for-students.html, Aug 2026)

Confirmed Developments:
- New York City Public Schools imposed a one-year moratorium for 2026-27 on student-facing generative AI for grades 2-K to 8 (about 600,000 students), banned companion chatbots in all grades, and is disabling AI features in 38 existing citywide edtech contracts (including Amira and Google Gemini on student Chromebooks), with exceptions for assistive technology, multilingual learners and career programs and high school pilots limited to five tools (source: https://www.nyc.gov/mayors-office/news/2026/09/mayor-mamdani-and-chancellor-samuels-put-students-first-with-nat, Sep 2026) (source: https://www.chalkbeat.org/newyork/2026/09/02/what-to-know-about-nyc-public-schools-generative-ai-ban-screen-time-limits/, Sep 2026)
- Los Angeles Unified School District restricted student use of generative AI tools on school-issued devices across all grades for the 2026-27 school year, with a committee to develop recommendations for later years (source: https://www.edsurge.com/news/new-york-and-los-angeles-ban-student-facing-ai-for-now-should-other-schools-follow, Sep 2026) (source: https://www.route-fifty.com/artificial-intelligence/2026/09/largest-us-school-districts-restrict-student-ai-use/415866/, Sep 2026)
- The Florida State Board of Education on 16 Sep 2026 approved the Rule 6A-1.0957 amendment (parental notice and opt-out with non-AI alternatives, extra review for PreK-5, a ban on relationship-simulating AI, policies due 1 Jul 2027) and new Rule 6A-14.0719 requiring the 28 Florida College System institutions to bar student AI use on graded assignments unless the instructor permits it (source: https://www.cfpublic.org/education/2026-09-16/florida-approves-statewide-ai-rules-for-k-12-schools-and-colleges, Sep 2026) (source: https://www.clickorlando.com/news/2026/09/16/florida-board-adds-ai-guidelines-for-schools-and-colleges-what-you-need-to-know/, Sep 2026)
- A two-year cluster-randomized trial of Khanmigo in 18 Hamilton County, Tennessee middle schools found gains of about 0.06 to 0.08 SD per school year, similar to Khan Academy practice without AI, with 96 percent of students trying the tutor but the median student messaging it on only a third of practice days (source: https://www.nber.org/papers/w35620, Aug 2026) (source: https://www.chalkbeat.org/2026/08/25/ai-tutoring-students-khanmigo-khan-academy-engagement-study/, Aug 2026)
- The University of Sydney announced on 14 Sep 2026 a multi-year OpenAI partnership giving all students and staff ChatGPT Edu (staff from Oct 2026, students from Semester 1 2027), five days after UNSW announced ChatGPT Edu for its 80,000 students and staff with full student access planned before the 2027 academic year (source: https://www.sydney.edu.au/news-opinion/news/2026/09/14/students-staff-to-receive-free-chatgpt-edu-access.html, Sep 2026) (source: https://www.unsw.edu.au/newsroom/news/2026/09/unsw-partners-with-openai-to-support-the-first-generation-of-ai-, Sep 2026)
- NTEU members at the University of Sydney held a 24-hour strike on 2 Sep 2026 in part over AI safeguards in the enterprise agreement, while the university stated AI should be governed by updatable policy rather than the three-year agreement (source: https://www.sydney.edu.au/news-opinion/news/2026/09/02/university-of-sydney-response-to-industrial-action.html, Sep 2026) (source: https://au.news.yahoo.com/staff-major-uni-strike-over-082737478.html, Aug 2026)

Emerging Signals:
- 24 of 45 Chicago Board of Education candidates signed a pledge for a three-year moratorium on generative AI in Chicago Public Schools ahead of the first fully elected 21-member board race on 3 Nov 2026; CPS separately shelved plans to give high school students Gemini access (source: https://chicago.suntimes.com/education/2026/09/10/chicago-school-board-candidates-sign-pledge-to-ban-ai-use-in-schools, Sep 2026)
- Stanford's SCALE Initiative published a research brief mapping AI tutoring models on a human-involvement spectrum, finding strong evidence only where humans remain central and citing one study in which 40 to 47 percent of students never used the AI platform (source: https://www.edweek.org/technology/when-does-ai-help-most-with-tutoring-what-emerging-research-says/2026/08, Aug 2026)
- Unions and student bodies are contesting procurement itself: the NTEU and the Sydney SRC said neither was consulted before the OpenAI deal was signed (source: https://honisoit.com/2026/09/breaking-usyd-signs-multi-year-partnership-with-openai/, Sep 2026)

Counter-Signals:
- South Korea's AI digital textbooks, downgraded to optional "educational material" in 2025, were used by only 622 schools (5.2 percent) in the first semester of 2026, down from 4,095 (34.2 percent) a year earlier, per Ministry of Education data released to lawmaker Baek Seung-ah, with half of remaining users in Daegu (source: https://www.khan.co.kr/article/202609211632001/, Sep 2026)
- Against the K-12 restriction wave, Google's default-on expansion put Gemini in Classroom in front of students of all ages wherever districts had not already disabled it, and districts learned of it from news coverage (source: https://www.edweek.org/technology/schools-caught-flat-footed-after-google-makes-gemini-chatbot-available-to-all-students/2026/09, Sep 2026)

Regulatory Shifts:
- Florida Rule 6A-14.0719 is the first system-level rule located that sets a default prohibition on student AI use in graded college work, applying to 28 institutions serving more than 700,000 students (source: https://www.cfpublic.org/education/2026-09-16/florida-approves-statewide-ai-rules-for-k-12-schools-and-colleges, Sep 2026)
- Maryland's 120-day window under the Artificial Intelligence Ready Schools Act (SB 720, effective 1 Jun 2026) closes this autumn; MACo reports many systems compliant or updating, and the new Maryland AI Education Collaborative is not expected to report until 1 Dec 2027 (source: https://conduitstreet.mdcounties.org/2026/09/10/deadline-for-ai-policies-in-local-schools-approaches-in-maryland/, Sep 2026)

Capital Movements:
- London-based Medly AI raised 8 million dollars in a seed round led by Felix Capital for exam-preparation AI tutoring (GCSE, A-Level, IB), reporting more than 400,000 UK users (source: https://www.vestbee.com/insights/articles/medly-ai-lands-8-m, Aug 2026)
- The University of Sydney paired its OpenAI deal with an AUD 1 million AI Sustainability Research and Innovation Fund (source: https://www.sydney.edu.au/news-opinion/news/2026/09/14/students-staff-to-receive-free-chatgpt-edu-access.html, Sep 2026)

Technical Changes:
- Gemini in Google Classroom became on by default for students of all ages (web 10 Aug, mobile 17 Aug 2026) except where admins had disabled it; restricting minors requires admins to build a separate organizational unit (source: https://workspaceupdates.googleblog.com/2026/08/gemini-in-google-classroom-is-expanding-to-users-of-all-ages-with-contextualized-Gemini-starter-prompts-for-students.html, Aug 2026)
- NYC is implementing its moratorium at the contract layer, switching off AI components inside 38 approved products rather than blocking whole platforms, while allowing five tools (Quill, Edia, Brisk Teaching, Playlab, Intel AI-Ready Schools) in high school pilots (source: https://www.chalkbeat.org/newyork/2026/09/02/what-to-know-about-nyc-public-schools-generative-ai-ban-screen-time-limits/, Sep 2026)

Contradictions:
- Higher education and K-12 moved in opposite directions in the same fortnight: Sydney and UNSW committed to all-student frontier-model access while NYC and LAUSD removed student-facing generative AI from classrooms.
- Aggregate Korean adoption collapsed while one vendor reported growth: Bisang Education said schools using its English and math AI materials rose about 39 percent from first to second semester 2026 on its own contracted-school data, which is not comparable to the ministry's national count (source: https://www.fnnews.com/news/202609220857114392, Sep 2026)
- Source disagreement on the Chicago pledge count: Chalkbeat (1 Sep 2026) reported 23 of 45 candidates, the Sun-Times (10 Sep 2026) 24 of 45, and search-engine summaries of other outlets 24 of 42; Route Fifty says only "approximately half". The later Sun-Times figure is used above (source: https://www.chalkbeat.org/chicago/2026/09/01/chicago-school-board-candidates-pledge-three-year-ai-ban/, Sep 2026)
- A search-engine summary described Boston Public Schools' AI literacy program as a mandate launched Sep 2026; the underlying WBUR and GBH reports are dated Mar 2026 and a city spokesperson said it is not yet a requirement. Not used.
- Unverified: a Sep 2026 claim that the Maryland deadline falls "sometime this month" (MACo) conflicts with a local report of 22 Oct 2026 used in grading; neither is an MSDE primary statement.
