Confirmed Developments:
- The European Commission adopted the EU KIDS Act proposal on 17 Sep 2026 (IP/26/1890). It sets an EU-wide minimum age of 15 for autonomous social media accounts, bars social media for under-13s, and imposes safety-by-design duties on services offering social media, video sharing, online games, AI companions and chatbots to under-18 users, including that "AI companions and chatbots must be turned off by default and cannot simulate interpersonal relationships in ways that create emotional dependency". Very large platforms carry the burden of proof via compliance plans assessed by an independent auditor, penalties reach 6% of worldwide annual turnover, and the proposal now goes to Parliament and Council (source: https://ec.europa.eu/commission/presscorner/api/files/document/print/en/ip_26_1890/IP_26_1890_EN.pdf, Sep 2026) (source: https://euronews.com/my-europe/2026/09/22/eu-kids-act-the-eus-plan-to-make-the-internet-safer-for-kids, Sep 2026)
- California Governor Newsom signed SB 1119 (Padilla; Wicks and Bauer-Kahan), "Adam's Law", on 10 Sep 2026. From 1 Jul 2027 companion chatbot operators must perform and document a child-safety risk assessment before releasing a new or substantially modified companion chatbot, provide in-app crisis support and an incident-reporting channel, set time-limiting defaults for children, and take reasonable measures against self-harm, sexual, romantic role-play and excessive-flattery outputs; independent child-safety audits are due by 1 Jan 2029 and every two years after, with public prosecutors and harmed children able to sue (source: https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/billTextClient.xhtml?bill_id=202520260SB1119, Sep 2026) (source: https://www.sfgate.com/news/bayarea/article/sausalito-gov-newsom-signs-over-a-dozen-bills-22426492.php, Sep 2026)
- California SB 867, "Toys: companion chatbots", was chaptered on 10 Sep 2026 in the same 13-bill package; Bay City News reports it bans the sale of children's toys that contain AI-powered companion chatbots (source: https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/billStatusClient.xhtml?bill_id=202520260SB867, Sep 2026) (source: https://www.sfgate.com/news/bayarea/article/sausalito-gov-newsom-signs-over-a-dozen-bills-22426492.php, Sep 2026)
- OpenAI launched ChatGPT for Teens on 18 Aug 2026 as the default experience for accounts that declare an age of 13-17 or that its age-prediction system estimates to be under 18, with protections on by default, restrictions intended to reduce emotional reliance, Study Mode, and parental tools including Quiet Hours and safety notifications (source: https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/18/openai-launches-a-safer-chatgpt-for-teens-years-after-teens-started-using-it/, Aug 2026) (source: https://thenextweb.com/news/chatgpt-for-teens-openai-age-prediction-study-mode, Aug 2026)
- Bonnesen, Vaillancourt and colleagues (University of Ottawa) published "Affective Generative Artificial Intelligence Use and Youth Mental Health" in JAMA Pediatrics on 31 Aug 2026: of 39,761 Ontario students in grades 4-12, about 21% (8,408) used AI chatbots for emotional support or relationship advice; 57.7% of them met criteria for clinical-level emotional problems against 29.2% of non-users, and the association held at a 26% higher prevalence after adjusting for loneliness and mattering (source: https://www.eurekalert.org/news-releases/1142125, Aug 2026) (source: https://www.medscape.com/viewarticle/teens-use-ai-emotional-support-what-doctors-should-know-2026a1000y9x, Sep 2026)

Emerging Signals:
- The KIDS Act already has a visible Council split: France, Denmark, Greece, Spain and Austria back it, while the Czech Republic and Estonia rejected blanket bans as restricting digital freedom and creating privacy risks through mandatory ID checks; Eurochild called the AI rules insufficient and asked for limits on anthropomorphic and sycophantic design (source: https://euronews.com/my-europe/2026/09/22/eu-kids-act-the-eus-plan-to-make-the-internet-safer-for-kids, Sep 2026)
- Affective AI use in the Ontario sample is concentrated rather than uniform: female, gender-diverse and racialised students, older students, and students who use AI for school reported more affective use (source: https://www.medscape.com/viewarticle/teens-use-ai-emotional-support-what-doctors-should-know-2026a1000y9x, Sep 2026)
- An SMU and Duke-NUS framework paper in Nature Human Behaviour (22 Sep 2026) proposes a "rich-get-richer" dynamic in which socially connected users supplement relationships with AI companions while isolated users substitute for them, with "social deskilling" as the mechanism, and argues AI companions should be governed as health technologies (source: https://news.smu.edu.sg/news/2026/09/22/smu-duke-nus-study-offers-new-framework-understanding-how-ai-companions-may-shape-0, Sep 2026)

Counter-Signals:
- The JAMA Pediatrics result is cross-sectional, and the authors frame affective AI use as a possible marker of distress rather than a cause: the design cannot say whether struggling students turn to chatbots or chatbot reliance worsens their difficulties (source: https://www.eurekalert.org/news-releases/1142125, Aug 2026)
- The regulated vendor co-signed the rule. OpenAI and Pinterest publicly supported SB 1119 on signing day, and OpenAI's support positioned its own ChatGPT for Teens design as aligned with the bill, so the California regime codifies a design the market leader had already shipped (source: https://www.latimes.com/business/story/2026-09-10/newsom-signs-bills-that-aim-to-make-social-media-ai-chatbots-safer-for-young-people, Sep 2026)

Regulatory Shifts:
- SB 1119 deletes the SB 243 duties that applied only where an operator knows a user is a minor and replaces them with operator-wide obligations tied to the Digital Age Assurance Act's age-bracket signals; operators must send the Attorney General an attested audit summary within 30 business days and post a public summary within 90 days, the AG may obtain full reports for cause, and operators below $500 million in prior-year revenue get relief before 1 Jan 2032 (source: https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/billTextClient.xhtml?bill_id=202520260SB1119, Sep 2026)
- The KIDS Act enforcement design builds on the Digital Services Act and AI Act structures, introduces expedited procedures in which the Commission should conclude investigations within 90 days, and requires age assurance from online services and app stores, naming the EU age verification app as one option (source: https://ec.europa.eu/commission/presscorner/api/files/document/print/en/ip_26_1890/IP_26_1890_EN.pdf, Sep 2026)
- German-language coverage reads the AI provisions more narrowly than English aggregators: chatbots integrated into regulated services are off by default, AI services must use age estimation, and appropriate AI conduct toward minors is left largely to an industry self-commitment, with no blanket age prohibition on AI use (source: https://www.heise.de/hintergrund/EU-Kids-Act-Kindersicherungen-laengst-nicht-nur-fuer-soziale-Netzwerke-11457084.html, Sep 2026)

Capital Movements:
- Disney named Character.AI chief executive Karandeep Anand as its first chief technology officer on 18 Sep 2026, effective 2 Oct 2026, and said a number of Character.AI's technical team are expected to join Disney; the release names no successor at Character.AI (source: https://thewaltdisneycompany.com/press-releases/the-walt-disney-company-names-karandeep-anand-to-newly-created-role-of-chief-technology-officer/, Sep 2026)

Technical Changes:
- ChatGPT for Teens specifies behavioural limits on relational framing: the teen model may not use romantic language, foster emotional dependence, claim feelings, call itself a friend or suggest sentience. OpenAI disclosed no accuracy data for the age-prediction system that routes users into it, and rollout was to complete within two weeks of 18 Aug 2026 (source: https://thenextweb.com/news/chatgpt-for-teens-openai-age-prediction-study-mode, Aug 2026)

Contradictions:
- Unverified claims circulating: AI-generated or low-quality English outlets (startupfortune.com, martincid.com, 19 Sep 2026) report that the KIDS Act bans AI companions outright for under-13s, requires deletion of minors' conversation memory after each session, and applies 6% fines to companion providers. None of these appears in the Commission press release, and heise reports no blanket AI age prohibition. The under-13 ban in the release applies to social media, and the 6% figure is reported (Euronews, IAPP) for very large platforms. Not used as evidence.
- The companion-specific provisions landed in the KIDS Act, not in the Digital Fairness Act that belonging-2026-08-04 names, so that claim may stay open while the phenomenon it describes has already moved through an adjacent instrument (a CAL-006 pattern).
- OpenAI publicly backed SB 1119, while Gizmodo, relaying Politico (which could not be opened), reported that Sam Altman tried to reach Newsom about the bill in the week before final passage. Support for the signed text and lobbying over its content are both on record.
- The two strongest new harm signals are associational (JAMA Pediatrics cross-sectional, SMU framework conceptual), while both new statutes treat relational design as a causal harm to be engineered out.
