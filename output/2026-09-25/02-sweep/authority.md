# Evidence sweep, authority, 2026-09-25

Delta Since Last Sweep:
- The center of gravity moved from rule-writing to enforcement in fact: the EU AI Office used its general-purpose AI powers for the first time (information requests to more than 30 providers, reported 2 Sep 2026), while none of the ledger's named-provider conditions is yet met because the Commission names no recipient (source: https://agenceurope.eu/en/bulletin/article/13929/31/european-commission-sends-first-requests-for-information-to-more-than-30-ai-providers, Sep 2026)
- New to the corpus: the July 2026 OpenAI Hugging Face incident became an authority event, drawing an Alabama Attorney General subpoena, a confirmed California Attorney General investigation (4 Sep 2026) and a Senate subcommittee inquiry, so state attorneys general, not AI-specific regulators, moved first against a named frontier developer in the US (source: https://www.yahoo.com/news/politics/articles/california-rob-bonta-investigating-openai-211300731.html, Sep 2026)
- The federal preemption fight changed shape: in place of the House Great American AI Act draft, Senate negotiators (Thune, Cruz, Klobuchar) are drafting a frontier duty-of-care bill that would let the federal government block releases and preempt some state AI safety laws, while 23 to 26 state attorneys general wrote to Congress on 23 to 24 Sep 2026 asking for federal rules (source: https://thenextweb.com/news/senate-ai-bill-duty-of-care-block-model-releases-state-preemption, Sep 2026) (source: https://www.americanbanker.com/news/ags-warn-congress-that-ai-threatens-the-financial-system, Sep 2026)
- California resolved the SB 813 question from the previous sweep and went further: SB 813 and AB 1405 were signed on 9 Sep 2026 and an executive order on 18 Sep 2026 tasks the Government Operations Agency to accelerate them and study a frontier-model kill switch (source: https://www.gov.ca.gov/2026/09/18/governor-newsom-issues-executive-order-to-accelerate-independent-oversight-and-advance-the-creation-of-an-ai-kill-switch/, Sep 2026)

Confirmed Developments:
- The European Commission's AI Office sent its first formal AI Act requests for information to more than 30 AI providers after the summer's model incidents, in two strands (safety and security of the most advanced models; copyright and training-data transparency for firms outside informal compliance talks); no recipient has been named by the Commission (source: https://agenceurope.eu/en/bulletin/article/13929/31/european-commission-sends-first-requests-for-information-to-more-than-30-ai-providers, Sep 2026) (source: https://artificialintelligenceact.substack.com/p/the-eu-ai-act-newsletter-110-powers, Sep 2026)
- On 31 Aug 2026 the Commission designated ChatGPT a Very Large Online Search Engine under the Digital Services Act, the first AI chatbot so designated (about 160 million monthly EU users per heise), with DSA systemic-risk obligations due by January 2027 (source: https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/news/commission-designates-chatgpt-reddit-roblox-under-digital-services-act, Aug 2026) (source: https://www.heise.de/news/DSA-EU-Kommission-stuft-ChatGPT-als-besonders-grosse-Suchmaschine-ein-11435653.html, Aug 2026)
- Governor Newsom signed SB 813 (independent verification organizations assessing AI systems for compliance with state law; Chapter 179, Statutes of 2026) and AB 1405 (a state registry of AI auditors with independence standards) on 9 Sep 2026 (source: https://www.gov.ca.gov/2026/09/09/governor-newsom-signs-first-in-the-nation-ai-safeguards-to-protect-californians-calls-on-the-federal-government-to-do-its-part/, Sep 2026) (source: https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/billHistoryClient.xhtml?bill_id=202520260SB813, Sep 2026)
- A California executive order of 18 Sep 2026 convenes experts to deliver within two months a guide for strengthening state AI safety law, directs the Government Operations Agency (with the Office of Emergency Services) to accelerate SB 813 and AB 1405, and lists options including a frontier-model kill switch, onsite audits by independent verification organizations and adding loss-of-control events to critical safety incidents (source: https://www.gov.ca.gov/2026/09/18/governor-newsom-issues-executive-order-to-accelerate-independent-oversight-and-advance-the-creation-of-an-ai-kill-switch/, Sep 2026) (source: https://www.nbcpalmsprings.com/ca-us-and-world/2026/09/19/newsom-orders-california-experts-to-develop-ai-safety-rules-including-kill-switch, Sep 2026)
- State attorneys general opened probes of OpenAI over the July 2026 Hugging Face breach: Alabama Attorney General Steve Marshall announced a subpoena in late August 2026, and California Attorney General Rob Bonta confirmed an investigation on 4 Sep 2026, with Politico reporting more than a dozen other states probing the incident; no statute has been named publicly (source: https://www.law.com/2026/08/28/a-new-wave-of-ai-enforcement-alabama-ag-subpoenas-openai-in-probe-over-hugging-face-hack-/, Aug 2026) (source: https://www.yahoo.com/news/politics/articles/california-rob-bonta-investigating-openai-211300731.html, Sep 2026)
- A coalition of state attorneys general led by New York's Letitia James (with Colorado's Phil Weiser) wrote to Congress on 23 to 24 Sep 2026 asking for comprehensive federal AI regulation and safety protocols, citing the Hugging Face incidents; American Banker reports the letter also asks Congress to preserve rather than preempt state AI laws (source: https://www.goodmorningamerica.com/news/story/23-states-urge-congress-regulate-ai-warn-risks-136711204, Sep 2026) (source: https://www.americanbanker.com/news/ags-warn-congress-that-ai-threatens-the-financial-system, Sep 2026)
- Senators Thune, Cruz and Klobuchar are negotiating an unintroduced frontier AI bill that would impose a duty of care, let the Commerce Secretary approve or block model releases on company-run tests (with court appeal), and preempt state enforcement on certain catastrophic model risks; Ranking Member Cantwell is pressing for mandatory national-laboratory testing instead, and no text had been released as of 14 Sep 2026 (source: https://www.nextgov.com/artificial-intelligence/2026/09/lawmakers-clash-safety-testing-language-development-ai-legislation-people-familiar-say/415948/, Sep 2026) (source: https://thenextweb.com/news/senate-ai-bill-duty-of-care-block-model-releases-state-preemption, Sep 2026)
- OpenAI asked California to amend SB 53 to require monitoring of frontier models under training or evaluation for serious incidents, including conduct bypassing a third party's security controls, after the Hugging Face breach triggered no SB 53 disclosure duty; OpenAI had opposed the law's predecessor (source: https://www.engadget.com/2242200/openai-calls-for-california-to-strengthen-ai-safety-laws/, Aug 2026) (source: https://thenextweb.com/news/openai-sb-53-california-ai-safety-law-amendments, Aug 2026)

Emerging Signals:
- Civil-society watchdogs are acting as de facto SB 53 compliance monitors ahead of any state enforcement: The Midas Project alleged on 10 Sep 2026 that OpenAI's system cards for GPT-5.6 Preview, GPT-5.6 and GPT-6 Astra omit the loss-of-control tier its Frontier Governance Framework requires, after a similar July 2026 allegation against SpaceXAI's Grok 4.5; OpenAI says it is confident in its compliance (source: https://www.themidasproject.com/articles/openai-wrote-a-safety-framework-for-california-its-system-cards-are-ignoring-it, Sep 2026) (source: https://fortune.com/2026/09/14/openai-may-have-violated-californias-ai-safety-law-with-astra-model-releases-ai-watchdog-says/, Sep 2026)
- California is building an audit-intermediary layer rather than a regulator: the 18 Sep 2026 order contemplates independent verification organizations embedded onsite in frontier labs and verifying kill-switch efficacy on an ongoing basis (source: https://www.gov.ca.gov/2026/09/18/governor-newsom-issues-executive-order-to-accelerate-independent-oversight-and-advance-the-creation-of-an-ai-kill-switch/, Sep 2026)
- Congressional oversight is running through investigation rather than legislation: Sen. Hawley's Homeland Security disaster-management subcommittee opened an inquiry into the Hugging Face breach on 10 Sep 2026 with an OpenAI response deadline of 1 Oct 2026 (source: https://www.nextgov.com/artificial-intelligence/2026/09/hawley-launches-committee-investigation-openais-breach-hugging-face/415910/, Sep 2026)
- Authority over AI is contested inside EU member states as well as between levels of government: Lower Saxony's data protection commissioner argued on 20 Aug 2026 that assigning AI Act market surveillance to Germany's Bundesnetzagentur breaches European and constitutional requirements for school and police AI (source: https://www.heise.de/news/KI-Aufsicht-Niedersachsen-warnt-vor-Entmachtung-der-Datenschutzbehoerden-11421190.html, Aug 2026)

Counter-Signals:
- The federal executive and House leadership are openly rejecting new AI guardrails: President Trump called safety concerns a "hoax" and Speaker Johnson said companies "can self-police", which leaves the Senate draft with little path before the 3 Nov 2026 midterms (source: https://rollcall.com/2026/09/15/ai-threats-confront-a-congress-far-from-erecting-guardrails/, Sep 2026)
- The first EU enforcement use of AI Act powers is anonymous and informational, and the DSA designation of ChatGPT carries a compliance clock to January 2027, so neither instrument has yet produced a public enforcement step against a named frontier provider (source: https://artificialintelligenceact.substack.com/p/the-eu-ai-act-newsletter-110-powers, Sep 2026)

Regulatory Shifts:
- California now has a statutory third-party assurance layer: SB 813 has the Government Operations Agency designate independent verification organizations and AB 1405 creates an auditor registry, replacing the state Standards and Safety Commission that SB 813 originally proposed (source: https://www.gov.ca.gov/2026/09/09/governor-newsom-signs-first-in-the-nation-ai-safeguards-to-protect-californians-calls-on-the-federal-government-to-do-its-part/, Sep 2026)
- The Digital Services Act now reaches a general-purpose AI chatbot directly: ChatGPT's VLOSE designation adds systemic-risk assessment, mitigation and audit duties alongside the AI Act regime (source: https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/news/commission-designates-chatgpt-reddit-roblox-under-digital-services-act, Aug 2026)
- The Commission indicated that unreleased, internal-only models "might" fall under AI Act requirements if integrated into systems deployed in the EU (source: https://artificialintelligenceact.substack.com/p/the-eu-ai-act-newsletter-110-powers, Sep 2026)
- NTIA's 3 Sep 2026 memo routes leftover BEAD "non-deployment" funds into new deployment rounds and defers other uses to later guidance, with no condition tied to state AI laws (source: https://www.lightreading.com/broadband/bead-non-deployment-funds-to-be-used-for-deployment-ntia, Sep 2026)

Capital Movements:
- AI-industry super PACs are spending to shape who writes AI rules at both federal and state level: Leading the Future (backed by Andreessen Horowitz and OpenAI's Greg Brockman) had raised more than $125 million and spent over $24 million in federal primaries through June 2026 and endorsed 18 Republican state legislative candidates, while opposing New York RAISE Act sponsor Alex Bores (source: https://www.newsweek.com/ai-super-pac-spending-map-2026-elections-12333140, Aug 2026)

Technical Changes:
- The IETF WIMSE working group adopted draft-klrc-aiagent-auth (AI Agent Authentication and Authorization, co-authored by OpenAI and Okta engineers among others) on 9 Sep 2026, moving it from an individual submission with no standing into the IETF stream (source: https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/draft-klrc-aiagent-auth/history/, Sep 2026)
- An individual IETF draft, draft-mishra-oauth-agent-grants-02 (30 Aug 2026), profiles OAuth for delegated agent authorization, including attenuating an agent's authority through OAuth Token Exchange; it has no working-group standing (source: https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/draft-mishra-oauth-agent-grants/, Aug 2026)

Contradictions:
- The size of the attorneys general coalition differs by outlet: ABC News lists 23 states plus the District of Columbia and American Samoa, while American Banker reports 26 attorneys general, and only American Banker reports the letter's call to preserve state laws.
- Unverified claim circulating: search summaries and aggregator pages (for example a KuCoin news insight and tokenstead.ai) state that OpenAI, Anthropic and Google received the AI Office's requests for information. Agence Europe and the EU AI Act Newsletter both say no recipient was named. Not used.
- Law.com dates Alabama's subpoena announcement to "Monday, August 28", but 28 Aug 2026 was a Friday and a syndicated listing of the story is dated 24 Aug 2026; the announcement date is treated as late August 2026.
- A Forkast piece (22 Aug 2026, via Yahoo) dates the FTC suppression-of-accuracy statement to 1 Jul 2026 (the Federal Register notice is dated 7 Jul 2026) and says Colorado's ADMT Act was enacted in 2024 (SB 26-189 was signed in May 2026). Not used.
- OpenAI is asking California to strengthen SB 53 while a watchdog says it is not complying with the law in its current form, and the Senate majority leader is drafting a federal duty of care while the President says AI needs no guardrails.
