# Evidence sweep, perception, 2026-09-25

Delta Since Last Sweep:
- Anthropic moved from announced to operational. The 2026-08-13 sweep recorded marking as documented but detection as forthcoming; on 1 Sep 2026 Claude Fable 5.1 and Mythos 5.1 shipped as the first Claude models to watermark text and file outputs, and a detection API opened in private preview for eligible third parties (source: https://www.pcworld.com/article/3224834/claudes-updated-fable-and-mythos-models-watermark-their-replies.html, Sep 2026) (source: https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-text-watermark, Sep 2026)
- First platform-imposed distribution limit tied to AI disclosure in this theme's record: on 31 Aug 2026 Instagram replaced its "AI creator" label with "AI-generated profile" and said undisclosed AI-generated profiles will be withheld from recommendations to non-followers (source: https://www.cnet.com/tech/services-and-software/instagram-ai-generated-profile-label-fake-influencer-profiles-2/, Aug 2026)
- Apple slipped: iOS 27 reached general release on 14 Sep 2026 without SynthID, which Apple now places in a software update later in 2026; the iOS 27.2 beta (16 Sep 2026) feature list reported by Macworld does not name SynthID (source: https://www.macworld.com/article/2986799/ios-27-features-compatiblity-siri-ai-updates.html, Sep 2026)
- Static layers and a gap closed: the European AI Board's 17 Sep 2026 plenary set no new Article 50 deadline and no enforcement action (source: https://labs.cloudsecurityalliance.org/research/csa-research-note-eu-ai-act-article50-watermarking-deadline/, Sep 2026); C2PA published nothing after its 31 Jul 2026 implementation guide; and California's AI Transparency Act (SB 942 as amended by AB 853) became operative on 2 Aug 2026, an item absent from both August sweeps (source: https://www.morganlewis.com/pubs/2026/08/new-california-ai-disclosure-rules-become-operative, Aug 2026)

Confirmed Developments:
- Instagram introduced an "AI-generated profile" badge beneath the handle and on feed posts, Reels and Stories of accounts built around AI-generated people, replacing "AI creator", and will limit the reach of undisclosed AI-generated profiles by restricting them from Reels, Explore and in-feed suggestions to non-followers until the label is added; Meta said detection and notification of unlabeled profiles would follow in the coming weeks, and CNET found none of six prominent AI influencer accounts labeled on 31 Aug 2026 (source: https://www.cnet.com/tech/services-and-software/instagram-ai-generated-profile-label-fake-influencer-profiles-2/, Aug 2026) (source: https://www.business-standard.com/technology/tech-news/instagram-tightens-ai-profile-rules-targets-undisclosed-virtual-personas-126090100321_1.html, Sep 2026)
- Anthropic's first post-2 Aug 2026 models, Claude Fable 5.1 (generally available) and Mythos 5.1 (trusted access), launched on 1 Sep 2026 watermarking text and file outputs, and Anthropic opened a watermark detection API in private preview for regulators, law enforcement, media, fact-checkers, independent researchers, educational organizations, EU civil society groups and obligated enterprises, with access to be expanded over time (source: https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-text-watermark, Sep 2026) (source: https://www.pcworld.com/article/3224834/claudes-updated-fable-and-mythos-models-watermark-their-replies.html, Sep 2026)
- South Korea's National Assembly Science, ICT, Broadcasting and Communications Committee bill-review subcommittee took up AI Basic Act and Network Act amendments on 15 Sep 2026 that would specify visible or invisible watermarks on AI outputs and sanction tampering (Kim Dae-sik bill: up to 2 years' prison or KRW 20M fine; Lee Sang-hwi bill: administrative fine up to KRW 30M), plus platform duties to preserve marks, and deferred all of them to continued review in the October regular session (source: https://www.msn.com/ko-kr/%EC%9D%BC%EB%B0%98/%EC%9D%BC%EB%B0%98/ai%EB%A1%9C-%EB%A7%8C%EB%93%A4%EB%A9%B4-%ED%91%9C%EC%8B%9C-%EB%96%BC%EB%A9%B4-%EC%B2%98%EB%B2%8C-ai-%EC%9B%8C%ED%84%B0%EB%A7%88%ED%81%AC%EB%B2%95-%EA%B5%AD%ED%9A%8C-%EB%85%BC%EC%9D%98-%EB%8F%8C%EC%9E%85/ar-AA2cf5TU, Sep 2026) (source: https://www.imaeil.com/page/view/2026092412193369072, Sep 2026)
- Lasso Security published "The Provenance Tax" on 17 Sep 2026, reporting that SynthID-Text watermarking (the scheme Claude's text watermark is based on), tested in its non-distortionary configuration through Hugging Face's unmodified processor, changed refusal behavior on HarmBench prompts and, under a fixed prompt-injection technique, made several open-weight models more likely to answer harmful requests they otherwise refused; it named the effect "sampling drift" (source: https://arstechnica.com/security/2026/09/ai-text-watermarking-can-make-models-more-vulnerable-to-adversarial-prompts/, Sep 2026) (source: https://www.unite.ai/lasso-study-finds-text-watermarking-shifts-llm-refusals-and-tool-calls/, Sep 2026)

Emerging Signals:
- The marking obligation is migrating toward distribution platforms: Korean Network Act amendments by Jo In-cheol and Park Choong-kwon would require platforms to take technical and managerial measures to prevent removal of AI marks, and a companion proposal carries an administrative fine up to KRW 10M for platforms that fail to preserve watermarks (source: https://www.imaeil.com/page/view/2026092412193369072, Sep 2026)
- Provenance marking is acquiring a measurable safety cost in agentic use: Lasso reports that in structured output the watermark has most room to alter uncertain argument values (queries, paths, recipients), so a lexical variation in prose can become a different executed tool argument (source: https://www.unite.ai/lasso-study-finds-text-watermarking-shifts-llm-refusals-and-tool-calls/, Sep 2026)
- Synthetic real-time personas are shipping with embedded provenance from launch: Google's Gemini 3.8 Live with Live Avatar, for enterprise customers under allowlisting, watermarks every avatar with SynthID (source: https://www.engadget.com/2268587/google-video-avatars-gemini-3-8-live-agent/, Sep 2026)

Counter-Signals:
- Users moved to strip the mark as soon as it shipped: Gizmodo reported searches for "AI watermark remover" up 60% week over week and a GitHub "Watermarks Remover" project above 15,000 stars within about a week of Anthropic's announcement (source: https://gizmodo.com/people-are-rushing-to-find-ways-to-remove-claudes-ai-text-watermark-2000800433, Aug 2026)
- Korea's operative regime is softer than its headline: the Ministry of Science and ICT reads Article 31 as binding only AI business operators, so individual creators are exempt; the KRW 30M fine applies only after a corrective order is ignored; fact-finding and fines are deferred until the grace period ends on 22 Jan 2027; and no current provision penalizes stripping a mark (source: https://www.imaeil.com/page/view/2026092412193369072, Sep 2026)

Regulatory Shifts:
- Korea: a National Assembly subcommittee opened formal review of watermark-tampering sanctions and platform preservation duties on 15 Sep 2026 and held them for continued review (source: https://www.msn.com/ko-kr/%EC%9D%BC%EB%B0%98/%EC%9D%BC%EB%B0%98/ai%EB%A1%9C-%EB%A7%8C%EB%93%A4%EB%A9%B4-%ED%91%9C%EC%8B%9C-%EB%96%BC%EB%A9%B4-%EC%B2%98%EB%B2%8C-ai-%EC%9B%8C%ED%84%B0%EB%A7%88%ED%81%AC%EB%B2%95-%EA%B5%AD%ED%9A%8C-%EB%85%BC%EC%9D%98-%EB%8F%8C%EC%9E%85/ar-AA2cf5TU, Sep 2026)
- Korea: the Ministry of Science and ICT's AI Basic Act casebook requires deepfake outputs to carry a visible mark across the full playback and does not accept a hidden watermark alone, while allowing metadata or invisible marks for artistic works such as webtoons; its support desk logged 513 inquiries in two months, 52.6% of online inquiries about how to label (source: https://www.imaeil.com/page/view/2026092412193369072, Sep 2026)
- EU: the AI Board's ninth plenary on 17 Sep 2026 granted no further relief on Article 50(2) marking, so the 2 Dec 2026 date for pre-2 Aug systems stands (source: https://labs.cloudsecurityalliance.org/research/csa-research-note-eu-ai-act-article50-watermarking-deadline/, Sep 2026)

Capital Movements:
- KPMG LLP took an undisclosed minority stake in deepfake detection developer Reality Defender on 11 Sep 2026 and will integrate its synthetic media detection into KPMG cyber and fraud-prevention services (source: https://www.biometricupdate.com/202609/kpmg-investment-in-reality-defender-backs-hiring-spree-to-fight-deepfakes, Sep 2026)

Technical Changes:
- Claude text watermarking went live at model launch for the first time with Fable 5.1 and Mythos 5.1; Anthropic describes the mark as invisible to anyone without detection API access and as nudging word choice during inference (source: https://www.pcworld.com/article/3224834/claudes-updated-fable-and-mythos-models-watermark-their-replies.html, Sep 2026)
- SynthID now covers live, interactive synthetic video and audio: all audio and video generated by Gemini 3.8 Live Avatars carries an invisible SynthID watermark (source: https://www.engadget.com/2268587/google-video-avatars-gemini-3-8-live-agent/, Sep 2026)
- Lasso measured watermark effects on tool calling with BFCL v4 and found reduced tool-call accuracy on six of seven models tested, with effects that differ by secret key and can cancel out in aggregate scores (source: https://www.unite.ai/lasso-study-finds-text-watermarking-shifts-llm-refusals-and-tool-calls/, Sep 2026)

Contradictions:
- Possible Phase A miss on perception-2026-08-04: Instagram's 31 Aug 2026 reach limit is a distribution limit on a category of AI-generated content (undisclosed AI-generated profiles), dated after the claim's logging, yet the 2026-09-25 grade reports no new friction treatment and does not mention it. It penalizes non-disclosure rather than adding friction to labeled content, the CAL-006 mirror image already noted for this claim, so whether it satisfies the claim text is a judgment for the next grading pass.
- Anthropic states the watermark has no practical impact on output quality or content, while Lasso reports that the SynthID-Text scheme it is based on shifts refusal and tool-call behavior; Lasso tested open-weight models through the public Hugging Face processor, not Claude, so the finding does not directly measure Claude.
- Korea mandates marks at generation but has no sanction for stripping them, while the dominant visible user response to marking in the interval was stripping tools.
- Unverified claim circulating: an Inc. headline (2 Sep 2026) says Instagram's new rule will not apply to Facebook or Threads "yet"; the page returned HTTP 403 and was not read, so the scope statement rests only on Instagram-only framing in CNET and Business Standard.
