# Evidence sweep, dependency, 2026-09-25

Delta Since Last Sweep:
- A correlated, same-morning outage now enters the record. On 3 Sep 2026 OpenAI, Anthropic and xAI went down within the same few hours. OpenAI attributed its part to a "routing error", SpaceX attributed Grok's to its Memphis compute center, and Anthropic declined to comment. No shared cause and no root-cause account has been published. Earlier ledger entries covered single-provider degradations only (source: https://www.wired.com/story/nobody-is-saying-why-openai-and-anthropic-had-outages-today/, Sep 2026)
- Forced migration moved from the API layer to the consumer and work surfaces. OpenAI will remove GPT-5.5 from ChatGPT, ChatGPT Work and Codex on 14 Oct 2026, about 174 days after launch, while keeping it on the API. On 11 Sep 2026 it also posted a 20-day API deprecation window for gpt-5.4-cyber (source: https://www.businesstoday.in/technology/artificial-intelligence/story/openai-to-retire-gpt-5-5-from-chatgpt-work-and-codex-on-october-14-what-changes-to-expect-555782-2026-09-16, Sep 2026) (source: https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/deprecations, Sep 2026)
- Pre-window item the 2026-08-13 sweep missed (its Regulatory Shifts said None): on 31 Jul 2026 the three ESAs issued a joint statement on frontier-AI ICT risk that updates supervisory expectations for DORA oversight of critical ICT third-party providers. It is listed here as context only, not as a delta (source: https://www.esma.europa.eu/press-news/esma-news/eba-eiopa-and-esma-call-enhanced-governance-and-consistent-supervision, Jul 2026)
- The availability-disclosure gap persists. Anthropic's status API logs 29 incidents from 13 Aug to 22 Sep 2026, including a critical Claude-wide disruption on 16 Aug. Each incident was closed with impact-and-resolution text only (source: https://status.claude.com/api/v2/incidents.json, Sep 2026)

Confirmed Developments:
- On 3 Sep 2026 Anthropic, OpenAI and xAI suffered near-simultaneous outages. Anthropic: errors on Claude Mythos 5.1, Fable 5.1 and Opus 5 from 6:23 am PT, resolved by 9:16 am PT. OpenAI: ChatGPT and Codex unavailable for some users from about 7:43 am to 8:17 am PT, attributed by a spokesperson to "a routing error". xAI: Grok down across all platforms from 6:30 am to 10:05 am PT, blamed by SpaceX on its Memphis compute center. Cloudflare, AWS and Azure reported no outage, and neither OpenAI nor Anthropic named a shared cause (source: https://www.wired.com/story/nobody-is-saying-why-openai-and-anthropic-had-outages-today/, Sep 2026) (source: https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/artificial-intelligence/anthropic-confirms-claude-is-down-multiple-models-affected/, Sep 2026)
- OpenAI announced on 15 Sep 2026 that GPT-5.5 will be retired from ChatGPT, ChatGPT Work and Codex on 14 Oct 2026 across all plans, Business, Enterprise and Edu included. It points Codex users to GPT-5.6 Sol or GPT-6 Astra, keeps GPT-5.5 on the API, and says saved scripts and custom agents pinned to GPT-5.5 must be updated before the deadline (source: https://www.businesstoday.in/technology/artificial-intelligence/story/openai-to-retire-gpt-5-5-from-chatgpt-work-and-codex-on-october-14-what-changes-to-expect-555782-2026-09-16, Sep 2026) (source: https://mixed-news.com/en/chatgpt-plus-pro-stop-switching-to-thinking-gpt-5-5-retires-october-14/, Sep 2026)
- On 26 Aug 2026 OpenAI notified developers that whisper-1, gpt-4o-transcribe, gpt-4o-mini-transcribe and gpt-4o-transcribe-diarize will be removed from the API on 26 Feb 2027, with replacements gpt-live-transcribe or gpt-transcribe. By 18 Sep 2026 at least one downstream reseller (HyperWhisper Cloud) was still selling three of the deprecated models and had no migration alias for its mini tier (source: https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/deprecations, Sep 2026) (source: https://github.com/ray-amjad/hyperwhisper-app/issues/797, Sep 2026)
- The FSB Chair's letter to G20 finance ministers and central bank governors (dated 28 Aug 2026, published 31 Aug) makes two warnings. Frontier AI could undermine market confidence system-wide "especially due to highly concentrated third-party service providers". Separately, cross-investment between AI companies and hyperscalers could amplify a market correction. The letter does not address the substitutability of AI model providers specifically (source: https://www.fsb.org/uploads/P310826.pdf, Aug 2026) (source: https://www.investmentnews.com/regulation-legal-compliance/global-finance-leaders-warned-that-ai-poses-systemic-risk-to-markets/268002, Aug 2026)
- JAMA Pediatrics published a cross-sectional study on 31 Aug 2026 (Bonnesen et al., University of Ottawa) of 39,761 Ontario students in grades 4 to 12. About 21% reported using AI chatbots for emotional support. That group showed clinical-level emotional difficulties at 57.7% against 29.2% for non-users, plus higher loneliness. The authors state the design cannot establish causality. The publisher page returned HTTP 403; the DOI metadata was confirmed via Crossref (source: https://phys.org/news/2026-08-ai-chatbot-emotional-loneliness-students.html, Aug 2026) (source: https://doi.org/10.1001/jamapediatrics.2026.3904, Aug 2026)

Emerging Signals:
- A shared-compute channel for correlated failure may be forming, but it is not confirmed. On 3 Sep 2026 SpaceX apologised to "our impacted compute partners" for the Memphis outage, and Anthropic and xAI had announced a compute partnership with SpaceX in May 2026. Anthropic's errors began minutes before Grok's, and Anthropic declined to comment, so any causal link is unestablished (source: https://www.wired.com/story/nobody-is-saying-why-openai-and-anthropic-had-outages-today/, Sep 2026)
- Moody's warned on 7 Sep 2026 that banks' reliance on a small set of foundation-model and cloud providers "risks creating a systemic dependency". It also noted a bank can see changes in "accuracy, latency, tone, reasoning or risk controls" without touching its own code, because the external model has been modified. The trade report does not name or link the Moody's publication, and the original was not opened (source: https://qa-financial.com/moodys-warns-banks-over-ai-tech-dependency/, Sep 2026)
- The provider runtime is absorbing more of the agent stack. On 10 Sep 2026 OpenAI released an Agents API in public beta: a managed Codex harness in which OpenAI handles session orchestration, context compaction and recovery, with agents running in OpenAI-hosted sandboxes or a customer-supplied sandbox (source: https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/changelog, Sep 2026)
- In German-language coverage, the June Anthropic access suspension is now being used as a sovereignty sales argument. A 4 Sep 2026 press release from surveillance firm LivEye GmbH calls it a "Weckruf" (wake-up call) for German companies to reduce dependence on foreign AI providers. This is vendor PR, low evidentiary weight (source: https://www.presseportal.de/pm/170731/6345470, Sep 2026)

Counter-Signals:
- Anthropic's model deprecations page lists tentative retirement floors about a year out for its newest models: claude-mythos-5-1 and claude-fable-5-1 "not sooner than September 1, 2027", and claude-opus-5-5 not sooner than 22 Sep 2027. Every model released since mid-2025 is still Active, which bounds forced-migration risk at that provider (source: https://docs.claude.com/en/docs/about-claude/model-deprecations, Sep 2026)
- China's anthropomorphic-AI measures have produced no named enforcement action. Jun He LLP lawyers writing on 10 Sep 2026 cite only product withdrawals that companies framed as product adjustments. They state that scope and enforcement for foreign providers without a domestic entity "remain unclear" (non-English source) (source: https://finance.sina.com.cn/roll/2026-09-10/doc-inirixkq5310231.shtml, Sep 2026)

Regulatory Shifts:
- The FSB's supervisory work programme widened. In the 28 Aug 2026 Chair's letter, the FSB says it is exploring the safe deployment of frontier models for cyber defence by financial firms and "ways to enhance capabilities to respond to and recover from significant operational disruptions". It also stresses resilience "amongst critical third-party technology providers and other common service providers". No new binding instrument or designation was found in the window (source: https://www.fsb.org/uploads/P310826.pdf, Aug 2026)

Capital Movements:
- Mistral AI announced a €3 billion Series D on 8 Sep 2026, led by Samsung Electronics and co-led by EQT's Scaleup Europe Fund and PSG Equity, at a post-money valuation above €21 billion. The stated use is compute capacity and frontier research. Mistral pitches itself explicitly on substitutability, saying customers "are never locked into a single vendor's roadmap, pricing or availability" (source: https://mistral.ai/news/mistral-makes-sovereign-open-weight-ai-to-frontier/, Sep 2026) (source: https://www.pymnts.com/news/artificial-intelligence/2026/mistral-raises-3-billion-dollars-ai-research-record-european-union-funding-round/, Sep 2026)

Technical Changes:
- OpenAI posted a deprecation on 11 Sep 2026 removing gpt-5.4-cyber from the API on 1 Oct 2026, with migration to gpt-5.6-cyber. That is a 20-day notice window, against six months for the transcription models deprecated on 26 Aug 2026 (source: https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/deprecations, Sep 2026)
- OpenAI released GPT-6 Astra on 3 Sep 2026 and GPT-6 Sol and GPT-6 Luna on 22 Sep 2026. Astra drops the `none` reasoning effort, custom temperature and top_p, and logprobs, and requires the Responses API for tool calling, so existing integrations must change code to adopt it (source: https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/changelog, Sep 2026)
- Tier 1 MCP SDK releases in the window hardened the deprecated transport and registration paths instead of removing them. The Go SDK v1.8.0 (14 Sep 2026) caps SSE event size and limits Dynamic Client Registration responses to 1 MB. The Python SDK v2.1.0 (24 Aug 2026) extends the 4 MiB request-body limit to the SSE transport. The TypeScript @modelcontextprotocol/server-legacy bridge shipped 2.1.0 on 23 Sep 2026 (source: https://github.com/modelcontextprotocol/go-sdk/releases, Sep 2026)

Contradictions:
- Unverified claim circulating: a syndicated MSN headline of 3 Sep 2026 says "Azure is at fault" for the ChatGPT, Claude and Grok outages. WIRED reports that Azure, AWS and Cloudflare reported no outages that day, and SpaceX attributed Grok's outage to its own Memphis facility. The Azure attribution was not used.
- Unverified claim circulating: a personal blog (tonishatagoe.com) says the EU AI Act transition period for general high-risk systems "concluded on August 2, 2026" and that a first wave of AI Office inspections began in September. This conflicts with the Digital Omnibus deferral of Annex III obligations to Dec 2027 already recorded in this ledger. Not used.
- OpenAI's notice periods vary 9x within one month: six months for transcription models (26 Aug) and 20 days for gpt-5.4-cyber (11 Sep). Its consumer surfaces retire a frontier model after about 174 days while the API keeps it. Dependency risk therefore depends on the access channel, not only on the provider.
- The 2026-09-25 grading of dependency-2026-08-02 says the FSB Chair's letter "does not address AI provider concentration or substitutability". The letter does cite "highly concentrated third-party service providers" and AI-hyperscaler cross-investment, but generically and without naming AI model providers or substitutability, so the open grade stands on the claim's specific wording.
- Moody's dating is inconsistent. TechRound dates a Moody's AI-vendor-concentration warning to 12 Aug 2026, while QA Financial reports one on 7 Sep 2026. Neither names or links the report, so whether this is one publication or two is unresolved.
