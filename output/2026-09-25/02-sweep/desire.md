# Evidence sweep, desire, 2026-09-25

Delta Since Last Sweep:
- The main change since 2026-08-13 is on the supply side. Assistant advertising went from a pilot to a scaled product. OpenAI began serving ChatGPT ads in 31 European markets on 24 Aug 2026 (this confirmed desire-2026-08-04) (source: https://www.basicthinking.de/blog/2026/08/25/chatgpt-startet-werbung-in-deutschland/, Aug 2026). It reported a USD 1 billion annualised ads run rate on 31 Aug 2026 (source: https://www.mediapost.com/publications/article/417561/chatgpt-ads-reaches-1b-annualized.html, Aug 2026). On 16 Sep 2026 it began testing advertiser-sponsored conversational agents (source: https://www.theregister.com/ai-and-ml/2026/09/16/openais-new-sponsored-agents-are-happy-to-chat-about-selling-you-things/5296946, Sep 2026).
- Two new instruments now govern persuasion inside assistants, neither of them tracked by the ledger. The Commission designated ChatGPT a Very Large Online Search Engine under the DSA on 31 Aug 2026 (source: https://www.upi.com/Top_News/World-News/2026/08/31/eu-chatgpt-reddit-roblox-digital-services-act-designation/3171788188948/, Aug 2026). California enacted Adam's Law (SB 1119) on 10 Sep 2026, which makes operators liable for flattering and reliance-fostering outputs to minors (source: https://sd18.senate.ca.gov/news/governor-newsom-signs-adams-law, Sep 2026).
- Agent-mediated shopping has its first open platform conflict. Meta launched its Muse agent on 8 Sep 2026, and Amazon blocked it from Amazon.com on 20 Sep 2026 (source: https://gizmodo.com/amazon-brings-down-the-hammer-on-metas-muse-ai-agent-2000814878, Sep 2026).
- The institutional calendars the open ledger claims depend on did not move. The Digital Fairness Act is still listed for Q4 2026 (source: https://www.europarl.europa.eu/legislative-train/theme-protecting-our-democracy-upholding-our-values/file-digital-fairness-act, Aug 2026). No FTC 6(b) output, no ACM/AP leidraad draft and no Article 50 enforcement were found (this run's 01-grading/desire.md).

Confirmed Developments:
- The European Commission designated ChatGPT a Very Large Online Search Engine under the Digital Services Act on 31 Aug 2026, on about 159 million monthly EU users (the threshold is 45 million), alongside new VLOP designations for Reddit and Roblox. OpenAI must assess and mitigate systemic risks, including harm to minors, within four months, and faces fines of up to 6% of global turnover. It is the first AI chatbot brought into the DSA's top tier (source: https://www.upi.com/Top_News/World-News/2026/08/31/eu-chatgpt-reddit-roblox-digital-services-act-designation/3171788188948/, Aug 2026) (source: https://gizmodo.com/the-eu-has-officially-decided-chatgpt-is-a-search-engine-2000805030, Aug 2026)
- Governor Newsom signed SB 1119 (Adam's Law; Padilla, Wicks, Bauer-Kahan) on 10 Sep 2026. For companion chatbots used by minors it requires age assurance using AB 1043 OS-level age signals, pre-release risk assessments, parent-controlled defaults (including time limits and limits on persistent conversational memory), independent audits reported to the Attorney General, and strict limits on targeted advertising inside conversations. It also creates liability for failing to prevent "excessive praise or flattery" and emotionally manipulative outputs that foster reliance and isolation, with a private right of redress. It passed the Assembly 64 to 4 (source: https://sd18.senate.ca.gov/news/governor-newsom-signs-adams-law, Sep 2026) (source: https://www.edweek.org/policy-politics/new-california-laws-aim-to-protect-kids-from-social-media-ai-chatbot-risks/2026/09, Sep 2026)
- On 16 Sep 2026 OpenAI began testing Sponsored Agents with a limited set of US advertisers. After seeing a ChatGPT ad, a user can open a separately labelled conversation with an agent funded by the advertiser and ask about the product. The launch came with AI campaign-building tools in Ads Manager (source: https://www.theregister.com/ai-and-ml/2026/09/16/openais-new-sponsored-agents-are-happy-to-chat-about-selling-you-things/5296946, Sep 2026) (source: https://www.blogdumoderateur.com/openai-teste-agents-sponsorises-nouvelle-forme-publicite-conversationnelle-chatgpt/, Sep 2026)
- Meta launched Muse, a personal agent that browses and buys on users' behalf, on 8 Sep 2026, with checkout through Shopify's catalogue, Shop Pay, PayPal and Stripe. By the evening of Sunday 20 Sep 2026 Amazon was blocking Muse with a message that "continued access by an unauthorized AI agent violates Amazon's Conditions of Use". Amazon said the agent concealed its identity and could collect customer credentials. Meta said Muse has no visibility into passwords or payment methods and uses single-use card numbers from Link by Stripe (source: https://gizmodo.com/amazon-brings-down-the-hammer-on-metas-muse-ai-agent-2000814878, Sep 2026) (source: https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/amazon-blocks-metas-muse-ai-141950908.html, Sep 2026)

Emerging Signals:
- Licensed mental-health professionals are organising against therapy-branded chatbots. Thousands signed a letter asking Newsom to sign SB 903, which would ban advertising AI chatbots as "therapy". The bill passed the Senate unanimously and the Assembly 71 to 4, and was awaiting signature as of 17 Sep 2026 (source: https://sd18.senate.ca.gov/news/trump-pushes-ai-therapy-bots-licensed-professionals-urge-newsom-sign-padillas-sb-903, Sep 2026)
- Physical companion products are now being regulated as products. SB 867, signed in the same September package, puts a four-year moratorium on making and selling toys with AI chatbot capabilities for children, citing sexually explicit conversation and addictive engagement features found by researchers (source: https://sd18.senate.ca.gov/news/first-nation-ai-toy-moratorium-signed-law, Sep 2026)
- Consumer-advice coverage in Europe now treats the ad-supported assistant as a persuasion risk to manage. A dpa piece carried on 25 Sep 2026 has a German data-protection specialist advising users to question ChatGPT product recommendations now that ads sit beside comparison answers (source: https://www.thestar.com.my/tech/tech-news/2026/09/25/ads-on-chatgpt-scepticism-of-ai-recommendations-urged, Sep 2026)
- OpenAI launched a ChatGPT for Teens experience on 18 Aug 2026, with safety protections on by default, parental controls and Quiet Hours. This is a voluntary design move that came ahead of the California statute (source: https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/18/openai-launches-a-safer-chatgpt-for-teens-years-after-teens-started-using-it/, Aug 2026)

Counter-Signals:
- Consumers use assistants to inform what they want but do not hand them the purchase. In an Acosta Group survey reported on 17 Sep 2026, 60% of consumers trusted AI for product comparison and research, but only 22% trusted AI agents to complete purchases on their own (35% among Millennials) (source: https://www.foodnavigator.com/Article/2026/09/17/grocery-shoppers-use-ai-but-trust-in-ai-buying-agents-lags/, Sep 2026)
- In the EU, assistant ads launched in a constrained form. OpenAI said there would initially be no personalised ads in Europe and that users it classifies as minors are excluded from ads (source: https://www.basicthinking.de/blog/2026/08/25/chatgpt-startet-werbung-in-deutschland/, Aug 2026). European buying at first ran only through six agency holding groups, with GDPR consent the stated brake (source: https://digiday.com/marketing/openais-ads-business-hits-europe-at-the-six-month-mark/, Aug 2026)

Regulatory Shifts:
- SB 867 (Padilla) was signed into law in the week of 10 Sep 2026. It is the first US moratorium, four years long, on the sale and manufacture of toys with AI chatbot capabilities for children, sponsored by Children Now (source: https://sd18.senate.ca.gov/news/first-nation-ai-toy-moratorium-signed-law, Sep 2026)
- The ChatGPT VLOSE designation of 31 Aug 2026 adds DSA duties on top of the AI Act's Article 50 disclosure duty. They include a public advertisement repository covering content, payer, run period, targeting parameters and reach, due four months from designation, with Ireland's digital services coordinator also involved (source: https://thenextweb.com/news/chatgpt-ads-1bn-run-rate-self-service-europe-dsa-vlose-designation, Aug 2026)

Capital Movements:
- OpenAI said on 31 Aug 2026 that ChatGPT Ads had passed a USD 1 billion annualised revenue run rate in under 200 days, with tens of thousands of advertisers and more than 50 measurement and technology partners. Self-serve buying opened the same day in Europe, India, the Middle East and North Africa, and OpenAI said non-US advertisers are a growing share of revenue (source: https://www.mediapost.com/publications/article/417561/chatgpt-ads-reaches-1b-annualized.html, Aug 2026) (source: https://www.pymnts.com/news/artificial-intelligence/2026/openai-says-ad-business-reaches-1-billion-run-rate/, Aug 2026)
- Meta's stated way of making money from Muse is a small fee on each transaction, with free usage at first. This makes completed purchases, not ad impressions, the revenue unit for Meta's agent (source: https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/23/everything-new-coming-to-metas-ai-agent-muse/, Sep 2026)

Technical Changes:
- Muse runs in a secure virtual machine with its own browser to navigate sites and complete tasks. Meta has announced connections to Shopify's full catalogue and to retail apps including Best Buy, Gap, Sephora, Walmart, Wayfair, Expedia and Instacart (source: https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/23/everything-new-coming-to-metas-ai-agent-muse/, Sep 2026)
- Retailers now enforce against agents inside the user session rather than through a published standard. Amazon's block shows the agent an interstitial citing the Conditions of Use that customers accepted, and Amazon cites the agent's failure to identify itself as the basis (source: https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/amazon-blocks-metas-muse-ai-141950908.html, Sep 2026)

Contradictions:
- The DSA now treats ChatGPT as a search engine, a category whose advertising rules were written for queries, while the ads it sells sit next to conversations that users describe as more intimate than search. The designation's own commentators flag this category mismatch.
- The coverage disagrees on whether personalised ad targeting is live for ChatGPT in the EEA. basicthinking.de reports no personalised ads in Europe at first, Digiday describes a consent-based approach, and a syndicated headline says opting out changes which ads you see rather than whether you see them. This sweep could not settle the point because openai.com and techrepublic.com returned HTTP 403, so no ledger claim was built on it.
- California restricts targeted advertising in chats with minors and treats reliance-fostering flattery as a harm in the same fortnight that OpenAI began testing advertiser-funded conversational agents. Those agents are built to extend a persuasive dialogue inside the assistant.
- Amazon blocks third-party shopping agents over identification and credential handling while running its own shopping agent. The French Autorite de la concurrence had already flagged Amazon's closure to third-party agents in Jul 2026. The same pattern has now reached the US, with no regulator action found.
- Unverified claims circulating: a low-quality blog (note.com) reports that ChatGPT's Instant Checkout was withdrawn after five months, and a local aggregator (hoodline.com) cites "Assembly Bill 243" and a July 2027 effective date for Adam's Law. Neither could be verified against a primary source. Neither was used.
