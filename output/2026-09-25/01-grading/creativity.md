Surprises:
- Both confirmations are genuinely forward-looking, which reverses last month's pattern (CAL-004). Both claims were concrete and near-term, and both were written around an actor's stated next step: Suno's own announced model transition, and a litigation tactic that had just been validated in Reddit v. Perplexity. Round Hill sued within 17 days of the cutoff.
- The litigation channel is widening, not narrowing. Suno licensed Warner, BMG and Believe and shipped v6 on licensed data, yet Round Hill (a publisher, not a label) sued Suno and Anthropic for up to $1B each on 17 Aug 2026. Licensing labels leaves publishers and collecting societies (GEMA) outside the deals. Settling with one class of rights holder does not buy peace with the others.
- creativity-2026-07-01 turns out to rest on a reference deal that collapsed four months before the claim was logged. "At least two more" franchise-scale deals assumed the Disney and OpenAI deal as a baseline, but it never closed and Sora was cancelled in Mar 2026. Neither the 2026-07 logging nor the 2026-08-04 grade caught this. In addition, all 2026 music-AI deals keep their value undisclosed, so the $100M+ threshold looks unobservable rather than unmet.
