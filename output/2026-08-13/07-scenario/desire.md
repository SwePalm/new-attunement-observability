## 1. Title & Core Question
- Title: Ask Two
- Core Question: When the decision that allocates a purchase is which shops were candidates, and nobody anywhere records that, what is left for an ordinary household to do except compare two answers and guess?

## 2. Context Summary (Translation Layer) – Why This Future Exists
In the first week of August 2026 two American grocers moved their assortments into assistant surfaces, one building its own with sponsored placement included at launch and measured against loyalty data covering 95% of its transactions, the other renting a room inside somebody else's assistant. Three weeks earlier a French competition authority had published the only hard measurement anyone had: agents carried under 5% of French e-commerce traffic, one assistant cited 42 domains where another cited 112 on the same request, and the largest marketplace in the market simply blocked third-party agents from entering. The habit arrived on a retail clock and the measurement on a regulatory one. This scenario sits in late winter 2028, which is roughly one grocery-app adoption cycle later and still well before any European instrument aimed at commercial ordering could be in force: a proposal indicated for late 2026 is a negotiation through 2028 and duties after that.

## 3. Future World Snapshot (The Lived Experience) – A Day in This Future
Sunday, and Rosalie does the list on the kitchen table because her mother likes to watch.

She asks both. The King Soopers assistant and the Safeway one get the same eleven items, and she reads the two baskets side by side the way her mother used to read two weather forecasts before a drive. Nine lines match. On the tenth they diverge for no reason either of them can construct, and on the eleventh, cinnamon, one of them offers a house brand and the other offers Teo's, the two-aisle grocery on Havana with the good bark cinnamon and the tape on the freezer door. Rosalie has never learned why Teo appears in one list and not the other. She has stopped expecting to. The disagreement is the information; the reason is not available at any price she can pay.

Her mother wants to look at a recliner, so they use the clean account. It has no history in it and that is the point, the way a coat pocket is empty on purpose. Looking on your own login is a purchase you have not made yet. Her mother finds this funny and does it anyway.

At Teo's, the chalkboard by the register lists the month's costs in his handwriting: rent, the walk-in compressor, insurance, findability. He charges the visibility service to the shop the way he charges the dumpster. He is not bitter about it. What he does, when Rosalie asks about the cinnamon, is tell her that the Vietnamese place four blocks down has better cassia and is cheaper on quantity, and that she should go there instead, and he says it while ringing up her other things.

She walks the four blocks. It is not efficient. Nothing on the way there is optimised for her at all, and that is the entire reason she does it: a man with nothing to gain told her something true about a competitor, and there is no surface in her life that can produce that, so she gets it by being in the room where it happens.

## 4. Behavioral Shifts (Human Lens) – How People Adapt
Households stop asking whether a recommendation is honest and start asking who benefits from it, then discover that the question is unanswerable, then settle into folk methods instead of rights. Comparing two assistants becomes ordinary domestic competence, taught to teenagers alongside checking a receipt, and quoting only one answer in conversation reads as slightly credulous. Nobody expects the comparison to explain anything. It only establishes that a different list was possible, which turns out to be the durable thing a person can hold onto.

The second adaptation is record hygiene. Once it is common knowledge that both what you are shown and what you are charged move with your purchase history, browsing acquires consequences, and people build small rituals to keep exploratory attention off the ledger. Shared clean accounts circulate inside families. It is not resistance and nobody calls it that; it is closer to putting shoes by the door.

The third is a quiet re-valuation of proximity and of people. Local shops recruit through channels whose enumeration they control, which mostly means other people: standing orders, community boards, a neighbour who texts. Merchants themselves become the most trusted recommenders precisely when they recommend against their own interest, and they learn that saying so out loud is the only advertising available to them that cannot be bought by anyone else. Distance stops meaning what it used to. A shop four blocks away can be further from a household than a warehouse in another state.

## 5. Structural Forces (System Lens) – What Holds This World Together
Three things keep this arrangement standing, and none of them is a rule.

The first is posture at the top of the market. Amazon blocks third-party AI agents from its marketplace, which means the largest single assortment in the country is not reachable by any agent shopping on a household's behalf, while the retailers who did open up did so inside surfaces they operate. Penetration and closure grow together rather than trading off, so the intermediate thing, an agent that shops the whole market for you, never gets built because neither large party wants it to exist.

The second is that the measurement is one-sided and the strong side owns it. A retail media network evaluates its own assistant's placements against its own loyalty data at 95% transaction coverage, continuously, privately, with no advertiser setup required to buy a position. Against that, the public record consists of a small number of sector studies, of which the fullest, the July 2026 French opinion, counted cited domains by hand and reported findings rather than remedies.

The third is that an answer set is not an object anywhere. No standard requires an assistant to log which merchants were considered, so the consequential decision leaves no artifact, and there is nothing for an auditor, a court or a journalist to ask for. Inference rather than established fact: the practical reason no second measurement appeared is that each one has to be constructed from scratch by whoever wants it.

## 6. Reflection & Implications – Questions This World Asks Us
If a harm is real, systematic and never felt by the person it lands on, which of our institutions is designed to find it, and what happens to a market where the honest answer is none of them?

We built disclosure rules for the speaker and left the ordering alone. Was that a choice about what mattered, or only about what was easy to write down?

The most trusted voice in this world is a person who recommends against their own interest. What does it say about the surfaces we made that this is the one thing they cannot reproduce?

## 7. Pullback Layer: From Possibility to Probability
### 7.1 Signals Emerging (Plausible Zone) – Early Signals We Already See
- A national competition authority ran comparative queries across assistants and found that one cited 42 domains where another cited 112 on identical transport-reservation requests, published in its opinion of 17 July 2026, which is the first public evidence that which merchants exist is a property of which assistant is asked.
- The same opinion recorded that Amazon blocks third-party AI agents from its marketplace, and that OpenAI, Google and Anthropic held more than 84% of the global AI-agent sector in May 2026.
- A US grocer launched an AI shopping assistant across most of its banners on 4 August 2026 with product listing ads carried at launch, no additional advertiser setup required, and measurement tied to loyalty data covering 95% of its transactions.
- A second US grocer put a retail app inside a general assistant on 6 August 2026, where a shopper assembles a cart by naming the retailer and is then handed back to the retailer's own checkout.
- Assistants began assembling transactions rather than only suggesting them: spoken cart-building for restaurant orders shipped on 6 August 2026 with payment-terminal vendors as launch partners and checkout left on the merchant's own systems.
- Measured demand is still tiny where anyone has counted it rather than projected it: agents produced under 5% of French e-commerce traffic as of July 2026, against projections of a quarter of merchant traffic by 2030.

### 7.2 Probable Direction (Near-Term Future) – Where We're Likely Headed
The likeliest path to 2031 is that adoption rises and visibility does not. Agent-mediated purchase becomes normal in groceries and restaurant ordering first, because those are the categories where a retailer already owns both the assortment and the purchase record, and most of the growth happens inside surfaces the seller controls, so closure increases with penetration instead of easing. Bilateral access agreements between assistants and large sellers get negotiated and never published. The instrument that would reach commercial ordering rather than disclosure moves slowly and should be assumed slower than announced: a proposal indicated for late 2026 is a text, then a negotiation, then a transition period, and this kind of date has slipped before under competitiveness pressure. Enforcement, when it appears, lands on the visible surface: an undisclosed sponsorship, a missing non-human notice, a dark pattern in a checkout flow. The ordering upstream of all of it stays unlogged, and therefore stays unlitigated. The genuine uncertainty is not law. It is whether a second and third authority decide, unprompted, to go and count.

### 7.3 Preferred Path (Intentional Future) – The Path We Could Choose Instead
- Make the answer set an object: require an assistant, on request, to disclose which merchants were candidates for a commercial recommendation and on what basis they were included or excluded.
- Fund and repeat the comparative query exercise as a standing time series across jurisdictions, so a domain-count gap becomes a measurement rather than an anecdote nobody can attach a remedy to.
- Give consumer and competition authorities an explicit own-initiative mandate for markets where the harm is omission, since a complaint-driven regime cannot see something that produces no complainant.
- Treat refusal of agent access by a dominant marketplace as a reportable market fact rather than a private commercial choice.
- Require that a retailer's advertising measurement inside its own assistant be independently verifiable at least once, so that the party grading the placement is not only the party selling it.
- Households and small merchants: keep and share the comparisons you make, because a thousand ordinary side-by-side lists are the only dataset anyone outside the companies is in a position to build.

## 8. Connect to Today
### Skills We May Need
- Asking the same question twice, of two different systems, and treating the difference as the finding rather than as a nuisance.
- Noticing an absence: learning to ask which shops were not in the answer, knowing you will not be told, and asking anyway.
- Reading a recommendation for whose revenue it is, including when the recommendation is good.
- Keeping exploratory attention separate from purchasing identity, without pretending this is privacy.
- Recognising a disinterested recommendation when you get one, and saying thank you in a way that keeps the person willing to give another.
- For merchants: knowing exactly what your visibility subscription buys, what it does not, and who else in your category has bought it.

### Signals & Refractions
- Sponsored placement inside a retailer's own assistant already ships with no additional advertiser setup, which means the ad and the recommendation are not two systems that had to be joined; they were never separate.
- The only comparative count of assistant answer sets in the public record was produced by one authority, in one language, on one class of query, and it is already the most-cited fact about this market, which is a measure of how little else exists.
- Households already check two navigation apps, two weather apps and two price trackers without expecting to reconcile them. The habit is in place; the object it will be applied to is arriving.

## 9. Final Insight
The thing worth holding onto from this window is a matter of timing. The terms on which people will buy things were being set in a period when fewer than one purchase in twenty went through the mediation at all, and the only parties counting were the ones with a position in the outcome, plus one office in Paris that decided on its own to go and look. That is not a scandal. It is how rail gauges and container sizes and card fees were settled too: early, quietly, by whoever was present. What is different this time is that the settled thing is not a dimension but a list, remade for every household on every request, and gone the moment it is answered. Rosalie will not know what her assistants left out this Sunday, and neither will anyone else, and in 2028 that will still be nobody's job to find out.
