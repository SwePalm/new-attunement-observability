## 1. Title & Core Question
- Title: The Clean Copy
- Core Question: If the version of your account that still carries proof of how it was made is the version nobody believes, and the version that has passed through enough hands to lose that proof is the one that gets read on its own terms, what is provenance actually for?

## 2. Context Summary (Translation Layer) – Why This Future Exists
Two instruments shipped past each other in the summer of 2026 and never met. One was a mark placed inside generated text during generation, applied at model level so it followed the model rather than the product, switched on worldwide with no opt-out, and released without the detection mechanisms that would let anyone outside the company read it. Those mechanisms were promised as forthcoming, with no date. The other was a commercial detector that reads no mark and no metadata at all, scoring prose against machine-written comparisons, funded that July and already installed inside a publishing platform, a question-and-answer site, universities, publishers and recruiters. The first answers a narrow question nobody can hear. The second answers a different question, loudly, at the moment a decision about a person is being made. By late 2027 the gap has not closed, and what fills it is a number.

## 3. Future World Snapshot (The Lived Experience) – A Day in This Future
The document is nine hundred words long and it is about a boiler.

It is written over three evenings in November 2027, on a phone, at a kitchen table in Montréal, by a personal support worker whose first language is neither of the two languages she is writing between. Eleven months of a dead boiler, black bloom along the window reveal, four unanswered messages, a child's cough. She pastes it once into an assistant and asks it to fix the grammar. Nothing else changes: her facts, her order, her sentences. That single pass is where the mark enters the file, and the mark will outlive almost everything else about it.

It leaves as an attachment to the tenants' association. Someone photographs their screen and sends the photograph to the group chat, and the photograph is the version most people will ever see. A picture of text is not text: whatever the file was carrying does not travel with it. Nobody notices, because nobody could have read it anyway.

In December a neighbourhood newsletter runs it verbatim. Verbatim is the mark at its strongest and it is still legible to no one.

A reader taps Scan. The number comes back in the high eighties. The editor had a per-post switch that would have turned scanning off, and did not use it, because using it would have looked like something. In the comments the number is quoted eleven times before the eleven months are mentioned once. The landlord's reply addresses the number and not the mould.

In January a paralegal prepares the filing for the housing tribunal and translates the account into French. She also advises leaving two of the original errors in the English, the missing article and the odd preposition, the way you would leave a thumbprint. The French copy carries no mark. Translation takes it out. It has been through enough hands to have nothing left in it to find, and it is the copy that gets read on its own terms: nine hundred words about a boiler, believed, acted on, unremarkable.

What the English version lost was not the case. The case is won in French in the spring. What it lost was the ninety seconds before the number existed, when a stranger might have read it and formed a view of a woman and a boiler and eleven months. She keeps both files on her phone. She does not open the English one.

## 4. Behavioral Shifts (Human Lens) – How People Adapt
Assistance gets rationed by the people who need it most. A second-language writer learns that a grammar pass is a risk and that errors are an alibi, so the polish goes and the roughness stays, deliberately, in measured amounts. Small publications develop informal guidance on how clean a submission can be before it starts to score badly, and nobody involved finds the conversation strange.

Self-laundering becomes hygiene. Running your own sincere writing through another language and back is understood the way clearing metadata off a photograph is understood: not deceptive, just sensible, and known to work because the instrument's own documentation says translation defeats it.

A percentage becomes the opening fact about a document, quoted before its contents in comment threads, admissions reviews and staff meetings, in the way a room used to note who had forwarded something.

And people begin routing their accounts toward venues where no scan is offered, which in practice means slower, smaller, more local, more official venues: a tribunal, a union rep, a paper form. Inference, not established by this run's evidence: the effect is that the most consequential accounts drift out of public view and into procedures, where they are read carefully by one person and by nobody else.

## 5. Structural Forces (System Lens) – What Holds This World Together
Three things hold it. First, the reader was never shipped. The provider's documentation deferred detection mechanisms to forthcoming technical documentation with no date, so marked text accumulated across every surface worldwide before any third party could read a single mark, and a mark nobody can read cannot correct a mark somebody guesses at. Second, the erosion paths are documented by the provider itself: the signal survives copy and paste and is defeated by substantial rewriting, translation, paraphrase and mixing, while file metadata is lost through format conversion or a screenshot. That makes the mark strongest on honest verbatim reuse and absent on exactly the handling that content undergoes on its way to being contested. Third, the instrument that does work is owned by the parties who hold gates. A detector that reads no watermark and no metadata took 9 million dollars in July 2026 and sells to a publishing platform, a question-and-answer site, universities, publishers and recruiters, which is the publication gate, the credential gate and the hiring gate served by one model of what human prose looks like. Nothing accredits it, nothing obliges it to state its error distribution, and no one it scores has a right of reply. Meanwhile the duties in force attach to generators and deployers and create no entitlement for a reader to read anything, so an unreadable mark remains a complete answer to the only question the law asks.

## 6. Reflection & Implications – Questions This World Asks Us
A mark can say that a text passed through a machine. A detector can say that a text resembles machine writing. Neither can say who had the thought. What did we build, if the one question everyone is actually asking is the one nothing in the system was designed to answer?

If a protection can only be used at the cost of looking guilty, is it a protection, and who was it built for?

We know how to measure a detector against documents and we have not decided how to measure it against people. One error in twenty-four thousand is a fact about a corpus. What is it a fact about, for the person who is the one?

## 7. Pullback Layer: From Possibility to Probability
### 7.1 Signals Emerging (Plausible Zone) – Early Signals We Already See
- Text marking became a production property of a frontier model line in Aug 2026: embedded during generation so it follows the model rather than the surface, covering the platform API, the chat product, the coding tool and the cloud-partner deployments, applied worldwide rather than only in the jurisdiction that prompted it, with no user opt-out.
- The same documentation that announced the mark deferred the means of reading it, describing detection mechanisms as forthcoming and giving no date, so on the day the duty became operative marked and unmarked text were indistinguishable to everyone outside the company.
- The provider published the mark's own failure modes: it survives copy and paste, is defeated by substantial rewriting including rewriting by another model, by translation, paraphrase and mixing, and file metadata is lost through format conversion or a screenshot, while a detected mark shows only that content may have been processed rather than establishing authorship.
- A watermark-free statistical detector raised 9 million dollars on 29 Jul 2026 and shipped new text and image models claiming one false positive per 24,000 documents, with customers spanning a publishing platform, a question-and-answer site, universities, publishers and recruiters.
- The objection to that detector was distributional rather than technical: on the publishing platform's rollout in Aug 2026, the argument raised was that scanning creates an environment of suspicion and that non-native English speakers and neurodiverse writers carry the cost in lost income.
- The publishing platform added a per-post writer disable within two days of launching reader-initiated scanning, in response to writer objections, so a discretionary control appeared at the platform layer in the same season a mandatory one appeared at the model layer with no opt-out.

### 7.2 Probable Direction (Near-Term Future) – Where We're Likely Headed
The horizon here is roughly twelve to twenty months out rather than the end of the decade, because that is where this evidence actually lands: the marking runway closes in Dec 2026, the state provenance bills in flight run through the 2027 sessions, and the six live claims in this theme resolve between Feb 2027 and Sep 2027, so late 2027 into 2028 is the first period in which genuinely new facts exist rather than restated calendars. Expect drift rather than resolution. Marking spreads because it is cheap and defensible, and stays inert because the reading tooling arrives late; announced-but-undated deliverables in this sector have slipped repeatedly enough that placing it in 2028 is the disciplined guess, not the pessimistic one. Enforcement stays thin: an operative duty with no named action in either jurisdiction is the current state, and the likely failure mode after an on-time commencement is enforcement latency rather than further deferral. The statistical channel compounds instead, because it has capital, customers on both sides of publication, and no accreditation regime to slow it.

### 7.3 Preferred Path (Intentional Future) – The Path We Could Choose Instead
- Treat shipping the reader as part of the duty: a mark that no third party can read should not discharge a transparency obligation, and the detection documentation should carry a date the way the marking obligation does.
- Attach ordinary procedural rights to statistical scores used about people: a duty to disclose that a scan was run, a published error distribution broken out by language background and writing style, a right of reply, and a bar on a score being the sole basis for a decision.
- Require accuracy claims to be stated per person rather than per document, because a corpus-level false-positive rate says nothing about a group whose prose is systematically misread.
- Publishers and editors: decide your scanning policy before you receive a piece that tests it, publish the policy, and stop treating a writer's use of the opt-out as information.
- Universities, employers and platforms buying detection: ask the vendor for the false-positive breakdown by first language and by neurotype, and treat inability to produce it as the answer.
- Individually: learn which of your own words carry what, and keep at least one route by which your account reaches a person before it reaches an instrument.

## 8. Connect to Today
### Skills We May Need
- Knowing what a mark can actually say, and hearing the difference between "passed through a machine" and "was written by one."
- Reading an accuracy claim for its denominator: per document or per person, and which people.
- Recognising when a control offered to you costs more to use than to forgo, and saying so out loud rather than quietly declining it.
- Getting an account in front of a person before a number gets there first, and knowing which venues still work that way.
- Helping someone write in a language they learned as an adult without making their writing more suspicious than it was.
- Noticing when you have started reading for provenance instead of for content, and asking who trained you to do that.

### Signals & Refractions
- The reader-initiated scan is already here, already free at the point of use, and already produced a public argument about who its errors fall on, within three weeks of launch.
- The opt-out that reads as an admission already exists: a per-post disable, added after writer objections, exercisable without scanning first, on a platform whose community was still split about it weeks later.
- The proofreading case is already the live complaint: the loudest objections to text marking came from people using an assistant on their own writing, not from people passing machine writing off as their own.

## 9. Final Insight
In the spring of 2028 the detection tooling finally arrives, and it works exactly as documented. Run the original nine hundred words through it and it returns the true thing it was built to return: this text may have been processed by Claude. That was true in November, and it was never what anyone in the comments, the reply, or the tribunal was asking. The apparatus is now able to say, precisely and verifiably, something adjacent to the question. What it cannot say is whether a woman lived eleven months in a cold apartment, which is the only claim the document ever made, and which was settled in the end by a copy of it that had been rubbed clean of every trace of how it was written. We have built machines that can be honest about themselves and we have not built anything that helps a person be believed. Those are not the same project, and only one of them was funded.
