### Political
Core Shift Thesis:
Authority over large computational load is being exercised through discretionary approval gates held by governors, state commissions and county boards rather than through statute, because an approval that can be withheld works immediately while a law takes a session and a standard takes years.

Forces:
1. Gubernatorial compliance regimes enforced through interconnection approval rather than penalty: Governor Abbott's Data Center Standards, with named-company commitments from Oracle, Edge ConneX and Stream Data Centers on 11 Aug 2026 and Core Scientific, Vantage, SB Energy, Google, Rowan and CleanSpark on 12 Aug 2026, and PUCT and ERCOT directed to deny approval to non-compliant projects (sweep-grounded).
2. Open-ended executive moratoria used as leverage on a process rather than as a decision: New York Executive Order 62 of 14 Jul 2026 suspending discretionary DEC permits for facilities at or above 50 MW, running until DPS submits a final Generic Environmental Impact Statement and findings statement, with no fixed expiry (sweep-grounded, institutional).
3. Municipal and county refusal as the operative national policy in aggregate: denial of the 2.6 million sq ft Air Products site outside Allentown on 13 Aug 2026, Hillsboro Oregon's 120-day moratorium of 12 Aug 2026, and the Pacific Missouri withdrawal of 12 Aug 2026, none coordinated and all binding on specific parcels (sweep-grounded).
4. Congressional cost-allocation politics moving on a legislative rather than an operational clock: H.R. 9340, the Ratepayer Protection Act, cleared House Energy and Commerce 52-0 on 22 Jul 2026 and did not receive a floor vote before August recess.

### Economic
Core Shift Thesis:
Capital is pricing the physical layer (megawatts, land, interconnection position, equipment) faster than any institution is pricing the obligations attached to it, so terms that will later be mandatory are currently being financed as if optional.

Forces:
1. Facility-level secured debt against equipment and offtake rather than hyperscaler balance-sheet capex: Lambda's $926M senior secured loan facility of 13 Aug 2026 for GPU acquisition and buildout, and STT GDC's up to $1.37B of 13 Aug 2026 for a 166 MW Johor facility with a 16 MW initial phase (sweep-grounded).
2. Delay repriced as the dominant project risk, with BloombergNEF assessing the Texas grid pause as putting roughly 20 percent of the US data center pipeline at risk, which makes an open-ended moratorium more expensive to a developer than a strict but dated standard (sweep-grounded).
3. Compensation for large-load flexibility becoming a revenue line: FERC's six Section 206 dockets (EL26-67 through EL26-72) were opened to make RTO tariffs accommodate large and co-located loads, so behaviour that PJM is examining as a reliability defect is simultaneously behaviour the cost-allocation track has been trying to procure and pay for (institutional).
4. Cost-of-service reallocation onto residential and small commercial classes as large loads self-supply, with Abbott's standards requiring operators to pay their own electric infrastructure costs and forgo taxpayer incentives, which shifts the fight from who pays for new build to who carries the fixed costs of the existing system.

### Social
Core Shift Thesis:
Electricity is returning from invisible utility to visible political membership, and the membership terms differ sharply by size, so a customer's relationship to the grid is becoming a marker of standing rather than a uniform service.

Forces:
1. Siting decisions converting energy policy into neighbourhood politics with ordinary land-use tools, evidenced by three separate in-window local refusals or moratoria in Pennsylvania, Oregon and Missouri within 48 hours of each other (sweep-grounded).
2. Community benefit converted into an administrative deliverable rather than a negotiation: Empire State Development's Community Investment Framework, required within 60 days of Executive Order 62, and the DPS Data Center Interconnection Working Group on the same clock (institutional).
3. Asymmetric exit becoming socially legible, as the largest participants design for on-site generation and battery storage (SpaceX stating its Texas TeraFab is not expected to use grid energy) while households, schools, clinics and small water systems have no equivalent option (sweep-grounded).
4. Reliability literacy spreading outward from utility engineers, as ride-through, voltage sag and backup transfer become terms that appear in local hearings, ratepayer advocacy and residential bill disputes rather than only in operating manuals.

### Technological
Core Shift Thesis:
The protective settings of privately owned equipment have become a system-level control variable, and the industry is moving to specify from outside what those settings must do, which reverses the historic assumption that a customer's internal protection is the customer's business.

Forces:
1. Ride-through performance moving from operator practice toward enforceable standard: FERC has directed NERC to submit a first phase of large load reliability standards by 31 Dec 2026, with minimum ride-through requirements for computational loads expected in 2027 NERC standards (sweep-grounded, institutional).
2. Interconnection agreements acquiring performance obligations rather than only capacity and cost terms, following PJM's assessment of interconnection ride-through standards after roughly 3,800 MW self-disconnected in two waves (2,970 MW then 1,099 MW) during a normally cleared 230 kV fault on 22 Jul 2026 (sweep-grounded).
3. On-site generation and battery storage designed as primary supply rather than backup, which converts a facility from a load with a generator into a self-balancing island that interacts with the grid by choice.
4. Event forensics as the scarce technical capability, since the July 2024, February 2025 and July 2026 events are known to be comparable only because a grid operator reconstructed them after the fact, and no standing telemetry obligation on large loads currently produces that record.

### Legal
Core Shift Thesis:
Obligations on large computational load are being created by consent and condition (pledges, permits, interconnection agreements) months to years before they exist as rules, so the legal character of the same duty differs by state, by facility and by date of queue entry.

Forces:
1. Section 206 show-cause proceedings as the formal path: six FERC dockets opened 18 Jun 2026 with RTO and ISO response filings due 17 Aug 2026, against which any merits order determines whether large load terms are tariffed rather than negotiated (institutional).
2. Voluntary company commitments awaiting conversion into binding conditions, with the open question being whether the Public Utility Commission of Texas opens a rulemaking making the Data Center Standards conditions of interconnection approval rather than pledges (sweep-grounded).
3. Environmental review used as the gating legal instrument rather than energy law, with New York routing a data center moratorium through DEC permits and a Generic Environmental Impact Statement and giving the moratorium no expiry independent of that document (sweep-grounded).
4. Vintage effects becoming the dominant legal fact, as facilities sited and financed under 2026 rules will operate for decades alongside facilities bound by 2027 and 2028 standards, producing a fleet governed by the year of its queue position.

### Environmental
Core Shift Thesis:
Environmental review has become the effective venue for decisions that are actually about electricity and money, because it is the process with the strongest procedural hooks and the longest lawful delay.

Forces:
1. Generic Environmental Impact Statement as an indefinite gate, with New York's moratorium running until DPS submits the final GEIS and findings statement and DEC directed to review water withdrawal regulations within 12 months (sweep-grounded, institutional).
2. Water reuse written into siting approval as a named condition of Abbott's Data Center Standards, alongside disclosure of water sourcing before approval, which puts a resource constraint into an energy permitting decision (sweep-grounded).
3. Combustion shifted behind the fence as backup generation carries real load, since a self-disconnection event of roughly 3,800 MW transfers that demand onto on-site generation whose emissions and permitting are governed as standby rather than as generation.
4. Local siting criteria consolidating around cumulative impact (noise, land, water, residential disruption) rather than a single pollutant, which is why refusals in Allentown, Hillsboro and Pacific rest on ordinary land-use grounds rather than on grid arguments.
