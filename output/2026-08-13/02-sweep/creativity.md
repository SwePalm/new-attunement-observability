# Evidence sweep, creativity, 2026-08-13

Delta Since Last Sweep:
- The window is nine days (2026-08-04 to 2026-08-13) and produced two substantive events, both from actors already standing in the ledger: Suno announced a global licensing alliance with BMG on 12 Aug 2026, and Spotify announced an AI Persona badge on 11 Aug 2026. A third, smaller item: Suno announced download caps and a Terms of Service change on 10 Aug 2026, effective 3 Sep 2026.
- Both events moved through channels the standing claims did not name. Suno responded to the 31 Jul 2026 Munich judgment by licensing a Berlin-headquartered rights holder and settling prior use, rather than appealing (creativity-2026-08-07) or licensing the winning collecting society (creativity-2026-08-08). Spotify shipped the recommendation-exclusion mechanism named in creativity-2026-08-03 but scoped it to synthetic artist identity rather than to how the music was made.
- No movement on nine other open claims. No EU market surveillance authority or the AI Office has opened an Article 50 marking or labelling proceeding since the duties applied on 2 Aug 2026. No US lawsuit filed after 31 Jul 2026 pleading DMCA section 1201(a) was identified. Neither Billboard nor the Official Charts Company has issued AI chart eligibility rules. Part 3 of the Copyright Office report remains in pre-publication form. The DSIT labelling taskforce interim report is unpublished.
- Deal-value opacity persisted rather than resolved. The BMG alliance discloses no consideration, matching the pattern flagged in this run's grading against creativity-2026-07-01.

Confirmed Developments:
- BMG and Suno announced a global strategic alliance on 12 Aug 2026 covering BMG's recorded music and music publishing repertoire, settling prior use of BMG recordings and publishing works, with participation structured as an opt-in for BMG artists and songwriters who choose to take part; no financial consideration was disclosed, BMG is the second major rights holder to license Suno after Warner Music Group nine months earlier, and Universal Music Group and Sony Music remain in ongoing US litigation against Suno over past model training (source: https://www.bmg.com/news/bmg-and-suno-announce-global-strategic-alliance-advancing-ai-music-opportunities-and-revenue-streams, Aug 2026) (source: https://www.musicbusinessworldwide.com/suno-inks-global-licensing-deal-with-bmg/, Aug 2026)
- Spotify announced on 11 Aug 2026 an AI Persona badge marking artist profiles whose public identity may be AI-generated and does not represent a real person; the badge appears on artist profiles, in search results and on track rows, excludes flagged artists from editorial, algorithmic and personalised recommendations by default unless a listener actively follows them, opens for artist self-disclosure through Spotify for Artists immediately, and becomes visible to listeners in mid-September 2026 (source: https://newsroom.spotify.com/2026-08-11/ai-persona-badges-transparency/, Aug 2026) (source: https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/11/spotify-will-label-ai-persona-profiles-and-exclude-their-music-from-recommendations/, Aug 2026)

Emerging Signals:
- Suno stated on 10 Aug 2026 that its next generation of models is being developed in partnership with the music industry and that all prior models will be retired when the new models launch, with existing user-created songs remaining accessible and usable for remixes; this makes licensed models a replacement for the unlicensed stack rather than an addition to it (source: https://suno.com/blog/suno-updates-tos, Aug 2026)
- Spotify's badge is not disclosure-only: the platform will proactively review artist profiles that meet pre-defined audience thresholds for photorealistic AI-generated identities, notify the profiles it identifies, and allow self-disclosure or appeal, which puts a streaming service in the position of adjudicating whether a performing identity is real (source: https://newsroom.spotify.com/2026-08-11/ai-persona-badges-transparency/, Aug 2026)

Counter-Signals:
- Spotify stated explicitly that the badge concerns the artist's public identity and not how the music was made, and routed information about the music's own production to separate voluntary disclosure features (AI Credits and SongDNA), so the enforcement-backed control surface covers synthetic performers while synthetic audio by disclosed human artists remains on the self-reporting track (source: https://newsroom.spotify.com/2026-08-11/ai-persona-badges-transparency/, Aug 2026)
- The licensing channel remains narrow and slow: BMG is only the second major rights holder to sign with Suno in the nine months since Warner, Universal and Sony are still litigating rather than licensing, and the BMG consideration is undisclosed, so the deal cannot be scored on value and does not indicate a market rate (source: https://www.musicbusinessworldwide.com/suno-inks-global-licensing-deal-with-bmg/, Aug 2026)
- Whether licensing revenue reaches performers is contested rather than settled: the American Federation of Musicians filed a first amended complaint in the Southern District of New York on 24 Jul 2026 against Universal Music Group, Warner Records and Atlantic Recording, alleging that the labels took settlement proceeds and licensing revenue from the Suno and Udio deals without sharing them with the musicians whose recordings were used, under the "new use" provision of Article 21 of the Sound Recording Labor Agreement (source: https://www.musicbusinessworldwide.com/us-musicians-union-files-amended-lawsuit-against-universal-and-warner-over-suno-and-udio-ai-deals/, Jul 2026)

Technical Changes:
- Suno announced on 10 Aug 2026 that from 3 Sep 2026 downloads are capped by tier (7 lifetime trial downloads for free users, personal use only; 20 per month on Pro; 60 per month on Premier; unlimited within Suno Studio for Premier), with additional downloads purchasable and re-downloads of the same song not counting against quota, stating the purpose is to make it harder for bad actors to mass-export music for upload to streaming services; new Terms of Service take effect the same day, clarifying commercial usage rights on paid plans and updating dispute resolution (source: https://suno.com/blog/suno-updates-tos, Aug 2026) (source: https://www.musicbusinessworldwide.com/suno-limits-subscribers-downloads-per-month/, Aug 2026)

Contradictions:
- Suno settled prior use of BMG's catalogue on 12 Aug 2026 while contesting the same category of past training against Universal and Sony in US court, so identical conduct is being priced and litigated simultaneously.
- Spotify's exclusion mechanism binds on synthetic identity while the volume problem recorded at scale is synthetic audio (more than 50% of daily uploads at Deezer in Jun 2026); the throttle actually aimed at upload volume in this window came from the generator, Suno's download caps, not from the distribution platform.
- Suno is under a Munich injunction covering GEMA repertoire and holds no GEMA licence, while signing a global alliance with a Berlin-headquartered rights holder in the same jurisdiction twelve days later.
- The BMG alliance is announced as generating new revenue for artists and songwriters at the same time as an active union suit alleges that the previous round of AI settlements and licences produced no such flow to musicians.
