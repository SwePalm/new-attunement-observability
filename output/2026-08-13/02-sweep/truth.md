# Evidence sweep, truth, 2026-08-13

Delta Since Last Sweep:
- Nine days since the 2026-08-04 sweep. The only structural movement is on the vendor side, not the regulatory side: Anthropic published its Article 50(2) marking policy on 11 Aug 2026 and became the first frontier lab to apply an imperceptible machine-readable mark to generated text rather than only to image and audio files.
- The 2026-08-04 sweep listed OpenAI, Google, Adobe, Meta, ElevenLabs and Stability AI as having deployed watermarking or credentials, without separating text from images. That distinction now matters: OpenAI has still not released the text watermark it has held internally, so the compliance line runs through modality, not through company.
- Absent from the 2026-08-04 sweep entirely: the statistical-detection channel, which answers the same question by a different route. Pangram closed a 9 million dollar round on 29 Jul 2026, shipped new text and image models, and powers reader-triggered AI scanning on Substack, whose writer community was still publicly split over it on 12 Aug 2026.
- No enforcement movement in either jurisdiction. No Commission or national market surveillance action under Article 50, no California Attorney General, city attorney or county counsel filing under SB 942, and no banking association or prudential regulator mandate on out-of-band verification.

Confirmed Developments:
- Anthropic signed the EU AI Act Article 50(2) Code of Practice on Transparency of AI-generated Content as a provider of both generative AI models and generative AI systems; new Claude models deployed in the EU on or after 2 Aug 2026 carry embedded watermarks and digitally signed provenance metadata from launch, models released before that date fall under the Code's transition period with marking support still being added, and generated .svg, .png and .jpg files carry signed provenance metadata following the C2PA standard (source: https://support.claude.com/en/articles/16266773-how-claude-marks-ai-generated-content, Aug 2026) (source: https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/11/anthropic-says-it-will-watermark-text-generated-by-its-ai-models/, Aug 2026)
- The marking is applied at model level rather than product level and covers the Claude platform API, Claude, Claude Code, Claude Cowork and Claude Tag; Anthropic enabled it everywhere Claude is available rather than only in the EU, and users cannot opt out (source: https://searchengineland.com/claude-ai-text-watermarking-484667, Aug 2026) (source: https://www.forbes.com/sites/maryroeloffs/2026/08/11/claude-will-put-invisible-watermarks-on-ai-text-and-images-and-the-internet-isnt-happy/, Aug 2026)
- Substack shipped reader-triggered AI-text detection powered by Pangram, covering posts and notes published on or after 21 Jul 2026 and text over 100 words, and added a per-post writer disable control on 23 Jul 2026; the feature was still publicly dividing the writer community on 12 Aug 2026, with CEO Chris Best framing the target as low-effort AI output rather than AI use as such (source: https://on.substack.com/p/how-writers-are-reacting-to-substacks, Jul 2026) (source: https://www.kunc.org/2026-08-12/substacks-new-ai-detection-feature-has-divided-its-community, Aug 2026)

Emerging Signals:
- The public objection to text marking is landing on the individual user rather than on the provider: reaction across r/artificial, r/Anthropic and r/ClaudeAI centred on workplace and academic AI use becoming visible, and on X objections came from users who had Claude proofread their own writing and from coders worried about signed metadata in outputs (source: https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/12/some-claude-users-are-mad-that-anthropics-new-watermarks-will-catch-them-cheating-at-their-jobs-classes/, Aug 2026)
- Detection is consolidating as a paid third-party layer with customers on both sides of publication: Pangram names Substack, Quora, universities, publishers and recruiters as clients, which puts the same detector in the hands of platforms, gatekeepers and hiring screens (source: https://theaiinsider.tech/2026/08/07/ai-detection-startup-pangram-announces-9m-in-funding-launches-new-text-and-image-detection-models/, Aug 2026)
- False-positive incidence is being raised as a distributional question rather than an accuracy question: on the Substack rollout, Professor Sam Illingworth objected that scanning creates an environment of suspicion and that non-native English speakers and neurodiverse writers, whose structured prose reads as machine-like, carry the cost in lost income (source: https://www.kunc.org/2026-08-12/substacks-new-ai-detection-feature-has-divided-its-community, Aug 2026)

Counter-Signals:
- The mark that satisfies the transparency duty cannot yet be read by anyone outside Anthropic. The company's own documentation says detection mechanisms will be described in forthcoming technical documentation and gives no date, so on the day the duty became operative the marked content is indistinguishable from unmarked content for every third party (source: https://support.claude.com/en/articles/16266773-how-claude-marks-ai-generated-content, Aug 2026)
- The largest text-generation provider has not followed. OpenAI, a signatory of the same Code, has held a text detector reported at 99.9 percent accuracy for about two years without releasing it, citing bypass through translation and rewriting and harm to particular user groups (source: https://the-decoder.com/anthropic-watermarks-all-claude-outputs-globally-with-marks-that-may-persist-through-some-editing/, Aug 2026)

Regulatory Shifts:
- None

Capital Movements:
- Pangram Labs raised a 9 million dollar round led by Menlo Ventures with participation from Haystack, ScOp Venture Capital, Script Capital and Cadenza, announced 29 Jul 2026, bringing total raised to about 13 million dollars, with the funding directed at text-detection accuracy and expansion into new modalities starting with images (source: https://siliconangle.com/2026/07/29/pangram-labs-raises-9m-launch-accurate-ai-detection-text-images/, Jul 2026) (source: https://theaiinsider.tech/2026/08/07/ai-detection-startup-pangram-announces-9m-in-funding-launches-new-text-and-image-detection-models/, Aug 2026)

Technical Changes:
- Pangram 4 and Pangram Image detect without watermarks or metadata: the text model compares authentic human writing against topic-matched and tone-matched AI versions and analyses stylistic pattern, the image model uses pixel-level statistical analysis, and the company claims a 0.0041 percent false-positive rate, about one error per 24,000 documents, and over 99 percent accuracy on AI-assisted and mixed human-AI writing (source: https://siliconangle.com/2026/07/29/pangram-labs-raises-9m-launch-accurate-ai-detection-text-images/, Jul 2026) (source: https://theaiinsider.tech/2026/08/07/ai-detection-startup-pangram-announces-9m-in-funding-launches-new-text-and-image-detection-models/, Aug 2026)
- Anthropic states the failure modes of its own mark: the text signal survives copy and paste and may persist through some editing, but substantial rewriting of the words and tokens, including rewriting by a different AI model, defeats it, file metadata is lost through format conversion or a screenshot, and a detected mark shows only that content may have been processed by Claude rather than establishing authorship (source: https://support.claude.com/en/articles/16266773-how-claude-marks-ai-generated-content, Aug 2026) (source: https://searchengineland.com/claude-ai-text-watermarking-484667, Aug 2026)

Contradictions:
- Statute led and the vendor followed. Anthropic marked because of a date set in EU law, which inverts the private-ordering-first pattern this corpus normally records, yet the same statute has produced no enforcement action in the eleven days since it became operative in either the EU or California.
- The mark satisfying the transparency obligation is unreadable by the public it exists to inform, while the detector that is readable today reads no mark at all and works by statistical inference over style.
- Anthropic applied marking worldwide and removed the user opt-out to comply with a regional obligation, so the strictest jurisdiction set the global default, while the largest provider of the same modality complied with the same voluntary Code without shipping the same control.
- Detection accuracy is claimed at the document level (one false positive per 24,000 documents) while the objection is at the person level, since the writers most likely to be misread are identifiable groups rather than random draws.
