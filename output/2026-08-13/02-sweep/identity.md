# Evidence sweep, identity, 2026-08-13

Scope note: the previous sweep ran on 2026-08-04, nine days ago. This sweep
covers 2026-08-04 to 2026-08-13 and is deliberately thin. Sections with weak
evidence are marked None rather than padded.

Delta Since Last Sweep:
- The OpenID Foundation moved from drafting to certification. On 7 Aug 2026 it declared the OpenID4VP and OpenID4VCI conformance test suites complete and open for self-certification. The 2026-08-04 sweep recorded only drafting-stage standards work (COAZ, COAZ-MCP, Ephemeral Subject Identifier, WebAuthn Level 3). This is the first certification-stage item in this theme's record.
- Three OIDF specification ballots opened inside the window, with voting periods that close in early Sep 2026. This is the first time this theme carries observable events inside a single grading interval, which the 2026-08-13 grading pass recorded as structurally absent.
- The MCP interoperability event's participation commitment deadline (10 Aug 2026) passed inside the window with no public participant list. The event schedule was verified directly against the OIDF call for participation: commit by 10 Aug 2026, demonstrate at least one successful cross-partner test by 16 Oct 2026, results presented by OIDF and Gartner at the Gartner IAM Summit in Dec 2026.
- Correction to the previous sweep rather than new world motion: Microsoft Entra Agent ID was already generally available, with agent identity constructs, governance, Conditional Access templates and non-Microsoft integration paths for AWS Bedrock, GCP and n8n. The 2026-08-04 sweep dated agent identity's arrival as a shipping enterprise category to Jul 2026 via Okta, which named a follower rather than the first mover.

Confirmed Developments:
- Digital identity wallet protocols reached independent conformance testing on 7 Aug 2026: the OpenID Foundation opened self-certification for OpenID4VP and OpenID4VCI used with the High Assurance Interoperability Profile, free to run and chargeable only to go on the record, after the Digital Credentials Protocols Working Group confirmed both suites ready in Jul 2026; the protocols are in use across more than 30 jurisdictions including all EU member states via eIDAS 2.0, the UK, Switzerland, India and California, and OIDF Executive Director Gail Hodges stated that until now there was no independent way for governments or regulators to verify that different implementations would work together or meet security requirements (source: https://openid.net/openid4vp-and-openid4vci-conformance-tests-are-complete-and-open-for-self-certification/, Aug 2026) (source: https://www.biometricupdate.com/202608/openid-opens-certification-for-digital-id-issuance-verification-protocols, Aug 2026)

Emerging Signals:
- Three OIDF ballots opened in the window with vote windows closing in early Sep 2026: the proposed Implementer's Draft of OpenID Connect Key Binding (notice 10 Aug 2026, 45 day review 9 Jul to 23 Aug 2026, voting 24 Aug to 7 Sep 2026) and two OpenID Federation extensions (notice 7 Aug 2026, voting 21 Aug to 4 Sep 2026) (source: https://openid.net/notice-of-vote-for-proposed-implementers-draft-of-openid-connect-key-binding/, Aug 2026) (source: https://openid.net/notice-of-vote-to-approve-proposed-implementers-drafts-of-two-openid-federation-extensions/, Aug 2026)
- National digital identity moved from framework approval to live load testing: the Czech Digital and Information Agency asked citizens to open the eDoklady app simultaneously at 13:00 on 13 Aug 2026 to measure performance under a sudden surge of concurrent logins ahead of municipal elections, after the 2025 parliamentary elections saw 1.5 million requests and demand that agency director Martin Mesrsmid put at half to two thirds above estimates (source: https://www.biometricupdate.com/202608/czechia-enlists-citizens-to-stress-test-digital-id-ahead-of-elections, Aug 2026)

Counter-Signals:
- The agent identity leader named in the previous sweep was not the first mover: Microsoft Entra Agent ID is generally available with agent identity blueprints, sponsor and owner accountability relationships, lifecycle workflows for orphaned agents, Conditional Access templates for autonomous and on-behalf-of agents, and documented integration for AWS Bedrock, GCP and n8n, while the admin center blueprint creation wizard remains labelled Preview (source: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/entra/agent-id/whats-new-agent-id, Jun 2026)
- Certification maturity is asymmetric between human and agent identity: OIDF opened self-certification for the human credential protocols in the same week its agent-side MCP interoperability event passed its 10 Aug 2026 commitment deadline with no participant list published and no test results due until 16 Oct 2026 (source: https://openid.net/call-for-participation-demonstrate-mcp-based-ai-agent-security-with-open-identity-standards-2/, Jul 2026)

Regulatory Shifts:
- Age assurance enforcement moved from penalties to infrastructure on 11 Aug 2026: Texas Attorney General Ken Paxton obtained a court order directing domain registry operator Verisign to suspend the motherless.com domain for noncompliance with the state age verification law, with site owner Kick Online Entertainment required to comply or post a $9.14 million bond conditioned on implementing compliant age verification (source: https://www.biometricupdate.com/202608/texas-targets-internet-infrastructure-to-enforce-age-assurance-law, Aug 2026)
- Italy approved an interministerial decree establishing national guidelines and an experimental framework for the IT Wallet, published in the Gazzetta Ufficiale on 23 Jul 2026 and approved by the Presidency of the Council of Ministers with the finance and public administration ministries; technical specifications are to be drafted by the Department for Digital Transformation, applicants must report on security, reliability, organizational readiness and compliance with European standards, AgID oversees compliance through documentation requests, inspections and audits, and priority credentials include income indicators, educational credentials, residence data, health insurance cards, mobile driving licences and disability cards (source: https://www.biometricupdate.com/202608/italy-approves-it-wallet-framework-ahead-of-eidas-rollout, Aug 2026)

Capital Movements:
- The US federal government concentrated identity proofing spend on commercial vendors: LexisNexis Risk Solutions won approximately $218 million in GSA awards for Login.gov remote identity proofing on 12 Aug 2026, split as roughly $55 million across Functional Areas 1 and 2 (document authentication, biometric verification) and roughly $163 million for Functional Area 3 (identity resolution, attribute validation, behavioral intelligence, fraud detection), following a separate $163 million Login.gov award to Xcelerate and Socure in Jul 2026 (source: https://www.biometricupdate.com/202608/lexisnexis-wins-218m-in-login-gov-identity-proofing-awards, Aug 2026)
- Non-human identity consolidation repriced sharply, a movement the previous sweep did not capture: Cyera announced a letter of intent on 28 Jul 2026 to acquire Oasis Security for roughly $1 billion, about five times what SailPoint agreed six weeks earlier for Israeli non-human identity specialist Entro Security at a reported $200 million, with Cyera itself valued at $12 billion after a $600 million round and Oasis having raised roughly $195 million including a $120 million Series B in Mar 2026 (source: https://www.kuppingercole.com/blog/care/cyera-buys-its-way-into-identity, Jul 2026)

Technical Changes:
- Conformance became a runnable artifact rather than an event: the OIDF test suites can now be run free of charge by wallet providers, issuers, verifiers, government agencies and vendors, with self-certification for a fee, validated across multiple interoperability rounds that recorded over 90% passing on 153 OpenID4VP tests (May 2025), 87% on 47 OpenID4VCI pairs (Jul 2025), and at the Nov 2025 HAIP-focused event 98% for OpenID4VP against 73% and 82% for OpenID4VCI on different configurations (source: https://openid.net/openid4vp-and-openid4vci-conformance-tests-are-complete-and-open-for-self-certification/, Aug 2026)
- Deepfake detection shifted from generator-artifact matching to physical-consistency modelling: Resemble AI released DETECT-World on 12 Aug 2026, a third-generation model using a World-Vision Hybrid Encoder that returns a manipulation probability score, per-frame scores and a spatial heatmap, reporting 99.47% on audio, 98.2% on video, 95.8% on images, and 95% against Haotian-style real-time face-swap attacks that the article states leading detectors misclassified at rates near 100% (source: https://www.biometricupdate.com/202608/resemble-ai-expands-multimodal-deepfake-detection-approach-for-new-accuracy-highs, Aug 2026)

Contradictions:
- Human credential protocols reached independent certification in the same window that agent identity protocols had no published participants and no test results, so the identity layer being standardised fastest is the one for humans while deployment pressure is on agents.
- Governments moved in both directions at once: the US concentrated roughly $218 million on a single commercial identity proofing vendor for a centralized login service while Italy and Czechia stood up decentralized wallet frameworks and tested citizen-held credentials, in the same nine-day window.
- Detection accuracy claims rose while enforcement moved to blunt infrastructure controls: Resemble AI reported 95% to 99% detection accuracy on 12 Aug 2026 while Texas escalated on 11 Aug 2026 to suspending a domain at the registry, which implies verification at the endpoint is not trusted as the operative control.
- Microsoft ships generally available agent identity governance while the OIDF's cross-vendor agent authorization work is still pre-interop, so the operative agent identity controls in production are vendor-proprietary rather than standards-based.
