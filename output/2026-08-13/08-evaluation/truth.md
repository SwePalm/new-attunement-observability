## 3. Institutional Realism Audit

Assessment: **moderate realism**

Notes:

- Friction is present in the right places and is not decorative. The duty is in force and produces no enforcement anywhere in the narrative, which matches the graded record exactly (Article 50 enforceable since 2 Aug 2026 and California SB 942 operative from the same date, with no named action by the Commission, a national market surveillance authority, the California Attorney General, a city attorney or a county counsel as of 13 Aug 2026). The protective control exists and is not used, for a stated social reason. The reading tooling is late. Nothing in the world works as intended.
- Power asymmetry is the artifact's strongest realism feature. Three parties are visible and none of their interests align: the generator owes the duty, the gatekeeper buys the capability, the writer absorbs the error. The writer is never party to either transaction. That structure is inherited from the sweep rather than imposed on it.
- The setting choice is a genuine realism gain. Placing the artifact in Canada, where neither the EU regime nor the California statute reaches, and having the mark be present anyway because a firm applied a regional duty as a worldwide product default, is the correct dramatisation of the sweep's most distinctive fact. It also prevents the commencement date from becoming the structural engine, which it is not in this evidence.
- The world is too elegant in one identifiable way, and it is a consequence of the chosen form. The document passes through six stations and each station demonstrates exactly one documented property of the mark: generation attaches it, a photograph loses it, verbatim republication preserves it, a scan bypasses it, translation strips it, and late tooling reads it. That is a demonstration sequence rather than a life. Real documents do not arrange themselves into a syllabus of a technology's failure modes. The artifact partly earns this by keeping the sequence short and by letting the outcome be mundane, but the tidiness is real.
- A second elegance problem: the case is won, cleanly, in the spring, by the stripped copy. The inversion is the point, but the world offers no counter-instance in which the number was right, or in which stripping provenance served a deceiver rather than a truthful person. A world where the honest party benefits from erosion and no dishonest party does is not a fully modelled world.
- Transitions are implied rather than shown for the detector's spread from a publishing platform to admissions and hiring; section 5 asserts the installed base (which is upstream) but the narrative never touches those gates.

## 8. Overall Evaluation

Score (1–10): **7.2**

Forecast Integrity Level: **moderate**

Stability Risk: **medium**

Summary:

The artifact's epistemic strength comes from a single structural observation that the upstream evidence genuinely supports and that no previous scenario in this corpus has made: two instruments now answer the question "was this written by a machine," they have opposite failure profiles, one is compulsory, free and unreadable while the other is optional, paid and readable today, and neither of them answers the question anyone actually asks, which is whether to believe a person. Every element of that is quoted from provider documentation and vendor claims dated within the sweep window, including the concession that a detected mark evidences processing rather than authorship. The scenario converts the observation into lived mechanism without inventing an institution, and it holds a horizon it argues for rather than assumes, deriving it from Phase A's finding that this theme's claims cannot move inside a monthly cycle. That is the graded evidence doing forecasting work rather than decorating it.

Three weaknesses hold the score below eight. The load-bearing structural claim in section 5, that nothing accredits the detector and no one it scores has a right of reply, is an unverified negative inferred from the sweep's silence, which is the exact failure mode CAL-005 records against this pipeline's graders and which here migrates into world-building. The chain of custody is a demonstration sequence: six stations, each illustrating one documented property, which is legible and too tidy for a world. And the compression to a single logic costs the artifact the image side of the same announcement, where a working standards layer and a functioning reader exist, making the world look more uniformly unreadable than the record supports.

Stability risk is medium because the artifact's spine is a documented absence, the unshipped reader. If detection documentation ships early, the world's premise weakens quickly, though the deeper point about processing versus authorship would survive intact.
