## 3. Institutional Realism Audit

Assessment: moderate realism

Notes:
- Friction is present and unusually well distributed. Nothing in the world resolves: the French authority issues recommendations rather than remedies, the Dutch supplementary guidance is offstage, the instrument aimed at commercial ordering has not arrived, and the household's adaptation is explicitly inadequate to the problem it responds to. The scenario resists the temptation to have anyone win.
- Power asymmetry is visible and correctly located, on the axis of measurement rather than of intent. The seller measures the buyer continuously and privately; the buyer observes the seller through one foreign study. No actor in the world is villainous, which is the honest reading of the evidence.
- The principal realism deduction is that the closure is uncontested. Across a two-year interval the scenario shows no attempted challenge: no state attorney general, no merchant coalition or class action, no discount retailer differentiating on open access, no assistant marketing neutrality as a feature, and no defection by a large seller from the refusal posture. Real markets in a growth phase generate at least one such attempt, and its complete absence is what makes the world feel more settled than a 2028 date can support.
- Section 5's assertion that "penetration and closure grow together rather than trading off" is stated as a structural law without hedging, and it is an inference from posture rather than an observed rate. Section 5 marks a different, weaker inference explicitly and leaves this stronger one unmarked, which is the inconsistency worth naming.

## 4. Horizon Discipline Check

Pass/Fail: Pass

Notes:
- Horizon is late winter 2028, inside the 0-5 year bound, and it is argued from the evidence in section 2 rather than defaulted: one grocery-app adoption cycle after the Aug 2026 launches, and deliberately short of any European instrument aimed at commercial ordering being in force. This is a departure from the previous run, where all five scenarios were set in 2029 without justification.
- No sci-fi drift. No AGI, no new autonomy capability, no infrastructure that does not exist in Aug 2026. The technical content of the world is entirely retrieval, ranking, placement and checkout hand-off.
- Institutional shifts are proportional and mostly negative, which is the correct shape for a two-year window: the state does not acquire a new instrument, and the changes that occur are commercial and behavioural.
- Calibration check. CAL-002 (the pipeline overestimates the firmness of announced regulatory timelines) is applied as restraint rather than repeated: section 7.2 explicitly treats the Q4 2026 Digital Fairness Act indication as a text, then a negotiation, then a transition, and section 2 dates the scenario so that the instrument cannot have arrived. CAL-003 (non-state hardening leads statute) is respected: every mechanism holding the world together is private ordering, and no force in section 5 is a rule. CAL-004 is not directly applicable to a scenario artifact, but the run's retrodiction problem is engaged rather than routed around, since the scenario's subject is the gap between what happened and what the observer could see.
- One calibration bias is repeated rather than avoided, and it is scored against Signal Integrity below. CAL-005 records that this pipeline treats "not found" as "did not happen" with an Anglophone bias, and that this exact theme was burned by it when opinion 26-A-05 was missed. Section 5 and section 8 both assert that the French comparative count is the only one in the public record. That is the same inference in the opposite direction, made about the same corpus that already proved incomplete once. The scenario should have said that it is the only one this pipeline has retrieved.

## 5. Signal Integrity Check

Pass/Fail: Pass
Signal Anchoring Strength: strong

Notes:
- Six of six Signals Emerging bullets are present-day, dated and sourced upstream, and each is rephrased rather than invented. The strongest is the domain-count asymmetry, which is a measured comparative fact rather than a projection and is the only such fact this theme has ever carried.
- The scenario relies on no unobserved breakthrough. Every extension is incremental: more households using assistants that already exist, more retailers doing what two retailers did in one fortnight, and merchants paying for a visibility product already sold.
- The scenario also carries the sweep's hardest counter-signal instead of suppressing it. The sub-5% measured traffic figure appears in section 7.1 and again in the final insight, and the world is built to be consistent with a small measured share rather than with the 2030 projection, which is the discipline this check is for.
- Two deductions. The personalised-pricing assertion in section 4 has no upstream anchor. And the "only comparative count in the public record" framing in sections 5 and 8 converts a retrieval result into a fact about the world, which is the specific error this theme's grading pass recorded twice.

## 6. Narrative Inflation Risk

Assessment: moderate

Notes:
- The scenario is not dystopian, and its restraint is structural rather than tonal: the recommendations in the world are good, the assistants are genuinely useful, the merchant is not bitter, and the harm is explicitly unfelt by the person it lands on. This is a harder and more honest register than a scarcity narrative, and it avoids the emotional over-concentration the check targets.
- It does exaggerate coherence in one place. Section 5 offers three mutually reinforcing forces with no counterweight and no internal dissent, and section 7.2 extends them to 2031 along a single line. A market where every force points the same way for five years is a tidy market.
- Mild set-piece inflation in section 3: the four-block walk is given an explanatory sentence ("that is the entire reason she does it") that tells the reader what the scene already showed, which pushes the Irreplaceable Thing slightly toward moral instruction.
- The ending avoids the corpus's characteristic inflation move. It does not close by naming the scarce good, and it does not close on a paradox reversal. It closes on a timing claim and a small factual absence, which is a lower-temperature exit than the 2026-08-04 scenarios took.

## 7. Cross-Theme Convergence Risk

Assessment: moderate

Notes:
- Comparison data available: the five 2026-08-04 scenarios, of which trust and coordination were read in full for this evaluation.
- On surface form the convergence is broken on every axis the previous run shared. The protagonist is a household rather than a named mid-senior professional at a headcount-specified organisation; the frame is a Sunday shopping list rather than a workday of scheduled meetings; the date is 2028 rather than 2029; the setting is a US metropolitan suburb with no European entity in the structure; the load-bearing force is retail media economics and access posture rather than the 2 Aug 2026 EU commencement, which appears in neither section 5 nor the final insight; and the close is a statement about when the terms were set rather than a named scarce good.
- The deeper skeleton is not fully escaped, and it would be dishonest to score this as if it were. The trust scenario, the coordination scenario and this one all run the same argument: the authoritative record belongs to the strong party, the weak party improvises a folk practice at trivial scale, and the moral lands on a small unscalable human act. Ines keeps records nobody requires; Tomas pre-signs one decision a day; Rosalie walks four blocks. That is three variations on one move across two runs, and it is now the corpus's actual house form, one level below the surface conventions this scenario successfully broke.
- Tonal homogeneity is reduced but not absent. The registers differ (domestic and warm here, procedural and cold in the previous two), but the closing cadence still resolves into a quiet observation about what nobody is responsible for.

## 8. Overall Evaluation

Score (1-10): 7.4

Forecast Integrity Level: moderate

Stability Risk: medium

Summary:
The epistemic strength of this artifact rests on a single document, and that is both its distinction and its exposure. Opinion 26-A-05 supplies the only measured facts this theme has ever carried: a domain-count asymmetry between two assistants on identical queries, an outright access refusal by the largest marketplace, and a traffic share below 5% that contradicts every projection the theme had been running on. The scenario is built on those measurements rather than on the projections, which is the correct choice and a visible improvement on a corpus that has been narrating a market it had not measured. But a world resting on one foreign study inherits that study's coverage, and the scenario compounds this by asserting that no other measurement exists, which is a retrieval result stated as a fact about the world and the exact failure mode this theme's grading pass recorded twice in two runs.

The grounding is otherwise clean and unusually free of invention: no new regulator, protocol or product category appears, and the two grounding defects are specific and named rather than diffuse. The realism ceiling is set by the absence of any attempted challenge across a two-year interval, which makes a contested market look settled, and by one structural assertion presented as law when it is an inference from posture.

The horizon discipline is the strongest element. The scenario chooses a date and argues for it from the evidence, slows the regulatory clock in line with the pipeline's known bias rather than repeating it, and builds a world consistent with a small measured share rather than a large projected one. The forecast is fragile where the evidence base is narrow and sound where it is dated.
