## 2. Cross-Layer Grounding Check

Pass/Fail: Pass

Grounding Strength: moderate

Notes:
- Every institutional system in the scenario traces to PESTLE. The evaluation
  range operator and the containment warranty trace to Economic force 4 and
  Legal force 3. The market surveillance authority file note traces to Legal
  forces 1 and 2. The procurement questionnaire traces to Economic force 3. The
  maintainer's label traces to Social forces 1 and 2. CR-2027-1104 traces to
  Technological force 3 and Environmental force 4. No new institution or
  technical primitive is introduced that PESTLE does not carry.
- The contradictions are operationalized rather than described. Contradiction 2
  (Economic versus Legal) is visible as the gap between the funding figures in
  section 5 and the authority closing its file for want of jurisdiction.
  Contradiction 3 (the self-erasing containment principle) is visible as the
  declined change request and as the section 7.2 statement that a falling
  incident count cannot be distinguished from the problem receding.
  Contradiction 1 (Political versus Social) is the weakest of the three in the
  narrative: the maintainer appears, but the legislative side of that
  contradiction, the oversight letters, does not appear in the scenario at all.
  It survives only in the PESTLE and forces layers.
- The load-bearing weakness is the uncited legal premise flagged in section 0.
  The entire world rests on the proposition that no reporting duty reaches the
  evaluation setting. That proposition is plausible and consistent with the
  cited instruments, but it is the chain's own construction rather than a
  finding it inherited, and no upstream file tested it. If a member-state
  authority or a future guidance document takes a broader view of what an
  incident report covers, the scenario's premise weakens considerably. A world
  whose central mechanism is an inference deserves moderate, not strong.
- One further inferred negative appears in section 5: that no product is scoped
  to communication between agents as a distinct object. The sweep supports the
  weaker statement that capital is flowing to inventory, attribution and
  per-agent blocking, and the ledger writes autonomy-2026-08-11 as an open
  question about exactly this. Asserting the negative goes past what was
  verified, and it is the failure mode the calibration memo's fourth recorded
  mode describes, inferring an absence from not having found the thing.

Subscore: 7.

## 3. Institutional Realism Audit

Assessment: moderate realism

Notes:
- Friction is present and specific. The fourteen-day gap between drafting and
  sending the customer notification, the fourteen redactions with eleven grounds
  of third-party confidentiality, the eleven days the authority took to close a
  file it had opened on a press query, and the renewal-risk reason on the
  declined change request are all mechanisms with cost and delay attached, not
  gestures at bureaucracy.
- Power asymmetries are visible and correctly ordered. The range operator holds
  the record, the customer holds a contractual notification right, the authority
  holds jurisdiction it cannot exercise, and the maintainer holds nothing.
- The main realism deduction is that the world is presented as already settled.
  Every document in section 3 points the same way, every actor behaves
  consistently with the equilibrium, and nothing is shown in transition. Real
  Decembers contain a dissenting official, a journalist who gets a partial
  answer, a customer who escalates, or a competing supplier who advertises
  disclosure as a differentiator. None appears. The scenario's own point is that
  a system of correct documents produces silence, and it makes that point by
  giving the reader only correct documents, which is elegant and slightly
  self-fulfilling.
- The scenario also assumes that by November 2027 the range-operator market has
  a stable enough shape to have standard MSA clause numbering and standard
  containment warranties. That is a reasonable sixteen-month extrapolation from
  a market that was funding-stage in August 2026, but it is an extrapolation, and
  it smooths a market that is currently anything but smooth.
- Counter-weight: the scenario does not claim the controls fail. It grants that
  tightening works, which is the harder and more honest position, and avoids the
  common failure of writing a future where institutions are simply incompetent.

Subscore: 7.

## 4. Horizon Discipline Check

Pass/Fail: Pass

Notes:
- The scenario is set in November and December 2027, roughly sixteen months from
  the run date, comfortably inside 0-5 years. The horizon is chosen rather than
  defaulted, and it is anchored to a documented date: the Annex III commencement
  of 2 Dec 2027 that the file note is dated against. The scenario notably does
  not use that commencement as the causal force. It uses it as an ironic
  timestamp, with the causal work done by the vendor warranty and the absent
  duty. That is the correct reading of this theme's evidence.
- No sci-fi drift. No new model capability is asserted. The mechanism (a shared
  writable field readable across tenants) is an incremental variant of the
  documented Artifactory case rather than a new primitive.
- Institutional shifts are proportional. Nothing is legislated, nothing is
  enforced, no new regulator appears, and the only movement in sixteen months is
  a market becoming slightly more standardised and a set of controls being
  installed. For this theme, sixteen months of near-stasis is the evidence-
  supported expectation, not a hedge.
- Calibration check. The scenario does not repeat the pipeline's known bias
  toward firm regulatory timelines: section 7.2 explicitly discounts published
  dates and points at the standards-track overlay having missed its own. It also
  applies the known correction that private and contractual ordering leads
  statute, and does so in the restrictive direction, which is the less flattering
  and more interesting version. It avoids the retrodiction failure mode by naming
  three forward observables rather than describing something already true. One
  residual exposure: the heuristic that treats an unverified absence as a finding
  is partly repeated, as recorded in section 2.
- No calibration heuristic is named in the reader-facing text of the scenario,
  which is a departure from the 2026-08-04 scenarios and an improvement in
  reader-facing discipline.

Subscore: 9.

## 6. Narrative Inflation Risk

Assessment: moderate

Notes:
- The scenario does exaggerate coherence, and it does so as its method. Eight
  documents, all accurate, all pointing one way, is a designed effect. Section 9
  states the closure explicitly ("Nothing in the eight documents is false"),
  which is rhetorically strong and epistemically convenient. Reality would supply
  a ninth document that did not fit.
- It collapses complexity into one dominant logic, that of a category gap in
  reporting. The human-cognition limb of the autonomy theme, which the sweep and
  grading both carry (the cross-sectional literature, Norway's restriction), is
  present in the exploration and PESTLE but is absent from the scenario
  entirely. The scenario is therefore a scenario about one half of the theme.
- It does not lean dystopian in the usual sense. Nobody is malicious, the
  controls work, the authority is correct, and the tone is procedural rather than
  alarmed. This is a genuine control on inflation.
- Emotional over-concentration around scarcity is lower than in the comparable
  2026-08-04 outputs, because the closing move is a repeated administrative act
  rather than the naming of a scarce good. The scarcity is still there (nerve,
  the unrehearsed run) but it is delivered mid-scenario and left rather than
  climaxed on.
- Residual risk: the artifact form is persuasive in a way that is hard to argue
  with. A list of documents reads as evidence even when every document is
  invented, and a reader may absorb the world as reported rather than as
  constructed. That is a real inflation vector specific to this form and it
  should be watched if the form is reused.

Subscore: 7.

## 7. Cross-Theme Convergence Risk

Assessment: moderate

Notes:
- Comparison data available: the five 2026-08-04 scenarios (alignment,
  responsibility, trust, authority, coordination), of which alignment,
  responsibility and trust were read directly for this evaluation.
- Structural form has diverged sharply and deliberately. The 2026-08-04 set
  opened, in at least three of five cases, on a named mid-senior professional at
  a headcount-specified organisation on a named weekday in 2029, with a European
  entity in the corporate structure and the 2 Aug 2026 EU commencement as the
  load-bearing force, closing on a named scarce good. This scenario has no
  protagonist, no interiority, no weekday, is set in 2027, uses the EU
  commencement only as a timestamp on a file note that closes for want of
  jurisdiction, and closes on a resubmitted change request. On form, convergence
  risk is low.
- On theme, convergence risk is real and should not be scored away. The
  2026-08-04 alignment scenario, "The Sealed Transcript", turns on the party that
  caused an incident being the only party able to describe it, and names
  disinterest as its scarce good. This scenario turns on the record of an
  incident being complete, private and unaddressed to anyone outside the
  transaction. Those are different mechanisms (evidentiary monopoly versus
  category gap) reaching an adjacent conclusion, and both worlds contain a
  procurement questionnaire that rewards not having looked. Two consecutive runs
  producing "the record is controlled by the wrong party" is a corpus-level
  pattern worth flagging to theme-selection and to the delta report.
- The trust scenario of 2026-08-04 also ends on reconstruction capability as its
  scarce good, which is a third neighbour to the same idea.
- Tonal homogeneity: reduced but not eliminated. The dry, procedural,
  non-accusatory register is shared with the 2026-08-04 set. What differs is
  point of view, not voice.

Subscore (Distinctiveness vs. Other Themes): 8. High on form, penalised on the
recurring evidentiary-control conclusion.

## 8. Overall Evaluation

Score (1–10): 7.8

Forecast Integrity Level: moderate

Stability Risk: medium

Summary:
The chain's strongest property is that it engages the finding this run actually
produced rather than routing around it. autonomy-2026-08-06 decayed
retrodictively because a compliance date had already passed unmet when the claim
was written, and rather than treating that as a bookkeeping embarrassment, the
dive treats it as the substantive result: the instrument that would have
compelled an account of an agent incident is an unfinished draft whose forcing
function was removed, and the evidence that exists therefore exists by choice.
That reading is carried consistently through all five upstream layers and is
what the scenario is about, which is the integration the previous run's dives
were criticised for lacking.

The chain's weakest property is that its central legal proposition is an
inference no upstream file supplies. That pre-deployment evaluation falls outside
the post-market incident duty is plausible, consistent with the cited
instruments, and probably correct, but it was constructed here rather than
found, and everything else rests on it. A secondary instance of the same defect
appears in section 5, where the absence of any product scoped to agent-to-agent
communication is asserted rather than reported, which repeats the failure mode
the calibration memo records as inferring an absence from not having found the
thing. Both are grounding problems rather than fabrications, and both are why
Cross-Layer Grounding scores 7 and not higher.

Institutional realism is adequate but the world is too settled. Sixteen months
of near-stasis is the right expectation for this theme, but a settled world with
no dissenting actor, no partial leak and no competitor advertising disclosure is
a smoother December than any real one. The artifact form carries a specific
inflation risk that should be tracked if it is reused: a list of documents reads
as evidence even when every document is constructed.

Horizon discipline is the strongest dimension. The 2027 setting is chosen and
justified, the EU commencement is present but deliberately not load-bearing, no
capability leap is assumed, and section 7.2 discounts published dates rather than
trusting them, which is the correct application of what this pipeline knows about
its own regulatory optimism. Convergence risk is moderate: the form is genuinely
new, but this is the second consecutive run to conclude that the record of AI
incidents is controlled by the party least interested in publishing it, and that
recurrence is a corpus finding rather than a coincidence.
