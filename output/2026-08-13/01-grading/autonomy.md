Surprises:
- autonomy-2026-08-06 decayed on its first grading pass, and the decay was
  already visible when the claim was logged. The claim was written on 2026-08-04
  against a Sep 2025 consultation page, two days after the applicability date
  that was supposed to force publication had passed, and roughly a week after
  the Omnibus deferral entered into force. This is CAL-004's failure mode
  inverted: not a retrodicted confirmation but a retrodicted decay, a claim
  whose mechanism was dead before the ink dried. The sweep read the Commission's
  own draft page without checking whether the deadline it referenced still
  existed.
- autonomy-2026-08-07 has a near-miss retrodiction of the CAL-004 type. OpenAI
  published a named successor governance document, the Frontier Governance
  Framework, on 28 May 2026, more than two months before the claim was logged
  and before the July 2026 evaluation incident that motivated it. The claim
  survives only because of its second condition (explicit containment and
  isolation requirements for internal capability-evaluation environments), which
  the May document does not carry. A looser wording would have confirmed on
  contact with evidence predating the claim.
- The two regulatory claims in this batch fail in opposite directions and both
  support CAL-002. autonomy-2026-08-06 assumed a published compliance date was
  firm when it had already been vacated, while autonomy-2026-08-01 is anchored
  to a consultation whose own closing date makes resolution before Q4 2026
  arithmetically impossible. The pipeline is reading regulatory calendars as
  schedules rather than as intentions.
