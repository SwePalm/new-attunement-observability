## 8. Overall Evaluation

Score (1–10): **7.5**

Forecast Integrity Level: moderate

Stability Risk: medium

Summary:
The scenario passes the citation gate on every Confirmed Development and anchors all six emerging signals in dated, sourced sweep items. Its structure is compliant, including the declared vantage with its permitted clause, the assigned form and the word ranges. Its horizon is disciplined: it slows the supervisory clock, treats contractual pressure as the faster channel and labels that as inference. The principal weakness is that the premise of the world, that live exit rehearsals are rare and that a firm can run one, rests on a regulator's finding about untested exit strategies and on a checklist summary, so the scarcity mechanism for the Irreplaceable Thing is partly inferred and in one upstream sentence stated as fact. The institutional picture is moderately realistic: friction is visible in the approval, the failed test set and the region problem, but the providers are faceless, the exposed customers with no second provider are off-stage, and the drill resolves more cleanly than real migrations do. Narrative inflation is moderate because one logic (practice as continuity) organises everything. Distinctiveness is acceptable: the Irreplaceable Thing, vantage and form are new in kind, but the closing gesture of declining automation to keep a manual act in human hands echoes the security scenario, and the tolerated-cost motif echoes labor.
