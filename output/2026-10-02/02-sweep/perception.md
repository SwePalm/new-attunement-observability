Counter-Signals:

- SynthID Bio cannot yet resist deliberate tampering, and DeepMind itself recommends layering it with provenance metadata and central repositories (source: https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/2026/10/01/synthid-bio-watermark/, Oct 2026)

Technical Changes:
- Google DeepMind's SynthID Bio embeds a signature in both the amino acid sequence and predicted 3D structure of AI-designed proteins; lab tests on three binder targets (VEGF-A, SARS-CoV-2 RBD, PD-L1) found watermarked designs matched unwatermarked ones on hit rate and affinity; methods paper, code, in vitro data and model weights are being released, with Stanford's Hie lab and Arc Institute as early partners (source: https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/2026/10/01/synthid-bio-watermark/, Oct 2026)
