# Evidence sweep, intelligence, 2026-10-02

- The ARC-AGI-3 harness gap widened without moving the Standard-harness maximum: GPT-6.1 Sol scored 52.73% (Standard) and 96.37% (Provider Adapter), so the Standard maximum is still GPT-6 Astra's 62.7% (source: https://arcprize.org/results/openai-gpt-6-1-sol, Sep 2026)
- Prior sweep's Gemini-gap observable (no Google AI-R&D measure) did not move: the Gemini 4 Argon launch coverage opened this week carries no such figure (source: https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/09/30/google-deepmind-unveils-gemini-4-argon-with-1m-output-tokens-for-coding-knowledge-work-and-cyber-defense/amp/, Sep 2026)

None of the institutional ledger observables (intelligence-2026-08-02, -08-03, -08-04, -08-07) moved on a primary-source check (source: https://thenextweb.com/news/white-house-openai-anthropic-uk-ai-security-institute-models, Sep 2026) (source: https://metr.org/time-horizons/, May 2026)

Confirmed Developments:
- OpenAI released GPT-6.1 Sol on 29 Sep 2026 at DevDay: $2/$10 per million tokens, which it says is one-fifth of GPT-6 Astra's price, "nearly the same level of intelligence" as Astra on agentic coding, computer use and professional work, matching Astra on DeepSWE v1.1, available in ChatGPT Work and Codex and as gpt-6.1-sol in the API. A TechCrunch summary reports factual error rates at low reasoning effort falling from 11.4% to 7.7% (source: https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/29/openai-launches-gpt-6-1-sol-says-it-nearly-matches-gpt-6-astra-and-costs-less/, Sep 2026) (source: https://thenextweb.com/news/openai-gpt-6-1-sol-price-astra-devday, Sep 2026)
- ARC Prize lists GPT-6.1 Sol (released 29 Sep 2026) at 52.73% on ARC-AGI-3 under the Standard harness (max reasoning) and 96.37% under the Provider Adapter harness (xhigh), plus 96.5% on ARC-AGI-1 and 94.2% on ARC-AGI-2. The page text names the Provider Adapter as one that "preserves opaque reasoning state between requests and uses compaction" (source: https://arcprize.org/results/openai-gpt-6-1-sol, Sep 2026) (source: https://arcprize.org/blog/astra, Sep 2026)
- Google DeepMind announced Gemini 4 Argon on 30 Sep 2026: 1M-token output (up from 64K), 77.9% on DeepSWE v1.1 and first place on the Vals Index (68.9%) by Google's account, but 55.0% on FrontierSWE v2 against GPT-6 Astra's 65.5%. Access is limited to trusted cyber defenders through the Fairwind Program, with paid API and Google AI Ultra access "next" and no date; introductory pricing is $2/$10, rising to $4/$20 (source: https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/09/30/google-deepmind-unveils-gemini-4-argon-with-1m-output-tokens-for-coding-knowledge-work-and-cyber-defense/amp/, Sep 2026) (source: https://neuraltrust.ai/blog/gemini-4-argon, Sep 2026)

Emerging Signals:
- The Wall Street Journal reportedly found that OpenAI scrapped the planned GPT-6.1 Astra release, due in ChatGPT and Codex in October, over safety concerns raised by researchers in internal testing. OpenAI shipped the cheaper GPT-6.1 Sol the next day instead. The WSJ original could not be opened (paywall), OpenAI's own materials never documented a "GPT-6.1 Astra", and the specific concerns are not stated in the pages opened, so this is reported, not confirmed (source: https://runtimewire.com/article/wsj-reports-openai-scrapped-gpt-6-1-astra-over-safety-concerns, Sep 2026) (source: https://thedailyguardian.com/business/openai-shelves-new-ai-model-after-internal-safety-tests-wsj-reports-2-761221/, Sep 2026)
- The frontier is splitting into a restricted top tier and cheap near-frontier tiers: GPT-6.1 Sol sells near-Astra capability at one-fifth the price while the most capable cyber-relevant models (Gemini 4 Argon, Anthropic's Mythos 5.1) are gated to vetted users (source: https://thenextweb.com/news/openai-gpt-6-1-sol-price-astra-devday, Sep 2026) (source: https://thenextweb.com/news/white-house-openai-anthropic-uk-ai-security-institute-models, Sep 2026)

Counter-Signals:

The live Artificial Analysis index page opened today lists neither Argon nor GPT-6.1 Sol (source: https://neuraltrust.ai/blog/gemini-4-argon, Sep 2026) (source: https://artificialanalysis.ai/evaluations/artificial-analysis-intelligence-index, Oct 2026)
- Public measurement of capability velocity still lags releases: METR's time-horizon page remains marked last updated 8 May 2026, with Claude Mythos Preview as its newest model, across a period with at least eight frontier releases (source: https://metr.org/time-horizons/, May 2026)

Regulatory Shifts:
- The White House Office of the National Cyber Director asked OpenAI and Anthropic to withhold new frontier models from the UK AI Security Institute until the US government finishes its own review ("they're American companies and this has been our policy with every new frontier model"). Anthropic complied for Mythos 5.1 (US organizations only); OpenAI declined to comment; the UK said it will keep testing advanced models. Reported 25 Sep 2026 by outlets that appear to draw on the same Bloomberg report (source: https://thenextweb.com/news/white-house-openai-anthropic-uk-ai-security-institute-models, Sep 2026) (source: https://itpro.com/security/openai-and-anthropic-snub-uks-ai-security-institute-on-new-model-testing, Sep 2026)
