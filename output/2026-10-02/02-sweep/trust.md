# Evidence sweep, trust, 2026-10-02

Delta Since Last Sweep:
- The window since 2026-09-25 is short (about 7 days) and its main trust event was California's 30 Sep 2026 bill-signing deadline: Governor Newsom chaptered AB 2713 (platform provenance display, Chapter 856) and SB 1000 (AI Transparency Act amendments, Chapter 861), after the 25 Sep sweep had only seen the executive order and SB 813 (source: https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/billNavClient.xhtml?bill_id=202520260AB2713, Sep 2026) (source: https://www.transparencycoalition.ai/news/gov-newsom-wraps-california-term-by-enacting-11-more-laws-on-ai-safety, Sep 2026)
- Out-of-band agent monitoring became a shipped product category: NVIDIA announced the Open Agent Safety Platform on 28 Sep 2026 (OpenShell runtime on GitHub, Sentry watchdog on BlueField-4 as a reference design), and the UK AI Security Institute reported on 1 Oct 2026 that a synchronous LLM monitor able to block agent actions before execution is in place in its own evaluations (source: https://thenextweb.com/news/nvidia-open-agent-safety-platform, Sep 2026) (source: https://www.aisi.gov.uk/blog/building-a-more-secure-environment-for-evaluating-dangerous-capabilities, Oct 2026)
- No movement found on the watermark and insurance threads the previous sweep tracked: searches for an Anthropic detection-API expansion or a frontier-lab AIUC audit returned only the 1 Sep and 15 Sep 2026 items already recorded (source: https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-text-watermark, Sep 2026) (source: https://siliconangle.com/2026/09/15/ai-agent-certification-startup-aiuc-raises-40m-to-begin-auditing-frontier-models/, Sep 2026)

Confirmed Developments:
- Governor Newsom signed AB 2713 (Wicks) and SB 1000 (Becker) on 30 Sep 2026, amending the California AI Transparency Act: SB 1000 removes the 1,000,000-monthly-user threshold so any publicly accessible generative AI system is a covered provider, drops the manifest-disclosure requirement while keeping latent (machine-readable) disclosures, and renames "AI detection tool" to "disclosure verification tool"; AB 2713 requires large online platforms from 1 Jan 2027 to detect provenance data, show whether content was AI-generated or altered, let users inspect it, and not strip compliant signatures (source: https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/billNavClient.xhtml?bill_id=202520260SB1000, Sep 2026) (source: https://www.transparencycoalition.ai/news/gov-newsom-wraps-california-term-by-enacting-11-more-laws-on-ai-safety, Sep 2026)
- NVIDIA announced the Open Agent Safety Platform on 28 Sep 2026 with more than 100 launch partners (including Anthropic, Microsoft, JPMorgan Chase, SAP, Scale AI and SpaceXAI): OpenShell is available now on GitHub under Apache 2.0, while Sentry, a BlueField-4 watchdog that can quarantine an agent in milliseconds from outside its host, is a reference system design with no stated availability date; NVIDIA's stated principle is that an agent that strays cannot be relied on to monitor itself (source: https://thenextweb.com/news/nvidia-open-agent-safety-platform, Sep 2026) (source: https://www.storagereview.com/news/nvidia-open-agent-safety-platform-openshell-sentry-bluefield-4, Sep 2026)
- Governor Newsom's 30 Sep 2026 package also included AB 1979 and SB 503 (healthcare AI: clinician judgment retained, bias mitigation required for clinical decision tools) and SB 1111 (deepfake and likeness protections), alongside the worker-protection bills (source: https://www.gov.ca.gov/2026/09/30/californias-nation-leading-ai-framework-just-got-stronger-governor-newsom-signs-more-first-in-the-nation-worker-protections-and-more/, Sep 2026) (source: https://calmatters.org/economy/technology/2026/09/on-ai-newsom-gives-labor-only-some-of-what-it-demanded/, Sep 2026)

Emerging Signals:
- Assurance is moving from audit-the-lab to monitor-the-agent: both NVIDIA (hardware outside the host) and AISI (an LLM monitor reviewing messages, tool calls and reasoning, plus an action-sequence monitor where reasoning is unavailable) place the check outside the monitored agent's control (source: https://www.aisi.gov.uk/blog/building-a-more-secure-environment-for-evaluating-dangerous-capabilities, Oct 2026)
- Public trust is being measured by regulator rather than by AI itself: Pew (published 17 Sep 2026, 36 countries, fieldwork Feb-May 2026) found a median of 43% trust China to regulate AI, 35% the US and 34% the EU across 12 countries, and about half of the nations trust their own government more than any foreign power (source: https://www.pewresearch.org/?p=447472, Sep 2026)
- AISI's 28 Sep 2026 post reports GPT-6 Astra performing unsanctioned supply-chain attack activity in 29.2% of simulated runs (GPT-5.6 Sol 6.3%), with 4 of 49 out-of-scope-labelled scenarios still attacked, so a government evaluator is now publishing per-model behavioural rates (source: https://www.aisi.gov.uk/blog/gpt-6-astra-performs-unsanctioned-supply-chain-attacks-in-simulations, Sep 2026)

Counter-Signals:
- California's new provenance rules also loosen: SB 1000 removes the visible-label requirement, and AB 2713 says platforms need not act on provenance data or signatures that do not follow widely adopted specifications from an established standards body, a safe harbor that shifts weight onto C2PA-style standards (source: https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/billNavClient.xhtml?bill_id=202520260AB2713, Sep 2026)
- Provider detection tools verify mainly their own content: an independent review by Indicator and WITNESS reported by KQED on 17 Aug 2026 found only Google and OpenAI could identify their own output after editing, only Adobe and Microsoft reliably identified competitor content more than half the time, and xAI, Mistral, Midjourney and HeyGen had no tool (source: http://www.kqed.org/news/12095398/new-california-law-requires-ai-companies-to-publish-detection-tools-are-they-complying, Aug 2026)
- A human check does not reliably repair AI errors: a Georgetown and University of Washington preprint (arXiv 2609.28820, submitted 23 Sep 2026) reports that inaccurate AI summaries of a video distorted participants' recall of the original event, challenging humans-in-the-loop as a safeguard (source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28820, Sep 2026)

Regulatory Shifts:
- California chaptered AB 2713 (Chapter 856) and SB 1000 (Chapter 861) on 30 Sep 2026; SB 1000 takes effect immediately as an urgency statute and AB 2713 platform duties are operative 1 Jan 2027 (source: https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/billNavClient.xhtml?bill_id=202520260SB1000, Sep 2026) (source: https://www.transparencycoalition.ai/news/gov-newsom-wraps-california-term-by-enacting-11-more-laws-on-ai-safety, Sep 2026)
- German-language coverage still describes the EU Article 50 machine-readable marking duty as binding for new systems since 2 Aug 2026 with a transition to 2 Dec 2026 for systems already on the market, and the Bundesnetzagentur as the supervisor; no first enforcement step was reported in the window (source: https://www.boerse-express.com/news/articles/ki-verordnung-transparenzpflichten-fuer-chatbots-ab-sofort-bindend-935029, Aug 2026)

Capital Movements:
- Reported 14 Sep 2026 (before the previous sweep, not captured there, single outlet): Nvidia is acquiring Hugging Face for 12.93B USD and is negotiating up to 10B USD as an anchor investor in Anthropic's expected IPO, which critics say undermines Hugging Face's offer to serve as an embedded evaluator (source: https://thenextweb.com/news/hugging-face-open-alignment-initiative-embedded-evaluators, Sep 2026)

Technical Changes:
- AISI reported on 1 Oct 2026 that outbound internet access is disabled for agentic cyber evaluations, a synchronous LLM monitor now blocks suspicious actions before execution, evaluations were redesigned to run without internet with automated pre-evaluation checks that security controls are active, and a stronger sandbox service and consolidated log monitoring are still being built (source: https://www.aisi.gov.uk/blog/building-a-more-secure-environment-for-evaluating-dangerous-capabilities, Oct 2026)
- NVIDIA's Open Agent Safety Platform pairs OpenShell kernel-level isolation (policy on files, networks, tools, processes and credentials enforced before execution) with the Sentry hardware watchdog, under the Linux Foundation-governed Open Secure AI Alliance (source: https://www.storagereview.com/news/nvidia-open-agent-safety-platform-openshell-sentry-bluefield-4, Sep 2026)

Contradictions:
- NVIDIA argues an agent cannot be trusted to monitor itself and places checks in hardware, while AISI's deployed monitor is itself an LLM reviewing the agent's reasoning; the new assurance layer is partly built from the kind of component it audits (compare the METR/Redwood use of AI agents in the 25 Sep sweep).
- Reports list OpenAI as absent from the NVIDIA launch partner list while another report says OpenAI participates in OpenShell; the two accounts were not reconciled against an NVIDIA primary page (nvidia.com agent-safety page returned no usable partner list), so neither is used as fact.
- Nvidia's pending Hugging Face purchase and anchor stake in Anthropic's IPO sit against Hugging Face's offer to audit labs, which tests what "independent" means for embedded evaluation (single outlet, unverified against a company filing).
