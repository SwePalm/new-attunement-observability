Confirmed Developments:
- Finland's law implementing the European Digital Identity Wallet entered into force on 1 Oct 2026: the Digital and Population Data Services Agency will provide a free wallet whose first credential is a national digital ID based on valid passports or ID cards, Traficom will supervise providers and keep a service registry, and the wallet is expected in 2027 (source: https://www.biometricupdate.com/202610/finland-bulgaria-establish-oversight-for-eudi-wallet-but-differ-in-practical-readiness, Oct 2026) (source: https://mobileidworld.com/finland-sets-rules-for-a-free-phone-based-identity-wallet-expected-in-2027/, Oct 2026)
- South Africa's Home Affairs Minister Leon Schreiber demonstrated the first working prototype of the national smartphone digital ID on 1 Oct 2026 at Constitution Hill (QR-code selective sharing, biometric verification, MyMzansi app), with green ID book production ending 31 Mar 2027 and books invalid from 31 Mar 2028; citizens cannot yet obtain the digital ID and no public issuance date was given (source: https://techcentral.co.za/south-africa-digital-id-schreiber-demo/286764/, Oct 2026) (source: https://www.ewn.co.za/home-affairs-quantum-leap-schreiber-unveils-1st-working-prototype-of-digital-id/, Oct 2026)
- NSW announced on 30 Sep 2026 an A$11.5 million pilot upgrade of the NSW Digital Driver Licence starting in Western Sydney and the Blue Mountains: venues scan a Service NSW app QR code for instant age confirmation without further personal data, over 19,000 residents have registered, Panthers Penrith is among the first venues, and a statewide rollout is planned for 2027 (source: https://www.miragenews.com/digital-licence-upgrade-for-western-sydney-and-1752293/, Sep 2026) (source: https://www.biometricupdate.com/202610/nsw-to-upgrade-digital-drivers-licenses-to-enable-proof-of-age-with-selective-disclosure, Oct 2026)
- Germany's d-you preparations continue on the 2 Jan 2027 date: about 80 organisations have signed the memorandum of understanding and over 120 companies are giving practical feedback, with identity and age verification at launch (source: https://www.iphone-ticker.de/d-you-diese-partner-bereiten-sich-auf-den-start-im-januar-vor-287125/, Sep 2026) (source: https://www.biometricupdate.com/202609/eudi-wallet-deadline-nears-as-europes-rollout-picture-remains-uneven, Sep 2026)

Emerging Signals:
- Enterprise identity vendors are repositioning around agent action control: Cisco's Duo is described as treating agents as first-class identities with OAuth-scoped permissions and MCP authorization, and Cisco's identity product VP argues for time-bound, action-specific permissions instead of application-level access; single trade-press source, product date not stated (source: https://venturebeat.com/security/ai-agents-need-more-than-access-control-they-need-identity-at-runtime, Oct 2026)
- Deepfake forensics is being sold into identity-verification workflows: Cyberette (Amsterdam) launched on 30 Sep 2026 a platform that localises manipulated regions and flags face swaps and cloned voices through an API; the 98 percent accuracy figure is an unverified company claim (source: https://www.biometricupdate.com/202610/cyberette-launches-deepfake-forensics-platform-to-show-where-media-was-manipulated, Oct 2026)
- A reported 2026 voice-cloning fraud keeps the case for voice deprecation alive: IBTimes (30 Sep 2026) describes a fake WhatsApp message and an AI-cloned lawyer voice that led Fideuram's chairman to authorise about EUR 95 million of transfers in Feb 2026, with about EUR 36 million still missing; single outlet, event predates the window, and it shows process failure rather than a bank retiring voice checks (source: https://www.ibtimes.co.uk/ai-voice-cloning-bank-fraud-108-million-1822570, Sep 2026)

Counter-Signals:
- The EUDI end-2026 deadline looks unmet at scale: IN Groupe's Kristian Thure-Sorensen does not expect a big bang by year end, technical standards alignment is put at 50 to 60 percent, Sweden is not expected before 2028, and Germany's launch functionality will be very limited (source: https://www.biometricupdate.com/202609/eudi-wallet-deadline-nears-as-europes-rollout-picture-remains-uneven, Sep 2026)
- A prototype is not an issuance: South Africa's digital ID is a demonstration, public credentials are tied to the 2027/28 financial year, and what is live today is Smart ID cards via 515 bank branches (source: https://techcentral.co.za/south-africa-digital-id-schreiber-demo/286764/, Oct 2026)
- US banks are permitted but not required to accept digital credentials: the five-agency FAQs carry no mandate or deadline, and American Banker's only named in-branch acceptor is America First Credit Union (since Aug 2022) (source: https://www.americanbanker.com/news/five-agencies-say-banks-may-accept-mobile-drivers-licenses, Sep 2026)

Regulatory Shifts:
- Finland's EUDI law took effect on 1 Oct 2026 (see Confirmed Developments); Bulgaria's Council of Ministers approved development steps and appointed the Minister of Innovation and Digital Transformation as supervisory authority, and is reported unlikely to meet the deadline (source: https://www.biometricupdate.com/202610/finland-bulgaria-establish-oversight-for-eudi-wallet-but-differ-in-practical-readiness, Oct 2026) (source: https://mobileidworld.com/finland-sets-rules-for-a-free-phone-based-identity-wallet-expected-in-2027/, Oct 2026)
- On 8 Sep 2026 FinCEN, the Federal Reserve, FDIC, NCUA and OCC published joint FAQs confirming that banks and credit unions may treat an unexpired government-issued verifiable digital credential, including a state mobile driver's licence, as government-issued identification under the Customer Identification Program rule if it is signed by the issuer, device-bound and protected by an activation factor; the rule neither requires nor prohibits acceptance.
