# Structural question, meaning, 2026-10-02

Structural Question:
Over the next 6 to 24 months, as California's September 2026 AI workplace and clinical statutes come into force, will legal protection for human judgment in AI-assisted work attach to the role (the licensed professional kept "advisory", the discipline notice that says a human reviewed it) or to the individual who dissents from the system's output, and until a successor to the vetoed retaliation protection passes, will the cost of an override be set by each employer's own record-keeping and by whatever a collective agreement happens to say?

Why It Matters:
On 30 Sep 2026 the Governor signed AB 1979 (Chapter 854, AI kept in an advisory role and barred from replacing licensed clinical judgment), SB 947 (Chapter 859, human review noticed on automated discipline, effective 1 Jul 2027), SB 951 and AB 1883 on the same day he vetoed AB 2575, the bill that would have protected a care worker from retaliation for overriding or relying on clinical AI (meaning-2026-08-05 graded falsified this run; the veto reasons were not read). The same session enacted AB 1609 (Chapter 733, a good faith effort to connect a customer to a person within 15 minutes; meaning-2026-09-01 graded confirmed, forward, pre-announced), so the statutes now protect the existence of a human at the role and at the counter while leaving the person who acts as that human unprotected on the day they disagree (source: https://www.gov.ca.gov/2026/09/30/governor-newsom-issues-legislative-update-9-30-2026/, Sep 2026) (source: https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/billStatusClient.xhtml?bill_id=202520260AB1979, Sep 2026). Where protection is moving fastest is contractual and local, not statutory: the Verve Coffee Roasters tentative first contract carries a clause that AI cannot be used to make decisions about employees, while Kaiser's Los Angeles bargaining summary lists no AI item (source: https://lookout.co/inside-verve-coffee-workers-tentative-union-contract-higher-sick-pay-ai-limits-and-guaranteed-hours/story, Sep 2026) (source: https://about.kaiserpermanente.org/who-we-are/labor-relations/cna-bargaining/cna-bargaining-lamc/cna-talks-continue-at-los-angeles-medical-center, Jul 2026). The aggregate displacement story is cooling at the same moment (AI at about 9% of September cuts against a May peak near 40%), which removes the headline pressure that usually carries labour bills (source: https://www.challengergray.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Challenger-Report-September-2026.pdf, Oct 2026).
