# theme-exploration

## Purpose

Produce a structured philosophical exploration of the theme, grounded in
the structural question and the evidence sweep.

This is interpretive but must remain anchored to evidence.

## Input

theme: string
structural_question: string
sweep_output: output/YYYY-MM-DD/02-sweep/<theme>.md

## Output Format (strict)

Turn 1 – Conceptual Frame
<300+ words>

Turn 2 – Societal Reframing
<300+ words>

Turn 3 – Psychological Implications
<300+ words>

Turn 4 – Institutional Dynamics
<300+ words>

Turn 5 – Long-Term Trajectory
<300+ words>

## Constraints

- No divider markers.
- No meta-commentary.
- No user Q&A simulation.
- No invented follow-up questions.
- Anchor insights to the sweep; reference at least 3 distinct sweep items
  across the five turns (rephrased for readability is fine).
- Maintain near-future framing (0–5 years).
